“Is it great to be obstinate? I thought fools were generally obstinate.” To which I reply, “Fools are obstinate in their folly, and great men in their wisdom.” If Columbus had not been obstinate, America might not have been discovered; and if Galileo had not been obstinate, we might yet have believed that the earth stood still. Obstinacy, in short, is a powerful <i>pièce de resistance</i>, which one would rather see in the hands of a friend than of an enemy.

Miss Clara at first hesitated whether she would invite the Edwardses or not; but we all know the effects of example in this world of imitation.
