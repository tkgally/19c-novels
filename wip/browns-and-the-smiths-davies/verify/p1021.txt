“And yet he always pretends to despise Goslingford,” thought Miss Julietta, with an inward smile at the illustration afforded even by so great a man as her brother, of the weakness of human nature.

Shortly after this conversation, Mr. Edgar Smith sauntered into the town, and dropped in upon the Westcotes just as they were sitting down to tea. Their unfeigned satisfaction at seeing him, felt soothing to him at that minute; yet there was something in Miss Westcote’s dreadfully fashionable flounces and exaggerated style of hair, which jarred upon his taste. Then she giggled, and there was such a want of repose about her. Still the warmth and cordiality of both the mother and daughter were now very pleasant to Edgar Smith.
