It was the first time of their appearance on the ice, and the first time Hannah had met Mr. Edwards since her father had spoken to her about him. Mr. Edwards’s back had been towards her, and he had not heard her coming. Two days already had he been there with the hope of meeting her, and had hitherto been disappointed. So noiselessly had the two new-comers advanced, that he was only made aware of their presence by Miss Clara’s voice, as she answered the last remark—
