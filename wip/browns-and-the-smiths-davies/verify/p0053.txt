People would laugh at her, and say she was copying the Smiths, and “anything rather than that,” thought Hannah Brown. And besides, surely her own style of dress became her better; and though she scarcely acknowledged it to herself, and doubtless she might be mistaken, she instinctively preferred her own appearance to that of the Miss Smiths.
