Mrs. Splint “remembered—it could not have been more than a week ago—that she had seen him devoted to Hannah Brown.” Even Mr. Greenfield, who rarely made a remark about such things, confessed that, “Nothing had ever surprised him more in his life;” and though he and Mr. Edwards continued perfectly friendly, their intimacy seemed at once to decrease. Mrs. and Miss Westcote confessed themselves “highly amused, though at the same time not the least surprised. They had never thought there was any truth in the report about Hannah Brown. They were very sorry for her to have twice made herself a laughing-stock; and as to Miss Wellby, they really wondered how she could make such a goose of herself.”
