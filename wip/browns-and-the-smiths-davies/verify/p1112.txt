[[cont]]Westcote instantly did the same, and then Edgar bade him go home to his mother, and dare ever in his life to come near that cliff again. And the boy, blubbering and bewildered, and apparently amazed at the possession of so much money, pulled his forelock sheepishly, and scampered away on the ice.

“And thank God you are safe!” said the two Miss Smiths with fervour. To which Edgar answered, loud enough for everybody to hear, but addressing no one in particular—

“If I had died, I should have died happy.”
