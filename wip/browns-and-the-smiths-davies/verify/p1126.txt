“Indeed, dear Clara, you do me injustice; but you know if I don’t think with you about Mr. Edwards and Hannah Brown, how can I say I do? I am very sorry for poor Hannah.”

“Poor Hannah!” said Miss Clara, indignantly, and seizing her cloak and bonnet, would not be persuaded to remain another instant, even to partake of mulled port wine and biscuits, which poor Harriet pressed upon her almost with tears.

The following morning Miss Wellby had the additional trial of communicating the disagreeable intelligence to Mr. Brown. She went to his office for the purpose. Miss Clara was pained by the effect it had on him.
