[[cont]]actions are as often determined by our impulses as by our intentions.

The same evening that Mr. Edwards spent with Miss Wellby, Hannah and her father passed alone together as usual. Hannah had rather a dread now of evenings alone with her father. They had never at any time been particularly lively, but lately they had had an effect on her spirits which she could not shake off. This evening in particular she dreaded. It was not like Mr. Brown; but still, if he should refer to the subject of their conversation the previous day, how very disagreeable it would be!
