Miss Wellby was not at home, and, though he had persuaded himself that he should have disliked extremely to meet Hannah Brown, yet, no sooner did he know it was impossible, than he felt an additional pang of disappointment. He walked slowly through the town, on his way home, feeling now that “every link between them was severed.”

He was just passing the gates of Tudor Lodge when he was accosted by Miss Laura Victoria Smith, who was coming in an opposite direction.

“How do you do, Mr. Edwards? Mamma is at home. Were you coming to call?”
