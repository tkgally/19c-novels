“I will call for them in the course of the day, and bring the cheque with me.”

“You are going at three o’clock,” said Miss Westcote.

“No; I am afraid I cannot have the pleasure of returning with your mother and yourself, as I had hoped at one time. I may possibly be obliged to dine with a friend, and altogether I think it will be better to make no engagement. I think Mrs. Westcote must be at Slade’s by this time—perhaps we had better not keep her waiting.”

“Oh, mamma is always late. She will be so disappointed you cannot go back with us, after our being so fortunate, too, as to meet accidentally.”
