[[cont]]very ordinary Hannah Brown of all people in the world—Hannah Brown, whom even that zealous Smithite, Miss Richards, praised because she “never flirted.” Ah! Miss Richards,—

<verse>
“Things are not what they seem.”
</verse>

Quiet, sensible Hannah Brown, who never dressed fine, would have had no objection to flirt, as you call flirting, if she could have found an eligible opportunity; for—

<verse>
“The lads like lasses,
And the lasses, lads too”
</verse>

And were they not meant to do so?
