Mr. Brown and Edgar Smith were both standing. Edgar at the window, apparently looking out on the church-yard; Mr. Brown at a writing-table in the middle of the room.

Hannah dared not look at her father’s face; but she instinctively felt it was a bad omen that her lover did not advance to meet her, or even turn to her on her entrance. He made, however, an involuntary twitching movement as he heard the door open and close. Hannah took hold of the back of a chair. The moment was sickening.
