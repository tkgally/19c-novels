“I am very much obliged to you indeed, sir, for your kindness.”

“You are most welcome, Mr. Brown,” said the young man, with more outward self-possession, but probably with not less real perturbation, “I only wish that it were in my power to do you a kindness.”

Mr. Brown looked full in the young man’s face as he spoke, and could not deny to himself that its expression was ingenuous and sincere.

“You are very good, sir,” he said, “and I thank you.”
