And these remarks somewhat consoled the two Miss Smiths for the quietness of their brother’s wedding, which, as contrasted with that of Mary Westcote, had slightly mortified them. They were sadly afraid, notwithstanding their brother’s continental education, that he might, after all, fall into dowdy, Brown ways.

Mr. and Mrs. Edgar Smith took up their abode, with old Mr. Brown, in the Old Red House. Many persons prophesied that this arrangement “would never answer.” It did answer, however, pretty well.
