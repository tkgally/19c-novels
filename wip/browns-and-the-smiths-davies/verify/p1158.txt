“Give up dissent! My dear Edgar! is religion a thing of mere family alliances and politeness?”

“What is it a thing of then?”

“Of the conscience, my dear. How can you ask?”

“What about Laura’s conscience, then?”

“Laura has always had a strong inclination to belong to the Church, so it is all right there.”

“All right with her soul, you mean?”

“Yes, of course. It would be very illiberal and unchristian, and copying the worse portion of Church people, to doubt that they may be quite as good Christians as ourselves.”
