He was not exactly Miss Clara’s type either of man or clergyman; still she recognised in him a gentleman, and Miss Clara was always glad to have anyone to speak to. So, while the storm lasted, she related to him, in her own voluble style, how they had been touring, how they had walked up to see the view, and had been caught in the storm; descanting on her own nervousness about a thunderstorm, her responsibility in having the care of Hannah, her anxiety on her account, and her earnest desire to take her home to her father, whose only child she was, in good health.
