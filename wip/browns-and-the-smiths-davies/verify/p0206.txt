Poor Clara Wellby sat in dismay. She was cross and perturbed, and when Edgar handed Hannah to her carriage, shawling and cloaking her with the deepest interest, and beseeching her not to catch cold, she fumed and chafed and almost scolded.

“Hannah,” she said, as they drove off, “I have seldom spent such a night of vexation and annoyance. You know I warned you at the very beginning what this folly and nonsense would lead to.”
