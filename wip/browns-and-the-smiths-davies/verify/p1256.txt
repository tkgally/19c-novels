Mr. Thomas Smith actually started at the suddenness of this announcement.

“Propose to Hannah Brown! The boy is mad!”

“I never was more collected in my life, sir.”

“And pray, what may be the reason of this sudden freak?”

“It is neither a freak, sir, nor sudden. I have always preferred Miss Brown to any other woman in the world. I think more highly of her than ever, and if I cannot marry her, I will marry no one else.”

“And Miss Westcote. Do you think you are using her well? It is always discreditable to a young man to trifle with a woman’s feelings. I will have no hearts broken by my son.”
