“My dear Mr. Brown!” she said, “are you ill?”

“I don’t know, Clara. I hardly know anything. I—I should like to be alone. Thank you, Clara.”

Clara felt obliged to go; but as she went out she told the head clerk she thought Mr. Brown was very ill.

She then went home. She felt so angry with Hannah she would not trust herself to go to see her then; and yet her heart softened to her when she thought of how ill her father looked.
