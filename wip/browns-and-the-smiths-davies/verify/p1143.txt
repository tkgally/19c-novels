There was a magic lantern, and music of a trifling character, and other little things savouring too much of “the world.” But Mr. Greenfield insisted it did good, that it brought people together, promoted brotherly feeling, and showed that the rich were not indifferent to the enjoyment of the poor, and did not despise sharing it with them.

Perhaps Mr. Greenfield’s opinion weighed more with Miss Richards than his arguments. In fact Miss Richards did not greatly differ from the majority of mankind, and more especially of womankind, in this respect. With most persons, it is not so much what is said, as the man who says it, that carries weight.
