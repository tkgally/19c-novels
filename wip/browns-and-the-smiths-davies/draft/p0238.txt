As the spring advanced, Hannah became worse and worse. She looked miserably weak and ill. Clara Wellby groaned and bemoaned, and fussed about her looks and her want of appetite, till Mr. Brown himself became alarmed. Mr. Brown was not easily alarmed, but if he once gave way to anxiety it was apt to become excessive. It was a long time ere he would admit that there was anything the matter with Hannah; but when at last the fact was forced upon his belief, his nervousness lest anything should befall this, his sole remaining child, knew no bounds.
