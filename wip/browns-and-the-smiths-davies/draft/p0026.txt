I would not, however, have the reader to suppose, from anything I have said, that Miss Clara laughed at religion, for that would be giving an altogether wrong idea of her character. She only laughed at her friend’s fashion of it; and her friend thought it could not be genuine in any other fashion.

One point, however, these ladies had in common—they were very fond of a thorough good gossip.

“What are our tongues made for but to talk?” said Miss Clara.

“Yes, dear Clara, but,” sighing, “we must not forget that the tongue is a world of iniquity.”

“It is St. James says that, Harriet, and I thought all your kind of people did not think much of St. James.”
