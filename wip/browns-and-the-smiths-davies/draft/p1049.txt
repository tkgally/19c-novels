To reward her brother for his returning amiability, Venetia now said—

“You joke about women being so fond of clergymen, but all women are not. Mary Westcote does not like clergymen, and says she would rather be anything than a clergyman’s wife. She does not admire Mr. Edwards at all, will hardly even allow that he has fine eyes, which shows she must either be blind herself or have a prejudiced mind.”
