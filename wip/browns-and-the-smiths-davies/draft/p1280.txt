Hannah was in good spirits this morning. The post had brought her a note from Mr. Bradley, telling her he had sold two of her sketches for five guineas each; and on this foundation she had begun to build many little castles in the air. Then her thoughts for a moment took a less agreeable turn, as the sale of her sketches suggested the remembrance of Mr. Bradley’s shop, and Mr. Bradley’s shop brought back the picture of Edgar Smith bending down to talk to Mary Westcote, in her bright, fashionable dress.
