“I declare, Mr. Silk, there is young Smith going past again! Have you remarked how often he has been passing lately?”

“Well, I have now, Miss Griffin. Is the match on with Miss Brown again, do you think?”

“Oh, la! no, Mr. Silk. I should think not, considering the way he was used before, and how fond the Smiths are of money. I am afraid Miss Brown has not much chance now, poor thing!”
