“Hannah, have you ever thought what the world will be to you alone when I am gone?”

She got up, and sat down on a footstool at his feet.

“Is that what troubles you, papa?”

He did not answer; but Hannah was not quite satisfied with herself. She wondered if Mr. Edwards, too, were very unhappy—then she remembered she might have made both him and her father happy.

Dull as her life now was, how frightfully lonely it would be without her father!

She wished—she wished she could have made him happy.
