“I thought so; but I am sure I don’t know why, people don’t generally admire her.”

“Don’t they? If you don’t know why, it is not to be wondered at that I don’t know why either. Do you think her pretty?”

“I! Oh no; nobody does, that I know of.”

“I will tell you of one person, then, who does.”

“Oh, pray do. Who?”

“I do.”
