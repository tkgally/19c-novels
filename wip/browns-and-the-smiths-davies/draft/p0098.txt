As they returned to the house, Miss Wellby remarked—

“I never saw anybody so changed as Edgar Smith, but five years is a long time at his age. How ridiculous his moustache looks! I have no patience with such foreign airs, though he seems a nice enough young man, too. But all the Smiths are so—so—”
