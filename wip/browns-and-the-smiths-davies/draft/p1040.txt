[[cont]]Westcote meanwhile bridled and smiled. Hannah Brown said nothing, showed no emotion; but tender-hearted Miss Richards fancied she became pale.

The drawing-room door was opened, but instead of Mr. Edgar Smith, to the surprise of everyone present, Miss Clara’s well-trained maid announced, “Mr. Edward Edwards.”

A bombshell falling in the midst of the little party could scarcely have created a greater sensation. Miss Clara rushed forward with exclamations of surprise and welcome; and if Hannah Brown had been pale before, she became scarlet now.
