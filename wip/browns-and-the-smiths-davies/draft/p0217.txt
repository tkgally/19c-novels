For a moment there was a pause.

If William Brown had been pale, his daughter became many degrees paler; but her trembling and her nervousness were over for the moment. She stood upright, and said in a firm though very low tone:

“Then, father, I will not marry him.”

But now Edgar started forward, his eyes sparkling, and his chest heaving: “You will not marry me! I could not have believed this unless I had heard it from your own lips—I could not have believed that you—you would have been so heartless and cruel!”

Hannah burst into tears—this was more than she could bear.

“Oh, Edgar! I am not heartless. If you knew—Don’t reproach me, for I cannot bear it!”
