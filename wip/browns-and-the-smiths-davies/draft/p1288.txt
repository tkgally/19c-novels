But their circle was of course not the circle of the Browns and the Smiths, though it was, to do it justice, quite as much interested as if it had been. Clara Wellby was at first a little hurt that she had not been more confided in. But when it was explained to her how there had been nothing to confide, she was pacified, nay delighted. “Hannah would be provided for—Hannah would be married—much happier for Hannah, who had not <i>her</i> independence of feeling or expansiveness of interest.”
