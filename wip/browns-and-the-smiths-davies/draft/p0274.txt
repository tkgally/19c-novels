“I did not say it was as pretty—I only said—”

“I have a great mind, Hannah, to go and tell that lazy high-cheek-boned-looking Welshman that he ought to be ashamed to stand there smoking his pipe, and looking contentedly at all that rubbish, instead of removing it at once.”

“Oh, no, Aunt Clara, pray don’t. It will only—”

Here Hannah was interrupted by a peal of thunder.

“Thunder!” cried Clara. “Let us go home at once.”
