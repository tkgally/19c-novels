“Then I must tell you myself. Do not waste your feelings upon me.”

“Do you leave me no hope then—not any?” said poor Mr. Edwards, hardly believing it.

“None,” said Hannah, with a forcible brevity which was unmistakable.

It was a cruel word. At another time Hannah could hardly have been so cruel; but now there was but one subject on which she could feel, and this absorbing emotion had made her selfish. When about to risk his life, Edgar Smith had thought of her. He had said if he had been killed he should have died happy!
