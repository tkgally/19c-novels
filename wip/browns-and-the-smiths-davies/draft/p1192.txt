I must bear with her little obstinacies.” And then, good Miss Clara, with as much reason as many other people, congratulated herself on her own magnanimity, and maintained to the world how Hannah Brown was little short of an angel.

But when the announcement Mr. Brown had made to his creditors became public, Clara’s admiration knew no bounds.
