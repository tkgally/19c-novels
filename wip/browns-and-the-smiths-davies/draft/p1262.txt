“But he is a fine old fellow, too, sir. I must say so, in spite of his treatment of me. And you must confess, my dear father, his daughter has acted nobly.”

“Very romantically, Edgar; and if I had been her father, I do not know that I should have thought myself justified in permitting her to make such a sacrifice.”

“Oh! do not say so, my dear father! You would, I am sure. Such a sacrifice ought to be accepted when it is offered, though, perhaps, not many—perhaps nobody but Hannah Brown herself, would have thought of making it.”
