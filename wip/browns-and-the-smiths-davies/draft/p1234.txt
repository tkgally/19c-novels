It was pleasant to her, too, to believe that even if Edgar Smith loved her no longer, he understood her, and wished to be understood by her. Now that the Edwardses were gone, and that Goslingford had settled down to its usual state of calm monotony, she rarely ever saw him, and never except in the street. It was still generally believed that he was engaged to Mary Westcote; but for the present, the public mind was composed upon the subject, and had arranged that the wedding would likely take place in the summer.
