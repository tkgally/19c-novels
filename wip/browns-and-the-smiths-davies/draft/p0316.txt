My dear reader, if you are like Hannah, one whose thoughts cling to the past, and in whom memory is stronger than hope, you will, perhaps, say there is nothing so noble as constancy; but if, on the contrary, you are an admirer of strong common sense, and take a practical, I don’t say a worldly, view of the advantages and disadvantages of life, you will say that true wisdom and true religion really lie in Miss Clara’s theory of making the best of things. If you ask me what I think, what can I answer, but confess that, according to my mood, I have sometimes been of the one opinion and sometimes of the other; and so I suspect had Miss Clara, who, like most preachers, had not, at all times acted up to her own sermons.
