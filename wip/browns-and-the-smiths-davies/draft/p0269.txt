“But don’t expect me, Hannah,” said Miss Clara, “to be either a Lady Eleanor or a Miss Ponsonby. You know I have no turn for romantic seclusion. Mountains may be very well, but men and women are better. I wonder how often Lady Eleanor and her friend quarrelled!”

“Never, I should think, Aunt Clara, or they would not have remained so long together.”

“Impossible, my dear; I tell you they must have quarrelled. Only fancy Harriet Richards and me living alone together in a Welsh valley!”

Hannah burst into the heartiest laugh she had had for many a day.

“Indeed, Aunt Clara, I cannot fancy it.”
