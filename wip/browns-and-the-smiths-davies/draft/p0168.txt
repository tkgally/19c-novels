[[cont]]is never conscious of defeat? When you imagined you had beaten Miss Clara to the dust with a sledge-hammer on one side, there she was on the other, aiming her arrows at you as lively as ever, and with all the pride of victory.

According to her intention, she called on Hannah Brown the next morning.

“Hannah,” she said, “I do not approve of this foolish flirtation with Edgar Smith. It can come to no good. And if it were to go further——”

If it were to go further! Hannah’s heart beat at the ideas this “going further” vaguely raised in her mind, and then it suddenly sank. Nothing pleasant was right for her. It was the old story again.

“You must put a stop to it at once.”
