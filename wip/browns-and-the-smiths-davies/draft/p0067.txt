[[cont]]angry and very awkward, she said abruptly—

“I have never been anywhere but at Buttonborough, in my life.”

“Never been anywhere but at Buttonborough in your life!” and he looked infinitely amused, but too well-bred absolutely to laugh. “Why, you are the wonder of the age. I might have travelled all over Europe without meeting the one personage who has never been anywhere but at Buttonborough. You are indeed a <i>rara avis</i>.”

“Not an avis at all, I assure you,” said Hannah, blushing, and feeling a little indignation, at the same time not blind to the humorous light in which this, to her mortifying fact, might appear to the traveller; “if I had, I should have flown away long ago.”
