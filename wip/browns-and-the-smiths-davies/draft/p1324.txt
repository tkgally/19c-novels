## The New and Popular Novels, Published by Hurst & Blackett.

QUEEN MAB. By J<sc>ulia</sc> K<sc>avanagh</sc>, Author of “Nathalie,” “Adèle,” &c. 3 vols.

GOOD SOCIETY. By M<sc>rs</sc>. G<sc>rey</sc>, Author of “The Gambler’s Wife,” &c. 3 vols.

“‘Good Society,’ in all its various phases, comes in for a complete anatomisation at the hands of our author. That very many of the characters are sketched from life can admit of no doubt. The dialogue is lively, and the ‘interiors’ are painted with a well accustomed hand, whether the scene is laid in the marquis’s mansion, the squire’s hall, the country parsonage, the Romish chapel, or the lodging-house in Belgravia. Altogether, to our mind, this surpasses any of the former works of the writer.”—<i>United Service Magazine</i>. “Mrs. Grey writes well, and with spirit.”—<i>Post</i>.

MARY LYNDSAY. By L<sc>ady</sc> E<sc>mily</sc> P<sc>onsonby</sc>.

“The best-written and most interesting of the author’s works.”—<i>Post</i>.

“An original and graceful story. The character of Mary Lyndsay is most beautifully and nobly conceived.”—<i>John Bull</i>.

LOST AND SAVED. By THE H<sc>on</sc>. M<sc>rs</sc>. N<sc>orton</sc>. Fourth Edition. With a Letter from the Author. 3 vols.

“‘Lost and Saved’ will be read with eager interest by those who love a touching story. It is a vigorous novel.”—<i>The Times</i>.

LEFT TO THEMSELVES. By the Author of “Cousin Geoffrey,” &c. 3 vols.

“A very graphic, forcible, and interesting work.”—<i>Sun</i>. “One of the most charming tales we have seen for some time.”—<i>John Bull</i>.

FLORIAN’S HUSBAND. 3 vols.

THE BROWNS AND THE SMITHS. By the Author of “Anne Dysart,” &c. 2 vols.

A WIFE’S EVIDENCE. By W. G. W<sc>ills</sc>, Esq., Author of “Notice to Quit,” &c. 3 vols. (In November.)

CHURCH AND CHAPEL. By the Author of “High Church,” “No Church,” and “Owen: a Waif.” 3 vols.

“This novel sustains the credit of the author’s previous works. It is a story of well-sustained interest.”—<i>Athenæum</i>.

VERONIA. 3 vols.

“This novel exhibits no mean powers of conception and expression.”—<i>Post</i>.

RESPECTABLE SINNERS. By M<sc>rs</sc>. B<sc>rotherton</sc>, Author of “Arthur Brandon.” 3 vols.

“‘Respectable Sinners’ is very clever: the title is a happy one. The style is bright and lively. It is a readable, entertaining book.”—<i>Athenæum</i>.

LIVE IT DOWN. By J. C. J<sc>eaffreson</sc>, Third Edition. Revised. 3 vols.

“This story will satisfy most readers. The interest goes on increasing to the last page. It is by far the best work of fiction Mr. Jeaffreson has written.”—<i>Athenæum</i>

HEART AND CROSS. By the Author of “Margaret Maitland.” 1 vol.

“A delightful work. The interest is preserved from the opening to the closing page.”—<i>Post</i>.

THREE LIVES IN ONE. 3 vols.

“This story abounds with life and character.”—<i>Sun</i>.

VICISSITUDES OF A GENTLEWOMAN. 3 v.

“A pleasant tale, naturally told.”—<i>Sun</i>.
