Miss Clara professed to despise romance, and to stick to matter-of-fact. She pronounced novels very good to read of a winter’s night; but very bad to make precedents of for the actions of real life. But Miss Clara was, like many other people, false to her own theory. This very affair of Hannah Brown had to her all the interest of a novel, combined with the stronger interest her warm heart took in the welfare of a girl, whom she had come to regard almost with the affection due to a daughter.
