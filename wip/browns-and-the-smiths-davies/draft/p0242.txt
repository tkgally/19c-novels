“I do not see what good change could do her. Where could she possibly be so comfortable as at home? She must have everything she wants, and if she has not, let it be got for her.”

“But young people want—everybody, Mr. Brown, wants change sometimes.”

“I am sure I don’t; and I thought you agreed with me, Clara, about the restless, unsatisfied spirit of the present day, which is for ever driving people away from their homes and their duties.”

“So I do, Mr. Brown. All this going to and fro upon the earth reminds me of Satan in the days of Job; and these snorting, murderous, fire-vomiting trains are surely the devil’s carriages.
