“And so you were acting for your child’s happiness. Who can doubt it, when one looks on the heartless way he has behaved? And she will be happy—happy with a better man.”

“Do you think so, Clara?” with a painful eagerness, “that is what I doubt. She says she will never marry. I don’t understand girls, but—but I think she means it.”

“Nonsense, my dear sir; you may well say you don’t understand girls. When did she say so?”
