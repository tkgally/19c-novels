As Miss Clara had a very high character for truthfulness, neither Miss Harriet nor any one else could doubt her after so positive an assertion; but no further light on the interesting subject was to be gained from her. Indeed, she feared her zeal had already led her to say rather too much. And so the Goslingford world was left to the solution of the puzzle presented by this new phase of the vexed question. There appeared to be no way of deciding it but by appealing to Edgar Smith himself; and even the curiosity of Goslingford, inimical or friendly as the case might be, was not quite capable of such effrontery.
