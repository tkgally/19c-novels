“Quite well—of course,—why do you ask?” in a tone which, though not ungentle (for he was never ungentle to Hannah), was yet annoyed, and evidently intended to forbid any further questioning on the subject. So Hannah said no more; but the dreary winter days were made drearier by this new and uncommunicated anxiety. Some circumstances, however, occurred about this time which tended a little to distract her attention.
