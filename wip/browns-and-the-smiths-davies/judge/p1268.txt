“It is a great deal better than Hannah Brown,” said Venetia, “I could never have got on with her—such a dowdy, too! How Edgar ever could admire her, I could not understand, but one never can understand men.”

“Laura used rather to admire her, you remember. Till she fell in love with Edward, she always agreed with Edgar. But there is no use in speaking about her now, as, of course, she is out of the question.”

“I should think so,” said Venetia, contemptuously.
