Hannah would have guessed the reason, and would have proved restive. Miss Clara had never been so accommodating and deferential to anybody before as she was at present to Hannah. Hannah perceived it, and guessed she had some end in view, and felt indignant; and, however unjust, the indignation extended itself to Mr. Edwards. She began to feel less uncomfortable at having made him unhappy—to doubt that she had made him unhappy at all. She still felt curious to see him again, and the curiosity was less accompanied with dread. Hannah knew full well that if Clara had abandoned all hope of her marrying Mr. Edwards, she would have been angry with her; instead of that, she bore even with something like petulance, which, I am ashamed to say, Hannah occasionally displayed.
