Since her death, up to the present time, he had not once seen the face of any woman beyond the pastoral Mollys and Jennys of his flock, and he had persuaded himself that he did not wish to see the face of one. In his own language, his heart was “wedded to the grave.” One might infer, however, from the eagerness with which he had sought the society of the two stranger ladies, that he was not quite dead to female charms, and to-night especially he looked so animated, that one would never have guessed that he was an inconsolable widower.
