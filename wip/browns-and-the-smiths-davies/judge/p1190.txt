The long straight walks, the lines of espaliers, she had been used to think so formal, the flowers which had seemed so old-fashioned, the sameness which had appeared so tedious, all now appealed to her heart with the claim of familiar and life-long association. It seemed sad, even to think that her sadness must now be endured apart from the objects which she had once fancied increased it, but which, with the strange perversity of human nature, she now viewed as the dearest and most soothing scenes on earth.
