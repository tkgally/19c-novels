# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] The Spring Winds.

H<sc>annah</sc> B<sc>rown</sc> had kept her resolution. She had not given way, she had not fainted, she had not fallen ill, or taken to her bed, or shut herself up from the world; but it had been a hard struggle.
