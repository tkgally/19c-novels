It was some minutes ere Mr. Brown answered his daughter, and in the meantime he seemed lost in thought. Then he said:—

“Hannah! I humbly thank God, who has given me such a child as you. And I pray Him that the blessing of a grateful father may rest on you for ever! But it would not be right to let you decide on impulse. Think the matter over for a week, and then let me know.”

She promised that she would, but she knew she should not change her mind.
