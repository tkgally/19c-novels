“She is certainly sufficiently without taste, to have been a dissenter.”

“Taste, my love!” said his mother, “I like good taste, and I hope,” looking round the handsome drawing-room, “I am not very deficient in that way; but what has taste to do with religion?”

“Very little with our religion, certainly, mother.
