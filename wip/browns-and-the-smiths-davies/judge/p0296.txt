Mr. Edwards said nothing, but he looked straight at Hannah, intensely gratified—so intensely gratified that she felt quite annoyed, and passed the rest of the evening in perfect silence. As for Miss Clara, she seemed to think Hannah had gained a complete victory on their side of the question, and made up, by her liveliness and talkativeness, for her young friend’s silence. Mr. Edwards, too, seemed in excellent spirits; but the argument appeared, for the time, to have lost all interest for him, and he replied to Miss Wellby almost at random.
