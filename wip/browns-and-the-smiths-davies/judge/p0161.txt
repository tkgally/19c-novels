It was a fine moonlight night, the sky without a cloud, and the midsummer twilight in the north-west seemed to struggle for ascendancy with the beams of the moon, which rose, round as a wheel, and looked over the chimney tops and the ancient gables into the cruciform streets of Goslingford.
