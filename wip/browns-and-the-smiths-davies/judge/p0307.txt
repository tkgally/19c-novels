But as Hannah spoke she could not conceal a slight accent of annoyance. He answered quickly, without a shade of ill-humour, but making no attempt to conceal his mortification—

“If it gives you no pleasure, Miss Brown, it will give me none. I thought it would have pleased you.”

Poor Hannah felt very ungrateful, for she remembered well having expressed a strong wish to climb this very hill, and how kindly he had promised to watch for a good opportunity for her.

“You are very kind,” she said, penitently; “and I should like extremely to see the fine view, if Aunt Clara does not mind.”

His face cleared up a little, though it was far indeed from having the look of entire satisfaction it had had before.
