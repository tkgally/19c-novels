“I rather wonder,” said Hannah, “how he could speak of his dead wife and his innermost feelings to such strangers.”

“I daresay he has nobody else to speak to about them. I don’t approve, however, of his visiting her grave in that way; it is quite morbid. People should not brood and sentimentalise over their sorrows, Hannah, as I have often told you. I will try to cure him as I have cured you, that is, if he is worth curing. I will ask the landlady about him.”
