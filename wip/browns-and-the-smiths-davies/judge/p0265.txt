This, of course, to romantic readers, if any such indeed have followed me so far in my prosaic tale, must appear very uninteresting in Hannah Brown. But then, you see, Hannah Brown was—Hannah Brown, and not Lady Clarinda Capulet, or Lady Meliora Montague. Doubtless, if she had been one of these exalted damsels, she would have found poor Miss Clara’s uncongeniality unbearable rather than amusing.
