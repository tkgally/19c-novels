“No, no, Harriet, it will never do. We should quarrel all day long. The sight of that heavy sideboard, and those great green blinds, would throw me into low spirits. I must have something cheerful to look at, and every time I got into the coach I should fancy I was going to a funeral. And then, you know, I must have my novel in the evening, and that would make you miserable, and you would think it so worldly in me to have my dresses made like other people, though, by the by, I understand it is quite the thing now for your very pious people to go visiting the poor, and preaching to miners, dressed out in silk and flowers and jewellery. Everybody must preach now-a-days, even fine ladies, so you see, it would be quite the thing for you, Harriet, to have a decent bonnet.”
