“He had been so disagreeable, she had a mind not to put up with him any longer.”

But she had often said this before.

Hannah now became more absent than ever. The scene on the ice all at once seemed to melt away into dreamland, and the lonely old heartbreak fell on her again.

Edgar, in the meantime, was as much occupied with the Edwards phenomenon as everybody else.

When the party broke up he took care of his two elder sisters, Mr. Edwards having already secured Miss Laura. All he said, however, on the subject was—

“I was so surprised to find you all at the church tea-party. What will happen next?”

Venetia laughed.
