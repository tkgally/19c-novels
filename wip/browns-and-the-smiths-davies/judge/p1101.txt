But not the wisest diplomatist—not even a Miss Clara—can foresee every thing that may happen. Had she only yielded to her own feeling of fatigue, after the first half hour on the ice, there is no saying how it might all have ended; but when Mr. Edwards pressed them to accompany the rest of the party further up the river, and Hannah did not seem disinclined, Miss Clara, to forward her own scheme, said she was not tired, and consented. Poor Miss Clara! she told a story, and was punished for it, as all story-tellers ought to be.
