Some days ago she had heard one of the Miss Smiths say her brother was going to Buttonborough on Wednesday, and as she and her mother had intended going for some time, they fixed on that day, and, as it turned out, went by the same train. Mary, as I have already said, was well-dressed and in a good humour. Edgar had seemed pleased enough at the rencounter, and not sorry to escort his pretty companion about in the fashionable streets; but now—
