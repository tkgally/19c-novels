“Edgar,” said the old man, thoughtfully, “once it was the fashion, more than it is now-a-days, to talk of happy old age reposing in the consciousness of a well-spent life. I have just been wondering if any one could look back on his life, and feel it well-spent; or if anyone ever did look forward with confidence resting on this.
