Hannah’s silence passed for assent, and he led her down the steps into the garden.

It was by no means a very tasteful garden; still, smooth shaven turf and bright flowers, however arranged, must always possess some beauty. The evening too was very fine—bright and clear as fine evenings generally are in early September—and the golden radiance of the autumn twilight had begun to give place to the colder light of stars, while the crescent moon grew every moment brighter as it sank in the darkening sky, over the fading meadows.
