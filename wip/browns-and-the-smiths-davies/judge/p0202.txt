Hannah Brown leant on the arm of Edgar Smith. He could feel that her fingers trembled a little, but that might have been from the heat and the dancing. His own heart, however, thumped so loudly that he fancied she must hear it.

“You have read ‘Romeo and Juliet,’ Miss Brown, have you not? though you have never had the advantage of being at a ladies’ college?” Ye—yes,” said Hannah, and he felt sure now her hands did tremble.

“And what do you think of the Montagues and Capulets?”

“Think of them!—How?”
