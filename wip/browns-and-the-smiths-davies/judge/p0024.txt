Miss Wellby spoke quickly. She was not fond of demonstrations, and was eager to prevent her friend pressing her offer. She could scarcely keep from embracing Harriet, and crying heartily as it was. Miss Richards now murmured something about “vanity,” on which Clara asked triumphantly—

“If there was not quite as much vanity in paying two shillings a yard for a ribbon, when one at one shilling would do just as well, as in putting it on like other people after it was bought?”
