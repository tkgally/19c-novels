Mr. Edwards had now been three weeks at Halton. During that time he had seen a good deal of Hannah Brown, but he did not feel that he had made the progress he had hoped. Three weeks only remained of the period he had yet to stay. In the uncertain state of his feelings he made a confidant of Miss Clara Wellby. She met him with warm but fussy sympathy, assured him that he should have her most cordial co-operation, and advised him to break the matter to Mr. Brown.
