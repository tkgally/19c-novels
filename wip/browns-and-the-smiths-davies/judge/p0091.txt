Miss Wellby started from her seat, upsetting her tea.

“Hannah Brown! Hannah Brown went to Buttonborough this morning, and was to come home by this very train. Harriet, I must go to Mr. Brown’s this instant. Oh, those trains! How different it was in the good old days of the Buttonborough mail!”
