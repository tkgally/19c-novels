Edgar Smith spoke gravely, and all the time he was speaking his eyes were fixed determinedly on the face of Hannah Brown. But he did not seem as if he expected her to answer, and the minute he had finished speaking, he turned his eyes in another direction, and Miss Harriet, who was much delighted to find him in so serious a frame of mind, said in her kindest manner—

“It is no new thing, dear Mr. Edgar, when we would do good, to find that evil is present with us.”

He listened to her with much respect, but answered, somewhat absently—

“I suppose not.”
