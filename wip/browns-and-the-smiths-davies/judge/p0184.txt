Poor Harriet returned no answer. This especial backsliding of the Smiths was a sore subject with her. If they had been any persons but themselves, she would have had, and would have declared, very decided opinions on the point. But the Smiths! And she thought and said it was some great and temporary delusion under which such pious people would not be permitted long to remain.
