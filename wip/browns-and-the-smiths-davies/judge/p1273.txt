And Mr. Silk and Miss Griffin stood still, together, watching with interest from the dusty staircase-window the meeting of the representatives of the families of Brown and Smith.

It was a windy day, more especially for the season, and it did seem too much for the unsteady steps of the poor old man. He was within twenty paces of Edgar Smith, and just passing the entrance of a narrow cross street, when a sudden gust of wind blew his hat off his head right across the way.
