A generation or two back Mr. Smith’s progenitor had been a clerk in the office of Mr. Brown’s grandfather, or great grandfather; but, having quarrelled, the Smith of that day set up for himself. While he had been with Mr. Brown it had always been supposed that he shared that gentleman’s sentiments; but, after the separation, it was discovered that his opinions were exactly antagonistic. He was immediately taken by the hand by the anti-Brown faction in the borough, and, ere long, was put forward for the town-council, and, in time, for the mayoralty.
