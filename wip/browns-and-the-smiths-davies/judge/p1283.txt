But Edgar Smith had now drawn close to her.

“He has left me then to tell you, I am here by his consent—Oh, Hannah! I am not changed. Are you?”

What Hannah answered, I cannot take upon myself to relate. In truth I am not sure that she answered at all. I can only say that in a few seconds she and Edgar Smith were seated together on the sofa, that her lips and cheeks were quite pale, and that she trembled violently. He held her hand, which was very cold, firmly in his; he had recovered his self-possession, and was speaking to her calmly. Probably he was setting her mind at rest about her father.
