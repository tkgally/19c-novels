“Oh! she has been one of a heathen family hitherto you think; and so to reward her for giving up the worship of idols of wood and stone, you are going to give her quantities of silver, not being one of those who can say, ‘Silver and gold have I none.’”

“Oh, Clara!” was poor Harriet’s rejoinder; and then Miss Wellby, satisfied with her triumph, entered with her friend into a lively discussion on the merits and beauties of the trays, salt-cellars, coffee-pot, &c., and then of the <i>on dits</i> of the Smith and Brown factions, who mostly found themselves, at present, between the horns of a dilemma.
