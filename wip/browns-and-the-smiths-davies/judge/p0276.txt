“But we should not have had time to get back.”

“Time! Don’t talk nonsense, Hannah. How can you be so provokingly cool?”

“Indeed, Aunt Clara, I am very hot.”

“All the worse! You will have a fever, or you will be struck with lightning. Don’t go near the tree, I say;” and Miss Clara, looking wildly about for shelter, seized the handle of the church door, and shook it as if she would break the door open.
