“My child!—my Hannah!” Then, turning fiercely to the lad, “Go, boy, this instant to the Queen’s Head, and order a fly and pair—their best horses—any money for them—quick! I must go and look for my child. She was my last” and his lip quivered.

“My dear Mr. Brown,” and poor Clara, as she spoke, was agitated and weeping, “there are hundreds, you know, in a train, and Hannah was first-class, and that is so much safer.”
