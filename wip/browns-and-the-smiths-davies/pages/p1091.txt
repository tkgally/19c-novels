[[cont]]to this moment she had felt something like irritation towards him; but now, when the step was irrevocably taken, irritation began to soften into pity.

Hannah brought the newspaper as usual to her father. He took it from her, but soon put it aside. He was seated in his own easy chair by the fire—Hannah at the opposite side, seeming to be busy with some needlework.

“Shall I read to you, papa?” she said.

“No—not to-night.”

And he seemed to sink into deep and depressing thought.

Hannah felt uncomfortable now as well as anxious, for she feared she had a share in creating his cares.
