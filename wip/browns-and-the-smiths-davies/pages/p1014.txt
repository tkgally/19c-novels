In the meantime, the Brownites were triumphant. Miss Clara’s hints were magnified in an outrageous manner. It was reported that Hannah Brown was engaged to a young clergyman of high family, most fascinating manner, extremely handsome, and with an excellent living. “It had been love at first sight,” some people said; “he had saved her life in a thunderstorm; and, what a good thing it was she had not thrown herself away on Edgar Smith.” And then, again, Hannah began to be spoken of as a pretty girl, as one who was much admired, though nobody could say exactly by whom; and, altogether, she was more talked about than was agreeable to Mrs. and Miss Westcote, or to herself, had she known it.
