And now a murmur of applause, checked only by respect, ran the round of the assembly, and then Mr. Brown, in his own methodical and practical way, proceeded to business; and it was soon apparent that Hannah’s fortune and the proceeds of the sale of the Old Red House would be more than sufficient to satisfy every claim. And then applause could no longer be restrained. Mr. Brown’s creditors crowded round him, shook hands with him, and thanked him.
