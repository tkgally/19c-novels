It did strike Edgar Smith that it was rather a pity that religious writers should imitate the style of a newspaper advertisement, as if they meant to puff off the Kingdom of Heaven. He did not, however, express this sentiment to Miss Richards, but took leave, well satisfied that he had gained the object of his visit.

The next day Miss Richards told her friend Clara, that she had had a visit from young Edgar Smith, and how very much she was pleased with him, and with his evident wish for a reconciliation with the Browns.
