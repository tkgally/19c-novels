“They framed Magna Charta, too,” said Edgar.

“Well, well, they were thought fools in my day, and it was not the fashion then for young people to be wiser than their parents. Somebody, I have no doubt, will tell us some day that Bloody Mary was a humane queen, and old King Harry a just ruler and a moral man, though he was fond of cutting off the heads of his wives.”
