Sensitive Hannah Brown blushed up to her forehead at her own awkwardness, and could scarcely find voice to say—

“I beg your pardon.”

“Oh! pray don’t mention it!” said the gentleman addressed, who was young and well-looking, though with a certain air of superciliousness, which was not altogether becoming, and made Hannah Brown feel more vexed still.
