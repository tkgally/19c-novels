But a few seconds’ reflection was enough to make him ashamed of this idea. Hannah Brown would not laugh at him; but perhaps she would pity him, and in the present juncture this was scarcely less offensive.

The conversation at the Smith breakfast table the next morning, again turned upon Mr. Edwards. Venetia and Julietta both expatiated upon his eyes, his voice, his figure, his pale complexion; and Laura Victoria listened with the deepest interest.
