“Well Harriet,—one thing I will allow in praise of Eliza Greenfield. She has not published a book yet about her own doings. In the good old days we used to be taught not to sound a trumpet before us; but now-a-days your alms-givers, and your missionary ladies, have all a printer coming up at their heels.”

But I have kept the reader waiting long enough for the appearance of the Smiths—not longer, however, than they kept Miss Richards and the rest of her guests.
