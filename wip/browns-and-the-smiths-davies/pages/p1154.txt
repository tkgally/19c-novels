In the meantime Mr. Edwards and Laura lagged behind.

The night was cold and damp, and dark and windy; but no night before had ever seemed so pleasant to Laura Smith. After she got home she did not go to bed, but sat up nearly all night talking to Venetia.

Mr. Edgar Smith now found Mr. Edwards almost domesticated in his father’s house. The two young men did not, however, get on much better than they had done in the previous part of their intercourse, which was chiefly, it must be confessed, the fault of Mr. Edgar Smith.
