The two elder Miss Smiths snuffed the air very much, and the sweep of their flounces created a greater gale than ever, when they heard of Hannah’s delicate health and projected journey. They did not say much, but their looks were eloquent enough, and seemed to proclaim how contemptible and silly Miss Brown was, and how differently they would have acted. Miss Laura Victoria was the only one of the family who spoke much on the subject, and it was to defend Hannah Brown. She “had not seen much of her, but she had always liked her.
