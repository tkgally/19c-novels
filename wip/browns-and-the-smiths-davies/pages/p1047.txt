“The most astonishing thing,” said Venetia, “is what he can see to admire in Hannah Brown.”

“That,” said Edgar, “is the only thing which is not astonishing.”

“My daughters,” said Mrs. Smith, “I am happy to say, do not understand such arts.”

“Mother,” said Edgar, “believe me, Miss Brown has no arts but her sweet face and gentle manners.”

Mrs. Smith and her daughters only replied by an incredulous look. Mrs. Smith said—

“I hope none of <i>my</i> daughters will ever marry a Puseyite parson.”

“They will probably never have the opportunity.”
