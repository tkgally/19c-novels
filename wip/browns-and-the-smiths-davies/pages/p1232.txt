Hannah gladly complied with this request, and left Buttonborough in tolerable spirits, though not very sanguine. She thought if they were to fetch ten shillings, or even five each, it would be a great help to her.

Two days after Hannah’s visit to Buttonborough, the post brought her a letter from Mr. Bradley. It was couched in the most polite terms. Her drawings had been much admired, and the president of the Archæological Society had proposed that they should be lithographed and published in a volume, of which he himself would furnish the letter-press.
