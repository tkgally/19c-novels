To do the elder Mr. Smith and his daughters justice, they tried to make the best of matters, but came at last to the conclusion that it was best to leave them alone.

Yet Edgar and Hannah were happy, though there were moments when they both thought themselves miserable. But these were only moments—and how many among us are happier? We all paint to ourselves a perfect picture of domestic happiness—an orderly house, where there are never ashes on the hearth or litter on the carpet
