At thirty years of age Harriet Richards was deeply impressed by the preaching of one of the Goslingford curates, and from that time forth gave up cards, novels, fashion, and might be seen constantly going about in a bonnet shaped like a Quaker’s, a lanky dress, and a hideous shawl, with a basket of tracts on her arm.
