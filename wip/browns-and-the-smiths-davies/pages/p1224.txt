[[cont]]Edgar Smith rose and mingled with them. Mr. Greenfield again began to talk to Hannah Brown, but she answered at random. All this time she had merely exchanged a bow with Edgar Smith—the sort of salutation with which one greets a slight acquaintance.

But the time had now come to disperse. The Smiths’ carriage was at the door for the elder and younger married pairs; but like most of the rest of the party, Venetia and her brother announced their intention of walking home. There was, of course, much bustle at the Smith departure, and the bride had many leave-takings to make, as it was almost her last appearance in Goslingford.
