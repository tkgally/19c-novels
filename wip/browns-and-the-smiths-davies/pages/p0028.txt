# Chapter II.

[[subtitle]] Hannah Brown.

W<sc>illiam</sc> B<sc>rown</sc> and [[corr Thoms|Thomas]] Smith were two solicitors, of great respectability, and the chief people in Goslingford. In everything they were rivals. At elections, Mr. Brown was agent for the Reds, and Mr. Smith for the Greens. Mr. Brown was a zealous Churchman, Mr. Smith a dissenter. Mr. Brown liked everything old—old houses, old gardens, old acquaintances, old fashions.
