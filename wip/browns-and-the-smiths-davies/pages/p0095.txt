They were now at the end of the alley which led from Mr. Brown’s house to one of the four main streets of Goslingford. Here he began to fume for the fly, long before the horses could possibly have been harnessed, while Clara comforted, expostulated, sympathised, and kept saying, for Miss Wellby could never be silent, that—

“These things could not have happened in the days of the Buttonborough Mail.”

Just as Mr. Brown’s impatience was getting beyond all bounds, the jingle of a conveyance was heard in the distance.

“The fly at last!” cried Miss Wellby.
