Hannah Brown was always there, and, like most of the ladies, had a tea-tray of her own. Poor Mr. Edwards had fondly imagined himself assistant-in-chief at this tea-tray, and enjoying afterwards a conversation as good as a <i>tête-à-tête</i>, as they could have it all to themselves. But all that was over now, and with all his predilections for things parochial, Mr. Edwards could not persuade himself that he looked at the party now in any other light than that of “a bore.” He really did not wish to meet Hannah Brown now.
