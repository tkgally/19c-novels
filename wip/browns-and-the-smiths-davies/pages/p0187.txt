“Don’t talk to me, Hannah. Is she not saying to all the other girls in Goslingford, ‘Stand by, I am holier than thou?’”

“I cannot see that, Aunt Clara. Dancing, without being a sin to other people, might be a sin to her, if it made her vain or jealous, or took her mind away from her duties.”

“Then if it did so, she should have been ashamed of having such a mind, and have humbled herself instead of setting herself up. She ought not to have allowed herself to feel such things.”

“Perhaps the very reason she has given up dancing is because she will not allow herself.”
