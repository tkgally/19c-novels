As for the factions themselves, we all know that, though those may slumber for a short period of excitement, they never die. And a good thing, too, on the whole, while human nature is what it is. Can we imagine a country without its liberals and conservatives, without its Guelphs and Ghibellines, or a town without its Montagues and Capulets, without its Browns and Smiths? The liberals need the conservatives to keep them in order, the Guelphs the Ghibellines, and <i>vice versâ</i>.
