“The truth is I want to have a little talk about Hannah. I don’t understand girls. I hoped the Welsh trip would have done her good.”

“And so it has, my dear sir. She looks just as she used to do. I am sure it is all right with her; and will soon, I trust, be better than ever it was before,” said sanguine Miss Clara.
