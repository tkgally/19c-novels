Yes, I firmly believe that nine out of ten, or to be moderate, at least six out of ten of those bald-headed and spectacled, elderly gentlemen, with legal faces, who sit in musty, fusty apartments, surrounded by tin boxes and dingy folios, have been in love—desperately, over head and ears in love—at some period of their lives, however antagonistic may seem their surroundings. And, by the way, it has often struck me—why are their surroundings what they are? Why must lawyers’ chambers always be such dens of dust and dinginess?
