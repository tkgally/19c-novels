“But you do not rob me, father—I give it gladly. I should be far happier—all my life I should be happier.”

“You do not know what you undertake, Hannah—the privations you would have to undergo—the change it would make—how poverty is looked down upon.”

“I should not be looked down upon, father. I should be respected.”

Her father regarded her with fond admiration, and her strength of mind seemed to pass into his own feelings.
