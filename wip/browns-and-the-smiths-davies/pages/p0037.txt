“He always treats me as if I were no better than a pettifogger,” said Mr. Thomas Smith, not unreasonably affronted, “as if I were going to refuse Sir George’s business when it was offered to me, with my large family to provide for. It is very different with Brown, who has only that girl Hannah, poor man!” added Mr. Smith, with a momentary feeling for the family misfortunes of his rival; then adding, with a mingling of compassion and bitterness, as he continued his after-dinner <i>tête-à-tête</i> with his wife, “I believe he thought I was glad when that poor boy of his died.”
