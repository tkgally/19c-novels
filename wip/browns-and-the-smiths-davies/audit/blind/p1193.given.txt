We cannot have every good quality unitcd in the same person, except in novels; and one great secret of happiness, then Destiny has condemned us to live in a Goslingford, peopled by Browns aud smiths, and people with the mere ordinary characteristics of humanity; is to take them as we find them, and make the best of them. If a valet-de-chambre is incapable of seeing a hero in the man whom he shaves (and Carlyle says the fault is the valet’s, not the hero’s), it is just possible the master may be able to see a hero in the valet.
