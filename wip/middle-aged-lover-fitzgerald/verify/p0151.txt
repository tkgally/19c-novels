The night of the concert had now come round, and everything promised exceedingly well. A handsome amount of tickets had been disposed of. Mr. Nagle was not to be spoken to, and seemed to be marked “dangerous.” His eyes, as they encountered other eyes, peered into a mist of tickets or audience beyond; of ordinary mundane things he seemed now to have no ken. He was spurring down to the rooms every hour, and appeared overpowered with a weight of business.
