You will be pushed against the wall, your little game of tactics is very poor and transparent. I see through it perfectly. The whole place sees it; but it won’t do.”

“You forget yourself,” said the clergyman, now very angry. “Let me tell you, you are considered in Brickford not to be so very clever after all. You haven’t managed your own affairs so cleverly. Don’t shout before you are out of the wood!”

Will Gardiner gave him a furious look, and passed on. Again, alas! for Pylades and Orestes! The two affectionate brothers, when they next met in the street, passed each other without recognition.
