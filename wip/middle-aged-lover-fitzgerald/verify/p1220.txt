Mr. Birkenshaw was delighted to undertake the task. After business details had been despatched, Lady Duke was heard to give utterance to these memorable and comforting words:

“I fear I have been very remiss in calling on Mrs. Birkenshaw; but that is a pleasure I have long looked forward to. Perhaps,” this with a gracious courtesy, “I might be so fortunate as to find her at home now?”
