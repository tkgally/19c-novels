# Chapter VIII.
[[subtitle]] A Grand Scheme.

A GREAT scheme was now on foot. Braham Nagle’s <sc>concert</sc> was in every one’s mouth!

Every one had joined in the natural speculation as to the musical merits of the Nagles. The natural answer was, “Give ’em an opportunity of hearing you!” It was Billy Gardiner that was most eager with this idea.
