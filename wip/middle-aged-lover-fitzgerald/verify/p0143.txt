# Chapter XI.
[[subtitle]] Cold Water.

W<sc>hen</sc> Corinna and her friend arrived to report progress, and while Mr. Gardiner was descanting loudly on the success of their mission, Mr. Nagle listened with scarcely concealed indifference.

“Corinna brings about that result very often, I can tell you; but, of course,” this sadly, “no one ever thinks of asking, ‘who set the types?’ But that would never do, oh never! Glück is well enough at the Monday Classical Sawpits——”
