Putting everything together with his old sagacity, which was now returning to him unclouded, he felt by a sort of instinct that he had divined the true causes of all the late events that had attended his illness, and in his terror drops of perspiration broke out upon his forehead.

He felt that he must have all his powers ready to protect him. A sort of bitter dislike against these cruel conspirators took possession of him, while an eagerness to expose and defeat them filled his whole soul. He felt a new strength, and half the oppression of his illness left him. But, at the same time, he felt that he should have need of all his caution and self-restraint; for any emotion or show of resentment would be welcomed, and only be playing into their hands.
