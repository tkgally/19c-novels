He went back to his place, leaving Corinna not a little puzzled, and yet pleased, by his sympathy. Brighter hopes, too, were rising before her. But here was her turn approaching, and she had now to get ready for the performance.

Every musician knows that dainty piece and its piquant graces, its dancing measure, and when Corinna began, her delicious warbling at once riveted attention; her attractive presence added to the charm; and the buzz was gradually hushed down.
