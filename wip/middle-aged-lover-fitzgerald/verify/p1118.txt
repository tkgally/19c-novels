Lady Duke reflected a moment.

“This is all very honourable and creditable on your part; but I do not see how you are to be recompensed for so great a service.”

“You are quite right. In whatever way the matter is looked at, it will be seen that nothing can come to me. That rather shabby reward, ‘the consciousness of having done one’s duty,’ is all that I can reasonably look for.”
