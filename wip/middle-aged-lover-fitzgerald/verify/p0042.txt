Corinna drew herself up proudly and with dignity, as her father thrummed through the jocund symphony of that once popular melody. Then she began to sing; and with a voice so rich, and round, and daring that it seemed to be kept in reserve, as it were, and to be quite capable of emulating the dashing exploits of the more famous prima donna “Catalawney.” Heads nodded in delight as she tripped over the airy quavers of the pleasant lilt.
