“Nonsense! Why, that’s said often enough already. Their tongues are never idle a moment. As she’s got the credit of it, she might as well have the reality. You should use your authority. These girls require to be ordered about.”

“But what can I do with her?” said the other. “She’s so high-flown and romantic. Why—would you believe it?—I forgot to tell you this—she wants to go away! to leave the place!”

Mrs. Gardiner started.

“Go away! When?”

“At once. To-morrow, she says. Talks of earning her bread, and all that folly.”

“Oh, then it looks very bad indeed. After that you may draw off, and let the others pillage the poor fellow to their heart’s content.”
