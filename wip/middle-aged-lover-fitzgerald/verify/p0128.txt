# Chapter X.

[[subtitle]] A New Ally.

T<sc>his</sc> little incident soon got abroad in Brickford. It was a delightful piece of excitement, and people began to take sides in the matter. Every one knew that Lady Duke had come back to rescue her child; indeed, the success of her attempt could be read in the downcast, rueful faces of the family. Still she had not gone away.
