# Chapter XXIII.

[[subtitle]] The Duel.

THE following morning was to usher in a day of momentous excitement for the various actors in the Brickford drama.

Early that day a new guest had arrived at Crockley’s Family Hotel. This was known to be one Doctor Craggs, who came without luggage, save indeed a sort of professional hand-bag, which might contain papers, or surgical instruments, or shaving materials. He was a quick-eyed gentleman, with a sort of treasury clerk manner.
