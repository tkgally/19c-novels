# Chapter XXV.

[[subtitle]] The Evening Before.

ALL Brickford, as may well be conceived, was in commotion at the news now spread about. It was known that Mr. Doughty’s relations had at last felt it their painful duty to interfere, and that the “unfortunate gentleman,” as he was called, at that moment was in restraint, or at least carefully watched in his own house. There was much astonishment, and much more moralising over this sad news.
