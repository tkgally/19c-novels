“I suppose they sent you on this errand; I mean the clever gentleman who would like to have me for his son-in-law,” said the young man, growing more and more angry. “But you must tell the whole truth when you go back. I have nothing to say about her, and stand to what I have said.”

“Don’t be afraid,” said Mr. Doughty, with spirit; “there is no need of my speaking in the matter. You have taught her to appreciate you perfectly by this time. You said that, but for the want of money, you would fulfil your implied engagement. That obstacle is removed, and you still decline.”
