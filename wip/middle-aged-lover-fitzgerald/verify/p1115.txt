Lady Duke bowed to him to go on.

“It is no secret here that these Nagles have obtained an extraordinary influence over him. What the foundation of that influence is, you know as well as I do. Now I have reason to believe that they intend to exert that influence presently, in a way that may have most serious results. I understand he has not as yet made any will disposing of his vast property”—

At the mention of this word “Will”—one of the most talismanic of words—Lady Duke gave a very perceptible start.
