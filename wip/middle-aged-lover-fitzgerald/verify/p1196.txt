“No, no,” said the other, deprecatingly. “She will come back, I know she will. I’ll make her.”

“Make her? No! Let her do what she pleases—I do not care to hear of her now. Never breathe her name to me. I suppose you fancy that I shall in time become again the soft, foolish dupe I once was, and lavish everything on her and the lover for whom she has sacrificed me.”

“Who do you mean?” asked Mr. Nagle, quite bewildered. “I vow and protest I don’t understand——”

“No, of course, you know nothing. Not that she has gone after her idol—that his departure for India was all a delusion. They are up in London together. She loved him from the beginning—loved him all this time—loves him now. It was an artfully contrived scheme.” He went on:
