# Chapter XXII.
[[subtitle]] Will Gardiner Established in the Fortress.

WILL GARDINER came forth after the interview much excited, and in a glow of generous enthusiasm, such as attends on the performance of some noble action. He felt a little chilled as he met the expectant face of his wife, and suddenly remembered that he had come away without performing the task which he had undertaken. Still had he not succeeded to a certain extent?

“Well,” she said, eagerly, “is it all settled? Have you managed it?”
