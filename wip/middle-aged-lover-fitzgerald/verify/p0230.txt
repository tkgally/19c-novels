He seemed to Corinna quite to change as he spoke these words, which had a tone of menace in them rather than of friendly encouragement. She felt a little indignant, but took his arm without another word.

This night appeared to the gay and airy guests who were fluttering through Mr. Doughty’s rooms, to be no more than some pleasant evening of amusement, like many others. But for the personages of this little piece it was one fraught with the highest dramatic interest.
