Actuated by this fine feeling for his art, Mr. Nagle assumed a worn and persecuted air, and then changing to a cheerful tone, would exclaim:

“Now for our friend Do-re-mi-fa!”

The lesson would continue until about four or five, and indeed could scarcely be styled a lesson, there was so much conversation intermixed with the instruction. Very often, indeed—a quaint method of imparting musical instruction—the master was not present at all; nevertheless, the effect on all parties was as though a lesson <i>had</i> been given.
