Lady Duke winced at this good news.

“I fear Alfred will soon have to go and join his regiment. He can’t be idling on here always.”

“Oh, I see! Then you ought to hurry on matters. Everything ought to be settled at once.”

“Hush! my dear Mr. Doughty,” said the lady, with vexation. “You go a little too fast. I was indeed coming to you, to speak to you about this very matter.”
