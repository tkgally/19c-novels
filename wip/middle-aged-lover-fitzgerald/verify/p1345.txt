She seemed to be Corinna Victrix—the heroine who had won the victory through all the little vicissitudes of the story. Her gentle gaze rested without hostility even on Lady Duke.

Mr. Doughty, no longer old Doughty, so bright and happy was his face, advanced to meet her, and taking her hand, led her into the room.

“At last,” he said, “my troubles and trials have come to an end. Yet all through I have had my guiding star. True, I have lost all my wealth, but I have found a compensation and consolation, which I dared not have looked for, had I kept my riches.
