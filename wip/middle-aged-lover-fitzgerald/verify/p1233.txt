Doctor Spooner glanced at the door, which was slightly ajar, and whence was heard, during this conversation, a slight rustle.

“Most natural,” he said, “you feel a relief in the action. It gives an occupation for your over-excited nerves.”

“Why, I believe,” said Mr. Doughty, smiling, “I must have torn up whole reams. It seems like tearing hopes, joys, sorrows—the whole past and all the future.”

Again the doctor looked towards the door with a curious expression. The other, glancing at him quickly, saw it.
