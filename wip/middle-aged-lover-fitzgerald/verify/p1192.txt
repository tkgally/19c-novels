The music-master stared wildly. It had begun to force itself on his rather narrow faculties, that a mysterious chain of events was being somehow woven about him, and that skilful hands were raising up impenetrable barriers between him and the object of all his hopes. For the first time he began to feel that he was rather a helpless creature.

Somewhat cowed, he answered:
