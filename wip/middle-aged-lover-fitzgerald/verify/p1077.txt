“Just the thing that our singing-master would do. And see here, my dear lady, how they have got all their own party about him, from the doctor down to the nurse. I know what I’ll do. They shan’t dispose of me in that fashion. As soon as the poor fellow gets better, or worse, and he must do one or the other, I’ll just walk in, and not stir from the house until I see him—not if there were fifty Nagles and Spooners to prevent me.”
