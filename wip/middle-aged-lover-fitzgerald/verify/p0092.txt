“Hunted, sir, hunted!” he would exclaim. “I envy the cab-horse, and would change with him this moment. My dear Mr. Duke,” he added, solemnly, “take this warning: break stones, sweep up the mud in the streets, pick oakum, go down and live in the sewers, but—never embrace tuition as a profession!” Which, as addressed to the young gentleman, might seem a superfluous warning.

“It’s heart-breaking, sir! Not for the work, but for the character of the work. There are girls brought to me, sir, by their mothers, poor ignorant creatures, with no more voice than the chimney-pot on that house. I suppose they expect me to find them voices!
