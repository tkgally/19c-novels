Be on your guard, I tell you,” he added, rising; “they will try something to-day or to-morrow. And think as badly as you like of me.”

“No, no,” said the other, smiling; “could you do nothing for me? But how am I, a poor helpless being, to protect myself? You will not hand me over to their mercy? Will you not aid me?”

Will Gardiner shook his head.
