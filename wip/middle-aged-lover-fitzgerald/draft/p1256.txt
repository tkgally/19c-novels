Will Gardiner did not quite understand. But his brow cleared, and he seized on Mr. Doughty’s hand and wrung it.

“Then what am I to do?” he asked. “Only tell me.”

“Be with me as much as you can. Support me by pretending that you have the same scheme in view that they have. It will drive them to desperation.”

Will Gardiner, always mercurial, entered with delight into this new idea, and forgot all his own private distresses in the anticipated enjoyment. He forgot, also, the very important share he had proposed to take in these obnoxious schemes. However, he was a thorough creature of impulse, and illogical enough not to see this inconsistency.
