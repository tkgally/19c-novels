Gradually the lady was drawn into coquettish and rather shrill remonstrance; Mr. Nagle pattered over what seemed a paved causeway; while her fingers strove almost ineffectually to keep up with his stronger vocal steed; and finally the whole party, Mr. Nagle, Mrs. B. N., and the struggling hands, came panting and struggling in together, just “pulling up” for one protracted cry, roar, and crash before riding in!
