The Gardiner party had now scored one, and there was consternation in the looks of the other side, as the successful lady tripped away to report her success.

But before night arrived, another startling piece of intelligence had got abroad. Birkenshaw, the private solicitor of the Gardiners, had gone up to Mr. Doughty— sent, of course, by “that party”—for the purpose of drawing up his last will and testament, by which all that he had in the world would be given to those miserable Nagles, with a jackal’s slice to the Gardiners, who had helped so effectively to

secure the spoil. Here was news, indeed!
