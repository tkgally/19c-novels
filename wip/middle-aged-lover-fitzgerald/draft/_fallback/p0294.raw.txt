There was a good deal of excitement in Brickford when it became known that the old organist had been expelled, and some plain unmusical people, who had not been fascinated by the glittering manner of Braham’s pupil, or of Braham’s pupil’s bewitching daughter, spoke of the matter as a rather oppressive act. In a place like Brickford, every ordinary personage or functionary has his party of friends; and the case of this old retainer, who, indeed, had done his work respectably, justly excited a good deal of sympathy.

When, therefore, it was known that on the following Sunday Braham Nagle was to preside at the organ, there was quite a hubbub, and a more than usually large con-

gregation attended.
