This disastrous mistake was fatal to his prospects at Brickford, for the community could not pass over the double failure. Had he secured a fortune, his success would have gone a good way towards condoning the low character of the alliance. The girl, who expected to “be made a lady of,” was deeply mortified at this public neglect, which no less affected her husband. He was indeed disgusted with the place, and was thinking of setting out for Australia, or some other colony
