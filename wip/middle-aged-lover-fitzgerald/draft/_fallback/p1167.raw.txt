One or two of the watering-places had long ago been the scene of her exertions, when she had plotted against rival patronesses of society, and had acquired a certain fashionable unscrupulousness. Now her social moral sense had become blunted, the old keenness was» awakened, and she eagerly seized the materials now presented to her, as something that would give practice to these old disused faculties.

“We should see this gentleman,” she said, “and some notice should be taken of
