66 The Middle-aged Lover.

“Nobody denies that. What on earth do you mean by talking to me in that style? Who wants any trickery, as you call it? My dear friend, if you were to turn out as many pots of calf’s-foot as Crosse and Blackwell, and send them here in vans, it wouldn't do.”

Alas! for the brothers, with their arms encircling each other! Alas! for Orestes and Pylades, and the Sunday walks, which were to be from this time forth things of the past!

Thus the first skirmish round the prostrate Doughty found the affectionate Gardiners in fierce conflict, and all was

changed.
