She drew herself up, and poured out her rich - full tones, telling the fine story with a feeling worthy of the gifted Viardot herself. As she proceeded, the cold face lightened, and was turned to hers: the delicate fingers became firmer in touch: the two performers, reacting on each other, produced a result that kindled the enthusiasm even of Will Gardiner, to whom these severer efforts were usually unintelligible.

“You do sing,” said “Old Doughty,” warmly, “and you are an artist!”

“The song would make one sing,” said Corinna, enthusiastically.
