As to the relationship, Mr. Gardiner certainly did her injustice, for she was nearly connected, after a sort of second cousinship, with Mr. Doughty. Indeed, the families of Gardiner and Duke were all thus allied. Only a few weeks before they would have made this bond, so far as it concerned Mr. Doughty, as faint to the eye as the edging of a light cloud; now, curiously enough, they would have had it as stout as a ship’s cable.

The truth was, that Lady Duke was a person who had sunk all her capital in “floating” a financial enterprise, namely, the marriage of her two daughters, and had succeeded with one, only, however, to find herself comparatively embarrassed, and with limited means.
