“Vou are excited,” said the other, “but you will not excite me. Mr. Gardiner, your manner and language justify me now in saying that you are a most improper person to be admitted to a sick-room where a patient like mine is in so critical a way.”

Will Gardiner was beside himself with anger.

“Why, what authority have you here? I tell you I shall insist on my right of seeing my friend, whom you may be hocussing between you, for all anyone knows. Let me pass, sir!”
