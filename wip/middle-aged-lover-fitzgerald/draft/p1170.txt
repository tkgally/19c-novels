At the door she saw a fly waiting; a trunk was brought down past her as she ascended the steps; while across the hall stamped and strode an infuriated man, flourishing his long arms, and talking loudly. In his blind excitement, he actually welcomed the arrival of Lady Duke as that of a valued ally.

“Just come in here, ma’am,” he said, “and say what you think of this piece of idiotcy. Look at that girl—where has she gone to now? She’s mad, or losing her wits. She ought to be in the hands of keepers, so she ought. I can do nothing with her.”

“What is all this about?” asked the lady, coldly.
