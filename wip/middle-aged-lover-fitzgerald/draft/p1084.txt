“But what am <i>I</i> to do?” he said, at last; “this is all very fine. You think only of yourself. Who’s to pay the bills in this place? Here have I spent my time and abilities trying to get you ‘off,’ and now I am left in the lurch. This man will never recover, and how will we all look then?”

“Then Heaven forgive me and us all for the unfeeling, cruel part we have played; the unworthy game of deception and double dealing to which he has been made the victim.”

“You may say that,” said her father.
