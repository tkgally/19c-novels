In a fortnight Mr. Doughty had returned, and was established in his splendid new house. It was handsomely furnished; the music-room had been fitted up superbly, and a magnificent grand piano, with all the latest improvements, had made its majestic appearance. A small platform had been set up at one end of the room, on which the instrument, with two or three music-stands, was placed.
