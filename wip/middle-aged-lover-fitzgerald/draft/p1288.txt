It must be said that such was not the homage that was extended to the general performance, for every song seemed to be the signal for a universal buzz. There was a room beyond the second drawing-room from whence there was no convenient seeing or hearing, and here a large portion of the company bivouacked, as it were, more than content with their exclusion, and utterly unconscious that, at certain intervals, their bursts of chattering and genteel laughter were borne in upon the general audience, to the interruption of the music.
