[[cont]]snapperjack?”—a strange word that was born of sudden wedlock in Mr. Nagle’s brain, of two other words, “whipper-snapper” and “skipjack”—“a young snapperjack, who has nothing in the world but the coat on his back? D’ye mean to tell me that?”

“No,” said Corinna, haughtily, “I do not. But all this must be changed. It is your interest, no doubt, to keep up your friendship with Mr. Doughty. I cannot interfere in that. But as regards myself, I cannot go through this humiliating rôle any longer. I am weary of it. Now, will you understand me, father? From this day it must all end. You have secured whatever aims you have had in view by my means, though I blush that I should have lent myself to such things.”
