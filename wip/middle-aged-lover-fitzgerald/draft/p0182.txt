Meanwhile Mr. Duke was behaving in a curiously uncertain fashion. He could not bring himself to go away, and yet while he remained he affected a careless indifference and dignity. The change in Mr. Nagle affected him in the most mortifying way. That gentleman wore a certain good-humoured indifference, which contrasted oddly with his former empressement and almost nervous eagerness to anticipate every humour of the young man.
