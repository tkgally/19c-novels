“My good Nagle,” said Will, with a forced laugh, “I am sorry for your failure; you did your best, and deserved to win; but what can you do? And my holy brother there—have his sacred ministrations been declined with thanks?”

“This is a poor style of joking,” answered the clergyman, hotly; “but it won’t do. I know the work that is going on, and the whole town knows it too.”

“What d’ye mean, brother?” said Will, angrily; “you’re talking childishly.”

“Nothing of the kind, sir! I mean the trying to make out that certain people are not sound in their intellects, and don’t know what they are doing. We know the object of that.”
