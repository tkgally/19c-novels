“Oh, I declare!” cried William, in admiration. “’Pon my word! Quite a belle!”

“Yes, she is classical. Poor Braham! whose favourite pupil and aide-de-camp I may in a manner say I was——”

“I know that well,” said the other. “You often told me.”

“——said to me one evening—I think it was on the second day of the Festival in the Abbey—that she was like Malibrong’s own born child. The likeness is amazing. I can see it now.”

And Mr. Nagle closed his eyes, while his lips moved softly, no doubt as if addressing the departed songstress.

William Gardiner looked at him with an amused air.
