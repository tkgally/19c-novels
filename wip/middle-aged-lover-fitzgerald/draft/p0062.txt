Her voice would fill that vast house as effectively as that of any of “the Italian squad;” her bell-like organ would ring melodiously through that vast enclosure. To this glorious apotheosis Braham Nagle ever fondly looked, though he had as yet taken no serious steps in the direction. “Wait till she ripens,” he would say; “her voice wants matewering, and mellowing,” two delightful juicy words to which he was partial.
