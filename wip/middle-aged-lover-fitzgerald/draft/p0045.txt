Pick your steps!” Honest William’s eyes filled up, as he stood and listened, his arm affectionately round his parson-brother. At the close a burst of applause came forth. More songs were called, and by twelve o’clock that night Corinna was constituted a heroine, and a grand addition to Brickford!

Every one gathered round her as she finished her song.
