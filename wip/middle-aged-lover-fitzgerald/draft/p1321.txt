“I have not thought of it,” said Corinna. “Rather I am prepared to accept the worst as a penance and expiation. I disdain to be carrying on any hypocritical pretences any longer, or to be imposing on your noble nature. I did not really suffer from such things; I despised them too much for that. But there was quite another reason for this sensitiveness.”
