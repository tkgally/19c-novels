Awe-stricken and crushed, Mr. Nagle went up and tapped softly at the door. A wild face was put forth.

“You there! Go away at once—leave me!” cried Mr. Doughty. “Why not bring your cruel, heartless daughter with you? She has pierced me to the heart—she has killed me. What do you want with me?”

“Only to see you,” said Mr. Nagle, collecting himself for a desperate effort. “And I may only have this opportunity, for there is such a gang about the house that soon no one will have access to you at all.”
