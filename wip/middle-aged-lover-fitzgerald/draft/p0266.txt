“Why, I believe, since the church was built,” said the clergyman, a little alarmed at the sympathy. “It is, of course, a little hard on him, but when I saw that you were so anxious about the Nagles——”

“Why the Nagles?” said the other, coldly. “You are not going to appoint Mrs. Nagle, or Miss Corinna Nagle, to the office.”
