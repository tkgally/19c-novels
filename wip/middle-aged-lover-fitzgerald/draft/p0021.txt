# Chapter II.
[[subtitle]] The Braham Nagles.

THE dinner was given in honour of Sir George, Lady Duke (K.C.B.), and their son, young Duke, who were passing through Brickford. But they could not escape the vigilance of the Customs; for no better douanier in the matter of guests could be conceived than Will Gardiner. He stopped the whole party on the frontier and made them pay him the duty of a dinner. Sir George and his lady, as we have stated, were “far out” connexions of the Gardiners
