What an exquisite taste was his—and so refined! And how nicely he touched that curious instrument—what was it called?—the viola, giving it quite an individuality. Then the piano. To hear him accompany; what a treat! How he helped that girl through, who had not such a wonderful voice after all. <i>There</i> was the only blemish—one slight speck upon the sun. There was something really absurd and unbecoming in this infatuation for a singer’s daughter; though, of course, it might be only due to a pure love of art.
