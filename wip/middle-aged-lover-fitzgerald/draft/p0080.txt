The quartettos of Mozart and Haydn were thus interpreted, the host at times taking the viola, though his fingers were weak from nervousness and delicacy, and his tone not strong. He was certainly a true amateur, and all the money he could spare went for operas, chamber music, and concerts, his face being familiar to all musical habitués. His hair was thin and dark, but there was an extraordinary sweetness in his face which attracted those who knew him well.
