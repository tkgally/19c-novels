# L’Envoi.

THE rest the ingenious reader will readily supply. He can easily call up the image of the enchanting Corinna, stately and magnificent, living in town, happy, loving, and a queen of song, admired and loved by her husband. Never was she ashamed of, or did she disclaim, or banish into rural districts with an allowance, that “odd father” of hers. She rather lent all her exertions to get him on.
