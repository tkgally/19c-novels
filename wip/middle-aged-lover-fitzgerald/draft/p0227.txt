“I have not, indeed. At least, I never intended it. But I have my pride.”

“Then perhaps I deceive myself; that I suppose must be the truth. But think of my warning. It is not too late. I tell you as surely as I stand here, you will shipwreck all your happiness if you pursue the new course you have determined on. I know human nature, and man’s human nature. You are deceived now, and will be yet more deceived.”

Corinna looked at him steadily.

“I grieve to see that you do not understand me; but there is no remedy for that.”

His lips were trembling.
