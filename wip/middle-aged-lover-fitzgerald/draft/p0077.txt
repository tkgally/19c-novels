“I say, Jack,” said Will, on one Sunday walk, as he was dragging his brother along, “we must get Old Doughty, when he next comes down, to hear Miss Corinna. We must make the old boy useful. He knows all the musical swells and pundits, has Costa and Company to his scraping parties at Warwick-street. We’ll get introductions from him, and have Miss Corinna brought out at St. James’s Hall, my boy!”
