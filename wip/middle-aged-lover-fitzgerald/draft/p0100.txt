Braham Nagle greatly inclined to the idea. This was, indeed, seizing the Brickford populace en gros, instead of laboriously nibbling at them retail. It was a superb idea, and should be carried out magnificently. He at once proceeded to organise his plans.

Indeed, everything was favourable to the prospects of the family. Among the Brickfordians was the usual number of young fellows who, after singing “Let me Kiss Him for his Mother,” or for some other person, at a supper-table, discovered they had voices, and were eager to display them on more auspicious platforms.
