It was a curious circumstance, however, that, only a few yards from Doctor Spooner’s house, Mr. Nagle should have reined up the imaginary charger on which he was mounted, and have become gravely reflective. It occurred to him that he was now about to introduce into the sacred preserve, which he had guarded so jealously, another candidate sportsman, and an idea of danger from so agreeable and entertaining a physician suggested itself. Was it not rather Quixotic, tempting Providence, as it were, in this rash way; thus introducing one who might hereafter hoist him, Nagle, on his own private petard?
