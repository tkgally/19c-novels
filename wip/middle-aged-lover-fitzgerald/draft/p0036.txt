In this reciprocal strain the opening recitative was conducted.

Then Mrs. Nagle broke into her slow and piteous air, during which time her husband had his eyes fixed on the ceiling, now with a smile of pity, now glancing at her with withering disgust, and now interpolating some spoken, not sung, exclamation of contempt, such as “Ragazza!” Then came his turn.
