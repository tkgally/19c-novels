# Chapter XV.
[[subtitle]] New Allies.

A QUARTER of an hour later arrived Mrs. Gardiner, who found the bereaved Nagle almost tearing his hair with vexation. The lady was smiling and full of the good news she brought.

“I have come to carry off Corinna. Where is she? Poor dear Doughty insists on seeing her. She will do him more good than all the doctors.”
