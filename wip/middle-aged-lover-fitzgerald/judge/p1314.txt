“I can have no scruples now,” she said to herself again and again. “All that is at an end! Indeed, I allowed them to prey on me too long. Let all here say what they will, I am called on to act now. I should be the most ungrateful and ungenerous of creatures, as indeed he must long since have thought me, if I shrank from what is my duty.”

She felt a sort of elation at this casting away the bonds which had restrained her for such a time.
