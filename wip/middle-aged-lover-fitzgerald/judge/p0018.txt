“Did you hear darling John, to-night? Is he not droll? Oh! he is another Sheridan, my dear. Sir George always says if he was in London he wouldn’t be dining at home a single day.”

But this lady’s admiration, indeed, extended to all that concerned her or her family. Everything that was theirs, or connected with them, instantly became gorgeous, golden, and beautiful in her eyes. The reader will gather a fair idea of this idiosyncrasy from a remark of hers to a little girl present on this occasion of the dinner, to whom William had presented a doll as a birthday present.

“And did Willie give you that, dear? Oh how lovely! What a beautiful little thing! Now, you must take care of it. Oh!” this in rapture, “<i>no</i> one has such taste in choosing a present as Willie.”
