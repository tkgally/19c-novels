Every new pupil was expected to make him or herself master, or mistress, of this lyric; and it must be said that the entire sale of the work was confined to the human nature that was formed by Mr. Nagle. A number of copies were ordered down to meet the anticipated demand. The general appearance of this chef-d’œuvre at various houses in a small district, produced rather a monotony—swans, as Bill Gardiner remarked, dying about in every direction.

But for the gallant young Alfred Duke, when had Mr. Nagle a more eager pupil, or one that followed the Do-re-mi-fa with such enthusiasm?
