This good sort of fellow was not in the least “soft,” for all his coarse laughter and good humour. He was rather quick of temper, and when he felt he had been injured or unjustly treated, could show a warm resentment. He was, besides, proud and sensitive. But his affection for his quiet, bookish, and parsonic brother was very remarkable, who, he would declare, only wanted a sailor’s cutlass to go on as a pirate or bandit in a melodrama, so ferocious was his coal black ecclesiastical beard.
