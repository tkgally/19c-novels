“Why, I should say,” answered she, readily, “that it might be a fair case for discretion—and quiet temporising. As you say, another such scene as that of the other day, would be fatal.”

“It would not be so difficult to exclude these Nagles altogether; but then, you see, the agitation——”

“Not to be thought of,” she said; “they should be met with their own weapons.”

“So I think,” he said, “for the sake of the patient himself. Your taking this view quite encourages me, Lady Duke.”

“But as to his state, do you think he will recover?”
