She then passed over to Mr. Nagle, who, fuming in wounded dignity, affected to be completing a heavy correspondence.

“I was delighted,” she said, “to hear that all had gone off so charmingly. And I have been wishing to see you, to thank you for the way in which you acceded to my wishes about Alfred’s singing.
