“But, my dear Nagle,” continued the latter, “you should look round, and notice what is going on about you. There is something far more suitable in view. There is youth, and good looks, and good station ready to come to her feet, only waiting the proper encouragement. <i>There</i> would be an alliance that would do honour to any family. Not that I think Mr. Duke quite worthy of her; but still, would it not be a most desirable match?”

“Oh,” said Mr. Nagle, with open contempt, “that fellow. Why, he hasn’t twopence halfpenny in the world.”
