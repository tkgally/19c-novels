“I am glad you are remaining, Lady Duke,” he said, “as I should wish you to be present when I have to make a little announcement rather interesting to myself and one other person.”

“I have no interest in any matter that concerns you,” said the lady, haughtily.

“What, all gone within a few minutes?” said Mr. Doughty, good-humouredly. “Don’t say that, for consistency’s sake. Ah, here they come at last!”

“My goodness gracious!” said a familiar voice. “My poor fellow, how they have been treating you!” It was Mr. Nagle who had entered. “And so all the fortune’s gone to another.
