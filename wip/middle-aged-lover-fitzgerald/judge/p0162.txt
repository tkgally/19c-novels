# Chapter XIII.

[[subtitle]] The Concert.

THE concert now began. The wonderful Nagle family, as was to be expected, appeared under all the forms and conditions set down for them: Mr. Nagle being conductor, leader, accompanist, solo singer, duet singer—being off and on, and speech maker.
