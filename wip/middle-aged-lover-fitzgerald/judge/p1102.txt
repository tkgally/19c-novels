“Oh, that I wouldn’t venture to interfere with. You have a certain authority in this house.”

“Of course I have. Who brought you in here? Do you know your place, sir?”

“I owe that to you, I certainly admit,” said the other, humbly; “and, as I say, in the house no one should interfere with you. But the sick-room is my province. If it was my mother or my father, I would behave in the same way. My duty is to restore the patient, if I can, and I can let no consideration of friendship stand in the way. In that I won’t be interfered with.”
