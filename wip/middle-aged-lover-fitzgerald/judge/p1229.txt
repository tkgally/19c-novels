# Chapter XIX.
[[subtitle]] The Doctor Dismissed.

A CURIOUS change was coming over Mr. Doughty. Slightly better in health, he had grown eager, excitable, and highly irritable—a state of mind that did not promise to forward his recovery. He was eager “to do something,” to be gone, to rush out on the world, and, above all, leave Brickford, with its miserable and torturing associations, behind him.
