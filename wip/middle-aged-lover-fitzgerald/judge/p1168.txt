# Chapter XIV.
[[subtitle]] Corinna’s Flight.

THE news that Birkenshaw had been sent for by Mr. Doughty of course with the object of making “a settlement of his worldly affairs”—elegant and tender circumlocution for making one’s will—spread with rapidity through Brickford. In the wake of this piece of news followed another, of even more interest, for it was openly stated in the market place, as it were, that all his handsome fortune had been bequeathed to Corinna Nagle.
