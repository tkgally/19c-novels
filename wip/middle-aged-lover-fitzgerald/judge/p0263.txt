But this speech may have been uttered in the hope of damping Corinna’s chance. This second disappointment had left him well-nigh crushed, and the feeling that his last state was far worse than his former one. The only comfort in store for him now seemed to be the thought of the strange purpose which was working in his soul, to which he seemed to be bending all his thoughts with a feverish purpose. Was this some scheme of revenge, or some wild undefined plan of getting those who had trifled with him into his power? At this moment, perhaps, he hardly knew himself. He wished to do, to carry out, something, perhaps, just to occupy his thoughts.
