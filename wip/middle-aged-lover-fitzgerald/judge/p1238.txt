“And those Nagles?”

“Oh, they are out of the”—“running,” he was going to say, but he felt it was an inappropriate word, so he substituted “business.” “What right or title have they to interfere? They should not be tolerated for a moment. But, as I say, they are disposed of, and will give no more trouble. No; to-morrow will be time enough. I must then act formally in your name. After a few days he can be removed to a proper place, where he will be taken the best care of. I can see that all this will be most painful and disagreeable for you, but it really must be done.”
