“There!” cried the delighted father, when his young girl had finished. “That’s very creditable for the short time she’s been learning. She’s been grinding from morning till night, and she has got a little request, which she hasn’t courage to make herself.”

“Oh, if you wouldn’t think it <i>too</i> much trouble,” said Miss Chirrup, with an air which the experienced in such matters would have recognised as of being well tutored in the part, “just to let me come now and then and get a few hints——”
