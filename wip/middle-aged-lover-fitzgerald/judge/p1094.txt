This view could not but strike Mr. Nagle, though he still made some grumbling protest about it’s being “over smart and sharp.” He was not wholly satisfied with the behaviour of the protégé whom he had introduced.

He had scarcely gone away when there was another arrival—Mrs. Gardiner, who crept into the house in a mysterious, sympathising way, and with a sad face asked if she could see “Miss Spooner.”

“Tell her I am here, Anne.” She had picked up the name of the servant. “Say I would take it as a particular favour if she would come down to me.”

In a few moments the cold, stiff Harriet descended. Mrs. Gardiner flew to meet her.
