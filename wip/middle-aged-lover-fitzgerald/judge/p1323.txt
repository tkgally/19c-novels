Corinna looked at him with honest, beaming eyes. She said:

“As you have spoken so generously and openly, I shall do the same. Why should I let you believe such a thing, or think so meanly of me? No; of your love, the love of a noble, generous man, I should have been proud; I would have welcomed it as an honour. I was, indeed, caught for a time by the apparent devotion of another, but I soon saw how I had been led away. There there was no real worth.
