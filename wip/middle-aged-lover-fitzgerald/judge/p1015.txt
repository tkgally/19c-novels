“All going well?” she would say, with a fresh squeeze of congratulation. “I know it is. Dear, dear! My dear, how pale and ill you look. But you must promise me, dear, that you won’t be fretting yourself.” Then, very mysteriously, “Isn’t he here to-night?”

She was celebrated for this pleasant and welcome style of talk.

Corinna did not affect ignorance or wonder, nor was she put out. But she was too proud to accept such thrusts without return, and could not resist saying:

“Do you mean my father’s friend, Mr. Doughty? He is to be here.”

The lady tittered with a great deal of meaning. It was hard to put her down.
