“My dear sir, no; the servant will bring it.”

While this conversation was proceeding, a tall, bright-looking, and decidedly handsome young man was going about the room chatting to this lady and to that, with that almost boyish gaiety which is so engaging a quality. He was perfectly at home, was in good spirits, and, without attempting anything funny, was making them all laugh.

“Tell me,” said Mr. Braham Nagle, snatching at the host’s arm in a most mysterious way—“you could <i>not</i> tell me who that fine-looking young gentleman is, over there, sitting on the low chair?”
