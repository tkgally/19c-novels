It will be evident from the cast of the programme, that Mr. Nagle intended to make his daughter’s admirer as conspicuous as possible, and the astute musician felt that the singing of an amatory duet in public was not such a bad way of setting the public tongue agoing. He, Mr. Nagle, at the instrument, the young pair nervous and faltering, Corinna fortifying her companion by encouraging whispers, and coming to his rescue even, <i>must</i> awaken a softness, an emollient tenderness in the youth, which might lead to the happiest results!
