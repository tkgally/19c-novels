# Chapter XI.
[[subtitle]] Alliance.

A MORNING or two later Mr. Nagle was “junketing down,” as he called it, to the sick man’s house, when he saw Lady Duke entering, in a highly surreptitious manner. He smiled, half in contempt, half in enjoyment. “Some people,” he thought, “are strangely pachydermatous. Here is a woman that has been snubbed and re-snubbed at that establishment.
