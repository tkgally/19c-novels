“Do, Mr. Duke. I should so like to hear you,” added Corinna.

“Oh, absurd!” said the young man, almost blushing. “Why, I have never sung since the supper parties at college. You’d only laugh at me.”

“I laugh at you!” said Corinna, “no, indeed.”

The young lady searched among the music, apparently a little hurt, and then sought her chair. Mr. Duke, distressed, followed her.

“I did not mean—really no; but I am always saying stupid things.”

“Well, we only asked you to sing,” she said.
