Then Mr. Nagle went over to the instrument, and lifted the attenuated and lath-like flaps which contrasted with the solid and handsome frame-work of modern instruments, and insisted on having a private audition of Mr. Duke’s vocal powers.

“Just, now, let us do a little of the Do-re-mi-fa-sol!” and the old instrument gave forth from its yellow jaws what seemed a distant and jangling peal of cracked bells.
