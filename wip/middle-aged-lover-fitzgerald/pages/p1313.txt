# Chapter XXVI.

[[subtitle]] An Angel in the House.

IT was late that same evening, about the time that Brickford had nearly finished its dinner, and the lamps were lighted in the streets, that Corinna arrived. She did not go to her father’s house in the Crescent, but repaired straight to Mr. Doughty. It was, indeed, a different Corinna from the one that had left such a short time before.
