“Yes; but see here, my friend Nagle, you should look at this more seriously,” said Will Gardiner, who was really concerned. “How about Miss Corinna? He has been carrying on there very seriously. I hope to heaven she has been sensible enough not to think of it as more than a little flirtation. As it is, the fellow has behaved shabbily. I always said he was a thorough cad.”
