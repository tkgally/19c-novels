Professional esprit du corps came to aid, and Sir Harvey Tupper at once rallied to his brother with a “Quite so,” a form of agreement common to the legal and medical professions. “There must be no agitation,” went on the physician. “Things are going on very well as they are. Any discussions of this kind had best be adjourned until the convalescence—which we may hope for.”

Mr. Nagle darted a half-defiant, half scared look at his enemies; while Will Gardiner coloured and fumed. Lady Duke, however, was not to be routed so easily.
