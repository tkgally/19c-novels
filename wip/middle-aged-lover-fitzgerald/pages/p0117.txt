“No, it is not fair,” said the lady, unconsciously amending the phrase of her son, “to have our name hawked about in this style. I never was so disgraced in my life. But it must be put a stop to. I suppose you will not allow these people to prevent you showing respect to your mother’s wishes? Perhaps you will come with me now?”

“Certainly, mother,” said the young man, with deference. “But I can assure you, you are unjust to Braham Nagle and his family. They have had nothing to do with this, beyond being good enough to find that I would be of some assistance to them in their concert——”
