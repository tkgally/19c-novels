“I don’t believe that Mr. Doughty had anything to do with the matter,” said Corinna coldly. “I have long since dismissed it from my thoughts.”

And here was that gentleman himself coming in with the other gentleman, looking very pale and ill, as, indeed, was universally remarked.

The musical family were presently called upon for their performance, and Mr. Nagle took his place at the instrument in very different style from that of the first evening on which he had the honour of being presented to the reader.
