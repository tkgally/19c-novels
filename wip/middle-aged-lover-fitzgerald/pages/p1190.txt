“Yes, you had better go away,” said the doctor, impatiently. “You may depend on me.”

They descended, and passed Mr. Nagle with a haughty bow. That gentleman had lost all restraint, and was really desperate.

“Fine work this,” he said. “I know the game that is hatching up-stairs. But I’ll be a witness against the whole set. Don’t think I’ve been hoodwinked all this time. Don’t think that I can’t see what you are plotting.”

“Sir!” said the two ladies.
