“I could not have believed it! I actually saw these things,” she said, contemptuously, pointing to the placard, “on the walls—on the common walls of the town. It is disgraceful—and I cannot suffer it!”

“My dear madam!” began Mr. Nagle, a good deal confused, “it is merely a little music—we wish to bring forward Mr. Alfred here——”
