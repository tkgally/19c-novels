“Oh, but I say,” remonstrated the young man, “look at the size of the letters in which you have put my name! Why, the people will laugh at me.”

“Not a bit too large, amigo,” replied the reader, confidently, “the ‘caps’ are just right.”

“If the caps fit I suppose I must wear them,” said the young man, gaily; “but I feel convinced I shall make an ass of myself.”
