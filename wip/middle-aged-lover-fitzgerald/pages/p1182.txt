[[cont]]But the deception—the cruel deception—and the organised, determined purpose with which she pursued this end! It might seem very disinterested, this unflinching determination; but it is too noble, too cold, too much of duty. However, there, I must see it ended now. I tell you it is over for ever, and I wish there were more here to listen, and hear me say that there <i>shall</i> be an end of it. Who is there? Let them all come in.”
