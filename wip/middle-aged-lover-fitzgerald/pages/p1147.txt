# Chapter XII.
[[subtitle]] The Barrier Crossed.

NOT very long after Braham Nagle’s departure, Will Gardiner and his wife left their house, and went forth to make a fresh attack on the Doughty fortress. It was curious to see the alteration in that honest, open face, an eagerness and restlessness having come into his eyes, and a worn look on his cheeks.
