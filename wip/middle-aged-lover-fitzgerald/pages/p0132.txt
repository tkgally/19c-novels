Mr. Doughty listened without protest to this depreciation of the members of his honoured craft, but still was interested. Anything about music had an almost dramatic interest for him. Will Gardiner saw his advantage, and pressed him hard.

“If you only heard her sing—sing one of the melodies, or a thing out of a fellow called Gluck”—Mr. Doughty winced at the pronunciation where the composer’s name rhymed to duck—“a song about Eurydice and Orpheus, my dear boy, it would make you escape from your very skin!”
