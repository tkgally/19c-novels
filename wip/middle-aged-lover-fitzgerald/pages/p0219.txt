Alfred Duke could endure it no longer. The next piece being a serious task, a heavy Mozart business—so many square feet of earth to be dug out within half-an-hour or so with musical spades and shovels—he found his way to Corinna’s side, and after a whisper, led her away out of that crowded room to one of those improvised greenhouses where were scattered various pairs and parties for whom the music of their own discourse had a greater charm than the common crotchets and quavers. The Doughty eyes, though fixed on the Mozart notes which he was playing, strayed uneasily in the direction of the two departing figures.
