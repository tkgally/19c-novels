The truth was, Mr. Nagle’s musical erudition and acquirements had once been signally exposed by one of these gentry, and the outrage still rankled in his breast.

As for “the German scrapers,” he put them aside after his own fashion. “It was a very nice thought; but the Haben sie business doesn’t do.”

Mr. Doughty was glancing at the posters still displayed on the table. “You don’t mean to say you are going to put a young lady of her talent to sing rubbish like that, Dying Swans and such wish-wash nonsense?” and he pointed with his stick to the obnoxious ballad.

Mr. Nagle coloured. “Wish-wash, sir! As good judges as you, sir, have pronounced it first class. Ay, and may be better, too, sir.”
