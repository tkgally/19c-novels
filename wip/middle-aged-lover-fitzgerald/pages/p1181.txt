He was growing very agitated, his eyes were very wild indeed.

“You hinted something,” he went on, “as to the motive of this young lady’s flight. You said she had gone in the direction her inclinations led her. Can it be supposed that this is the old attraction? This would at least make things intelligible.”

Mrs. Gardiner was a little embarrassed, but such a chance of embittering his resentment was too good to be neglected, so she answered diplomatically:

“Well, you may be sure that such is what is believed in this place, and indeed it looks like the only rational solution.”
