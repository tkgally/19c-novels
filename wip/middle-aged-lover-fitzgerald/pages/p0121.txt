“I am right glad of it,” said Corinna, pacing up and down. “We have done with them for ever! But to have been so deceived in one I thought generous and chivalrous!”

“He is a mean cur,” said Mr. Nagle, in a sudden fury. “I always thought so, and always said so. But I should like to know who’s to pay for these posters where his trumpery name figures?”

There was some sense in this question, though it made Corinna almost writhe. It was a wretched state of things. The family were plunged in despair.
