The ladies gave him a haughty look of contempt, and swept away past him.

Thus the Nagle-Gardiner alliance was dissolved almost as suddenly as it was formed, while a new one, the Duke-Gardiner, had been constituted.

As soon as the door was closed upon them the doctor took Mr. Nagle mysteriously aside.

“He does not want to see you,” he said, in a low voice, “I assure you he said so a moment ago.”

“I don’t believe a word of it,” said the musician.
