“It’s about, and about, and about,” he answered, incoherently; he himself really seeming to require the restraint he spoke of. “Why here she’s going away. Going up to London—abroad—God knows where, and at such a moment.”

Lady Duke understood him perfectly, but affected astonishment. The next moment Corinna had entered, in her travelling dress, and regarded the visitor haughtily.

“Say something to her,” continued Mr. Nagle, quite beside himself with agitation. “There never was such folly. When all is settled now, and she may live like a princess for the rest of her life!”
