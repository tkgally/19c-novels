“Oh, I shall take care of all that,” said Doctor Spooner, gently. “I have a proper person selected, whom I can answer for professionally. It is very good of Lady Duke to take so much trouble, and it does her honour; but I must take on myself the responsibility, as we cannot run any risks in such a case. She is already engaged, and will be here this evening.”

“Quite proper,” said the London doctor, “and only the regular course.”

Lady Duke, as well as Mr. Gardiner, looked a little confounded at this method of settling the matter, but remained silent. There was, indeed, nothing more to be said.
