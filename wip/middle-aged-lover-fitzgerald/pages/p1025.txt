“You can congratulate yourself on the success of your scheme. To-night must be a genuine triumph for you.”

He could not resist answering, sadly, “A miserable triumph for my judgment. Alas! I foresaw all this from the beginning.”

“And you, who professed to care for me, could expose me to this!”

Her father was coming over to make her sing again.
