“No,” he said, “Doughty, I had better leave you to yourself, and to your own devices. I would give the world to help you. But I don’t want you to think more meanly of me than you do. To the people here I will seem even yet more mean, if I ally myself with you.”

“And why, pray?” said Mr. Doughty, with the same curious look; “because you will be supposed to have designs on me and my money? Speak candidly.”

“Well, yes——”
