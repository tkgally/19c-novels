“My dear Duke,” said Mr. Doughty, for it was he, “I am sorry to stop you, but will you let me ask you a question? Do you ever make up your mind on a particular matter?”

The other looked at him haughtily. “Make up my mind? Perhaps I do; perhaps I do not. Why do you ask?”
