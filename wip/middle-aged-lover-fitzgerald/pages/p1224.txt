Immensely gratified at this compliment, the solicitor’s wife could only smile and bow, and was so delighted that she did not perceive that her august visitor confined herself to the compliment, and promptly rose to descend to the office for the business she had alluded to.

Lady Duke was in a moment on the most confidential terms with the lawyer.

“Should not something be done in the matter?” she said. “It is getting very serious. There can be no doubt—if what I have heard be true—that he <i>has</i> taken up these strange delusions.
