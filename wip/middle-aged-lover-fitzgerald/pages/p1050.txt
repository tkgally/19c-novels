# Chapter IV.
[[subtitle]] The Gardiners “Do Something.”

MR. DOUGHTY reached London and spent a good deal of the day there. His excited eyes and hot hands were noted by the man of business, who conjured him to get home as quickly as he could.

“I feel dreadfully ill, indeed,” said the other; “and should there be any sickness coming on me, I may rely on your faithfully carrying out all that I have directed.”
