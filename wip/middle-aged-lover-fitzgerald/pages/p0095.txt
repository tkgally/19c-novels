Now there will be many who will consider this young lady to have been a low, artful young creature, angling according to her lights, in an inartistic way, for a young man superior to her in station, and doing her part with goodwill in this little conspiracy for entrapping a young fellow. Yet this would be an unfair judgment. She really liked—or fancied that she liked—this young gentleman, and felt the emotions which she so naïvely expressed. After all, there is no such prodigious harm in struggling to reach a higher station, and the same struggle is repeated in all ranks. As in revolutions, it is success that constitutes the legality.
