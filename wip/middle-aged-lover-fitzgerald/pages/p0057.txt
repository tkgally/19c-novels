# Chapter IV.
[[subtitle]] The Nagles at Church.

THE “hole” to which Mr. Nagle had made such contemptuous allusion was an obscure little town in Wales, into which the family had been driven, two or three years before, by stress of weather, as it were. It was a rude spot; and a music-master is about as much wanted in such places as he would be at some newly-found gold diggings. No one at such benighted places cares to learn music, even were it taught at sixpence a lesson.
