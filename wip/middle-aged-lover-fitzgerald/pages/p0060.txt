The impression left was that he preferred “to wipe out the past,” and suffer the consequences of his original error, whatever it was. An assumption, for which there was better authority, was that he had whilom trod the boards in English opera, and, it was believed, had played with the enchanting Caradori Allen, though some affirmed that he had done no more than carry a white night-gown as second Druid, and had been merely addressed collectively, in company with a large number of ladies and gentlemen, by the fair singer.
