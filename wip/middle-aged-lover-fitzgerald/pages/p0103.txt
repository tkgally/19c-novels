Mr. Nagle, therefore, felt that he must chiefly rely on his family resources, and on the assistance of a friend or two. After all, it made but little difference; for, as his ardent supporter and canvasser, Bill Gardiner, observed, the chief point was to dispose of all the tickets beforehand, and secure the cash.

“All you want,” he added, “is to get the people together. Once they were seated, any meats and dishes would do.”
