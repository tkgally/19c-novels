“Nothing can be done till then,” she repeated. “What do you mean?”

“I mean,” he said, “that we can do nothing <i>now</i>, Lady Duke. Things must take their course. It would not be proper to oppose him in this notion—he is determined upon it—and it would only increase his state of nervous excitement.”

“And he <i>is</i> excited?” she asked, eagerly, “nervously so?”

“If you had been there to-day,” he answered, “and unfortunately you were not, you would have thought his manner strange, very singular indeed.
