Mr. Doughty’s servant heard his master pacing about in this fashion for some hours, and was at last abruptly summoned to his room to receive orders to have the things packed for the first train in the morning.

“But you are not well enough to travel. Doctor Spooner, sir, said particularly——”

“I am not going to travel,” was the reply. “Be sure you call me in time.”
