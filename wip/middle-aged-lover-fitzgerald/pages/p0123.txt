A smile of triumph rather than of satisfaction lit up Corinna’s face. The rest of the family discreetly withdrew, and left her to meet the visitor.

Mr. Duke entered with a downcast air.

“I know what you must think of me,” he said, “and I appeared weak, and even mean; but you don’t know—you can’t understand how I am situated.”

“But I do know, I do understand,” she answered with a quiet scorn. “I am bitterly disappointed. I, who thought that you at least would not have deserted me.”
