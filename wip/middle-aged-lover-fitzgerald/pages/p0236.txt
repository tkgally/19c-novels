Lady Duke was the greatest lady there. So the host came to “take her in to supper.” He approached her as she sat, the centre of an obsequious group. “We must go in to supper,” he said. “But, first, where’s mademoiselle? Ah! There’s some pretty interesting work going on there. Master Alfred will have something to tell you when he gets home to-night.”

Lady Duke looked a little alarmed at these public compliments.

“Alfred,” she said, “is the most terrible flirt in the world.”

“Oh,” he answered gravely, “but this is no flirtation. Miss Corinna is a person who must not be trifled with in that style. Her disposition is too noble, too much out of the common, for such treatment. Ah!
