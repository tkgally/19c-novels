Mr. Doughty was indifferent. He had thought of sending for some one. But one physician seemed to him as good as another. So he left it to his anxious friend.

Away spurred Mr. Nagle, always in his element when on some mission of this kind. He has a good many brethren and sisters in the world, who in any situation of the kind arrive booted and spurred, as it were, and delight in hurrying off express to fetch this person and that. For such persons a sudden illness is quite a godsend. Mr. Nagle having selected Doctor Spooner, made straight for that gentleman’s house.
