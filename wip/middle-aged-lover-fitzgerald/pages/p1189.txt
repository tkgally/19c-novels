The conspirators were thus whispering with heads bent close together, when a voice came from below stairs which made them start.

“I say, Spooner, <i>are</i> you coming down? Am I to be kept below here all day?”

It was Mr. Nagle.

“This man here again,” said Lady Duke. “What effrontery!”

“The coolest piece of assurance!” said Mrs. Gardiner.

“Well, after all,” said the doctor, “we must make some allowance; he is not so much to blame. I’ll speak to him. I know how to manage him. May I ask you to go away now?”
