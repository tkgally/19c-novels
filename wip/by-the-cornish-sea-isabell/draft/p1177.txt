# Chapter XII.

[[subtitle]] A Merry Christmas.

<blockquote>
Hope deferred maketh the heart sick.
[[right]] <i>Proverbs.</i>
</blockquote>

C<sc>hristmas</sc> D<sc>ay</sc> came without bringing much mirth to the St. Enodoc fishing population. No little dainties graced their tables as in most of the years gone by—it was hard even to provide bread.

Arthur and his mother dined at the Trewhellas’, where the day was honoured, as Rebecca put it, by a small piece of roast beef, which in more prosperous times would have been despised for its insignificance.
