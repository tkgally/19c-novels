“This’ll never do, Becca,” said Dan, as soon as he became acquainted with the cause of the gathering; “teeake the cheeld a minute, while I send um about their business.”

This he soon accomplished, telling them that the child was well and was being cared for, and that if they wanted to see her they had better come the next morning.

When the morning came among the first who appeared was Mr. Carnanton the vicar, a kindly, middle-aged bachelor, who spent his life in trying to alleviate the miseries of the poor and distressed of his parish, and in carrying the message of his Master’s love both to the sick and well.
