“How are you feeling, Mr. Daniel?” inquired Mr. Burlington, peering at him through his green spectacles; “don’t you think a little brandy would be advisable to steady your nerves before you go into the cave?”

“Thenky, sir, all the seeame,” answered Daniel, “but I think brandy es wisht poor treeade to stidy a man’s nerves when he’s goin’ into deeanger; it’ll more like dazzle his eyes and meeake un all mizzy-meeazy. I’m thankful to say my nerves es ginerally putty stidy, but tedn owing to drinkin’ brandy, though I doant mind a drap now and then.”

“Oh, very well,” said Mr. Burlington, “you know best, of course. Are you going in by yourself?”
