[[cont]]if Polvarth beach were well-stocked with ghosts, there were so many lurking-places ready to their hand. The very buildings were ghostly, being dark cellars used for storing nets, and tenanted by lawless lodgers in the shape of rats. But even all these horrors failed often to keep Arthur from falling asleep at his post. Nature would assert her sway, and, as he could not venture to stretch out for a minute or two in a boat, not knowing when he would wake again, but was compelled to maintain an upright attitude, sent him to sleep as he stood.
