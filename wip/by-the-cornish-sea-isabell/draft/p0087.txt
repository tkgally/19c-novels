[[cont]]Arthur Treleaven was sent to such a school as this! Verily, O reader, I do, and make bold at the same time to affirm that good work was often produced from such unpromising material. A knowledge of the doings of Shetarbozny and the Apharsactites may not perhaps be of any great value commercially speaking, and Mrs. Warder’s pronunciation of the last letter of the alphabet as “ampassy” may be called in question, but learning how to avoid tangles and how to peel potatoes economically is by no means to be despised, especially when you have the multiplication table up to four times, and pothooks and hangers thrown in.
