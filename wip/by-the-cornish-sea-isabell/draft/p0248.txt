[[cont]]Canton. It ostensibly contained Cayenne pepper. No proof of this pretension had been sought—Cayenne pepper was not much in Daniel’s line—and the jar remained sealed, the central ornament of the mantelshelf. It was evident now, however, that it had contained Cayenne pepper, pure and unadulterated, for the pungent condiment set Mr. Carnanton and Jenifer coughing and sneezing with a vigour which the most powerful “cough-no-more” lozenges would have had some difficulty in coping with.

“Whatever—aticha—aticha—is the matter?—aticha,” asked the vicar.

“’Tis father’s china jar,” answered Jenifer as best she could through her sneezing; “the timber-waggon going down the street shook the shelf, and the jar fell, striking the fender, and smashing all to pieces.”
