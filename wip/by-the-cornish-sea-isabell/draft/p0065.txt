Mrs. Trewhella herself carried the child to the font, arrayed in a beautiful chrisom given by Mrs. Dunstable. As it was uncertain whether or not the baby had been already baptized, the vicar used the conditional form provided for such an emergency: “Jenifer, if thou art not already baptized, I baptize thee in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. Amen.” The cross was traced upon the brow, and the child was “made a Christian.”
