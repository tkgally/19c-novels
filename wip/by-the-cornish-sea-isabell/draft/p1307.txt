[[cont]]Bay and set their faces northwards, where lay their home, and hopes, and friends.

Cornish travelling is not usually done at express speed, and it was far into the afternoon before the trees of St. Enodoc appeared, although St. Michael’s Mount had been left behind before the sun was far advanced in the heavens.

“Home, darling,” whispered Arthur to his bride.

“Home,” she responded, pressing his hand.

As the words passed her lips the great bell of the church gave voice with a boom which rolled up the valley below them. Again it sounded as they entered the little town. Arthur stopped to ask a man he met whose funeral it was.

“Mr. Josiah’s,” was the answer.

“Mr. Josiah’s!” repeated Arthur in astonishment, “when did he die?”
