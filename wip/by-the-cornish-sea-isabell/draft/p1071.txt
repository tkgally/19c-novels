When he reached Deadman’s Haven, a little bay with a pebbly beach, bounded by two jutting points, he was astonished at hearing a cry from the bottom of the cliff. Whoever the cry came from was in great peril, for the advancing tide had passed the points and was rapidly covering the little beach. Arthur shouted in return, and peered over the lofty cliff. What was his horror to find that the prisoners exposed to such danger were Jenifer Trewhella and Esther Mansell. There was no time to be lost. Before he could run to St. Enodoc and get a boat round the point the tide would have reached them. The best plan would be to run to the farmhouse he had just left for ropes and assistance.
