[[cont]]Hand me my hat, ’Becca; I’ll be back again in five minutes.”

Daniel’s interview with Mr. Josiah was a satisfactory one. Mark was already somewhat in favour with the postmaster, and it could be arranged for him to begin work in a fortnight, if his parents wished.

This important point being settled, the company abandoned themselves to the enjoyment of grog, tobacco, and mild scandal.
