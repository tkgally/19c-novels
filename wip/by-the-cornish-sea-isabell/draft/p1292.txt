Daniel himself had undertaken the work of carefully dusting the impressive, albeit somewhat grotesque, pictures which adorned the walls, and at the same time imparted useful knowledge, and Samson and Joshua, and other well-known Scripture characters looked down blandly upon the roast goose and plum-pudding aforesaid.
