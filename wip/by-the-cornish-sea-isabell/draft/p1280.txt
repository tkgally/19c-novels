The boat drifted on, each day bringing greater misery to its occupants, until horrors were perpetrated which Arthur could only hint at. When the boat struck the land early one morning, Arthur Treleaven was the only one living of the six men who had left the side of the <i>Annie Lee</i>.

Awaking from an unquiet sleep, Arthur felt that the boat was gently grating against a coral reef which fringed a small island. He managed to get on shore, and soon found the water he was dying for, and a few sea-birds’ eggs, which served him for food.
