[[cont]]the sea was comparatively smooth, and there was no danger of a wave dashing in and sweeping them away before the tide fairly reached them. A bight was made in the rope, and Arthur, placing it round his waist, was lowered slowly down the hundred feet of the face of the cliff. He found the two girls cowering against the rock, their feet being already covered by the water.

“Now then, Jenifer,” said Arthur, “put this rope under your arms and hold on tight.”

“No, let Esther go up first,” asked Jenifer.

“Yes, do please let me go first,” exclaimed Esther; “don’t let me be drowned, don’t let me be drowned.”

“Very good,” replied Arthur, “there’s no time to stand here arguing. There’s no great danger from the tide, so she may go first if she will.”
