“But there’s nothing wrong with your health, is there?” inquired Mrs. Treleaven.

“That’s what the people do ginerally ax me,” replied Mrs. Craddock, “and it do worrit me to hear it. Tha’s cause I’ve got a rosy complexion, my dear; nobody weeant believe there’s anything the matter weth me, no, not if I’m dyin’ off my legs. But I do knaw from my own feelins that the seeds of mortality es sowed so strong in me that I ceeant last long.”
