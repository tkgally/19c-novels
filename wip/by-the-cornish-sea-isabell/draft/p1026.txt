“Good-morning, my friend,” said Mr. Burlington, “good-morning, it is rather earlier than usual. I prefer staying up late at night to getting up early in the morning. Indeed it seems to me, if I may so put it, rather—what shall I say?—indelicate, to intrude upon the world before she is properly awake.”
