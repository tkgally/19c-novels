“I should like to catch a bass or two before we go in,” said he. “I think, Jenny, I’ll row the boat a little nearer the lighthouse; they come in over the rocks there about this time of the tide.”

Jenifer had no desire to move from the chadding-ground, but she raised no objection to Mark’s proposal. Without further remark he pulled up the anchor and moved the boat three hundred yards away, where they were practically unseen and unheard. Mark seemed to have forgotten the object of his coming to this new station, and did not attempt to attract the aforesaid bass. He cleared his throat twice as if he had some idea of making a speech.

“Oh!” said Jenifer, when she found that Mark remained silent, “have you forgotten your text?”
