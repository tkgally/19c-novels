“But can’t you tell him?” asked Jenifer.

“Iss, I could, but you told he the lie first, and I think it ud only be fitty to let un knaw the rights of it yourself. You did wrong, my dear, and you must expect to suffer for it. Sin and suffer, sin and suffer, my owld mother used to say, and I’m sure she was right.”

Jenifer at last gave way and agreed that the vicar should hear the story that morning from her own lips.

Mr. Carnanton was deeply grieved to hear such a revelation from his most promising girl pupil, but glad at the same time that she had the moral courage to confess her fault. He did not expect to find human nature perfect; it was enough for him to know that it aimed at perfection.
