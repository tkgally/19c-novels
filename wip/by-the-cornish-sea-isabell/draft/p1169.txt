“But I will have it ef I’m sent to prison for it,” exclaimed Daniel.

“I should probably be sent to prison if I paid you,” said Mr. Philp. “You must wait until matters are arranged and the affairs wound up.”

“If that is so,” said Arthur, “we may as well go back at once then, quarrelling won’t mend matters.”

Daniel was at last brought to see the force of this, and the two unfortunate depositors left the town at once, and disconsolately wended their weary way home again.
