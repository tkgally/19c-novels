“What are you doing, my good woman?” he asked, drawing back.

“I want to wipe your boots,” was the answer.

“Wipe my boots! thank you very much, but they are quite clean now.”

“But they must be wiped,” persisted the fishwife.

“But I would rather not,” urged Mr. Burlington.

“I tell ’ee what it tes, sir; I intend to wipe they there shoes before you go out of this cellar.”

“Dear me,” said the antiquary, “this is very unpleasant. Is the woman mad?” he whispered to Arthur.
