Something white showed itself, but it was not the gleam of mackerel. For an hour fourteen men and boys had been toiling, and all the result was a large skate and a spider-crab.

“I knawed how ’twould be,” said Captain Vingoe; “all the fish went out while Treleaven and Nash was fiddling about. I doant knaw what the young fellahs are like nowadays: they’re like as if they was driving snails to market.”

“’Twodn their faut,” said one of the crew. “I doant believe we ever headed em at all; but wha’s the good of grumbling? Lev’ us overhaul the seine and look out for another school.”
