Do you know, Mr. Carnanton, of any old gate-posts which might perhaps be what we are looking for?”

“There is an old granite gate-post on the way to a farm called Tregulls; you know it, Mark, I am sure. Mr. Hobarts will give you any assistance you might want in examining it.”
