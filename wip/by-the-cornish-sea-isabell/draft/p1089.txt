The approach was up a lovely valley divided by a sparkling brook, crossed here and there by rustic bridges, which rippled and brawled over the pebbles. The sides of the valley were covered to their summit with ferns and wild flowers and stunted trees. The path which led to the fall was traced, sometimes one side of the brook, sometimes the other, now over a miniature mountain, through a gorge of which the streamlet rushed, now through the gorge itself, by the side of the cool water, over dripping moss and plants which love the dank recess.
