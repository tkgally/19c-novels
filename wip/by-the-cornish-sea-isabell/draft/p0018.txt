The darkness has fallen, and the gleam of the pier-head lights falls on the life-boat and the crowd assembled to witness the disembarkation. The coxswain leaps out on the quay, and heedless of the eager inquirers who attempt to stop him, hurries home with his treasure-trove—the little babe saved at such a cost—the child of the storm.
