A last dividend of 2<i>s.</i> 1<i>d.</i>, which was paid by the bank towards the end of the year, provided for the present distress; but this could not last for ever, and the necessity of taking immediate steps to avert semi-starvation or parish relief pressed itself with increased force upon Arthur Treleaven. The seine-owners, who had incurred considerable expense without any return, decided to employ one man only through the winter instead of two, and Daniel, being the older servant, was naturally chosen.
