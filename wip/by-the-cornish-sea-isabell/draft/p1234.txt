I would try to be a good husband to her, and she would be in comfortable circumstances and have no need to work herself to death, as I’m told she is doing now. And beside, I would see to it that you were cared for. You wouldn’t be afraid to trust her with me, would you?”

“Law, no,” answered Rebecca, “and ef she was well married we should be glad. We doant want to part with her, God knaws, but it goes to my heart to see her sleeavin’ for we.”

Mark’s frankness had won the heart of his old friend Daniel, who said,—

“We weeant foace her, my son, but ef you can git her to say ‘iss,’ we’ll say ‘God bless ’ee boath.’”

“Don’t tell Jenifer what we have been talking about,” said Mark as he departed,
