And she awoke and said to herself, “It is a token. I can save them only in one way, but God knows it is a hard way, yet if it is His will, I must not shrink from it.”

And a third time she dreamed.

She thought she was going to the church with Mark, and on the way met her long-lost lover, who smiled upon her, and said, “God bless you,” but when they stood at the altar, and she turned to look at Mark, behold, it was no longer he, but Arthur.

And when the morning came, calm and still and bright with sunshine, she resolved, calmly and bravely, for the sake of her father and mother, to say “yes” to Mark’s question if it should again be put.
