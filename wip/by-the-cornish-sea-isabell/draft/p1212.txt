“Well, Mark,” he said, “what do you think of her?”

“She’ll do unless I am much mistaken,” Mark replied. “I’ll write and ask Captain Tregurtha to come and look at her. So far as I can find out there’s plenty of tin in her, but the adventurers who opened her were a timid lot, and dropped her just when they should have kept her going.”

“What do you think of Mark’s ’cute mining speculations, George?” asked Jeremiah proudly; “he’s not very old, but he knows tin. He’s a Rundle if ever there was one; he’ll make some money, if he do live long enough. I suppose you were at church last Sunday night?
