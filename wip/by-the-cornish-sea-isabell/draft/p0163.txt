Mr. Burlington was an antiquary—irreverent people said a fossil. His long straight coat with immense pockets had an antediluvian appearance, and might have been worn by Methuselah in his declining years. The body which it covered was lean and long, and was surmounted when out of doors with an extremely tall hat, which had, apparently by natural selection, assumed the shape most in keeping with the substructure it was intended to crown.

“A charming county, charming county,” said Mr. Burlington, “so wild and romantic, and so full of interest to the antiquary.
