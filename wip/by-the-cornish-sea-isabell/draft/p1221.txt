# Chapter XVI.

[[subtitle]] A Man’s Love.

<verse>
Of a’ the airts the wind can blaw,
I dearly like the west,
For there the bonnie lassie lives,
The lassie I lo’e best.
</verse>
[[right]] <i>Burns.</i>

M<sc>ark</sc> R<sc>undle</sc> had been a week at St. Enodoc. He had finished his business, such as it was, and his leave from the post-office would expire on the morrow. It occurred to him that he should like to spend his last evening strolling about the cliffs and little coves which he had known from childhood.
