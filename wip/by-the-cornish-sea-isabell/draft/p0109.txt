[[cont]]and marched them off to the parish church, which was a good two miles away. This afforded great delight to Arthur. The quiet church by the river-side inspired him with great awe and admiration. Its three bells struck him as something sublime, for, poor lad, he had been accustomed only to one.

The ceiling never failed to interest him, for the bosses of the rafters had been painted by the rector himself with arms and devices, some natural, some grotesque, but all impressive. The tombstones, too, always afforded matter for rumination, they were so old and moss-covered, and bore such quaint inscriptions, and commemorated such paragons of virtue.
