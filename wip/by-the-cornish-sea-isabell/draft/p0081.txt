Indeed, but for this Mrs. Warder would never have assumed the professorial gown—she was not the one, as she herself said, to take the bread out of another woman’s mouth. Upon the deserted ruins, therefore, of Mrs. Harris’s seat of learning, the house of Warder, educationally speaking, was built.

Do not imagine that the school-building itself was an imposing one. No tall towers threw dark shadows across the street, no massive parables in stone soared aloft and half hid the sky.
