“No, not I; what’s the use of being confirmed? Aunt Craddock says it is only a form; you can be good without having the bishop’s hands put upon your head.”

“I don’t know, I’m sure,” said Jenifer, “I find it hard enough sometimes to do what’s right, try as much as I will.”

“Pooh! I don’t try, I do just what I mind to. Of course you’re very pious—you say your prayers, I’ve no doubt, as a good little girl should?”

“Oh, yes,” said Jenifer, “I never miss; but don’t you say your prayers, Esther?”
