Indeed, it was so uncomfortable that a stout Churchman once declared that only one parson had ever succeeded in sending him to sleep in it, whereas in the parish church the very dullest preacher would soothe him and give him something to dream about.

To this chapel of ease Arthur went as in duty bound. One Wednesday night, for some mysterious reason, he left the church before the time came when men take their rest in sleep, to find the reason for which it will be necessary to go back a step or two.
