[[cont]]I say we had better wait,” Jenifer answered. “You’ll say the same when you come to yourself. Come, dear, take me home, it is getting cold now, the sun is going down.”

They walked along the sand-hills in silence for some time, and then Arthur spoke,—

“Don’t blame me, Jenny, for my madness; I couldn’t help myself.”

“No, dear, I don’t blame you,” she said gently. “I knew you would go back to your first plan. You’ll trust me, won’t you, as I trust you?”

One long, long kiss was the answer, and then the young lovers sought their homes.
