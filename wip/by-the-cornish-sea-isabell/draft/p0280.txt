“In love, nonsense,” returned Mark; “do talk rationally, that’s a good fellow.”

“So I will, when you act rationally. If I may be so bold, may I ask what kept you gazing so long down Church Street? Was it the tower you were admiring? Or were you thinking how nicely Miss Jink brings on her pupils, as evidenced by the exquisite set of Miss Treleaven’s print dress?”

“I can’t stay listening to stuff like this,” said Mark; “I must run down to the quay at once, I’m wanted there.”

“Good-bye then, my love-sick friend,” shouted George after him as he strode off.
