“In the what, sir?” asked Daniel.

“In the com., that is to say, provision department.”

“No, sir,” said Daniel; “I think you’ve a cattered—doan’t they call it?—very well. I shouldn’t mind being cast away on a raft for a week or two with all that there provision. Keep her out a bit, capen, so as we may teeake the tide.”
