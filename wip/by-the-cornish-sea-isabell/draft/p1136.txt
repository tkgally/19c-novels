“Aw, where’s father and mother?” asked she.

“Gone to see Mrs. Treleaven,” answered Jenifer.

“And you’ve stayed behind to court a bit?” returned Mrs. Craddock.

“I stayed behind to finish some sewing,” answered Jenifer with a blush, for the sewing might have been postponed, and Mrs. Craddock’s suggested reason was practically the real one.

“Mr. Josiah doesn’t approve of courting,” said Arthur; “do you, Mrs. Craddock?”
