Arthur was employed as an attendant on the “bulkers” for a fortnight, when the last fish was put under salt, and had the satisfaction of carrying home to his mother twenty-five shillings, the odd shilling of which was a present from the owner of the seine for his good conduct. It is to be hoped that this amount more than sufficed to propitiate Mr. Sammy Webb, the Pendruthan shoemaker.

On the last day but one of the work in the fish cellar a stranger came in to see the pilchard curing. There was nothing singular in this—few visitors failed to see the curious sight; but there was a good deal of singularity in the appearance of the gentleman.
