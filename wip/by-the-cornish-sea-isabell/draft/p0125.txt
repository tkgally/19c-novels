Parsons are very fond of tellin’ people what to do, semmin’ to me, not that I’ve got anything to say against Mr. Carnanton, he’s a very kind gentleman, and sent some fust-rate soup to my owld man afore he died—not that soup could seeave un. I knawed he was goin’, and I says to un when Mr. Carnanton was there, says I, ‘You’ll never go out of this house, John, afore theert car’d out. Our passon here’ll soon have the buryin’ of thee.’ ‘Iss, I believe he will,’ he said, and so he ded.”
