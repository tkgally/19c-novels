“Can’t be, my son,” said Jeremiah, pushing up his spectacles in astonishment at this heresy, “can’t be. There can’t be nothing in this here mortal world more important than investments.”

“The fact is,” said Mark bluntly, “I am going to get married.”

“Married!” exclaimed Jeremiah, “married! I didn’t know you were courting. Who’s the young woman.”

“Well,” said Mark, “she’s not very well off, but she’s good.”

“Good,” grunted Jeremiah, “how much per cent. will goodness turn in?”

“It all depends,” replied Mark; “I’m inclined to think it pays very well sometimes. But, to cut matters short, I’ve asked Jenifer Trewhella to be my wife.”
