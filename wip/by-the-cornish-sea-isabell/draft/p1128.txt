“I don’t know about loving him, but we were brought up together, and from the time we were children used to look upon one another as different from other people, and now I—I don’t want to say anything to hurt your feelings, for you saved my life.”

“Don’t say that again,” said Arthur vehemently; “I did it for Esther Mansell, too, and I detest her. Forget that it ever happened, if you can. What I want to know plainly, Jenifer, is this, do you love me?”

“Please don’t press me for an answer now,” she said as the tears gathered in her eyes, “wait a little longer, Arthur.”
