[[cont]]lay tossing for hours before she could get any sleep. A struggle was going on in her mind between her inclination and her filial love. Her health was beginning to fail, that was painfully apparent, and with the loss of health would depart the ability to do anything for her father and mother. On the other hand there was a comfortable home offered her by one who undoubtedly loved her, and an assured provision for the old people. But she felt that she could not return Mark’s love. Ought she to accept him? But he had declared his willingness to accept her on her own terms. Could she say ‘yes’ to his proposal? Her whole soul recoiled from what she felt would be a profanation of marriage, and she resolved to abide by her decision. And, utterly wearied, she fell into a troubled sleep, and as she slept she dreamed.
