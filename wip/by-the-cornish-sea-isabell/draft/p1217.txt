The old lady did not recognize Mark, and her answer to his loud inquiry if Mr. Josiah was within was to produce a long telescopic ear-trumpet, adjust it carefully, and ejaculate,—

“Hey!”

“Is Mr. Josiah in?” shouted Mark.

“Ceeant hear a word you say,” replied Mrs. Pinnock.

Mark repeated his question at the top of his voice.

“No, I doant know nothin’ about your hin,” answered the old woman, imagining that Mark was making inquiries about some strayed poultry.
