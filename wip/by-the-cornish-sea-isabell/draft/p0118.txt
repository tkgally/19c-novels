“What are you doing here, you young rascal? Get up, and come into church. I’ll warm you to-morrow for this.”

“Please, sir,” moaned Arthur, “I’m bad.”

“That’s true enough,” said Mr. Potts, “but what’s the matter with you?”

“Oh, sir, I’m sick.”

“Well, get up then, and go home; you’ll make yourself worse lying there upon the grass.”

But Arthur’s legs seemed unequal to the work of transporting him home, and the relenting Mr. Potts said,—

“Wait a minute then, and I’ll send some one home with you.”
