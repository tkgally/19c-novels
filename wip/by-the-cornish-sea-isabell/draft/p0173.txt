Unfortunately a sunken branch lay across his path, and catching his foot in this he went floundering into the black greasy mud. Mark bravely rushed to the assistance of his patron, who, despite his mishap—nay, in consequence of it, was clinging to a large boulder which he had struck in his fall.

It is a matter for regret that Mr. Docton’s antiquarian bump was so poorly developed, but the truth must be told that, instead of assisting the over-eager Mr. Burlington out of his difficulty, he remained standing on the edge of the pond, while a broad grin spread over his face—perhaps this was the result of a twinge of the rheumatism!
