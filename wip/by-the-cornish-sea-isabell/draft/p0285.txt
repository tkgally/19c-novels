Mark had little to say to Jenifer, and she rallied him upon his absent-mindedness, when, after unhooking a fine fish, he threw the hook into the boat and the chad into the sea.

“Why, whatever is the matter with you, Mark? are you fretting because you left Esther behind?”

“Not I,” was the answer; “I wanted you, and nobody but you to come with me. You’ve got a fine fish to your line this time.”
