When Arthur reached home he found his uncle pacing what he called his quarter-deck, which was a pathway in front of the house about fourteen feet long. Here the old salt smoked his pipe and took observations of the clouds, varied by occasionally hailing passing vehicles in seafaring language.

“‘Back your topsails and I'll put a pilot aboard,’ he shouted as Arthur hove in sight. ‘‘Where are y’ from?”

** Back of the Gull Rock,’’ answered Arthur, laughing.

“Keep her full again.” continued the old mariner. ‘‘What’s your cargo?”
