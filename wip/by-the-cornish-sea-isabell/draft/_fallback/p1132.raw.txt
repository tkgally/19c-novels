Adjust it carefully—steady —now look. The calm exterior, after—all, is no criterion of the workings of the mind. She had fixed the day herself, but now she shrank from giving the final decision, perhaps more from nervousness than anything else. It is no light matter to decide by one monosyllable the course of a lifetime. It is nota thing to be taken in hand lightly unadvisedly, or wantonly, for itis a harbinger of joy or sorrow, nay, it is a sentence of life or of death.
