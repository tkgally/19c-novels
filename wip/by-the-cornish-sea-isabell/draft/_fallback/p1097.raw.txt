CHAPTER V. HA, HA, THE WOOING O°D.

Sigh no more, ladies, sigh no more, Men were deceivers ever, One foot in sea and one on shore, To one thing constant never; Then sigh not so, but let them go, And be you blithe and bonny. Much Ado about Nothing. Mrs. TrewHetta waxed very indignant when Jenifer arrived home and related her scene with Mr. Burlington by St. Conan’s brook.

** The owld rascal! to go meeakin’ love, as he do call it, to you. Why he’s owld enough to be your grandfather, and he do look owlder than that—owld enough to

be fust cousin to Methusalum.”’
