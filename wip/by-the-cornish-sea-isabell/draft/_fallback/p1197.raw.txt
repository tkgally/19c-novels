CHAPTER XIV.

THE VOICE OF MANY WATERS.

We thought of wrecks upon the main, Of ships dismasted, that were hail’d And sent no answer back again. Longfellow.

Artur was gone and Jenifer was left to wait for his return with what patience she could command. If the brig arrived safely a letter would come back in four or five months, and there was the chance of her being spoken to by some homeward-bound ship. News came when they were not expecting it.
