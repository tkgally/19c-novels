For a week the Nelson seine remained in the water, showing every day a smaller number of protruding fins, as the fishermen took them on shore to sell fresh or to place in salt for exportation to the Mediterranean.

When the following Saturday night came, a grand “sharing’”’ was held of the money realized by the sale of fresh fish. The owners of the boats and nets took half of the receipts, and the remainder was divided among the fishermen. Arthur had the proud pleasure of taking twenty pounds .to his mother as the result of the week’s work, while he announced :that another
