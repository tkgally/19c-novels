92 BY THE CORNISH SEA.

Rebecca became so indignant at this libel upon her sex that Daniel was fain to light his pipe and saunter off towards the quay.

Jenifer left the house to escape her father’s badinage, but she found things _ quite as unpleasant in the garden, for over the hedge was her whilom lover, Mark, enjoying the cool summer air as he smoked his cigar. The meeting was somewhat an awkward one. Both were conscious that their relation to each other had changed since the evening when Mark in the boat had made love to Jenifer.

‘**T was coming in soon to see you,” said Mark; “are you very well?”

“Yes, I am very well,’ was Jenifer’s answer; “father told me he had seen you. How long does your holiday last?”
