CHAPTER VI. THE GREAT SHOAL.

Fish that through the wet Sea-paths in shoals do slide, and know No dearth.

Milton. It was early October, and the trees around St. Enodoc were putting on their winter garments, making a lovely background of brown and red and green. The sea was calm—a sea of glass—and the town quiet as if a deep sleep had fallen upon the dwellers therein.
