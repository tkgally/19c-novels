“Will you fix a time when you will tell me yes or no?” he asked. “Say ina month.”

**A month is very short,’’ she replied, ** twill only be the beginning of February then. Suppose we say six weeks, that will bring us to St. Valentine’s Day. On the fourteenth of February then, Arthur, I will say yes or no, but you must promise to say nothing to me about it in the meanwhile.”
