The good, simple-minded vicar had acceded to their wishes, and the font had accordingly been wreathed with spotless flowers, and in a few minutes the children of the Sunday-school would be trooping in to see the ceremony.

Here they come. Throw open wide the great southern door, and let the long procession in. In they pour, chubby-faced boys and bright-eyed girls, each carrying a white flower, until the nave is full, and they overflow into the north and south aisles.
