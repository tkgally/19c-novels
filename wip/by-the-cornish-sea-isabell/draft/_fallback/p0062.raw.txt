But these venerable personages were looking quite gay to-day, if not absolutely juvenile. Beautiful sprays of clematis were wreathed round the four corner pillars, while the main support in the centre was hidden quite by a bed of snow-white dahlias and asters. And the panels in which the aforesaid figures had stood for ages were almost covered by wax-like camellias, leaving only the ashen grey faces of the Apostles visible. What was the meaning of the decoration of the font? All the rest of the Church bore its usual aspect.
