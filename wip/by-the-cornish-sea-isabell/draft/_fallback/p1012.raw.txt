Arthur, in the course of a week, entered upon his partnership with Daniel Trewhella. The profits were to be divided into tbree portions, one each of which the two fishermen took, while the other share went to Daniel, in return for his providing the boats and fishing-gear. Arthur, by this arrangement, was brought a good deal into contact with Trewhella’s wife and daughter. Rebeeca took very kindly to the strong, manly, young fisherman.
