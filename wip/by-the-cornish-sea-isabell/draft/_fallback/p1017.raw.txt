“We've done very well to-day,” answered Dan cheerily, kissing his buxom wife; ‘we've catched nigh upon eighty mackerel, and as they’re so scarce, we’ve meeade three ha’pence apiece of three score of ‘em to owld Betty to sill again, and the rest we’ve brought home. Where’s Jenny, mother?”’

“Why, she was here a minute agone,” was the answer; ‘I believe she’s in the back kitchen teeakin’ up the supper. I s’poase you'll look in after supper, Arthur.”
