“But what harm have I ever done to anybody?” repeated Mrs. Treleaven.

“No, theest never done no harm to nobody,” replied Betsy, “tes your man, my dear, tes your man. Haben ee reed in the Bible that the sins of the feeathers shall be visited ’pon the cheldurn? Well, your man stopped Peggy Pentruse from carring off some tember that was washed in t’other day, and she’ve a ill-wished the booy to sarve out his feeather.
