“Fix some desks with hinges to the seats in the chapel,” said he, “and the thing is done. Turn them up on week-days and you have your school; turn them down on Sundays and again the building becomes a chapel. Buy some slates and slate-pencils, tell the boys to bring their own copy-books and pens, and you can open school as soon as you like. As for reading-books, there are plenty of Bibles, wanting perhaps part of the book of Genesis and some of the Revelations, but still quite good enough for the purpose.”
