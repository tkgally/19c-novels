“Who from Thy throne in the heavens sends forth the winds, commanding them to lift up the waves on high, and by Thy word brings peace again upon the face of the deep; guard my boy from the perils of the sea. He is the only son of his mother, and she is a widow. Oh, loving Saviour, bring home again the widow’s staff, and wipe away the mother’s tears.”

Arthur had never been parted from his mother, and the thought of the separation combined with his mother’s prayer to overcome his composure, and he sobbed outright as in broken words he commended his mother to God.
