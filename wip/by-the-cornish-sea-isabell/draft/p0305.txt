“No, I expect not,” was the answer; “but you’re pretty well, arn’t you, Mrs. Craddock?”

“I look putty well, but I’m railly a poor wisht woman so fur as strength is consarned, and my life es a burden to me; I’ve got such a wisht lot of neighbours. I say sometimes to Mistess Trewhella—she married a second cousin to my poor owld man’s fust wife’s brother—I says to her, says I, ‘Mrs. Trewhella, we’ve all a diviated from the path of rectitude, but my neighbours! aw, my dear! their teeth are set on fire and their tongues are sharp swords.’”

“But why do they molest you?”
