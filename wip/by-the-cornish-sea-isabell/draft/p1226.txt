How would she receive it? And yet he could not let this opportunity slip. His love was stronger than his fears.

“Jenifer,” he began huskily, “don’t be angry with me; I can’t let you go like this. It is useless trying to keep my feelings under—I must speak.”

“I—I don’t know what you mean,” said Jenifer, somewhat alarmed.

“I mean, Jenny, that I love you with all the strength of my nature. Don’t be cold and cruel to me. If you can’t say ‘yes’ to me now, let me hope. I can wait. I am not a boy now, but a grown man.”
