“We must try to meeake up a raft weth the oars,” answered Daniel, “and then while the capen and George are pullin’ we’ll swem alongside so well as we can and keep his head out of the water. Will you stay here weth the poor fellow, Arthur, while I go off for the oars and things? A drap of the old gent’s brandy’ll rowse un up a bit I should think.”

Arthur did not mind staying now that he knew it was no ghostly personage he had to deal with, and he at once assented to the proposal.

Mr. Burlington and the captain and George were astonished to see Daniel coming out without the seals and to hear him exclaim excitedly,—

“There’s a man in the cave.”
