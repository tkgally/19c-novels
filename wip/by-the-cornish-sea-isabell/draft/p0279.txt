Jeremiah would have had his faith in the Rundle instinct rudely shaken if he had seen his son stare down the street after Jenifer. At any rate, Mark’s friend, George Roskilly, who had, unobserved, been watching him for some time, said in a stage whisper as Jenifer disappeared,—

“Hullo! Mark, my boy, what are you staring at in that moonstruck fashion?”

“Staring at?” said Mark, recovering himself; “what do you mean?”
