“Now fall to,” said Uncle Anthony when the luggage had been all brought into the house, “fall to, my dears, you must be hungry, sure ’nough, after such a long journey; and when you’ve finished your tay I’ll teeake Arter down upon the quay for a turn, and will shaw un to Capen Shortridge.”

“Show me to Captain Shortridge! what for?” asked Arthur.
