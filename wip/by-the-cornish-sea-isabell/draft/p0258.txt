There was the fascination of finding and capturing the fish, there was the peril of wind and wave and rock and darkness, there was the free life and the utter absence of monotony. He would have preferred a sailor’s life if he could have had his choice, but as his mother shrank with dread from any long absence, he settled down contentedly to work, which, he hoped, would enable him to maintain his mother and himself without depriving her of his company.

His uncle at certain seasons of the year gave up his building operations for a time to act as watchman over some oyster-beds. The pay was good, and there were no bad debts; this latter consideration especially making the post and pay of watcher extremely desirable.
