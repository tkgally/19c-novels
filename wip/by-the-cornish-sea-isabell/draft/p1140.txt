Two mothers were made happy that night.

“I want to tell you something, mother,” whispered Jenifer when Daniel had gone to bed.

“What is it, my dear?” asked Rebecca.

“’Tis about Arthur, mother,” she whispered again.

“Arthur?” said she; “there’s nothing the matter weth he, es there?”

“No, nothing the matter,” replied Jenifer, “but he has asked me and I have said ‘yes.’”

“Do ’ee mane that you’re goin’ courtin’?” asked Rebecca.
