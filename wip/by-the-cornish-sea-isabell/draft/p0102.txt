[[cont]]tell you what—when we’re married, Jessie shall come and light the fire, and clean the boots, and wash the clothes.”

Jessie agreed to take this humble position in the future household of her brother, and Mark went on,—

“I’m going to have a big house then, with a green door and a knocker; and when anybody knocks, you must open the door and say, ‘Now, then, wipe your dirty shoes, and don’t bring the mud in about the house,’ just like Mr. Josiah’s Mrs. Pinnock.”

“What’s that? what’s that?” said Mr. Josiah himself, emerging as he spoke from the back of Trewhella’s house; “what are you youngsters up to?”

“Oh,” answered Mark, “we’re playing at weddings. I’m going to marry Jenifer, and Jessie is to be the parson.”

“Playing at weddings!” said Mr. Josiah.
