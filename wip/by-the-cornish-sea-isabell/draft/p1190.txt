Jenifer entered the room just as Mr. Josiah began the quotation from his favourite prophet, and learning from a whispered remark of her mother’s that Arthur was going away, stood as if transfixed with dread.

“’Tis too bad, Mr. Josiah, for you to terrify women like this,” said Arthur indignantly; “there’s no more danger in going to the Cape than in going anywhere else: anybody would think I was going to live there, to hear you. It is not very likely that the lions and snakes will come on board the brig, even supposing there are any there, and as for fevers, they are to be got without leaving England. Don’t cry, Jenny, God can bring me home again.”
