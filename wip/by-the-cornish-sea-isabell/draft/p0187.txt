“Dear me, dear me,” said Mr. Burlington, “how very clumsy some people are; he should have left it for my friend here, he would have done it beautifully. If the skin is broken, he had better apply a fig to the wound—a most excellent thing, I assure you.”

The vicar went on his way, wondering what would be the state of Mr. Hobart’s feelings when he became aware of the fresh attack upon the foundation of his gate-post.

The following day he met him, and was not surprised to find that he was still more incensed.
