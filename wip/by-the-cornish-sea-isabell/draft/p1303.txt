Perhaps,” he continued with emotion, “they would never have been said if the death of as good a girl as ever trod this earth had not left me to walk the path of life all these years alone. But I mustn’t speak of that now. I hope your wedded life will be long and happy, and that heaven’s choicest blessings will rest upon you.
