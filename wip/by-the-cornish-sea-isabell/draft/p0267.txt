After an hour’s pull at the oars they reached the bay, and soon were fortunate enough to see a shoal of mackerel darting through the water.

“Shut away! shut away!” shouted Captain Vingoe in high excitement.

Tarpaulins were thrown off the nets in both boats, and in a few minutes the two nets were in the water. The boats rowed in opposite directions, the two making, but in unequal proportions, a complete circle, for only in this way could the swift-swimming mackerel be enclosed.
