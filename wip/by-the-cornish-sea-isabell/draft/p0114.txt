Why? Is it nothing for a lad to be able to disport himself with an enormous cigar in his mouth just like any grown person at no expense whatever? This desirable consummation—to boys—the worthy Austrians placed within their reach.

True the elementary stages of cigar-smoking are somewhat painful, and—so ribald scoffers say—the advanced stages are of little worth. But what of that?
