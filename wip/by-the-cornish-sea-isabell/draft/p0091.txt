He could not at all understand how the two sides of the orange-like globe could be got to look one way without presenting flat surfaces at the back. He managed to look behind the map one day when the master was out of the room, but failed to discover anything likely to help him out of his difficulty.

As a set-off against this it must be confessed that some of his difficulties were solved very summarily.
