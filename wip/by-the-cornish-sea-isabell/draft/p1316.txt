“Why, father, are you ill?”

“No,” he answered, “but I’m upset by Mr. Josiah’s will.”

“Are you disappointed at not being remembered?” inquired Mrs. Dunstable.

“No, ma’am, ’tes the other way. I’m to have a thousand pounds, and the news have a’moast tookt my breath away. And more than that, there’s another thousand for Jenny there.”

“I am delighted to hear that your declining years are so well provided for,” said Mrs. Dunstable to Daniel, “and” (turning to Arthur) “equally pleased to hear that your wife is not a dowerless bride.”
