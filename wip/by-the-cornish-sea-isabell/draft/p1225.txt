Jenifer was touched by Mark’s last words, and she therefore stretched out her hand, and the reconciliation was accomplished.

After a long conversation on the past, the two wended their way homewards together. When they reached the last stile and St. Enodoc came in sight, Mark paused as if to look again at the view seawards. The bar was shut off by an intervening point, and the roar of its surf was borne but faintly to their ears. The gentle lapping of tiny wavelets on the beach below was almost the only sound which broke the evening stillness.
