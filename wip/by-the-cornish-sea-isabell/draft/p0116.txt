When, by great pluck and perseverance, he had got half-way through, it occurred to him that perhaps, on the whole, it would be well to reserve the remainder of the cigar for a future day.

The bell struck out just then for the usual Wednesday service, and at once his mind was made up; he would spread the pleasure over two days instead of ending it in one. He therefore wended his way in pensive mood to the church.
