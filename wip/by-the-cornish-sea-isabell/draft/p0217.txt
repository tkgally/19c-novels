She felt therefore that she did well to be angry.

“You wicked girl,” she exclaimed to Jenifer, “to put this into your Bible; I shall tell Mr. Carnanton.”

“But I didn’t do it,” protested Jenifer.

“Hold your tongue,” said the teacher, “you are making matters worse by telling a lie.”

Jenifer’s tears began to fall at this, and she sobbed, “I didn’t, indeed I didn’t, Miss Summercourt. I wouldn’t do it for the world. How could you be so wicked, Esther, as to say you saw me with my book open during prayers?”
