But listen! what is that which is heard above the pealing of the organ and the sound of many voices and the roaring of the trees as they clap their hands together? It may be thunder, for the lightning is flashing and flinging its lurid glare into the dim light of the church. Boom! there it roars again, a sound of terror to the practised ear. What can it mean? It means that men’s lives are at stake, that a gallant ship is struggling with the mad waves on a lee shore. It is a signal of distress—pray God it may not be a funeral knell!
