“Mr. Thornlake, please leave the matter to me,” said his wife.

“But I was insulted, and I will ask,” exclaimed Mr. Thornlake.

“Remember, Mr. Thornlake, that I am the mistress of this school, and do not intend to give up the reins even to you.”

“Yes, you are the mistress,” retorted Mr. Thornlake, “and the sooner you teach your pupils how to behave the better.”

“Mr. Thornlake, I will not be lectured by you.”

“And, Mrs. Thornlake, I will not be dictated to by you.”

“If you do not know how to behave, Mr. Thornlake, you had better go and look after your bookstall.”

“Confound you and the bookstall too,” exclaimed the enraged Mr. Thornlake, rushing out of the room, and banging the door after him.
