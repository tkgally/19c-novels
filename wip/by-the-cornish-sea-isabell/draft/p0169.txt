“Quite,” was the answer.

“Then I’ll send for him,” said Mrs. Dunstable, ringing the bell. “Spry,” she continued, to the man who entered, “send to Rundle the ropemaker and say I want him to send up his boy Mark at once, if he’s at home.”

In about a quarter of an hour Mark arrived, and after some explanation was installed as guide to Mr. Burlington.
