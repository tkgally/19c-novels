But the year of grace had expired, and Arthur—“will he, nill he”—began to climb the steep hill of knowledge. There was no discussion in the house of Treleaven to settle what academy should have the honour of educating the heir. Ancient custom had settled that matter.

“Of course, Mrs. Treleaven, you will send Arthur to Mrs. Warder’s school?”

“Oh, of course.”

The man who would begin his child’s education at Pendruthan by sending him to any other preceptor than Mrs. Warder, would be set down at once by respectable people as a violent radical—a person who wanted to turn this rightly ordered world of ours the wrong side up.
