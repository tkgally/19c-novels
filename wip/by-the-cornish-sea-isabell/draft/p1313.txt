“Also I bequeath my body to the earth, to be buried therein, in a comely and quiet, and Christian fashion, constantly believing that it will rise again when the trumpet of the archangel is sounded.”

Here the cousins drew their chairs a little closer to the table and squared their elbows. They felt that Mr. Hercules was now coming to the point, and were concerned to know how the residue of the estate was to be disposed of. The above-mentioned personal effects they considered of but nominal value.
