A bashful blush overspread Rebecca’s comely face at this open avowal of her husband’s love, and she laid down her cup to make a fitting reply, when Mr. Josiah again took up his parable.

“A wife is very desirable to sew on the buttons to a man’s shirt, and to cook his dinner, but she can’t minister to the other side of his nature, she can’t feed his intellect.”

“I’m Joan plain,” snorted Mrs. Craddock scornfully, “and what I says es this—when a man wants hes dennar whas the good to talk about feeding hes hinterlect?
