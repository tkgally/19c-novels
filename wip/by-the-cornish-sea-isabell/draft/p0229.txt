“Is this the place where they are curing the fish, my lad? May I come in?”

“Yes, sir,” answered Arthur, “you can walk about where you like.”

“But will you tell me what is going on? What are these fish lying on the ground?”

“They are pilchards just landed.”

“And these boys and girls, what are they doing?”

“Carrying salt and fish for the ‘bulkers,’ and the women you see on the fish are the ‘bulkers.’”
