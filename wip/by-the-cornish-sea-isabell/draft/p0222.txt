“M<sc>other</sc>,” said Arthur, rushing in one day after school, “the seines are ‘shut,’ and they say they have caught a lot of fish; shall I go to cellar to-morrow? I dare say they’ll give me eighteenpence a day.”

“Go to cellar!” exclaimed Mrs. Treleaven, “of course not; your uncle will be put out if you come away from school now you are getting on so nicely.”
