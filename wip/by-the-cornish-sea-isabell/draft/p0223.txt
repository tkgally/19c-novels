“Well, my dear, I can’t say but that a few shillings would be very handy. I haven’t been well enough to do much lately, and there’s that bill to pay for boots; but I’d rather work my fingers to the bone than you should do such dirty work against your will.”

“But mother, I want to go; it won’t be worse for me than for the others, and I know that bill is troubling you.”

So it was decided that Arthur should “go to cellar,” and on the following morning he commenced his work.
