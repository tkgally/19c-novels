Again he heard the moan, which seemed now to come from a human being, and a cold perspiration was beginning to stand upon his brow when he was relieved by the reappearance of Daniel with the lines.

Daniel laughed when Arthur told him of the sounds and rallied him upon being nervous, but another and a louder moan coming from the darkness even while he was speaking, he began to think that after all there was something strange in the cave.

“Flare up your torch,” he said; “lev us see what ’tes.”
