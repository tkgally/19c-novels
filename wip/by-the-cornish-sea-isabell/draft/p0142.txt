“Did Mr. Thornlake know the boys?”

He could not lay claim to that pleasure. There was one boy he thought he could recognize, who came to him with an insulting request for a pennyworth of shoemaker’s wax.

“Would Mr. Thornlake endeavour to point him out?”

He would, with great readiness.
