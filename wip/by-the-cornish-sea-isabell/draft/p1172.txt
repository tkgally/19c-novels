The potato crop, too, in their little patch of ground, was almost destroyed by the ravages of disease, yielding barely enough to pay the rent of the ground, much less anything to help them in their time of need. Many of the fisherfolk were compelled to run into debt at the little shops, with the hope that the mackerel and pilchard fisheries would enable them to free themselves. The Trewhellas and Treleavens were fortunate enough to avoid this, the dividend of five shillings in the pound which the bank paid at the end of three months from the failure affording them present relief from the pressure of want.
