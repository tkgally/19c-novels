[[cont]]“what have ee got there? Why, seeave us, tes a cheeld. Poor tender dear, tes drippin’ weth salt water, and so cowld as a stooane. Where’s her mother, Daniel?”

“Dooant ee hear me say that all the other people es drowned and only this cheeld seeaved?” answered the coxswain.

“Aw, the tender dear,” said Rebecca, throwing away the black and white plaid shawl which was wound round and round the babe, “what a little beauty it tes to be sure, a regler little angel, edn she, Daniel?”

It was indeed a sweet child. Her age might be about ten or twelve months. The features were as regular as if shapen by the hand of a sculptor, and the complexion was like ivory for whiteness. The full black eyes of the child looked up at the kindly matron holding her so tenderly, and the little lips parted into a smile.
