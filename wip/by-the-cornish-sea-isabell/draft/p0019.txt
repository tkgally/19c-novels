# Chapter II.

[[subtitle]] Ill-Wished.

<verse>
As the old swain said, she can unlock
The clasping charm, and thaw the numbing spell,
If she be right invoked.
</verse>

[[right]] <i>Comus.</i>

“Don’t be so foolish, Betsy, who would be so wicked as to ill-wish me? What harm have I done to anybody? Besides I don’t believe in ill-wishing?”
