Arthur, on his part, was too much bewildered by recent events to realize his present position or future prospects: he only wanted to comfort again the heart of his widowed mother. As he passed through the churchyard he was hailed by the old sexton, who hobbled up to him and croaked out,—

“That’s where I put her—a capital bit of ground, never brokked afore.”

“What do you mean?” asked Arthur, a cold shiver running through him. “Whom did you put there?”
