“Hush, hush, Mrs. Craddock, doant ee talk like that; he’s gone, poor man, and I hope to a better pleeace,” said Mrs. Trewhella. “But I always says there’s faut boath sides. If Daniel or me have a word or two sometimes, tes generally six of wan and heeaf a dozen of the other, edn it, owld man?”
