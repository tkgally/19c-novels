“I’ve heard plenty say that in my time,” said Captain Penbole, “but they all picked up again afterwards. Drink a drop more brandy.”

“I am beginning to doubt the efficacy of brandy,” murmured the sufferer, “but I’ll try another sip.”

“Take a good swig,” suggested the captain.

Mr. Burlington, acting on this advice, emptied the flask, with the result of apparently setting his interior on fire. He gasped and coughed and groaned, while the tears ran down his face in streams.
