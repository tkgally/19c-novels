Mr. Josiah went as near smiling at this outburst of Mrs. Craddock as it was possible for that austere philosopher and despiser of women to go, but he drank his tea and munched his heavy-cake in silence—it was beneath his dignity to prolong the discussion with such an illiterate woman.
