Arthur’s demonstration of satisfaction was so outrageously contrary to the agreement just made that again Jenifer was compelled to remind him of his promise. By this time they had reached St. Enodoc, and had to take leave of one another.

“Good-night, Jenny,” said Arthur, pressing her hand. He felt sorely tempted to press her lips, but restrained himself by a strong effort. St Valentine’s Day was six weeks in front of him, and then—yes, what then?
