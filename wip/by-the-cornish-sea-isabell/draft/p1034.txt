“So he told me many a time. He said he was pulling a boat past the hole when a marmaid came out and made meanings to un a bit and asked un to come every evening, and so he did for a long time, till at last he tried to catch hold of her to lift her in the boat, when she gave the awfullest yowl that ever was, and went off with such a capperouse that she nearly filled the boat. He said he came along here a brave many times afterwards, but he never saw the marmaid no more.”

“But do you think your relative really saw all this?” asked Mr. Burlington.

“Well, sir,” answered the captain, “I heard un tell that he’ve a seen more stranger things than that. There’s that shurk, Dan, you’ve heard un tell about he?”

“Iss, a passel of times,” replied Daniel.
