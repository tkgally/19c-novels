“Oh, thank you very much,” said Mr. Treleigh hastily, “but please don’t trouble, my time is so taken up by one thing and another, that I am afraid I could hardly manage to read it in any reasonable period. ’Tis very kind of you to offer to send it me.”

“Would you care for it?” asked Mr. Burlington, turning to the hostess.

“No, thank you,” that lady replied. “As for poetry, I leave that to George; and with respect to arches and pillars, I think when they fall down the simplest thing is to replace them by new. I’m not at all sentimental in the matter.”
