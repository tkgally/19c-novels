“Well, never mind figures,” said Mrs. Trewhella, “draw foath and have your tays. Teeake the arm-cheer, Measter Josiah; you set there, Mrs. Craddock. That’ll do, Dan, you needn’ rock the creeadle any more, Jenifer’ll slape all right now. Kezia, you set here alongside me.”

“Iss, iss, Rebecca, to be sure,” sniffed Mrs. Sims, “tedn the fust time I’ve a sot alongside of you in this here parlour, and glad enough I am to set alongside of anybody, times es so bad—millinery edn what it used to be, I can tell ee, and so many new falderals and fashins do come in that I’m drove to me wit’s end. Ah! this is a good drap of tay, Becca.”
