“I am sorry he gave you all this trouble,” said the vicar, “but I dare say he forgot his appointment. People of that kind are apt to be forgetful. I am going into the town, and if I see him I will jog his memory.”

Mr. Carnanton found Mr. Burlington sooner than he expected, for when he reached the post which had caused all the trouble, there was that worthy standing in the middle of the road, with an enormous pipe in his mouth, gazing blandly through his goggles at the grave-digger, who was busily engaged in making a second pit on the other side of the post.
