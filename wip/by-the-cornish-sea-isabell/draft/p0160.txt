# Chapter XIV.

[[subtitle]] The Antiquary.

<verse>
Sometimes he angers me
With telling me of the moldwarp and the ant,
Of the dreamer Merlin and his prophecies,
And of a dragon and a finless fish,
A clip-wing’d griffin and a moulten raven,
A couching lion and a ramping cat,
And such a deal of skimble-skamble stuff
As puts me from my faith.
</verse>
[[right]] <i>Henry IV.</i>, Pt. 1.

A <sc>lady</sc> and two gentlemen were seated at breakfast in the dining-room at St. Enodoc Castle. The lady was Mrs. Dunstable. Twelve summers had come and gone since we first made her acquaintance, but they had treated her kindly, and she was still as stately and good as ever.
