Jenifer, who had been uneasy while Mr. Burlington was making his little speech, now left the room, her fair face covered with blushes.

“A very fine girl, a very fine girl,” said the antiquary, not putting it quite so mildly now that Jenifer was gone.

“Have ’ee got ovver the sail hunt?” asked Daniel, abruptly changing the subject.

“Well yes, I think I am all right again now,” answered Mr. Burlington, “but I have come to the conclusion that at my age it is well to abstain from such perilous adventures, especially on the sea, Mr. Daniel.
