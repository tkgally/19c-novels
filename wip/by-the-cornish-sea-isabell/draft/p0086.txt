[[cont]]“Shetarbozny,” repeats the boy, (“Twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-one,” counts Mrs. Warder), “and your companions,” (“I’ll give you the ceeane, Billy, you naughty boy you, I ceeant hear meself spake”), “your companions the A-p-h-a-r-s-a-c-h-i-t-e-s” (“Apharsactites” from Mrs. Warder), “Apharsactites which are beyond the river,” (“Doant ee spake so loud,” from Mrs. Warder again, “do ee think I’m deef?”), “be ye far from thence.”

“Forty-one, forty-two,” said Mrs. Warder as the boy concluded, “that’ll do, sit down and learn your teeables while I meeake a pasty for dennar. Jeeames, you skin the teeaties; Jessie, you peel they two onions; Tommy Waters and Freddy Wilton, you wind off they there two hanks of worsted, and mind you doant git it into a tangle.”
