He would gladly have picked a quarrel with Secretan, being jealous of him in more relations than one. For, before Secretan’s arrival in these parts, Ezekiel Doidge had been a little king in Chillington. He had organized and domineered over every club and society of which the little place boasted, until his self-conceit became insupportable; and, the county gentlemen always holding aloof from the town and its affairs, his position appeared to be impregnable.

But no sooner did Simon Secretan come into the neighbourhood, than he plunged eagerly, full of philanthropy and other dangerous fads, into the life and doings of Chillington.
