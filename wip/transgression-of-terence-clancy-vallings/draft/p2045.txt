The taciturn captain smiled again at the old man’s simple cunning, but the smile was not acquiescent.

“I agree with you,” he said moodily, “as to the increased respect; but it is so few days since that scene at the vicarage. Her pride will still be rankling, and she’ll make a scapegoat of you, and insult you, and—and I shall hate her more than I do already!”

“My dear lad, my dear lad,” murmured the father with a kind of despair, “is it possible that you really hate her? Why, she has never hurt me intentionally; it horrifies me to think that I should be the cause of your estrangement.”

“Not another word, father. It is your persistent kindness that makes me hate her so.”
