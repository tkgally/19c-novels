“Very likely.” Her lips formed the two short words with difficulty. “The man’s astounding blindness,” she mused, “is ridiculous—or sublime, I hardly know which?”

They threaded the garden paths, climbing gradually upwards to where the ferny wall cut off the grounds from the moor.

“Not a glimpse of them yet; they’ll be under the larches, as I said.” Simon’s voice was serene as the summer afternoon.

They were soon coasting along the low wall, with the blaze of gorse and heather on their right, and Chillington glimmering in hot mist away on their left. Then they entered the green gloom of the larches, their noiseless feet pressing the carpet of moss beneath.
