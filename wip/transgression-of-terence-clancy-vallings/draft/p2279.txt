“You’re really mistaken,” Kate insisted. “I have no more hope whatever of destroying Simon’s peace in that way.”

“But I have,” put in the old squire; “and I’m not agoing to drop it to please that captious fellow Julius.”

They both turned to laugh at the old gentleman; and Julius pressed with mock eagerness for an explanation.

“You shan’t bully me into giving reasons for my view of the case,” chuckled Mr. Rush; “but maybe I have one or two, though I don’t pretend to be as clever as you young people. You let me alone, and happen I shall triumph over you both one of these fine days?”
