The walk through the woods turned out to be stifling beyond expression, and the final climb to Moor Gates up a steep lane, whose high ferny banks cut off whatever breath of air might be moving, caused Mrs. French-Chichester to register a solemn vow never to do the distance on foot again; better the ravages of curiosity than such a squandering of vital power as this.
