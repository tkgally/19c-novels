“Poor little Mary! Is her health bad? Is she much upset by this? What can I do for her?”

“Ah, that just the point. She han’t written to the gentleman for some time, but now she <i>must</i> see him.”

“But, my good Joyce, how can I help her there?”

“Easy enough, sir. He’s an intimate friend o’ yours, as she tells me,—a man as you’ve been a brother to and started in life, and——”

She stopped abruptly. Simon had fallen back a pace or two, and was staring at her amazedly.

“He’s a young doctor, an Irishman——”
