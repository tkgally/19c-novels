Maybe I’ll drag on somehow, maybe I’ll muster courage to cut it short with a dose of poison. Anyhow, I’ll pester you no more. Give me another liquor, old fellow—’tis the last request I’ll ever make of you.”

Simon pushed over the decanter mechanically, then fell to striding to and fro, muttering to himself, “A horrible idea! to sit down and let them tear my character to pieces! Yet what do I care for any one’s opinion? My father would suffer, were the evil report to get round to him. Yet, would he suffer? Wouldn’t it be rather a satisfaction to him to find me even baser than he had imagined? I think it would pain Nell, yet not one-thousandth part as much as would the discovery of the true culprit.
