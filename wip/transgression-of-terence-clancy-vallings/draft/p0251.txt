“You’re utterly unjust to him, Kate. He is perfectly innocent; he has not the slightest suspicion of my weakness; he is simply a kind friend. You don’t know him—oh, you don’t know him; he’s the very soul of honour!”

“Then honour’s a poorer thing than I’ve been brought up to suppose. I’ll tell you what I do think about him: he’s a man of excellent intentions, soft heart, and double nature—the sort of man who does better as an instrument for spoiling another’s happiness than any downright scoundrel. I say Terence is a good-hearted shuffler, a kind of innocent sneak——”
