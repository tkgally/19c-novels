He should have furnished Terence with a large sum of money rather than have consented to this insane, dishonourable silence. But Terence had wrought upon Simon by his fatal persuasive faculty, and used Nell as a lever for the upsetting of his judgment. There remained only for Simon to break his word—in other words, to cease to be himself—or to lie upon the bed prepared by his own folly. “Terence will dun me for money at the end of the month, anyway,” he concluded with a hard laugh.
