“Keep down, for mercy’s sake,” cried the fisherman, “or you’ll scare his life out!”

Then he looked anxiously up and down the water. Above him all was plain sailing, with one large pool opening into another, but below was a boiling rapid studded with jagged rocks, down which it would be difficult enough to follow a hooked fish.

“I’ll try him with a big Jock Scott,” he whispered to the ladies, who were crouching behind a boulder; “no chance of killing a twenty-pounder like that on a small hook, and with all this rough water about.”
