Terence was right as to the flooded condition of Chilling Water. The vehement stream was ramping forth from its moorland gorge and down its rocky bed, with spume and fret and fury. Leaping into the cultivated parts above the town, it overspread Mr. Doidge’s meadows as though angered at the tamer scenery, and gurgled into the cottages of his labourers in a masterful regardless fashion suggestive of the great Ezekiel himself.
