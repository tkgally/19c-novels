The subaltern, catching sight of Rush, exclaimed to his companions—

“Why, that must be Rush, of the 20th Dragoon Guards, one of the best polo-players in the service. Had no idea he belonged to these outlandish parts; must go and have a chat with him, by Jove! Why, he rode one of my ponies in a crack polo match once, and I was doosid proud, let me tell you!”

Presently, on the lawn where tennis was beginning, Mr. Pardington had an opportunity of carrying out this threat. Rush was standing alone by some rhododendrons when the other lounged languidly across to him.
