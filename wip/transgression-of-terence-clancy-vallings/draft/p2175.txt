“What are you driving at? Whom do you suspect?”

“Why are you so fierce and strange, Terence? I suspect no one. I only long for the real culprit to be found and the innocent man righted. Ah, if I only had it in my power to punish the man who has ruined poor Simon——”

“You wouldn’t spare him, I suppose?”

“After what he has done for Simon, I should be merciless towards him!”

“God help him, if you do catch him! He had better have drowned himself at once than have injured your hero!”

“Well, well,” said Nell, with a heavy sigh, “I ought not to have introduced a subject that pains you so much, Terence. I know I have done wrong, but you shan’t be troubled about this any more. Tell me further about your political views, for I am sure you were joking with me in pretending to be so hypocritical?”
