# Chapter II.

“HARDLY ever, dear lad, do I venture to set up my opinion against yours. Come, now, am I a bitterly dogmatic father?”

Julius Rush smiled, though he happened at this moment to be in a mood more favourable to frowning. The father and son were traversing the long conservatory which bounded two sides of the drawing-room at Bickington; the former was pressing a point with all the mild warmth and gentle earnestness which he possessed.
