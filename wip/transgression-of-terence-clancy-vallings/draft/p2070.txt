For a moment or two the white blossoms fell upon a silent party; then Kate resumed, turning abruptly to Captain Rush with the air of one resolved not to shirk a painful topic.

“I hope you side with me against Simon’s accusers?”

“My mind is dark upon that question,” he answered slowly. “I can’t believe what they say, yet know now how to disbelieve it thoroughly.”
