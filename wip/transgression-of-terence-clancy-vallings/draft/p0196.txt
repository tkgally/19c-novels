“I wouldn’t <i>measure</i> any one. The mere sight of a yard wand is hateful to me.”

So the foolish pair wrangled on, and, thanks to a little infirmity of temper on both sides, this effort of Kate’s to hoist a needed danger signal proved entirely abortive.

“You’re little better than a Honeywood, Simon, and some day your good nature will land you in the mire.” This was Kate’s parting shot. She delivered it with an angry nod and a resentful glance at her companion, then lashed her mare into a gallop and shot away from him.

But neither Simon nor his tall grey were to be left in the lurch thus, and the argument ended in a neck-and-neck race along the glade.
