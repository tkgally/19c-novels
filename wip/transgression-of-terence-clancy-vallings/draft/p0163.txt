“She has got a fair start,” muttered Terence. “But may the devil fly away with that cock-a-hoop, omniscient fool for giving me such a scare!”

Then he wended his way homewards, somewhat chilled and depressed, as well as frightened at his narrow escape. Fate always seemed to thwart him in his good moments, and to hustle him forward in his bad ones.
