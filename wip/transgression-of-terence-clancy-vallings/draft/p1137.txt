Mr. Tredethlyn, as it happened, was to be the principal speaker at the cricket meeting mentioned in the last chapter. He was to publicly accept, on behalf of the committee, Mr. Secretan’s gift of a cricket-ground; gracefully thank Mr. Doidge for his equally kind offer, with a neat side glance or two at the fine public spirit so often exhibited by him; and afterwards to praise and thank every one who expected it—that is to say, every one who had moved hand, foot, or tongue in the interests of the embryo club.
