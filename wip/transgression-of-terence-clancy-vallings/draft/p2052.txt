Upon his promotion from lieutenant to captain, “A” troop, the rowdiest in the regiment, had been given into his charge, and for four weeks he had made the lives of “A” troop a burden. They used to boast about it afterwards, when they had learnt to understand him and swear by him. Briefly, his reputation had a foundation in solid fact.

This stern captain of dragoons now saw before him the faces of those who had been despitefully using the old man for whom he had a protecting love fathoms deep; whose very timidity he reverenced; who had never spoken otherwise than gently to any one of them; whose lavish kindness they had requited with vulgar scorn.
