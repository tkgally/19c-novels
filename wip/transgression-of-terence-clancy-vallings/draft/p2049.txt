“Well, Julius,” he admitted reluctantly, “sometimes I do rather wish myself in a snug little villa, with two or three maids and a man. They—they <i>have</i> punished me somewhat of late. I almost think I could see my way to letting you remonstrate with Children and McTavish—not harshly, you know, but——”

“Dear old dad,” said the soldier, tenderly as a woman, his voice bringing a delicate flush to the old man’s cheek, “I’m going to make what men who have served in India call a <i>bunderbust</i> with you. I will pay that visit, and will be at least civil enough to Miss Tredethlyn to get some information as to Simon from her, in return for which you’ll give me a free hand with the servants. ‘Is’t a bargain?’ as your modest McTavish would have it.”

“But, my dear boy, you—you wouldn’t dis—it would pain me to——”
