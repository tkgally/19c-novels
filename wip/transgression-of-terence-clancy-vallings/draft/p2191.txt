“We’ll arrange a day as soon as possible; and if the recognition does come off, will you send word to me at Bickington at once?”

“Yes, I’ll send on the man himself, and you’ll know him fast enough—a little dirty, hook-nosed villain, five-foot nothing high, with a grey beard hanging down to’s waist.”

“Very well, I shall give orders that any one answering to that description be sent in to me at once. What do you think of a market-day for our experiment? Every one in the neighbourhood will be driving into the town at one time or another on that day.”
