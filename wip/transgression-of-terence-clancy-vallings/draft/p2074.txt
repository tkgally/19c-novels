“Oh yes,” quoth Kate; “most people keep a little justice and charity locked up somewhere in chests and drawers, and offer us a spoonful or two when the struggle’s over and we need it no more. Why, we may look for kindness even, once we’re past using it!”

“I’m bound to say, however”—the exasperating captain seemed bent upon trying Kate’s temper to the utmost—“that Simon has not yet given his public a chance of unlocking the chests and drawers you were speaking of.”

Kate glared at the speaker and relapsed into silence. Mr. Rush murmured something deprecating at his son, but was quenched by a single glance.
