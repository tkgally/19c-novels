“Ay—unworthy to tie her shoe-lace, and that’s how she comes to care for him,” said Kate, bitterly. “And think of the scandal, the disgrace—horrible! It goes to the marrow of one’s bones to think what people will say of her. But trust me for knowing her, father; I say that no earthly power will prevent her having him now. If we choose to make a martyr of him, we may complete the disgrace by a clandestine match; but a match there will be.”

“But surely our gentle little Nell can be worked upon and made to see——”

“Our little Nell is a person of strong nature and unspeakable obstinacy; and think to what a pitch she must have reached to break her engagement in this disgraceful way! No hope, believe me—not a grain.”

“And poor Simon?”
