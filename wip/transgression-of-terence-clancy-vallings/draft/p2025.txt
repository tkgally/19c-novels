“Who <i>is</i> this Mrs. Saunderson?”

“The daughter of a famous house—a gin distillery at Lymport. I’m told a sister of hers married into serge the other day, and another into haberdashery; but she herself has been clever enough to secure a gentleman—Captain Saunderson, of the Royal Red Clayshire. He has had to sell out, of course. What married quarters in the kingdom could have held his wife? However, he’s short-sighted almost to blindness, so he <i>may</i> not take to drink.”
