If the Tredethlyns were invited, Sir Hamo would have draughts on the brain, and seemed to have a settled belief in a universal conspiracy against the life of Nell; who, having seldom known a day’s illness, was often distressed by elaborate precautions and schemes tending to asphyxiation.

Now it chanced, about a week after Simon’s return from town, that Terence Clancy had an opportunity of observing these phenomena peculiar to Monks Damerel Hall; for Nell, with her father and sister, was invited to dinner. From the moment of their acceptance he noticed that the atmosphere of the house became charged with electricity. Nor was he himself untouched by the prevailing epidemic of excitement.
