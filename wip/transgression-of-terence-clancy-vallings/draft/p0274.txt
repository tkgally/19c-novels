“Terence,” she said presently, her white ungloved hand resting upon his arm, “I tell you frankly that I came to see you on Nell’s account as well as your own. Do you know that the date of her wedding is very likely being discussed at this moment while we talk? That the event will probably take place, if no steps are taken to save her, in a few weeks?”

“I knew it,” groaned Terence, “I knew he had been asking her to fix the day. And she must consent; if not now, in a day or two.”

“Yes; if no one cares for her enough to come to her rescue.”

“I fear that’s impossible. She and I are both bound hand and foot by chains of honour——”
