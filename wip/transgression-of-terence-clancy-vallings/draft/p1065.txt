“Ah, be off wid your blarney!” laughed Nell. “Ye’re more Irish than I thought ye.”

“Faith, you’ve got a heap of my failings to discover yet, Nell; they’ll be pouring down on you soon, like last night’s rain. My word, how it did drive and pelt! I see the river’s in heavy flood this morning. Some of the people in the cottages down there’ll be having a bad time, I fear.”

“Let us go and see them presently, when you have time. But I suppose you’ll be hard at work all the morning, Terence? When do you expect to be free from your consulting-room?”
