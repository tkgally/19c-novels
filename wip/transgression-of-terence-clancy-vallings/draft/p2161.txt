They made wary but definite advances to the adventurer-personage, and found him both intelligent and sympathetic, insomuch that he promised to develop into a thorough-going people’s man in due time. So far, so good; but now for the cross-current which has drawn this open-hearted young fellow into another channel.

“Our Tory member is young, but a brainless fool—you see I take this last opportunity of speaking quite candidly before undertaking the yoke of a professional politician; do not fear that blunt and hardy truthfulness will ever distinguish (and condemn) me hereafter—I say frankly, he’s a brainless fool, and my countrymen in the House (more power to ’um!) smother him with ridicule before ever the foolish tongue can wag off a brace of sentences.
