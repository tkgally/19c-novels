# Chapter VIII.

TERENCE CLANCY’S spasm of remorse passed over for the time, as many others had done before now, and by Thursday morning he was once again his cheery, jubilant self. It was then his turn to console Nell, who had been a good deal unhinged by his fit of despair, and who had not his happy faculty of throwing off a wave of depression by a mere outpouring of the heart to a sympathetic listener.
