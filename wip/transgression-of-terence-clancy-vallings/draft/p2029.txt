“I—you—you don’t seem to understand. I’m your Aunt Mary, I am. Han’t Mr. Tredethlyn, my brother-in-law, told you? Didn’t he get my note this mornin’?”

Still Kate said nothing, but stood in a stupor of amazement. The would-be, or real, aunt paled again, and tears came to her eyes.

“I—I never thought,” she whimpered, “as my own niece would treat me so, and me married to a gentleman! John dear, will you kindly explain to the lady who I be, and then we’ll go, and never see her face again?”

But John Saunderson was too angry or too wretched to make explanations; and the vicar had to step forward from the crowd, for gather round they would, mere propriety shrivelled for the nonce by intense curiosity.
