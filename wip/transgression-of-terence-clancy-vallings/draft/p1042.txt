# Chapter III.

ON a certain windless day, late in December, Simon Secretan was leaning over the low stone wall of the Monks Damerel road, looking down over Chillington. Through the thin clearing mist a trickle of rather wan light fell upon the roof of the railway station beneath him, upon the grey old town-hall beyond, and the square Gothic tower of the old church. There was little traffic along this road in the winter; Simon had a long stretch of it all to himself now. He was gazing intently, moving no more than the great rough stones upon which his arms rested.
