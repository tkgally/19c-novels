[[cont]]but it soon became apparent that nothing of the sort could be done. It was found that Nell could not touch the bulk of the property during her lifetime; and should she desire to ease her mind by devising it to Simon at her death, Terence would never dream of dissuading her.

With these and countless other arguments did Terence crowd the imaginary foolscap of his brief; then, having demolished in the process every possible counterclaim that his conscience might elaborate, he mentally revised the whole and put it aside for the present, while he plunged anew into the surge of happiness which Fate had rolled to his feet.
