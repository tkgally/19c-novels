We know how, on the last occasion of our seeing them together, she had resolutely set herself to accept her husband as he was; to love and, if possible, respect him, limitations and all. To that course of conduct she had clung with stubborn fidelity, and none would ever know how much it cost her. The trial had begun at once, for the most earnest endeavour to accept his explanation had proved futile. It was plain to her, as soon as coolness returned, that he had some dark secret on his mind, that he had glided from the very brink of confession by one of those slippery turns which seemed to come to him so easily.
