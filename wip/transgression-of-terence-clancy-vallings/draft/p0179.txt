And these milestones on the road to disaster have the curious property of becoming bigger and clearer the farther we get from them.

To let an honourable man such as Simon Secretan be seriously suspected would a few weeks back have been low conduct in Mary’s eyes. Even now she shrank from the trickery; yet—yet this definite despatch of Suspicion upon a false scent would be highly convenient. She had a real dread of Ezekiel; she feared him for Terence’s sake, more and more as Terence grew more dear.
