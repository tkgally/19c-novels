As they cheered him now his delicate skin flushed like a rose, his eyes sparkled with pleasure, his smile was bright and happy as summer waves sun-smitten. Old men upon whom that smile fell thought of their boyhood; young men who caught it hoped their sweethearts might be looking another way.

“There, now—did I exaggerate?” cried Mr. Rush, triumphantly. “You see for yourself how he takes hold of people and charms them. Isn’t he a brilliant, handsome, dashing young fellow, now?”
