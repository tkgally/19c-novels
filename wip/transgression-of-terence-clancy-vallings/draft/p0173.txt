“Just so. You have improved because you <i>have</i> worked at it like a slave, not on account of any talent.”

“But I played well this afternoon? You said so yourself.”

“Yes, well for <i>you</i>, Ezekiel; but unfortunately you have what I may call a fatal ear, which is far worse than none at all. You keep true for a time, then play perhaps a quarter of a note flat, and half kill the listener. If a string runs down, you’re not aware of it; you rasp the ear just as you often rasp one’s feelings—from sheer inability to——”
