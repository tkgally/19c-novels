Thus, as soon as Simon decided upon extending his visit, it was requested that the child and her governess should be sent for from town—an arrangement upon which Julius Rush commented to his wife in this wise—

“You’ll be glad enough to be rid of her in a week or two—for isn’t one spoiled child scourge enough for one household?”

But, to the surprise of every one at Bickington Park, little Rose turned out to be under very good control—a bright and frolicsome child, but certainly better disciplined than Kate at all expected to find Master Julius at six years old.
