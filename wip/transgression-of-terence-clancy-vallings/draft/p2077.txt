You might right Simon in the face of all men, restore him to his father, save for him his patrimony, which now stands to be thrown to the dogs or handed over to public charities; and so might earn the deep gratitude of the one or two friends that he has left in the world.”

“I’m glad you’re hopeful, but shall try my own plan first.”

“I don’t doubt that for an instant. And the plan will fail.”

“Not until it has had a fair trial, however.”

Captain Rush then turned to his father and suggested that it was time to be going; and the squire, of course, rose obediently to the hint. Miss Tredethlyn and Julius then exchanged a final defiant glance, and the party broke up, the squire carrying with him a treasure in the shape of a new and piquant sensation. Kate had warmly pressed his hand.
