“D—n it, the man has pluck!” muttered Syme. Aloud he said something confusedly about an engagement. He was conscious that this man with the pale face and quiet voice was mastering him as well as the housekeeper; there was a kind of proud despair about Simon that awed and touched him at once.

“I’m sorry you won’t lunch with me; but in that case let us drive back into the village.”

Jack Syme did everything he was told like a schoolboy under the master’s orders; and when they drove off, Mrs. Henley stood gazing after them.

“Has he gone altogether?” she asked herself. “Have we ousted his philanthropic lordship for good and all? If so, the Lord be praised!”
