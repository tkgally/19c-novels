He had made love to Nell without a spoken word, pacifying his flexible conscience with the sophistry that comes so easily to a well-meaning, semi-false nature. He was now in a whirl of triumph. True, there were still some points of difference to be settled with his better self, some jagged fragments of good resolutions rasping him, some underlining of shame to his satisfaction; but for the moment these things could be thrust aside, and he could give his joy full rein.
