“I have scarcely a doubt but that it will be something unpleasant, father. Has Mr. Clancy been up here much lately?”

“He has—been staying here—for—a few days.”

“Staying here? At whose invitation, father?”

“My own, of course, my dear.”

“I see.”

Kate’s look resembled that of a schoolmaster when he turns to the corner where the cane stands; and if the large parson had not the face and figure of a schoolboy, he had the feelings of one at this moment. He winced painfully, his plump cheeks grew a shade paler. He was ready to agree twice over with everything Kate might say, and would have preferred almost any penance to the prickly possibilities of the next ten minutes.

“Pray tell me what has happened.”

Kate’s tone made him wince again; it had the shrewdness of an east wind.
