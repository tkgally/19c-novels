# Chapter IV.

THE brief two weeks which Terence had been able to snatch for his honeymoon were over; he had brought home his bride to the White House on the hill.

It was Nell’s first morning in her new home. Terence was awaiting her in the little breakfast-room, looking cheerfully out over the town and the hills, gleaming crisp and clear after forty-eight hours of rain.
