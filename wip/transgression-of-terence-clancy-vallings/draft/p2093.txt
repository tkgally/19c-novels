Damme, sirs, let us drink success to the new chase! I reckon us’ll kill afore the month’s out. Come, fill up, Doctor Clancy. Here’s to a hot scent and a fast run!”

Terence filled with his back to the others. The strong draught brought the blood back to his cheeks, his spirits began to rise. He felt now as a man with back to wall and face to foe; to fight had at last become easier than to run away. His brain had become intensely clear, every nerve was now on the alert; he had never realized until this moment his own power and skill and cunning.
