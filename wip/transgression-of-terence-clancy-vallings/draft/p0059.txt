# Chapter IV.

MEANWHILE, they drew near the border of Hollacomb Great Wood, into whose green embrace rushed the Chilling stream, with flash and flicker of foam by way of brief coquettish farewell to the sun. Nell drooped somewhat at the prospect of entering this woodland solitude; she would have liked better to walk with her lover along the most open and frequented stretch to be found on the Queen’s highway.

Just inside the wood, however, a stroke of unexpected good fortune befell her; she caught sight of two familiar figures at the turn of the path.
