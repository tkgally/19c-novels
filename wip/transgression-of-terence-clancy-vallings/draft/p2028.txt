Mrs. French-Chichester, eyeing him furtively, whispered—

“He <i>will</i> take to drink, if trotted about like this. Has the woman no mercy?”

A moment later Mrs. Saunderson stopped before the pair, exclaiming, with her smile narrowed a hair’s-breadth by a touch of nervousness, “Kate, my dear niece, I’ve just come round to make your acquaintance!”

What had happened? Had the sky fallen, or the earth opened, or the sides of the universe cracked? Was she falling headlong through infinite space, or had some one stunned her by an accidental blow? Kate felt as though her consciousness were split open like a pod, and the shattered fragments flying round her. “Niece—niece—<i>this woman’s niece</i>?”
