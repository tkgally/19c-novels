“I believe you are right, Kate. It will be well for me to write also, though a more painful duty was never laid upon me. What are we to do about Clancy? what steps shall I take?”

“Nothing we can do will make any difference in the end, be assured of that. We may procrastinate a little, and so water down our disgrace in a measure. Let Nell be sent away from now until, say, December, which will give Chillington about three months to masticate and digest the broken engagement, and will make the next news less shocking. I will answer for Mrs. French-Chichester’s silence. It will be generally understood that Nell and Simon have quarrelled, and there’ll be so many theories as to the cause that the truth may chance to get lost among them.”
