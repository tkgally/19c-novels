# Chapter VII.

THE next few weeks were a time of pause for Terence and his group of friends—a quiet period during which nothing seemed to happen, though the seeds of future events may have been in process of sowing by unwitting hands.

Terence himself was for the most part occupied in driving about the neighbourhood with Dr. Rose, learning the weaknesses and weak sides of the various county families whose ailments made the bulk of the practice.
