During this visit to the hall Terence had become better acquainted with this visionary from whose kind offices he had hoped so much. Why, Simon’s schemes, he had found, were the standing jokes of the neighbourhood; and this visit would prove no better than a disastrous waste of time, an aggravation of the drudgery to which he was returning so soon. Why had Simon asked him here at all, only to torture him with a glimpse of paradise? His friend had shown him a most cruel kindness in this matter.
