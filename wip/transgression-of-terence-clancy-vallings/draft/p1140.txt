When Mr. Tredethlyn’s figure rose to its height, and his comely face beamed a welcome to this great audience, the buzzing murmurs ceased. No need for any tapping upon tables or appeals for silence, for every man felt that he was about to hear just what he would have said himself—had he possessed the knack of speaking.

Kate Tredethlyn, looking down at her father—she had left Nell in charge of a friend at her earnest entreaty—felt proud of him as the smooth, strong current of his speech began to flow. His periods had the ease and quietude of perfect self-confidence, his grip of his hearers was both immediate and firm. When he ceased, they all felt that the proceedings had opened perfectly,
