The manœuvre was completely successful. The group of ladies, relieved against the mist-laden vale, and bathed in the warm light of the after-glow, made a picture not to be resisted. Peering through the window, Clancy espied them, and stopped in the middle of a sentence; Captain Rush, following the other’s glance, pulled moodily at his moustache, and had nothing more to say. Sir Hamo, catching sight of Nell’s bare shoulders, walked to the window to upbraid her imprudence. In three minutes the dining-room was empty.
