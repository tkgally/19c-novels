The general appearance of the room, lately Sir Hamo Secretan’s study, suggested that the new master of the hall had already made a pretty fair beginning of the joyous pastime which he called “having his fling.” The chamber was now a perfect gallery of modern French and English pictures, evidently chosen by one who had no taste for mediocrity; the new furniture was the richest obtainable; the table was covered with costly nick-nacks, while a goodly pile of unopened tradesmen’s bills, the crop of a single post-delivery, occupied one corner.
