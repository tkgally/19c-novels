# Chapter VI.

WHEN contemplating an entertainment, on however small a scale, Sir Hamo was nearly always compelled to seek the assistance and support of his lady cousin, Mrs. French-Chichester. Without some such extraneous aid his battalion of petticoats used to get out of hand as well as hysterical.
