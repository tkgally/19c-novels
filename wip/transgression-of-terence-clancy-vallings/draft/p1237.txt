The glimpse he caught of Miss Tredethlyn donning furs and wraps, and of Simon preparing to bear her company, sent him reeling away, half dazed, into the darkness. For fear fathers a thought quite as often as a wish does, and Doidge’s fear now begot the notion that this visit of Kate’s was made to a repentant sinner, that she would never have continued such friendly relations with a hardened man who had confessed nothing.
