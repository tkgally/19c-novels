# Chapter VI.

PROBABLY there is nothing in the whole universe, material or spiritual, of elasticity so miraculous as a sanguine man’s hope; nor would a year’s search in a crowded land reveal a more naturally sanguine man than Terence Clancy.

When he descended to breakfast next morning half his mental clouds were dispersed; the sly gleam was returning to his eye, the vivid colour to his cheek.

“I hardly know you this morning,” cried Kate as he shook hands with her. “And certainly I needn’t ask after our patient?”
