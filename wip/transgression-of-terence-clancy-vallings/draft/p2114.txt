“I’m miserable, Nell, miserable,” he groaned. “There’s a bitter enemy seeking to ruin me.”

“What can he do? What possible hold can he have upon you? Won’t you tell me?”

There was a flutter of Nell’s eyes now, but she managed to keep it out of her voice.

“He’ll ruin me, Nell! I tell you he has power to do it.”

“What is it, Terence? Oh, pray tell me at once!”

“You shrink from me already —I feel that you shrink from me.”

“No, I don’t shrink; but the suspense tortures me. It would comfort me to know the worst at once.”

“Nell, you might hate me if I told you.”

“I could never hate you, Terence—I think not, I hope not, I’m sure not!”
