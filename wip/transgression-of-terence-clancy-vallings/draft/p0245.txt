Nell drifted into the entrenched camp presently, still drooping, languid, and quite unlike herself. She had been upstairs to change her dress, yet was still in her riding-habit; her dark eyes had lost their sparkle, her ordinary brightness was quenched. But Mr. Tredethlyn failed to notice anything, having a full share of the self-concentration of his sex; to gain whose ear one needs not only to be very ill, but to proclaim the fact at the top of one’s voice.

“Come and sit by me, little girl. You don’t mind the smoke? Kate could hardly endure this atmosphere, I think—and hope,” he added with an inward chuckle.

“No, I don’t mind the smoke, father.”
