“I shall do nothing of the sort, Kate; nor shall I even speak to him again except as a distant acquaintance. I shall avoid your house until he’s gone—and if I do meet him in society ——”

“Yes, yes—I know all about it, my dear child. Don’t expend all this excitement upon me, for it is a mere waste of time. You won’t stay and dine, of course?”
