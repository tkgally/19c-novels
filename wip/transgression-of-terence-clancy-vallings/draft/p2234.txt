His solicitor, even, is not allowed to know his address, but conducts his affairs through another agent at the Cape. Yet I suppose we can convey to him the news of our decision, and that simple message will suffice to show our wish to welcome him among us again. And, gentlemen, I do believe that if he ever comes back we shall understand our <i>Quixote</i> better, be ready to accept him as one who practises as much, nay more, than he ever preached. I think, though he failed in many of his schemes, he has now succeeded in one thing—that is, in educating us up to the point of comprehending a man made of somewhat finer stuff than ourselves.”
