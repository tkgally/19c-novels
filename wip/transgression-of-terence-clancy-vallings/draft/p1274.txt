How could he have been so blindly fatuous as to expect poor, feeble, shifty, well-meaning Terence to keep so difficult a promise? Confession from such a man, under such circumstances? Who but a fool would have looked for such a consummation?

The more Simon reflected, the more certain he grew that his only chance lay with this sick woman. Joyce could set him straight with the world, and her statement could be kept a secret until Clancy was fairly started in some distant place, whither the effects of past misconduct could not follow him. He now realized fully how much depended upon poor Joyce, that without her help he would never be able to right himself with his neighbours; and the possibility of having to live out his life with his name uncleared, now that he had viewed it from so near a stand-point, filled him with consternation.
