After a more perturbing scene than usual Simon would walk fiercely over to Moor Gates with a view to taking it out of himself with violent exercise, and afterwards soothing his spirits with Nell’s company; and in this mood he had come over to-day.

Upon reaching the house Simon and his cousin were informed that Mr. Tredethlyn had not yet returned from Chillington, and that Miss Nell and Doctor Clancy were in the garden. The servant made this announcement to Mrs. French-Chichester in a specially significant manner, adding under her breath—

“Miss Tredethlyn returns in a few days, ma’am.”

The girl’s tone was a sufficient revelation that the maids were not as blind as their master.
