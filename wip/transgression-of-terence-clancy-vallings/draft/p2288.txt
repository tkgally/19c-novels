From the conversation that followed the new arrival gathered that Mrs. Clancy and little Rose had been developing a mutual friendship lately, and could now hardly see enough of each other. It seemed that Simon, as has been said, brought the child over almost every morning for half an hour or so, and that she came again with her governess for a longer visit later in the day. The inquisitive old gentleman elicited these details by innocent little questions; he also ascertained that the little programme had been followed for some weeks.
