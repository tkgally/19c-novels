“Well, yes; I’m up there pretty often. Mr. Tredethlyn has taken a sort of fancy to me, and they are all very kind and hospitable.”

“And Secretan is constantly driving you up there?”

“Simon’s a friendly old fellow, certainly.”

Terence shifted his feet uneasily; a slight flush of annoyance was detected by his companion, who sat mumbling the stem of his pipe between his thick lips. No need for further question. Syme was satisfied that he had gauged Terence’s mental condition to a nicety.
