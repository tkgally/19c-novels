If we get no real gratitude, by Jove, we thump and hammer till people pretend a little! That’s what I do. Virtue may be its own reward, but that’s not good enough for me. Well, now, before moving a cordial vote of thanks to Mr. Secretan, I’m going first to propose him as the most fit, proper, and efficient captain the Chillington Cricket Club could possibly possess—and may we prevail upon him to accept the post!”

At this point Simon rose and coldly asked to be allowed to say a few words. Kate, looking down anxiously from the gallery, knew well what their tenour would be.
