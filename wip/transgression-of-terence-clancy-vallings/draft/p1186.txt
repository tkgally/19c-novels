Yet, for all his self-confidence, Jack Syme could not bring himself to ask any prying questions; for not only had Secretan’s stoical bearing a suppressive power of its own, but his very courtesy magnified the same. Nor could the doctor, whose own experience inclined him to look upon poverty and physical suffering as the only serious ills of life, realize that this proud, healthy, well-to-do gentleman, could be suffering any pangs calling for the pity of a poor devil like himself.
