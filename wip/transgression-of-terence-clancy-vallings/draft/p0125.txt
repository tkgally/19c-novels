[[cont]]I have a curious piece of news for you, in the shape of a message from Simon. You mustn’t disappoint him! Oh, you <i>mustn’t!</i> He would feel it so deeply if you refused. You know how anxious he has been to have you settled near him? He has been working to that end without saying a word to any one but me, and now has actually achieved it this very afternoon; if you will but consent to let him do you that much service. You <i>must</i> consent, for he has handed over to me the privilege of persuading you, and I’ve promised to succeed. Will you say ‘Yes’ before I tell you?”
