How to verify, or else to clear his mind of, a vague suspicion which at present seemed merely to be floating in his senses, and unable to find a definite anchorage; which, in fact, was rather a quickening of speculation in a given direction than a formulated suspicion. That was the main condition of the problem still demanding a solution. At present he had but a gossamer-thread of evidence to go upon; and, as a fair-minded man, conscious of a strong bias against the suspected person, he feared to be precipitate. On the other hand, he could not be easy in his mind without making some effort towards obtaining the needed verification.
