To make up his mind to see Doidge was a nightly task, to escape the task through pressure of other business a daily achievement. But the mental perturbation increased, while his fear of Ezekiel lessened. There came a day, some two weeks after Julius Rush’s entry upon the new quest, when Terence had knit himself up to the requisite pitch, and was actually on his way to the mill, proposing to consult Doidge upon some question of the Angling Association, then in course of conversation to speak of his health, and so work his way on by degrees to the needful examination.
