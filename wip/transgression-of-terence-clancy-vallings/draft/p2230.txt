# Chapter X.

THERE is no commoner or more conventional phrase than that which describes any given community as being “like one man;” and in the case of Chillington, when the story of Secretan’s self-sacrifice and Clancy’s duplicity spread through the little community, any one man, almost, might have been picked out to give voice to the sentiments of all.
