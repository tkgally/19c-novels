Captain Rush spoke the words, and was received with a smile—not an effusive one, but sufficient, so few and far between were his encouragements, to upset good resolutions in a trice.

They fell in behind the others, but their conversation dragged. It is difficult to be agreeable when clogged by anxious pride; and neither would so far relax as to make a genuine effort. Presently they halted at the top of some steps, whence a path led down through the shrubberies to the brook side.

“What would I give to take her down there?” thought the soldier.

“Why doesn’t he offer to take me?” thought Kate.
