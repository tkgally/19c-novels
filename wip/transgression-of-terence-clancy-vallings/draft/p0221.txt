“There, that’s your first salmon, old man; have another pull at the brandy-flask and drink his health. That’s all right, and now I’m going to hale you off to the farm for a change of clothes.”

There was a gleam of enthusiasm in Kate’s eyes.

“Look at him,” quoth she; “strong, capable, cool as a cucumber; with a man’s power and a woman’s tenderness—what an odd, fine, whimsical old Simon it is!”

“He concerned himself a deal more about the fish than about his friend,” muttered Simon’s cousin.

“By the way, what have you done with Nell?”

Nell came towards them as her sister spoke. She had a curious, dazed look, and was deadly pale. When they asked her a question she burst into hysterical tears and laughter.
