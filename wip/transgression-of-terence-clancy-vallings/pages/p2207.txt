Ezekiel had by this time reached the steps giving access to the lower terrace, and again halted, clinging for support to a carved stone vase. His victim could read his face now, clear as print, and the tale might be summed up in the one word—“ruin.”

Once more Terence turned his despairing eyes to the group on his left. The two men and Mrs. French-Chichester were gazing surprisedly at the approaching figure; but in Nell’s face there was something greater than surprise. He saw, or thought he saw, a growing horror of expectation, as though his former half-confession had suddenly grown complete.
