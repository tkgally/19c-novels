“You mustn’t be too easily snubbed, sir,” Joyce remarked, wiping the tears from her cheeks; “for she’s just such an impudent little hussy as her mother was before her.”

“Perhaps she’s hungry?” suggested Simon, as though struck with a bright idea. “I don’t know exactly what children eat; but would some cold beef meet the case, do you think?”

“Oh yes—with plenty of pickles!” Joyce laughed again hysterically, then put up her hand with a deprecating gesture. “You must forgive me, sir; but I’m so happy that I’m forced to make game o’ somebody. No, no, Mr. Simon, a child of twenty months isn’t fed on beef and pickles; but happen your housekeeper would poach her an egg.”

“Eggy!” shouted the child, with a wriggle of satisfaction; and Simon promptly went in search of Mrs. Venn.
