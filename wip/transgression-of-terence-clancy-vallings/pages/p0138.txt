Thus, with every intention of avoiding the Tredethlyns, Terence constantly found himself on the road to Moor Gates. If there were no invitation from Mr. Tredethlyn, Simon himself would be sure to drive his friend over on some excuse or other.

Simon was in Chillington almost every day just now, looking after some allotment gardens, for which he had given a piece of land below the town, or putting finishing touches to the White House, as Terence’s new home on the hill was named.
