[[cont]]here I am. Mary’s in trouble. She was engaged to be married to——”

“Ezekiel Doidge? Yes, I know, and it was broken off.”

“Psha! that’s nothing. Why was it broke off?—that’s the question.”

“I haven’t a notion.”

“That looks bad; he’s a shrewd un, I fear! Harkee, sir—’twas because she was in love with some one else, with a gentleman, whose ring she’s still wearin’, who was her promised husband. He got tired of her o’ course—one man’s the same as another, as far as I know—and left off walkin’ out with her. Lord bless me, ’tis an old story enough. She wrote and wrote, but never got another word out of him. He was makin’ up to some lady of his own station, I doubt.
