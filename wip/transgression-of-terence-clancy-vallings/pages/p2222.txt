“That’s <i>all</i>,” he concluded; “that’s the story of my life since I first knew you; and the moral of it is sad enough—that kind feeling and good intentions go for nothing, worse than nothing, without principle and will to back them. You know the worst of me now, Nell, and I can pass into the other state free from the burden of any secret. But I fear you will turn?”

No, Nell did not turn from him now.
