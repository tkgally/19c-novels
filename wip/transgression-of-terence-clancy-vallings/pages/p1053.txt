“Disappointment!” she repeated fiercely. “Ah, how some of my husband’s cuss-words would help me now!”

Joyce walked aside for a space, with a sullen flush darkening her pale face, and neither spoke.

After a few moments the silence was broken by the sound of bells. A loud joyous peal clanged from the tower of the parish church below them.

“A weddin’ peal, I reckon?” Joyce burst forth with a harsh laugh. “I wonder who’s the victim this time? What’s the lady’s name, Mr. Simon?”

“The bells are ringing for Miss Tredethlyn’s marriage.”

“Not Miss Nell? not the one that was——”

“Yes, the one that was mine.”
