At a bend in the bridle path they found him awaiting them, and he at once drew Terence aside, whispering eagerly—

“Canter on, old fellow, and give me an innings, for I have something special to say to Nell.”

With anger and bitter jealousy Terence shook his friend’s hand from his shoulder and rode on, cursing Simon, and his own miserable fate, and the sickening conventions of the world. He knew well enough what the lover had to say: no leap so quick and sure as that of jealous intuition.

“He seems a bit put out,” Simon remarked simply. “I hope you have not been quarrelling with him, dear? You must leave that to Kate, you know.”
