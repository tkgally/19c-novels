The question of how to hoodwink this dangerous foe was occupying Terence as he strolled to and fro under his verandah after breakfast, when a maid approached him with this message—

“There’s some one a-waitin’ for you in the consultin’-room, sir. Mr. Doidge, from Chillington Mill.”

In a moment a fresh brood of fears swept down upon Terence and staggered him.

“Let him take a seat, and I will come immediately.” He got these few words out with difficulty.
