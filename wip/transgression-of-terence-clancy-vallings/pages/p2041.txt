The black-browns smoked along the road, whirled round the short drive from lodge gate to front door, and Kate sprung out. The boy who took the ponies shivered when he caught Miss Tredethlyn’s eye.

In a trice she was in the study. It was empty.

Her fierce peal of the bell was answered immediately; her hopes dashed to the ground the moment afterwards.
