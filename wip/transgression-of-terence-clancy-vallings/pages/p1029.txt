# Chapter II.

KATE TREDETHLYN was seated in the breakfast-room at Moor Gates next morning, awaiting the appearance of her father. Having received a telegram from him the night before, urging her immediate return home, she had thrown herself, full of gloomy forebodings, into the night mail, and had driven up to the door but a few minutes since.
