There was a laugh from within, full of mirth and sunny good-temper; at once rich and soft, and far too fine for that vulgar little chamber.

“Yet I don’t fear you, Terence, old boy. You’re a love’s-young-dream of a doctor—too bright, too fair to last; too gentle and pleasing and pretty for this cold hard world.”

Again the bright laugh, brimming over as with spring sunshine.

“There, there, Jack Syme, don’t be for trying sarcasm. Fat beasts shouldn’t gambol, but just chew on respectably, as you see them doing by the stream yonder.”

Syme grinned again, then laughed boisterously.
