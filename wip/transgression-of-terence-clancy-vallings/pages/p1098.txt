People were ashamed to speak in public against one of such blameless reputation and high position; but ugly whispers and innuendoes were crawling like serpents up and down the bylanes of Chillington.

Terence knew that it was so, and the knowledge made his life a nightmare, while narrowing day by day the precipice-path which he seemed to be treading.

Simon, who must surely now be a deadly enemy, knew of his guilt. The single glance this once-friend had thrown at him while lifting the dead girl from Nell’s knees was not to be misinterpreted.
