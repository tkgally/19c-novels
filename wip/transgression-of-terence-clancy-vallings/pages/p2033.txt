Captain Saunderson, who felt much for Kate—and would have felt more had his own misery been less acute—now came to the rescue. Never before had Kate quite realized the blessedness of what seems under ordinary circumstances but a mere outward grace—good breeding. Captain Saunderson, though quivering with discomfort, at once engaged her in easeful small-talk, and they were soon strolling away as though nothing had happened, the large aunt beaming beside them. There were tears of gratitude in Kate’s proud eyes when she turned them upon the little captain.
