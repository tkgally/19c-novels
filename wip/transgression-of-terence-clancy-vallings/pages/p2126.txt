Moreover, he had always looked upon Terence as too subtle and fanciful in his diagnoses, though capable often of making phenomenally good shots under difficult circumstances.

Later in the day, however, Syme found the miller in a tolerably favourable mood, and in obedience to orders carried out the desired physical examination with such skill as he possessed.

“Have you seen Doidge?” asked Terence, when his assistant returned.

“Oh yes, I’ve seen him, and gone through all the usual hocus-pocus; and I believe your aneurism to be a mare’s nest.”

“Well, after all, perhaps you’re right,” said Terence.
