“I’m sorry I confessed at all, now,” cried Nell, angrily.

“Don’t mention it, my dear; I shall manage to bear up against it pretty well—especially as I’ve known the whole story for months; moreover, it interests me to see how deep into lunacy a woman will wade, if given rope enough. Proceed, my dear child, I’m all ears.”

“I’ve quite done, Kate.”

“Finished already?—and I had composed myself to listen for half an hour. . . . I’m obliged to joke, little Nell, because I’m so vexed, do you see? And I should like to abuse Simon, because it’s so aggravating to have to admire him. I do admire him, though I try not; but I wish he had never seen you.
