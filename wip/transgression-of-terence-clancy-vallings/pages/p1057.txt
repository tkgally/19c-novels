And so they passed down the hillside, the child bubbling and babbling, and shrieking with laughter, only stopping now and then to cry, “Poor—poor; oh, poor—poor!”

Had Joyce known who was following them at a distance, she would have led Mr. Secretan anywhither rather than to Hollacomb Farm, for, unknown to her, Ezekiel Doidge had seen her arrival with Mary, seen the latter walk away homewards, watched Joyce get into Mr. Baker’s gig, and guessed her destination.
