Nay, more—for the whole truth seemed to burst upon him now in one vivid flash—it was she who had undermined him with Nell. It must be so; he could swear that it was so, though no such thought had ever entered his mind until this moment. She had favoured Terence, had been his backer and stealthy abettor, as Simon could perceive, now that the key of the situation was in his hand. Terence might have been her unconscious tool, but a tool he had been. She had used him to widen the breach between father and son, and so to wrench them asunder, and leave the old man with no one but herself to look to.
