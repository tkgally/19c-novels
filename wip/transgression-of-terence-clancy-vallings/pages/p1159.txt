This brief speech was received with loud applause. There was a closing in upon the spot where Simon stood, and not a few threatening gestures from those near him.

The threats and upraised hands energized Simon in a moment. He struck his fist upon the table in a spasm of fury, crying aloud—

“I have nothing to say. Take that for an answer, you curs—you curs! Make way for me there!”

They fell back from him right and left as he strode to the door; and not until his eyes were off them did the storm of hisses and jeers break forth again.
