Upon catching sight of the group on the pathway he flushed warmly, coming to a dead stop.

“Who the devil are <i>you</i>, with your woman’s skin and red hair?” muttered Ezekiel Doidge.

The question was answered immediately; for Secretan called out with a laugh at his friend’s embarrassment—

“Hallo, Terence! Well met, old fellow. I’m delighted to see you.”

Then Clancy came forward, stamping the water from his dripping waders.

“You’ve often heard me speak of my friend Terence Clancy, Nell? Well, now at last I can introduce him to you in the flesh.”
