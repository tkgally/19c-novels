“You haven’t met the Tredethlyns yet?”

“No; they have been away ever since my arrival in these parts. But I believe they have just returned, and Secretan, who will be home again to-night, is going to introduce me as soon as possible.”

“Mr. Tredethlyn is uncommonly well off. I believe there’ll be four or five thousand a-year to divide between his two daughters.”

“You’re thinking of Miss Tredethlyn, then?”

“Certainly not. Miss Kate’s a deal too proud to look at a country doctor, and would see through you in no time. I’m thinking of Miss Nell, the favourite—the dark-eyed, dainty little Nell.”

“Don’t you know that she’s engaged to Simon Secretan?”

“Of course I do.”
