“Silence, you Goth! That’s John Bull all over, with the shop and the till always pulling at his heart-strings. There, good-bye, Jack.”

Jack Syme stood looking after his friend with a puzzled frown.

“Can’t make him out,” he muttered; “don’t half know him yet. He’s a deuced taking fellow, fascinating as a pretty woman; but—but what? I don’t know what. I think he’ll have an eye on the Tredethlyn girls from this date. I think he’ll fail with Kate, and then? Then will come the rub; we shall see whether Miss Nell’s eyelashes, backed by two thousand a year and her other fascinations, will modify his sense of honour, cool the ardour of his conscience somewhat. Maybe not; yet, in Secretan’s place, I wouldn’t trust mine own familiar friend too absolutely. Clancy’s sympathies are too quick, his emotions too facile and tender, his complexion too good.
