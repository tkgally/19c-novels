“What is the matter with you, Kate? You look as solemn and portentous as though——”

“There’s some one come to see us,” said Kate, curtly.

Then Simon rose and walked to the window, and there was nothing more to be done.

Kate stared at them both in a perfectly imbecile fashion, and was unable to help out the awkward situation by a word.

But apparently no help was needed. Nothing could have been, at least on the face of it, more flat and commonplace than this meeting, which should have been a little romance in itself. Simon’s opening remarks, for any sentiment they contained, might have been taken straight from a guide-book, and Nell at once fell into his vein.
