With shivering hand he put two cartridges into his gun—“One for him, and one for me,” he muttered in a crazed whisper.

Upon reaching the gate which shut off the lane from the grassy slope of the knoll, Simon seated the child upon the top bar, and Joyce took possession of her. Then he stepped back a pace or two, standing with his broad chest not four yards from Doidge’s gun-muzzle.

“Still asleep?” he exclaimed; “why, nothing will wake the little creature now, Joyce. Last time I saw you she wouldn’t go to sleep for love or money.”
