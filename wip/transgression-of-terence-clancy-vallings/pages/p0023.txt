There was something appealing in his face, too, especially about the delicate, sensitive mouth—a look almost of deprecation, as of one anxious for your good opinion; meaning well, yet wanting your help to carry out his meaning. Not a strictly handsome man, but rather what they used to call a “pretty fellow,” with the rich red-brown hair that belongs so naturally to his fair skin and Irish eyes.

“Do you mean to say I’m a sneak?” he cried again, his eyes flashing.
