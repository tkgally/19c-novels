Upon seeing his enemy disappear from the terrace above, Ezekiel uttered a low, harsh cry, and began to drag himself up the last flight of steps. By the time he had reached the terrace level, Terence was entering the open study window.

Doidge stood pointing after him, his face black with passion, his arm and body quivering.

The moment afterwards Terence saw the others close round him. Doidge’s square figure was concealed behind the two tall gentlemen, and Terence seemed to hear the burning words wherewith his enemy was denouncing him. He stepped further into the room and disappeared.
