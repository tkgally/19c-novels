# Chapter XI.

“MY dear Kate, allow me to state that this son and heir of mine is shockingly spoiled.”

“I believe you’re right, Julius; and I think no one has contributed to this disastrous state of things more than yourself.”

“Tut—tut, you libel me! Why, every one knows what a stern parent I am?”

“Your wife has yet to acquire that piece of knowledge, however.”
