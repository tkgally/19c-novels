# Chapter VIII.

THE so-called “canal,” beside which stood her solitary home, Hollacomb Farm, was a gently sliding piece of water, that seemed to Mary Pethick to have been running through her life like a thread, binding the simple incidents together, and serving as a connecting link between the dull-grey home-existence and the brighter possibilities of Chillington town.
