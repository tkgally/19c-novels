He would first take her over the town bridge and down the river bank by the vicarage garden, because he must needs have a look at the little town in order to feel thoroughly at home again; and afterwards into his own woods of Hollacomb, because they were redolent of sweet association with herself.

As they passed down the steep hill, with the little town spread out before them, the rush of Chilling Water greeted Secretan with the voice of an old friend. The Chilling vale, with its farmsteads and town, had in fact an appeal for him, not only from sentimental and æsthetic points of view, but as being a kind of bank in which he had invested a good deal of money, much of his heart, and most of the energy of his young manhood.
