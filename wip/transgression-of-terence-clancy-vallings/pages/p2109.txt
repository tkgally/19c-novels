As Nell came to this admission in the course of her thinking, some tears fell upon the work with which her hands were resolutely busy. She perceived, moreover, that there was nothing to be done; by no conceivable treatment could she infiltrate strict honesty into a character like Terence’s. It was like a sieve very suitable for holding ordinary grain, but incapable of retaining the fine gold-dust of absolute truth. Had this disillusion come suddenly she might have despaired; but so gradual had been the process, that her present task consisted of the mere summing up of previous impressions, and the spreading out of their results for inspection.
