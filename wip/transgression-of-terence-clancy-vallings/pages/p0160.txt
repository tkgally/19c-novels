He was coming nearer to her: the gallant, the handsome, the admired Terence Clancy was really growing to love her. How gladly, with his love to sustain and prop her weakness, would she extricate herself from this hateful engagement, ay, and would make Terence a good and faithful wife. She loved him; she knew it now. It had come upon her quite suddenly. She turned her swimming eyes upon him, and his own caught fire at their shining.

He drew nearer to her; his flushed face was bending down to hers, his hand seeking her hand. Almost—almost she had won him!

Alas for poor Mary! At the very moment of her triumph Clancy paled, starting back hastily.
