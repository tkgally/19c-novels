She uttered a sigh of relief as Simon sprang to his feet with the words she looked for written clear on his face. He seemed to have been stunned for a moment, but now uprose, his whole figure dilating with the hot wrath of an honourable man falsely accused. Kate panted with anxiety when his hand went out as though to make way for burning words to follow. The next moment she was faint and sick with disappointment.
