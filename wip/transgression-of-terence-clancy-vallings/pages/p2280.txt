[[cont]]quoth Kate, pointing through the breakfast-room window. “Do you perceive how fast they’re mellowing? Well, when Simon first arrived they were hardly in full leaf; and do you suppose he would be all these months getting to the point of a mere cool friendship if he had any warmer feeling to bestow?”

“Don’t you be too clever, and logical, and all that, Kate, for I’m not agoing to argue with you. You just give me that note for your sister, if it’s done, and in an hour or so I’ll take it over to the hall, for Mrs. Nell and I are great friends, and I’m always glad of an excuse for a chat with her.”

Later on the squire took Kate’s note, and drove away to Monks Damerel.
