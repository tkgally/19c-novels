Why should he go near a woman who despised him? Contempt was the one thing in the world that he found insupportable, and none but Miss Tredethlyn had the temerity to curve a contumelious lip at him. As a Cambridge man, a sportsman, a good fellow (also a rich fellow), he had been well enough received by his regiment. He had distinguished himself in one of our small wars, and more—far more—was a polo-player of some note. How dared she despise him?
