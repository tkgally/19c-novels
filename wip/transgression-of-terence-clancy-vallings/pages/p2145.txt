In short, the soldier’s naturally gloomy view of human nature was heightened by this incident; and, as a man who had always succeeded better in winning respect than love, he was perhaps somewhat jealous on his own account as well as Simon’s. He certainly entered the hall and took a seat beside his father with a growing resentment against the general enthusiasm of the gathering.

Some one in the gallery watched Captain Rush’s passage across the room, and, noting the dark mood of his countenance, felt depressed and disappointed.
