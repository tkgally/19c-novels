Its driver was John Syme, who had been visiting a farmer’s wife on the moor a mile or two beyond the village. There was light enough for him to see the pair by the stream, even to catch Clancy’s eye as he passed. Syme was whirled quickly by, but his look of exulting comprehension remained with Terence.

“You’re wrong, though, Jack Syme, quite wrong,” he muttered, turning to Nell as she began to speak again.

“I do hope you’re not going to refuse,” she said uneasily. “The disappointment would be so painful to Simon.”
