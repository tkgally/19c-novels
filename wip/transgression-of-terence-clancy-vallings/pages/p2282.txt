The squire was tolerably certain that he had seen deeper into the returned exile’s mind than either his son or daughter had done, and that Simon’s former sentiments towards Nell had really undergone no change; but he was equally sure that the man’s stubborn pride would have nothing to say to offers of friendship or gratitude. The question was whether Nell would have anything better to bestow upon her old lover; and the squire had lately been inclined to answer this in the affirmative.
