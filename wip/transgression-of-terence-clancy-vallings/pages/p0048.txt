Most of the loungers touched their hats as Nell and Secretan passed, and a gentle trickle of remarks followed upon their passage: nothing very eulogistic or the reverse, however, for Chillington bridge viewed things largely and calmly, with an aristocratic distaste for any display of enthusiasm. Moreover, Secretan was too eccentric, too earnestly bent upon the amelioration of his fellow man, to be thought quite satisfactory; while too rich and powerful to be altogether found wanting.

Upon leaving the bridge the pair of lovers passed down the right bank of the stream, their path lying between the gleam and laughter of Chilling Water on the one side, and the grey old wall of the vicarage garden on the other.
