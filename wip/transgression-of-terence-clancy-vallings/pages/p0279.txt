# Chapter XIV.

A PARTY at Hollacomb Manor had all the surprise possibilities of a child’s bran pie. No one ever felt sure as to what would come of an invitation from Mrs. French-Chichester, whether a staid hyper-English evening in the drawing-room, with a quiet rubber, a little music, and the carriages at eleven sharp; or the liveliest of dinners followed by pool and “long drinks” in the billiard-room afterwards.
