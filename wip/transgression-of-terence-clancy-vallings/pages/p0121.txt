“Why don’t you two go and have a look at the stream? I shall be sending Nell and Clancy after you, directly.”

It was the voice of Simon, the ever-ready helper of lame dogs over stiles.

The pair stepped down immediately, without a word, each vowing secretly that Simon was a good friend.

Very different was Clancy’s plan of attack from the dragoon’s. He felt reckless of appearances. It was his last evening, his last chance; and in a forlorn hope audacity is the only policy. He walked boldly up to Nell, and easily succeeded in detaching her from the others; indeed, Nell fell in readily enough with his plans, for the simple reason that she had a message from Simon to deliver, and might not have so good an opportunity for some time.

Before they had gone many yards, Simon joined them.

“I won’t give her up,” muttered Terence.
