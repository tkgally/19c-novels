Mr. Clancy was effusively greeted by the ladies. They surrounded him with eager hopes, pityings, and kindly upbraidings. How could he so endanger his life for the sake of a mere fish? How rashly he had ventured into the rapid; how gallantly he had clung to his rod! Was he unconscious when Simon first caught him?

Terence stood laughing and blushing, and enjoying not a little the importance and interest wherewith the ducking had invested him. While Mrs. French-Chichester poured out sympathy, Nell’s lips were silent, but her eyes were eloquent.

Meanwhile the dragoon regarded this sentimental scene with a cynical glance. He felt oddly jealous for his friend, and perhaps a little for himself.
