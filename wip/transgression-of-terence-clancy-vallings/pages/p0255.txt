“And how about the other hero of the piece—the one who is ‘the soul of honour,’ etc.?”

Nell threw back her answer with flash of eye and an angry gesture.

“I shall avoid him; I shall never see him, if I can possibly help it. Should he guess my weakness he would know how to assist me.”

“I see.”

“Would that I could trust myself as absolutely as I can him!”

“Just so.”

“Kate, Kate, I’m very miserable; don’t be hard with me. Do tell me that you think me capable of doing my duty to poor old Simon, and keeping this wretched secret to myself?”
