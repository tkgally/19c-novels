For, weak and sore smitten as the man was, he would not then shirk the course which nights and days of hard thinking had laid down as the right one. He slunk after the pair down the dark lane between the leafless hedges, and out into the high-road.

Once or twice he got near enough to hear their words. Simon was talking astronomy, telling Kate of the wonders of the Saturnian system; he spoke of nothing but the stars in their courses, while his companion listened, only throwing in a word now and then.
