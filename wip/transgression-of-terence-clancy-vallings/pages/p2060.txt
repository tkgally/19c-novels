Mr. Rush, after straining his mind almost to the cracking-point for a topic, hazarded an inquiry after Kate’s father.

Mr. Tredethlyn was still at Lymport. He had written a letter which, while easing his own mind, had been to Kate’s as a tight boot to a tender foot. From the safe vantage-ground of the Royal Hotel he could express himself with a frankness impossible with less than forty miles between himself and Kate; and there was a peculiar charm in thus pouring forth home-truths from a safe distance upon one who was apt to have the best of it in close encounters.
