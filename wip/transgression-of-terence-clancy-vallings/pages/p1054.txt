“Who’s she marryin’, sir?”

“The gentleman you were speaking of.”

“Mr. Terence Clancy?” Joyce laughed loudly and bitterly. “Lord, Lord, ’tis such a bitter bad world that one must laugh or cry!”

Again they gazed down over the station-roof in silence.

The streets in the neighbourhood of the church were hidden by the houses, but there was evidently a stir in the place. People were hurrying across the open square by the town-hall, and the rumble of carriages could be heard through the swelling clang of the bells.

“How long has this affair been public news, sir?”

“Barely a fortnight, by the express wish of those concerned.”
