“I shall make the baldest remark I can possibly think of, then he’ll be glad to get rid of me,” muttered Kate. “What did you think of the speeches this afternoon, Captain Rush?”

“Oh, capital. Yes, the air is pleasantly cool, and you’ll have a pleasant drive home. I beg your pardon,” he added apologetically, “but I didn’t quite catch your question. Would you mind driving on a few yards out of the way of the people? I want to speak to you about the detective and his verdict.”
