He perceived that were his enemy in possession of the truth—and Ezekiel’s face, now that he was drawing nearer, left hardly a vestige of doubt on this head—his social ruin was more absolute than he had ever yet imagined. He had driven Simon out of the country; he stood in Simon’s shoes, in the full enjoyment of Simon’s inheritance.
