[[cont]]fool though I be, I’ve always loved you. Put that down as a makeweight to my other faults—nay, nay, don’t balance up my failin’s at all, sweetheart, but just bear with me a little longer. Give me one month to win you back in; for that time I won’t so much as speak of love to ’ee; I’ll be just a humble servant waiting on your pleasure.”

“Ezekiel, I’d rather not—be engaged any more.”

“You won’t give me even that chance?” He leaned upon the bridge parapet as though oppressed by physical weakness. “Is there—some one else, Mary?”

“You’ve no right to ask!” cried she, with flaming cheeks.

“No right—to—ask? and you—my—betrothed wife?”

“I wish to be so no longer; I entreat you to release me. Oh, I want to be free—<i>free</i>; I can hardly breathe under this miserable weight!”
