He smiled faintly.

“You always were a rebel against public opinion, Kate.”

“Well, put it down to sheer obstinacy, if you will, but let me have the credit of it—and shake hands.”

“Very well—if it gives you any pleasure.”

“It does,” she said, giving him her hand; “it is a pleasure to shake hands with one whose transcendent obstinacy is like an ocean beside my poor puddle of self-will. Oh, Simon, Simon,” she continued, suddenly throwing aside her mocking tone, “it makes me wretched to see you here! How long do you intend to keep up this miserable farce? Are you going to spoil your whole life by way of gratifying your insane pride?”

Then Simon, moved by her friendly warmth, began to let his numbed heart speak.
