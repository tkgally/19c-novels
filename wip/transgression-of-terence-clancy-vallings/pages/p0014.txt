[[cont]]the other voice ceased for a moment; “give us more of your blarney, for ’tis mighty refreshing. Your talk is like fizzing drink to me in this dull hole, where nothing ever happens. And I’m precious lucky to get your company for an hour now you’re so mighty popular, ain’t I, Terence? Why, hang me, if you could be more sought after with a fine property and twenty thousand a year! You’re making quite a splash in the neighbourhood, ain’t you, me boy? Thick with all the big county people; staying at Monks Damerel Hall with the great Sir Hamo himself; his son your intimate friend, loving you like a brother and shoving you into the arms of great ladies like Mrs. French-Chichester. Aha! you’re exploiting the district, prospecting for a practice, as I know very well; making wonderful strides, too, with the ladies all in a flutter about your winning laugh and piquant brogue.
