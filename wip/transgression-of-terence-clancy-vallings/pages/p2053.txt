[[cont]]it seemed that the silence must have been full of comfort; he seemed not merely to be speaking, but to be lashing and scourging them with whips. They were ashamed almost to breathe. Yet his language was not violent; he swore not once, but seemed to turn their hearts inside out, and to scorch them through and through. He said things of which the echo would hang about the house for many a long day. When he had finished this strange unconventional address, he dismissed with formal words, Children, the butler, McTavish, Tomlinson, the second footman, and two other delinquents.

Children plucked up courage so far as to say—

“I require a month’s warning, sir.”

“You require a sound thrashing, sir!” thundered the captain, “and that you’ll get if I find you anywhere within the ring-fence of Bickington Park after four o’clock this afternoon.”
