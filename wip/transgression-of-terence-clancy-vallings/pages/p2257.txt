But her drawing-room filled and emptied; routine talk buzzed on unflaggingly; and the commonplace decorum of the afternoon was unbroken by anything more dramatic than the announcement of a new engagement.

At length the cup-and-saucer parade was over, and Kate was free to pay a visit to the nursery. But hardly had she time to attract the notice of Young Hopeful, who chanced to be too busy destroying some new toys to concern himself about anything so unimportant as a fond mother, when she was informed of Simon’s arrival.
