“My dear, I never dreamt——”

“You should have dreamt,” sobbed Kate. “I often gave you hints; I often tried to impress you with a sense of Nell’s danger; and yet, the moment my back is turned, you overthrow all my precautions at a stroke. Oh, father, had you been but less wrapped up in your own little ailments——”

She stopped herself abruptly and covered her face with her hands. She had never spoken with such audacious candour before, and she saw that her words stung her father like a whip-lash. His large comely face flushed deeply as he drew himself up to answer her.
