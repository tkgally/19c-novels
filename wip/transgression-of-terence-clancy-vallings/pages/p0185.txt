Why, now he owned one of the best houses in Chillington, with a hack and a hunter in its stables—for he found to his joy that he would be expected to hunt one day a week during the season, Dr. Rose having firmly established that desirable custom—and, in short, everything he had ever hoped for. The practice was the best in these parts: he would need an assistant as soon as he settled down, and proposed to engage Jack Syme, thus setting the old fellow on his legs at a stroke. It all seemed like magic; Simon’s generosity had given him a new lease of life, converted the dim world into a joyous playground.
