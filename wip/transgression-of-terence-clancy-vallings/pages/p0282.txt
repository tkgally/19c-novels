Kate decided to hate the man before he had quite finished his bow. The moment afterwards she caught sight of Terence Clancy, and her hostess whispered, “So sorry, dear, but I <i>had</i> to ask Terence after all; one of my hussars was at Rugby with him, and I really couldn’t omit him without awkward explanation.”

This rapid aside was overheard by Captain Rush, who passed close to the pair at this moment, and it gave him the key to Kate’s anxious looks. It was clear that she had fallen into the same suspicious groove as himself, that she feared Terence Clancy.
