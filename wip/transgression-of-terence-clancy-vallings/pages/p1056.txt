“I mean to, able or not.”

“Then give me the child.”

This was more easily said than done. Miss Rose, finding herself perched upon the shoulder of a great tall stranger, screamed as though the universe were on the point of collapse, nor had big Simon the ghost of an idea how to pacify her. This gave him an opportunity, however, of watching the practice of an art, new to him, but old as the human race.

For the baby’s attention was in a trice distracted to a passing “puff-puff.” Before that was out of sight, a robin piping from the ash-tree hard by was pointed out as an object of breathless interest. Next, a small boy with clinking milk-cans was offered up in like manner, grinning to find himself so much admired. Simon, noting how suddenly the big tears ceased to roll, thought it all a marvel of cleverness.
