As to whether the quarry might suffer in the chase, why that was an awkward question. But—but Simon was known for a very proud man; doubtless he would keep aloof and scorn backbiters. And again, the hue and cry could not possibly last long. Some other scandal would certainly crop up in due course; in fine, Terence had serious hopes that he would after all be able to retain his practice and his position, and continue to show a bold front to the world.
