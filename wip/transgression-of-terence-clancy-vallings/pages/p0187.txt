Emerging presently from his reflections under the verandah, Terence hurried into his little study and wrote off a jubilant letter to his family in Ireland, requesting that two out of the six younger brothers should at once be sent over to be brought up and educated at his expense. The comfort of thus giving a helping hand to his people, whom he had rather forgotten during the last year or two, elated him still further. He strode out again into the sunlight, and glanced along the road which dipped steeply into the town.

Just rising the hill Terence saw a cavalcade, which sent his heart into his mouth. He had not been up to Moor Gates for some time, had resolutely refused the invitations brought by Simon, and now, as though to reward his self-denial, Moor Gates was coming to him.
