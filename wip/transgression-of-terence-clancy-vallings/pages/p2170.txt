Howbeit, from that time Terence had begun as it were to slip from her grasp. He openly resented her scruples as to stepping into Simon’s shoes, and could never endure even to have Simon’s name mentioned. He plunged into all social dissipations that lay open to him with a kind of feverish energy, and threw money about in a lavish way which frightened his quiet-going wife; nor did he seem to care much about pleasing her or retaining her respect, sustained as he now was by the intoxicating applause of a large neighbourhood.
