He and Simon had half lived at Moor Gates; and of that best week in his life Terence felt this evening to be the crown and finish.

After this the curtain must fall upon the too-charming pastoral piece, and the stage-lights must be put out. No more play-acting for one whose life was to be a round of commonplace drudgery. His pleasant holiday must come to an end; he must settle down to real work; quit the fine old hall, with its charming idleness and refinement, for some such dingy, evil-smelling little place as Jack Syme’s surgery.
