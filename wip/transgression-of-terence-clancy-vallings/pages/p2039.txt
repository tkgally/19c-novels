“Dear John” winced uneasily while his bride was descanting upon his feeble health. The man was as strong as a horse, and knew that Kate was aware of it. Yet it was not mere shame that was driving him abroad, the truth being that he was in a peculiar position. He had sold himself for money and was being rewarded beyond his deserts, and far beyond his expectations. It was embarrassing, but true, that his wife insisted upon bringing him affection over and above the longed-for cash; which made him a mean fellow in his own eyes.
