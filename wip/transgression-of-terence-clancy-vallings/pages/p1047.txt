At first Sergeant Melladew accepted her homage with good-humour; when it continued, he began to give ear to the reports concerning her stepmother’s successful pickings up at the Hall; then, having an even keener eye for the main chance than for female beauty, he decided to allow Joyce’s good worldly prospects to outweigh the moderate quality of her looks. The rest followed in due course. Sergeant Melladew married, retired, and began to drink away his wife’s earnings. Chillington was found too dull for him, and its profits too small. He carried her off to Lymport, and installed her in a larger establishment—and the increased profits were met and parried by superior thirst.
