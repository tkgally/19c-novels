“Come up and see the place,” Secretan called out, when he had unlocked the door. “Some of the old man’s furniture is quaint and curious.”

“No, thank you,” grinned Syme from below; adding, in a voice of some concern, “Surely you’re not going to spend a whole month in this desolate hole?”

Secretan certainly looked out of keeping with his surroundings as he stood upon the rocky steps, with the sodden thatch dripping mournfully upon him, and on all sides ruined woodlands weeping for the dead and gone summer.

“May I ask if you intend remaining in the neighbourhood when your hermit-month is up?” asked the doctor when Simon emerged from a brief inspection of the homestead.
