He knew nothing of the creeping rumours that had been eating away his good name lately; and he was too hard stricken to appraise judicially the crushing weight of evidence that had been brought against him. As for the direction of Mary’s farewell letter to himself, that was easily explained. He had offered to forward any communication she might wish to make to Terence, and had acquiesced in her suggested precaution that Terence’s name should appear nowhere in the epistle or its address.
