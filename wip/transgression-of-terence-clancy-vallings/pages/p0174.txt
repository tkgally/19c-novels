“Come, that is indeed a proof of my power, and a welcome one,” she cried, with a mocking laugh.

“What has come to you, Mary? You never used to find pleasure in jeering at me!”

“Because your teaching has taken some time to soak in. But now I am equal to the task of wounding anybody.”

A kind of despair now fell upon Ezekiel. With a sickening pain it dawned upon him that his reform had come too late; that Mary’s heart had really slipped from his grasp. The fear was not new, though hitherto his stubborn egoism had always been equal to the task of casting it aside.

“Mary,” he muttered with dry lips, “are you turning from me?”
