Terence sighed a little at the mention of work, proceeding with his breakfast a shade more thoughtfully.

“I shouldn’t be surprised,” he continued, after a pause, “if I have to take Jack into partnership pretty soon. It would save a deal of trouble in the end.”

“And would also diminish the profits a good deal, wouldn’t it, dear?”

“What a mercenary little woman it is!”

“Why, yes; and you know the reason, Terence.”

Nell also became thoughtful; and he was well aware of the drift of her thought. She was more than anxious about his debt—for so they both agreed to call it—to Simon, and was bent upon maintaining a course of rigid economy until it should have been paid in full.
