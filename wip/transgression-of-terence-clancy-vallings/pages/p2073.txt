“But my son has been away all these months,” urged Mr. Rush, anxiously.

“I’m talking generalities,” snapped Kate.

“Rather particular generalities,” the captain remarked drily.

“I shall be more particular directly. I say it’s a shame, a burning shame, that no hand should be raised to help Simon. He’ll leave the country soon for good—for I know his plans—drift away into the world without a spoken regret to follow him, without a soul to care whether he lives or dies.”
