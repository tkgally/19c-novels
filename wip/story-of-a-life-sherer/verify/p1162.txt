A thirst, too, a raging thirst, tormented him; and, at last, his little mind went straying. The wild delirium of manhood is less shocking to the sight than the dawning reason of a child closed up again, or scared by sickness. Then he swooned; often dying to my fears, before he did. In six and thirty hours my fondling lay cold, and spotted with the livid pest, in my trembling arms. Fatima, whom I had frequently sent away from the sad scene, and who had returned as often for the first twenty hours, had not, during the close of my babe’s struggle, been near us. Fully engaged with the dear nestling,—thinking of nothing else,—the time rolled by with a terrific swiftness. Now I remembered Fatima; and the peril of that dear young being flashed upon me.
