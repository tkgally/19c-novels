We came to a black ruined wall, (the Roman built it;) she led me round, beneath its shelter;—they lay pillowed on stones. Again she whispered, “He with the long hair is Sebastian, and the short red man is Spalatro—Ah! the blessed virgin! they were both brave—pistol them, Senhor;—Pietro will fly—tread softly—close, close—make sure.”

I stood above them, a pistol pointed in each hand, and the pale moon looked down upon us with her holy light; and they were sleeping in it. I had come prepared for blows, and blood, and brave encounter; but not for this.
