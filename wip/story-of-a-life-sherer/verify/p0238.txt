[[cont]]on Sundays and saints’ days I was as fine, as a bright coloured boddice, and a silver crucifix, and ribbons in my glossy hair, could make me; and that a kind and laughing mother dressed me. I was pretty too—a very pretty child, and had a sweet temper—no wonder—every body smiled on me; I never asked for anything that it was not given, and with pattings on my cheek; never, therefore, had I any thing that I was not ready to give. I was taught nothing but to read, and to learn, by heart, the paternoster, and some prayers, and hymns to our blessed Lady, and the holy St. Januarias, and the Santa Agatha of precious memory. Once a week all the children were catechized in the cloister of the church, just before vespers. The pastor was a kind, silly old man, whom we all laughed at, and were playfully fond of, and practised our sportive tricks on—so I learned nothing.
