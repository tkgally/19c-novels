Now first I broke the seal of the small packet, which seemed the only remaining link between me and the vanished idol of my heart. I found a short letter, and a manuscript of some length. The letter ran thus:—

“Osman,

“You love me. I never can return your love. I am surprised, alarmed, terrified at your ardour. Nothing pure, nothing peaceful mingles with your passion. It pierces like the sea-bird’s cry, wilder and louder than the storm. I look upon you with tenderness, with sorrow, but with dread. I fly to some distant cloister. Again I tell you, never can I, never could I return your love. In the life of my late lord I could not, on his account, shut myself from your sight.
