The Prince begged me to consider his villa as always open to me; and though he said the Princess saw no company, he was sure that of one, who had been introduced so singularly on that evening to them both, would be ever welcome. She gave a silent assenting bend, and I withdrew.

[[break]]

Most freely I gave myself to enjoy that precious privilege. He was confined to a sofa for some weeks: daily I visited him: I informed him of such news as I gathered from the Count; I played with him at chess, at cards; I did every thing to make myself agreeable in his sight, that I might be ensured the bliss, the innocent bliss as I thought it, of being near the lady Agatha.

Very, very seldom did she address herself to me, and then in a quiet, polite, but reserved manner.
