We now reached the spot, where he had left the two posted, whom he had taken out with him, and, beyond these some twenty paces, a white enveloped figure reined in an eager bit-champing snowy steed. He was alone, and the moon now shone out silvery on them; and I knew Malek and Saladin. “Salaam Aleikum,” said the sheick and his fellows, as they turned back. As briefly I replied, and a bound of my spurred steed brought me to the side of my young friend. As I had taught him, we grasped the hand.

He told me that he had been in great alarm, and feared that I had been taken by these Arabs for a Turkish Spahi, and fallen a victim to their stern notions of honourable revenge.
