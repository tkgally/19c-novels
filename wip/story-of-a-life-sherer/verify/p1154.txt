[[cont]]that angel frame with the shaft of death—that bade me give her to a ruthless throng of murderous fanatics,—well didst thou aim thy poisoned dart at me. The chain, the whip, the loathsome dungeon, and the scanty food,—I had borne these things;—here, here I fell—to save this being’s life, for another day, perhaps—or year—of gilded prison I threw away my faith, and took the badge of infamy and shame. I now feel that I should then have died,—died, though in vain defending, and perished with her; letting her die—why, what could have been better?

The world was now before us both;—both outcast—both despised. I took her in my arms; and my big [[corr tearsfell|tears fell]] down upon my open breast, and she wiped them with her hair, and clasped me with the fondling look of an affectionate, forgiven child.

For that day I left her; but, on the morrow, I took her as the wife of my bosom, and I loved and cherished her.
