They came at the hour agreed on to our loved haunt. I had been there with Dennis from sunrise, that no suspicion might be excited.

“I watched the approach of their boat. I received her again into my arms—Agatha—from whom I had now been separated months. She fell into them faint, and weeping; I trembled with love and apprehension; I bore her to a seat upon a grave-stone beneath the southern wall of the priory. As soon as she recovered herself a little, we mingled our tears, and were happy—what had passed, what threatened us, forgotten, or no longer thought of; we were again together.
