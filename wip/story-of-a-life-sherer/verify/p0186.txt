How well I recollect the scene;—the eager venturing, the anxious throw, the suppressedly exulting smile, the clenched hand, the bitten lip, the stricken forehead, the pale, motionless despair, the affected calmness, the maddening rage; the restless eye of doubt, suspicion, envy; the out-breaking of hate and defiance;—of this last we had a melancholy example. The very finest-looking young man of our party, stung by his losses, and viewing the smile of the winner as a taunt, insulted him. They withdrew with seconds; we were soon summoned;—alas! it was to see the young hero die, where no fame can crown the bowed head. He lay on a sofa speechless, his vest all red and dark with stiffening gore, his cheek bloodless—he died, and made no sign.
