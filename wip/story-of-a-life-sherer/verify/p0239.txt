“Not very far from our cottage, in a vineyard, belonging to my father, stood the noble ruins of an ancient Roman amphitheatre; and, below it, again, the marble remains of an old temple of Jupiter. I well recollect my early, and timid explorings in the arched and echoing passages of the amphitheatre, and among the columns of the temple; but, as soon as use had taken away our fear, they were the favourite play places of myself, and my young companions, and there we might always be found.
