Upon the sky-girdled plain, afar, a moving speck rose slow, and brave men might have trembled as the cloudy thing took form:—Of giant size, a warrior on his steed, with tall lance piercing the sky, paused for a moment, and then came careering on. The “Salaam aleikum” was ready on my lip, but, before I could give it utterance, “Revenge and no shame” was the cry I heard; and the poised lance, in menace, held aloft, he galloped close to me.

I drew my scimitar, and reined up my steed, and rode wide of his course, and watched his lance with a quick eye, that I might parry off his thrust.

“Salaam aleikum,” he hoarsely uttered, as he dropped his lance’s point, and drew up his bridle, and stood in peace before me.
