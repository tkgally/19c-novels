About three years ago he married a charming woman, to whom he had been long attached, and he has two sweet children. Ah, George, how little can Warner judge of what I have suffered! Who shall ever, in this world, presume to judge of the trials of another? The highest consolation, both to the sinner and the sufferer, is, that God alone is the searcher and discerner of hearts, and the ever-present recollection, that <i>he gives peace not as the world giveth</i>, is our most powerful support.
