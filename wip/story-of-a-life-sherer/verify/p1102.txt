Such, however, was not my feeling at the moment. I listened to him with delight; his cultivated taste, his well stored memory, his exhaustless anecdotes, his descriptions of all that seemed rich and magical in eastern climes, fired me with an eager desire to go and dwell among the people and the scenes he so fairly pictured. We soon learn to regard the nature of our temptations, and the peculiarity of our situation, as such that none but ourselves can understand, or make allowance for; we soon learn to dismiss those subjects that give us pain. My birth-right had been sold—my loves had withered. I was an alien, an exile, an outcast,—any change of scene or life which should bury the memory of these things, was welcome.
