How much more should the kindness of a God, who only asks our heart, be answered by its throb of gratitude and love; listen, friend, deep as is the die of your offences, deeper has been that of mine; your repentance is I see deep, for it shakes your bosom, and fills your weeping eyes; the hand is stretched out still, seize it on your knees. The fountain is open, flee to it, you shall be washed whiter than snow—linger not, up, and be doing; go to morrow to the city, and take a passage for your native country; fly to the forsaken altar and cling to it; take the book of life and make its promises your own. Remember that the Gospel was the sacred legacy of a dying Redeemer to dark and sinful man.”
