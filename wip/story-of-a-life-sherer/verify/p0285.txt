I knew it was a confessional, from whence those hallowed sounds came; it was the outpouring of some wounded heart, and the consoling voice of the absolving priest.

I was deeply impressed, and affected; for many days I thought of nothing else. I forgot the prison nunneries, the monkish slaves, the torture chamber, and the fagot of the inquisition; I thought but on the blessed aid and help, which, it seemed to me, their faith and discipline extended to the confessing sinner. True it was, that my reason told me that man’s absolving must be a form, a mere form, and could not serve; but I had a secret heavy, and hateful. I should at least be heard—perchance find comfort; the relief at least of disburthening my sorrows—the sound, the tone of consolation.

Deciding thus, I wrapped me one midnight in my cloak, and stole forth with my tale all ready
