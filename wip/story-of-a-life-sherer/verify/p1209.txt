The last note seemed to have been written not long before his death.

“Strange that, after twenty years, my heart, all heavy as it is, should feel so young in that sad power of loving. Hapless—hopeless has been my love, still is it strong, as when, in early youth, I daily fed it with a lover’s gaze. Solitary as has been my desert path, through the wilderness of life—thy image, my beloved Agatha, has soothed my lonely hours. This, heaven has permitted here; an earnest that we shall meet hereafter. Yes—we shall meet again—where there can be no more separation, no more sorrow,—no more death;—Even now, perhaps, the messenger of mercy awaits me—the plague is begun—that terror to so many; but which brings only hope to me.”
