My mother, dressed half in my clothes, and with a peasant’s frieze cloak wrapped closely round her, lay warm upon my lap: Dennis sate, a little forward, loudly thanking, and blessing his young honour: and I bent over my mother with a sort of fond exulting, that one, so very dear, had been so nigh the grave, yet snatched so mercifully from it, and given back to my heart its large its only treasure—two hours ago its only treasure. But there were new throbbings now in my young heart, and, as I looked up to answer the gentle voice, which asked to be directed to our garden bank, I felt them beat quick, very quick, and thrillingly. All things combined to kindle my young love. He was one of the first youths I had ever, if I may so say, seen even. He came like a guardian angel; flew to the perishing cry; was the preserver of my mother’s life. The moral beauty of his active kindness was much—enough to claim regard;—but O! there shone a sunny lustre on his fine, fair brow, such as I never saw in any other created being.
