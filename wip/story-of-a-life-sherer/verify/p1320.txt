There was not one person in that gallery dared stretch the hand to seize:—“Come,” said she, “come look upon your son;” and she led the way, and aghast all followed her. My brother lay upon the bridal bed, bloody and dead. I marked the action of her lifted arm, as she was about to plunge the reeking blade into her own bosom, disarmed, and secured her. She was immediately conveyed to a neighbouring convent, and confined as insane, to preserve her life, and spare her family the disgrace of an execution. The shock this gave to the feelings and hopes of my father, cast a heavy gloom over his spirits, and our villa became silent as a monastery; but to me it brought a change, my delight at which I could not repress the indulgence of.
