Just coming from the open glare of day and the blinding desert, I was too bewildered to see any thing distinctly; all thought or reflection concerning what it might be, or whither I was brought, escaped me; and so long and so often the sport of Fortune, the tales to which I had listened at Alexandria and Cairo came over me with all the power and the force of some terrifying reality. I stooped down, and would have gone forth again. I felt persuaded that all was magic around me; some dread illusion of an enchanter. The other Arabs were entering, and they rose and hoarsely laughed together, and led me on. “Fear not,” they said, “it is the palace of Pharaoh”—“Pharaon, Pharaon!” they loudly repeated, and invoked Allah with a low muttering.
