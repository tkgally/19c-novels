## <i>Gianetta’s Tale.</i>

“You have indeed, Senhor, been rightly informed. A few years ago, I was well known, as the first dancer at the grand opera at Naples, and I have also appeared in many other large cities with no small success. I was born, Senhor, among the poor, the happy poor, lowly in condition but rich in the fewness of their wants. My father was a Neapolitan peasant of the better class, and was the considered owner of a small farm, a vineyard, and a wine press, on that pretty height, which rises just above the small old town of Pozzuoli.
