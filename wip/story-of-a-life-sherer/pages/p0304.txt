He has left his large property, for her life, to his sister, Mrs. Charlotte Hamilton, and afterwards to Osman Beavoir, Esq., the only son of Walter Beavoir, Esq., of Beaulieu. The will bears date August, 1755. Some perplexity will arise on the death of Mrs. Hamilton, as Mr. Osman Beavoir disappeared from the town of Blois in France, where he was stationed, on his parole, as a prisoner of war, and has never since been heard of: little doubts are entertained of his death, but his disconsolate family still cling to the hope that he exists. His acquaintances at Blois are of opinion, that he destroyed himself in consequence of a very bitter disappointment. It appears he was distractedly attached to a very beautiful young lady of that city, who, with the strong prejudice of her nation, treated all his advances in a very light unfeeling manner.”
