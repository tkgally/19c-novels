The remembered things, too,—the storm—the dream—the earthquake—the sale of myself—the sinful hoard—and, the last spur to my poor mean soul, the engraving with its gaunt and threatening mocker, “Thou fool, this night.” It was too much for me to bear. Where did I go for comfort?—I remember well my loud wild cry. Strange, that through, my life such scenes as have impressed me remain!—the very looks, the words, the dialogues, the breaks, the order of them, and the manner of the speakers, as when I spoke and saw, and listened.
