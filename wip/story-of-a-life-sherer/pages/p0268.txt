If beggary was to be my lot, it would be more tolerable in my native climate; and I was determined, helpless as I was, without skill for any trade, and with a crippled limb, which forbade all rude labour, no longer to burthen her, who had so generously supported me. My application was successful. There lies the kind mariner, who brought me from England hither, four years ago; who long helped to support me himself, and who procured for me the charity of others. But his own power, and all his influence with others, have, with his little substance, now passed away. Shipwreck and losses, disease and helplessness, have brought him, not only to want, but utter destitution.
