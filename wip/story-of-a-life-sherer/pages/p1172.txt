“My dearest, dearest Agatha,

“There was only wanting this painful injunction to shew me how very precious was the blessing I enjoyed—daily with you—looking on you—listening to you. “<i>Your</i> Agatha,” how my heart thanks you for that word. Yes, you are mine—the spirits of the air have heard and registered our vows—mine now, for ever mine.
