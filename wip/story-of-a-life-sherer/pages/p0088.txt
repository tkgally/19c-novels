It ceased: I listened on—to nothing. I felt my heart too full—I felt it ache, sink, sicken;—my blood chilled, my brain swam round. What sound I uttered, whether faint sigh, or loud exclaim, I know not. What time elapsed I know not; but, at length, to the gentle pressure of a soft hand, that chafed my pale forehead, I opened my dull, dim eye.—Was it?—yes;—she of the sad, fair beauty—she, the once-seen, stood over me with a look of womanly compassion: no sooner did she observe me to recover, than addressing herself with some directions to those around, she left the apartment. It was a garden pavilion.
