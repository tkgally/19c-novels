[[cont]]garb, too, bespoke her sacred rank:—a small close vest of faint red colour, of a delicate tissue wrought over with flowers of gold, just covered the young breasts and well formed shoulders, a very long shawl of a muslin filmy, and thin as woven hair, was wrapped about her slender waist, and fell in numerous and decent folds before. But above where it mantled her body and head, or below, where it fell upon her strait limbs, it was no veil, but only softened to the eye her bright warm loveliness. Her round neck was polished as the sacred shell of Veeshnoo: her <i>sealed</i> forehead high, and light, and calm, like the beauty of a midnight moon; her eyebrows arched like the bow of the Boy God,—and glances flew about from her black eyes like the small swift arrows which he wounds young hearts with. Her teeth were like to milk-white flowers, and her lips to the deep red of the Guava fruit; and the palms of her fine fair delicate hands, and the little soles of her small feet, were tinged as if she had grasped the pink roses of G<sc>[[corr hazipooor|hazipoor]]</sc>, or trodden with a light unbruising step upon the scarlet flower buds of the sacred cusa grass.
