I stood silent, but strongly moved, and pressed her to my heart. It was broken, that soft silence, by the loud voices, and the shuffling tread of a noisy crowd; and the sound of a gathering tumult spoke fiercely at my gates.

The blood fled fast from the cheeks of the beauteous Fatima, and pale she looked as marble from the tomb, and like a startled dove, or a sobbing hare, her heart beat big—speechless and panting she clung to me.

An Italian servant, who had a strong attachment to me, and my Arab gardener, to whom I had shown kindness, made their way over a back wall, and now hurried to the apartment.
