“I had been many weeks an inmate of the convent, feeling all I could desire to experience of tranquillity, and undisturbed by any outward object, or indeed any image of the past. I had banished the visions of the world, and was become like a sea that is glassy, and deadly calm, when a mere trifle, or let me not call such a thing, in such a scene, a trifle, when the mere coloured shadow of a human face upon a wall changed every feeling of my bosom, and like a rushing wind upon the deep raised wave and storm.

“I have said that we assembled every Thursday in the refectory, and dined, or rather fasted, together, for the brothers never touch any flesh.
