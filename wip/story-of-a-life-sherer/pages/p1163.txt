A fever of the brain, violent but short, attacked me. In less than a week I was lying with recovered reason on a couch;—a Moollah with his beads sat mumbling near me; two of my black slaves stood with white eyes silent on either side; and, at the bottom of the chamber, I saw Hilla and the old woman.

There are situations of sorrow which the power of language is all unequal to describe. I never can say what I endured. Oh! what a relief it was to my mind when I could rise, and move, and be restless.
