I asked of other beggars. “What? a lame, pale, poor-looking woman?”—“Oh, there were <i>many such</i>.” Still I walked on. A cloked cavalier, walking by night in Venice, would not be guessed as on an errand of mercy; therefore I could not wonder, though I swore at and spurned them, that numbers of base men and old hood-wearing hags should pluck me by the sleeve, and bid me follow where loathing and reluctant beauties lay waiting for the hated but fawned-on purchaser.

I returned to my apartments harassed and unhappy. I lay me down, and as I felt sleep stealing on my eyelids, blessed, with a broken thanksgiving, the coming mercy.
