“I hastened on to my chamber, pleading the hour of prayer. I saw the sneer above his smile of courtesy: it entered into my bosom, engendering hate, and found young scorpions ready to rise and hiss around it within: they were unborn before, but in the pregnant heart they had lain a nest of evil passions in a dark womb, still; and now they struggled, as it were, which first should spring to life.
