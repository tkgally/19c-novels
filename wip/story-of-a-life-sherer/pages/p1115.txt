[[cont]]a neighbouring Sheick of a friendly tribe had sold it to Fakih Achmed, of Yemen, for a noble stranger; and he asked me to turn to the tents of his tribe—and we rode side by side, and he placed me on his left hand. A Sheick he was—a shepherd king; yet his vest was of a coarse blue cotton—a small dark turban, and a loose, outer haicke of a thick white serge. His face was thin, his form was spare; yet wiry were the starting sinews of his bare brown neck; one of his small hands held light the bridle of his lean, clean-limbed, and blood-veined mare, and his lighted pipe was between the fingers; his other grasped, firm and strong, a lance of prodigious length.
