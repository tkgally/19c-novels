[[cont]]my little playfellows; and we acted not one, but a hundred different things; spouting extempore: fighting with wooden swords, &c. &c.—to be sure, now and then, some of the stronger, and less intellectual boys would come and throw stones at us, and charge us, and roll us down the banks, but, upon the whole, I was a great favourite even with them. I gave of every thing I had freely; I helped the more stupid; and as I used to get story books, such as the abridgements of romances, and Arabian tales, and plays, why, I was listened to, and sought after, and gathered little dark groupes round the one candle at which I read, or leaned down with a little fond friend on either side looking over me. I used to be taken also to all the monthly concerts in that city, by some kind friends of my family, and learned to love sweet sounds.
