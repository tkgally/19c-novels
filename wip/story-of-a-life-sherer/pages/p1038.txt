On the following night, when all was hushed, and sleep was on the city, they brought dromedaries and asses to the waste ground, near the garden wall, and they lifted up the chest, and bound it on a dromedary; and they packed their goods on the other beasts; and I placed Rachel on her ass, and the boy with her;—
