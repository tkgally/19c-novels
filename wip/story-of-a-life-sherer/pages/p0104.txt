“Beavoir, I may not, must not listen to words and tones like these.”

“Pity me! Oh! say you pity me.”

“I am a woman, and have loved.”

“You love another, then.”

“I am a wife.”

“You love another.”

“Spare me, I pray you, spare me. Ah! do not waste the sweet affections of your young heart on such a wild and guilty love; place them where in honour you may; where in hope you may; where a pure, young, love-warmed heart may meet, in innocence, the fulness of your own. I am a wife, and cannot hear you.” So saying, she rose [[corr abruptly.”|abruptly.]]
