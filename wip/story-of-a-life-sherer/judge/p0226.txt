At night I again attended the Opera; again I passed through the recollected lane, but looked around in vain, for the figure I would have seen; however, I felt consoled by the thought, that I did, in heart, most earnestly desire again to meet her, and that, when I did so, I would bountifully assist her.

The theatre was brilliantly lighted up in every part, for it was again the evening of a festival, and all the company were now in masks; not a syllable could you hear of the performance, except, when, for some very favourite air, there was a momentary hush; immediately after, all again was the happy buzzing of a delighted crowd, like that of gathering bees on some rich, exhaustless bed of honey-giving flowers.—Well; I could not but feel pleasure. The masks rambled to all parts of the house.
