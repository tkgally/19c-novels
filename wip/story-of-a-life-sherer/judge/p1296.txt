When the priest turned from the altar, and joined his hands, his eyes looked wondering and sad upon me. It surprised him to see a moor in that place,—it grieved him to think of the broad way trod on in haughty ignorance by so many; he went through, however, calmly, and reverentially with the vesper service; and, at the close, passed into the body of the convent by a side door, leading out from the chapel. I was remarkably struck by the voice and manner of the monk; he was a friar, of the bare-footed Carmelites, and I saw by an inscription that this was a small missionary station of the Propaganda. The poor black people tottered and hobbled out to their huts. I gave them alms, and I could not but observe that they crossed themselves with some sort of fear, as they took them from my hand, which they judged to be worthy of amputation for having ever touched a Koran.
