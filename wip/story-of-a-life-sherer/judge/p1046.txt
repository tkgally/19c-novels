These lay afar, and died in the sun, while ruffians black-bearded, and strong, with bludgeon or with knife, forced in their way, and bore off the given loaves, and lived. Many too played usurers, and sold a day’s more life to the slowly dying for their little all.

My heart was sick with looking on such scenes. Strange it may sound, yet true it was, I almost wished myself again a chained and toiling slave, eating black bread, and sleeping sound at nights. I proposed to myself to leave the city, and quit this theatre of woe; but conscience said “No, it will be base.” Here you cannot doubt your power to serve, to save; some wretches—your purse—draw wide the strings, and feed the hungry.
