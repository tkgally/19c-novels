They had seized his horse by the bridle, and he had jumped off, made for the tree, and placing his back against it, had determined to sell his life dearly, and had successfully kept his ground till I so opportunely joined him. If to me all this was strange, confused, and exciting, what proved it to the lady Agatha?

In a full and sweeping night robe, with her head bound close (as for the grave), a broad white fillet passing tight across her brow, pale, very pale, yet beautiful, as if she was a love-worn woman, widowed by absence and neglect, she came down from her chamber with a taper in her hand, to learn the cause of our return, and of the confusion below. Her earnest attentions were immediately given to the care and comfort of her husband, while she thanked me in a most courteous, lady-like manner, but with a tone, which my quick and ready fears deemed full of a sad regret, that my acquaintance was thus in a manner forced upon her.
