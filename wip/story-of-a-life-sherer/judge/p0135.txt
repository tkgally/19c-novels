He had been sent on shore, on duty, four hours after the shock destroyed the city; had been to seek me, and feared, from the answers to his enquiries, that I was a victim. From him I learned that my amiable host, the old Count, was killed,—that father Antonio had escaped. He pointed out to me the nearest, and safest path to the street I sought, and which, but for him, I should not have found, and thither I flew to learn tidings of Agatha. She lived, and had been taken to a nunnery at Belem, by father Antonio; her husband, the Prince, was among the dead.
