In this scene of confusion, the offered proselyte had risen from his carpet and stood erect with undaunted eye, and the still settled aspect of a calm, resolved defiance.

“Whom seek ye?” he asked. It was in fury that the Aga answered him, “The Mussulman; the apostate; the worthless Turk, who hath stolen hither to abjure his faith; to deny our holy prophet, and to join himself to these accursed Israelites. You should be he; art thou he?—yes, it must be so.”

“There are none such as you describe among us. I am no Mahometan.”

“Whence then this Turkish garb?”—“A mere dress for travel.”—“A disguise, then?” “Not so.” As he spoke, he raised and threw off his turban; the thick tresses of his dark brown hair fell lordly on his shoulders; and he stood avowed to the eye, a Christian.
