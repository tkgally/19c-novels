“The garbs I see are generally mean, or the faces are wrinkled, or they are wan. I love to mingle my woes with theirs; and, though it is not at the sacring bell that I kneel,—and it is not before the picture, or the image that I cross myself, in the gloomy aisle of an old, a silent, a venerable church, my spirit always more freely prays.”
