“I remember the last evening that I passed in my father’s cottage: we sate out upon the stone bench, before the door, under the old trelliced vine; and a young neighbour came to see us, a youth of sixteen; one, who had used to be my play-fellow, till, as he grew, he became called on to labour; but still, upon saints’ days and holidays, he had always a choice nosegay for his little Gianetta; and if he went with his mules to the great city, he was sure to bring me back a new ribbon.

“I do not think that he knew, or I knew, or had ever dreamed any thing about love.
