[[cont]]clear they belonged to some lawless crew, and had probably been buccaneers or pirates all their lives.

It was awful to hear them joke about the earthquake, and liken the rocking of the solid earth to their vessel’s motion. They sung too snatches of old songs:

<verse>
“Play, beggars, play,”
“There’s scraps to-day,”
</verse>

I remember was one, and the chorus of another ran thus:

<verse>
“Drain, drain the bowl,
“Each fearless soul,
“Let the world wag as it will;
“Let the Heavens growl,
“Let the Devil howl,
“Drain, drain the deep bowl, and fill.”
</verse>
