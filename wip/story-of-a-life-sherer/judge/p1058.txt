I went down, and out into the dark street, calm and comforted. My own happiness was gone, but Maria’s, such as it might be, should not be troubled or lessened through me—although I would have thrown down the wealth of worlds, had it been mine, to tell her all, and see her tears, and listen to her sighs, and know they fell, and heaved in pity and in love for me.—I forbore.—I gave up the guilty bliss; a worthy sacrifice to the peace of her whom my very soul adored.
