“Arrayed in a light robe of so fine a texture, that my fair form was only veiled, I slowly descended on the stage in a cloud-borne car, and a pretty little infant Cupid, with silvery wings, waved his bright arrow, as he led me forth. The whole throng of figurantes parted, and grouped aside, and paused in their mazy dance.
