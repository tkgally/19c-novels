At last worn down, and preyed upon by the tyrannous passion, I determined on disclosing to her the state of my tortured feelings, and asking for a few words, mere words, to live upon, to love upon; of pity, friendship, counsel, any thing so she but heard me; and replied——.

The Prince, by degrees, recovered his strength of limb, and went out as usual. I heard him announce, one evening, that he should be absent on the morrow, and not return till the day following. I felt certain that I should not be expected by the lady Agatha, and might thus surprise her into an interview.
