[[cont]]Pompey is before you. And now the citadel, and now the white city, and the palm trees; and now the mole; the Pharos, and tall masts beyond;—and boats come out with turbaned pilots, bearded, swart, and bare-necked. I was no captive here;—a free, a fearless, a delighted gazer; a man of wealth, with purse, and servants, and plans of pleasure.

My welcome was cordial at a wealthy merchant’s. He was young, luxurious, voluptuous; had indolently sunk into all eastern customs. His conversation was all about the Arab and his tales, the seraglio and its secrets, the brave Mamelukes, and their fiery steeds. How soon, how very soon, cameleon-like, as the muser had called me, I yielded to the influence of all the seductive colouring he threw upon eastern life.
