[[cont]]Malek took out a purse and a small pistol, and presented them to our faithful guide. It was surprising with how different a look and manner he received the common valueless old pistol, and the rich well filled purse;—the latter was put hastily in his girdle; the rusty-looking weapon he pressed to his heart and forehead, and glanced down on it with pride; then he fell at the feet of Malek, and thanked and blessed him, and I too came in for my share of his blessing, though I had nothing to give him. He bowed his head down on my hand.—“May your father and your mother be blessed,” was his beautiful salutation, as I was about to step from the shore at going off. <i>My</i> father! <i>my</i> mother! whom I had sent early to their sad graves. I wept, and none knew why; and, though Malek was a young Pylades to me, I could not, dared not tell him.
