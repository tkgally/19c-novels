“We were yesterday at Devenish—in the evening, at the red sunset hour. The season and the scene were the very same, as when you came, Henry, to my wild cry, and saved the life of the mother on whom I doted. It was the wish of my father to show the tall tower to the Padre Ignazio, who has accompanied him from abroad. I do not like that Padre, and yet his conversation is very striking, pleasant I might call it, if I could listen to it with you;—his manners too are very gentle and polite, but then his look, it is so searching and so cold.
