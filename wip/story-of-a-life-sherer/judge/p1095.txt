“But I have something learned which soothes me, cheats me of my care, peoples my solitude with airy forms, and soft young voices of other days. Many countries have I visited, and many scenes distant, and far removed by space, I can call up at will. They come like those shown in the old enchanter’s glass. I gaze, and am beguiled.

“Musing, it is true, can only be called a sport; yet it is an art. By it we may warm ourselves to the deepest gratitude for every blessing, and feel our bliss enhanced. We may fan the faint spark of charity and love into such flames as cheer the cold, lone bosom; and stir the benevolence of our nature to look out far, and lovingly, from our solitude, on all mankind.
