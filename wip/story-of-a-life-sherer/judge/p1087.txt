“Then, between the courses, the lesson read, and after, the sweet dish of ancient times, the stuckling—all this in a vast hall, with saw-dust covered floor; and trenchers on the tables, and the beer in stiff black leathern jacks, and a chained tub for broken meats sent always to the poor, or to the captive!
