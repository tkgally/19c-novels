About this time my father died, and I became the heir of a title, and vast possessions. disgusted with the world, I determined on embracing a monastic life, and attracted by the severe rules of the melancholy and austere brotherhood of St. Bruno, I made a princely donation to the great and noble convent of the Certosa, near Pavia, and repaired thither with a morbid eagerness.

It is little to wondered at, if renouncing the world, in such a spirit, I was disappointed of that peace, the hope of which had led me to seek the seclusion of the cloister.
