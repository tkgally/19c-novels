We met in the evening—I played chess with the Prince, and afterwards we paced the saloon together. Agatha sat apart, silent and abstracted. I governed myself well; ventured on no long, and distressful gazings—scarce addressed her; put no emphasis in my tones.—She felt that I was behaving well. Once, and once only, I saw her, through a mirror opposite, look on me, in such sort as a tender mother would on a sick and wilful child; but the expression of her eye changed almost instantly, and you could see that her mind was looking back into years gone by, and scenes long passed away.
