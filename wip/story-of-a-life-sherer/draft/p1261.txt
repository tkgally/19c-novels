“The Brahmin sage lay reclining upon the shaded seat before his door, and he raised himself up at the sight of his young orphan pupil, and welcomed him with benevolent smiles. He was aged, but without one hair of gray, for he was entirely shaven; he only wore a cloth round his loins, and he sat the naked philosopher of his people, raised, in their opinion, above the highest of earthly kings.

“Feizi joined his hands, and spread them; then stretched them forth, and took hold of his feet, and touched them with his head.
