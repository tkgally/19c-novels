Malek bade him lead on; we followed him to the nearest hill’s side; where, near a small rude cavity in the broken ground, again lighting a torch he crawled into the earth, and we after him. With the knee and hand we felt ourselves pressing upon substances, that crunched under our weight, as leathery parchment, and rotten sticks might. It was not at first that I was aware of what broke beneath me; they were coffinless bodies, <i>dry bones that are to live again</i>;—it was very horrid.
