“Why,” said an aged Turk, with a beard of silver, “why do you wail like a girl? Is it for yourself? or for those who are gone to paradise? I have lost five sons—sons who rode with me in battle; they have died, as dogs die, in their bed. Shall I reproach the Lord?—he gave—he takes away; blessed be his name.” Well I went away within to my chambers, where I might shed my tears, and sigh my wild reproaches to the unconscious walls.

I rode without the city on that side, where the desert spreads;—there I murmured and raved, unheard, save by my starting steed.
