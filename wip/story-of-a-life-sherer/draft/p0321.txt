To me the sight gave food for wondering thought. Justice had been delayed; but the eye of heaven had followed the shedder of blood. Punishment had, like a blood-hound with a wounded limb, tracked him unceasingly, and found him in a den among violent and cruel spirits, like his own, where he had thought himself secure.
