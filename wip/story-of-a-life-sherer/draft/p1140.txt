In Europe I am certain I should have passed unregardingly the assembled beauties of a court, or a city; but here, the veil, the mystery, the high-walled haram, the surly eunuch—these things stirred and troubled my easily excited fancy.

A few evenings after, as I was standing alone, examining a fallen column of white marble, which lay bedded in the sand, not far from my residence, there rode up to me a little black slave boy.—The top of his little skull cap glittered with embroidery,—his turban was rose-coloured,—his vest and trowsers, gaudy and silken; and the furniture of his ass showed that he was in good service, as a young, petted, and favourite eunuch.
