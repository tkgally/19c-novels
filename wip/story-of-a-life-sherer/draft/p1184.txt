There were no dates to these letters. Lovers think not of time and place. The next too was without a date, but I should judge there must have been a lapse of weeks.

“Your Honor,

“I come to the lodge twice, but I would’nt see your honor, I would’nt be let. I am sint away from the cottage, and what will I do? I that have lived there man and boy, thirty year;—and the young jewel lies sick, and your honor that loves her in a fever, and what will I do?

“Sure the madam has give me all her money, bad luck to it, what will it do for a lone man?

“I am thinking they’re making a Roman of the young cratur, and I am glad of that, any way, for her sweet sowl; but the divil be with that pale priest, that talks Frinch. I thought no good would come of him. Sure I never see him take an honest sup of potcheen since he come.
