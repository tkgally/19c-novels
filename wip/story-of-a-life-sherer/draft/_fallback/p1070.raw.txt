62 STORY OF A LIFE.

«* How rich those English are!” said a poor fellow, with a spade in his hand, as he paused a minute by my side. ‘* Now the two old people that lay here, there came a young man, and had their bodies taken up, and carried down to the sea, and put into a ship, and took them home to England —as if they could be the better of that, poor devils!”

The man passed on, not heeding my emotion, and left me.

“Then Howard, I thought, has been to them ason. Howard is a brother —a husband to my sister—yes ;—I kneeled down, and thanked Heaven that it was so. In early life I had seen, though but for a short time, the noble promise of his boyhood; yes—there were men for Heaven to favour—Howards and Franklands. Such a being as myself only cumbered the

earth.

Surely my cup of woe was full; surely I had drunk it to the very dregs.
