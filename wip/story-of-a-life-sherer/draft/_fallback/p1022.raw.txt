‘Dost thou hear?” said the Aga. “Cry now, and lift up thy voice, and shout with joy, There is no God. but God, and Mohammed is his prophet.”

The German stood silent for a moment, and raised his eyes; and his thoughts seemed to wander—far away they wandered, back, long years back too; and thus he answered.
