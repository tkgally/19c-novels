And was it possible to look upon the scene below me, and ask to close my eyes on it for ever? Sorrento’s mountain arm encircling that blue bay; and isles towards the ruder ocean, checking its saucy waves, bidding them break, and flow in soft and hushingly; and the white and sunny city; and the green and_ vyilla-studded plain below. I paused, and gazed, and wept. Famine was on the city—and in my _ heart was hunger—the most aching, the most insatiate— the hunger of affection—that asked for looks, and tones of love, —that was ready to pour out all itself in answering love; but now the garner inwhich I had stored up my life’s future nourishment was another’s, and I, again famished in my hopes, a beggar in my very day-dreams, for I could build up no visionary home of happiness
