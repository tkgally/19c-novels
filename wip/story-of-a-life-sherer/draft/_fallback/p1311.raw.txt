« The evening came: a thousand coloured lamps threw their rich glare upon our gardens. Melodious sounds rose up from leafy bowers, which concealed young warbling choristers, and skilled musicians. Tables were spread with rare confections in our hall, and all Ferrara came to grace the princely festival, which my father gave upon the eve of the day appointed for the marriage of my brother. In one part of the garden there was an orchestra, erected for the performance of a more regular concert, and here seats were prepared for all the most distinguished guests. Here it was that the lady Lucretia sate with her friends and relatives, to receive that homage paid by common consent to affianced brides.
