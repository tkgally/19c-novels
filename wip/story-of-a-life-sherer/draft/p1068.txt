Was it on the brink of the dull vault where he was to be soon cast in among the bones of thousands? Was it on the eve of that sleep, from which he was to be roused, by a summoning trump, to the bar of Heaven, that I was to tread down the writhing worm?—No.—There are many ways of murdering; I was myself a murderer, a parricide. I went to a vintner’s, and sold my cloak for a trifle, and bought wine; and came back and gave him a draught. I started as if an adder had bit me, as I felt his lips kiss my hand; but then I checked myself, and asked pardon of Heaven for that crime of pride.
