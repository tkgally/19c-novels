The sky was lightning—lightning—not flashing, but fixed;—one wide and awful sheet of bluish flame. The seas were glassily transparent, though mountains high; while crowded beneath the surface of the waters multitudes of the long-drowned dead rose rushingly from their chilly resting-places. Livid the corpses were; yet they looked and swam with open eyes, and the action of the living. All looked on me—all called upon my name. In bubbling murmurs, in hissing whisperings, “Osman!” a million times repeated, was sounded in my ear.

How long could I have endured this? I awoke in terror. There was no sound but the gentle sucking of the rippling tide; and, through the half-opened door, I saw the statue-like sentinel with watchful protecting eye.
