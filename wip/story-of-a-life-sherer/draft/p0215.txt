Near this place stands the ducal palace, with its crowded arches; its two grandly ornamented central windows, with canopies of stone, figures and bas-reliefs below; its roof, all bordered with a rich and pointed fret-work. Guards sauntered beneath; nobles stood in the balconies; vast numbers of gondolas were gathered round the quay, and several open barks, with men and women of the lower classes rapidly filling them; while, farther out from the shore there lay some boats of strange, fantastic forms, with prows, and prow-like sterns, high curving, and carved with ornaments; the one all shells; the other flowers; another again fruits; and richly gilded all, with silken flags, and gay dressed rowers, and music in them, and songs, and all the happy noises of a holiday.
