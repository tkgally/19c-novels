The stir, the bustle, and the crowd of the vast city bewildered me. I purchased through my agents a small annuity, and gave all the rest of my money to those establishments, which shelter the houseless heads of weeping Magdalens, and give the breast and the cradle to little infants whom the fear of shame or the cruelty of vice would expose to perish. It is little strange that I should have now felt and indulged the wish to “cease from man.”
