Hugo would soon have delivered himself to his happy reveries, but that Phœbe spoke:—women are always the first to display presence of mind—if they be innocent; if not, they are more reckless.

“Precious time,” said Phœbe, “is indeed flying, and we shall soon regret having learnt so little while it lasted. Tell me, how came you here, and what hope have you of release? we depart to-morrow for our home, and oh! how welcome will now be that quiet from which, so lately, I yearned to escape!”

Hugo had little to tell, and that little was soon said. His questions were more numerous, and in reply to them he learned the following tale from Phœbe.
