[[cont]]And when I saw in the Mercurius that he had come to London, I made out his lodgings, for the sake of the bonny ladies, his daughters. I found him, poor man, little fit for all the honours that were promised him. Between the fever of his mind, and the chill of his body in coming from Holland, he had an attack of paralytic, and lay with just enough of life in him to keep his body from decay; and so he lies to this hour, I believe. I offered my poor service to the ladies, his daughters; but as I was speaking to them, in walked that terrible dark man, Hezekiah, and with a scowl that went near to frighten me, he accused me of all sorts of crimes, and declared that if he ever saw me again in Westminster, he would have me sent to the American settlements as a spy of the malignants.
