The sun struggled out from among the clouds before he set, and flung one parting smile over the agitated waters of the lake, that seemed as dark as my own doom. Only from heaven, thought I, can any light now shine upon my lot; so it is best. But lo! that sun-light moving along the waters, seems approaching; and now, it pauses, and beams steadily on yon green field, and lights up that cottage window as for a festival. There shall be my home!
