Then a dozen brass bugles sounded cheerily, and yet were drowned in the fierce voices of the hounds, as they dashed away after their flying victim. On, and away, through tangled covert and swampy meadow, and over wide stagnant ditches, did we sweep—but still in order—the Prince a-head, riding fearlessly; and soon we turned to the left, and emerged from field and forest on the <i>Dunes</i>, the long line of sand-hills that separates the Low Countries from the sea. Then, far away before us, we could see the noble form of the stag; but there were then few of us to see him—some half dozen, perhaps, besides the master of the hunt.
