On calmer reflection, however, he recanted his determination, for he considered that he could withdraw his parole at the last, and possess his liberty meanwhile.

We soon found ourselves before a stern tribunal of belted officers, who received our names and registered our word of honour, to pass beyond the Parliament lines within twelve hours, or surrender ourselves true prisoners.
