“Yes, Jeanie,” he muttered faintly; “yes, yes, we won’t scoff any more, but we will pray.”

And his parched lips moved, and his hands were clasped together, and with one loud moan, he died.

There had hitherto been perfect silence, except for the measured tramp of horses, and the faint clank of armour, and these sounds had become so monotonous, as scarcely to interfere with the repose of all around them. And when, through the stillness of all human sounds, that unearthly moan broke forth upon the night, a shudder seemed to thrill through every heart. Phœbe instinctively reined up her palfrey, and the waggoner stopped his horses with a loud Wo! that seemed an almost appropriate exclamation. The officer in command looked into the waggon, and perceived that one of his prisoners had escaped at once from him and life.
