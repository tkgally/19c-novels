Yes! though you return to toil and danger, and perhaps to death; though you are about to fight against the cause that I once—that I <i>still</i> hold to be the best—I know that you follow the dictates of your own true heart, and I would not have you waver—no, not for a moment—from that fidelity which alone can satisfy your conscience. I have seen enough of error among those whom I called my friends, to believe that there may be some truth among yours. May He who holds the world in his hands give victory to the rightful cause!”

Our host now entered the room, and told us that the hour approached when the travellers must depart, and our parole would terminate. He had “at length got rid of that drunken trooper,” he said, “and may Heaven defend our good King from such supporters.
