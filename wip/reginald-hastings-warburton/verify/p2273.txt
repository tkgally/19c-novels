[[cont]]I, the convicted felon, had a pulpit to preach from, and a house to shelter my mother. I preached too boldly. I was driven from my pulpit, banished from my home. I could not remain a pensioner on my parishioners. I wandered away with my poor mother, in midwinter. She died. I rebelled against the church. I made myself friends among the friendless; I became the favourite orator of the London people; and yet, I never spoke to them one word I did not feel.
