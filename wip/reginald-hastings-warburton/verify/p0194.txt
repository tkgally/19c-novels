“Mourn not;” he said softly, “no one ever so rejoiced to live as I to die—to die thus on my son’s brave breast, while my King’s enemies are scattered before him. Now I have no earthly fear to mingle with my hope of heaven! Long may you live, my son, loyally, righteously—and when you die, may you welcome death as I do now.”

He paused, and some inarticulate words rattled in his throat; but his last heart’s pulse lent vigour to his voice, as he saw some of our troopers returning from the chase; they flung themselves from their horses, and gazed with clasped hands and mournful eye upon their lord. He tried to raise himself, and pointing with his trembling hand to where the battle was still struggling, he exclaimed:

“The standard is taken! To the rescue—to the rescue! This day or never—fight—.”
