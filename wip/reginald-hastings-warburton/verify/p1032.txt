The Countess looked irresolutely towards the door by which she had entered. After a few moments’ pause, it opened, and gave admission to a good-looking man, somewhat past the middle age; he entered briskly, but paused cautiously, and cast a glance of inquiry at the Countess, when he perceived how the room was occupied.
