Sir Janus yielded to Bryan’s energy, and in sheer timidity took the boldest step he had ever ventured on. His daughters, of higher mettle, eagerly set about making their preparations, and in less than an hour Bryan joined me, with the assurance that all would be ready for our departure. “Except the poor Chaplain,” he added; “for the old man swears he will not forsake the remnant of the people committed to his charge.
