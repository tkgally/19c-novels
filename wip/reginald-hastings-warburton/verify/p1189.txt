# Chapter XII.

<verse>
The heaven under which I live is fair,
The fertile soil will a full harvest bear;
Thine, thine is all the barrenness, if thou
Should’st but sit still and pine, when thou should’st plough.
When I but think how many a tedious year,
Our patient sovereign did attend
His long misfortunes’ patient end.
</verse>

M<sc>any</sc> a long day has passed since last I saw this manuscript. The pages are stained and torn, and the ink is faded. Innumerable were the chances against its ever having seen the light; yet its old leaves are open to me once more, and claim their finishing.
