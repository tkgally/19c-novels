“To the volunteer troop, of which I spoke this morning, I have given no fighting orders; I have even recommended them to compound with the Roundheads, as they are a mere isolated force, without support in these parts, and must soon fall into the enemy’s hands. If any of them, however, were without homes or families, and bent on fighting, I told them that they should find a welcome amongst our little garrison at the Hall.
