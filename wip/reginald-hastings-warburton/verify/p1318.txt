[[cont]]however, soon retired to her embroidery frame, and I had followed her with my eyes, when I observed Phœbe start and point with an alarmed look towards her father. He seemed as if he had trodden upon a rattlesnake, and was restrained from moving by some fascinated fear. One foot was retired and his cane was in the air, but so he remained, motionless, gazing on a low seat that was unoccupied as it seemed to me. But suddenly what seemed to be a human head appeared from behind it, grinning delightedly at the alarm it had caused, and giving vent to its feelings in shrill exclamations of “keek, keek, keek.”
