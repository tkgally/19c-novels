# Chapter II.

<verse>
Sweet in manner, fair in favour,
Mild in temper, fierce in fight,
Warrior nobler, gentler, braver,
Never shall behold the light.
</verse>
[[right]]<sc>anon.</sc>

M<sc>y</sc> second brother, Hugo, was scarcely seventeen when my story commences. In spite of all their efforts to conceal it, he was the favourite of both our parents. I never grudged—I scarcely envied him that priceless distinction. Nor could I wonder at it; he was so gentle and generous, so brave, and good, and true. I was too proud of his genius and acquirements to feel jealous of the comparative shade in which they placed me.
