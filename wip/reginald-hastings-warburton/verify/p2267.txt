It awoke to a consciousness of its pollution; it endeavoured to purify itself, and conceived the great ambition of self-regeneration. From that time I was a changed man; new thoughts rushed in upon my mind, until my brain reeled beneath their pressure. As they assumed form, they gradually shaped themselves into a destiny. I felt that I had a great mission given to me to fulfil. By fasting and prayer, I prepared for a just trial of my faith: I resolved to escape from prison in the open day: if I failed, I should perish, and there was an end of all; if I succeeded, I should take it for a sign.
