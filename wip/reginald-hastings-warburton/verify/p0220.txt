# Chapter XV.

<verse>
One drop of blood drawn from thy country’s bosom,
Should grieve thee more than streams of foreign gore.
</verse>

Whilst I had been speaking, Prince Rupert’s trumpets sounded out the well-known call to “boot and saddle,” and as usual in such cases, we hastened to the great quadrangle in Christchurch.
