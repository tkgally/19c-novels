[[cont]]grudged the hour that Bryan whiled away with the sweet music of his harp. The next morning found us in sight of the low coast of Holland, but considerably farther north than we had intended. Before dark we had run into the little fishing village of Schevening, near the Hague, and disembarked our precious freight.

We easily found hardy sailors to help us to lay up the little craft in safety. I expected to want her again ere long; and Sir Janus, in his delight at finding himself and his wealth secured, enabled me to reward her faithful owner beyond his highest hopes.
