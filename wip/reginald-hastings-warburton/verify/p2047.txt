Astonished as I was at this new turn of affairs, I thought it better to tell the good-natured Bentinck everything. He immediately entered into my feelings; but confessed that there might be very great difficulties in obtaining mercy for Bryan, since there was no witness to refute the accusation and the testimony of the drowned corpse.
