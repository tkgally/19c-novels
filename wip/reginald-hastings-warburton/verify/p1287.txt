Nothing is easier; my Lord and I can manage the craft with the pilot already aboard of her, and by Heaven’s favour and good guiding, we shall be half-way to Holland before day.”

The feasibility of this project was evident as well as the necessity for its instant execution. Zillah felt so strongly the contagious power of the young Irishman’s cheerful confidence, that she determined to let him try the same influence upon her father. She knew the old man would be startled by such a sudden and bold measure; and if Bryan’s light-hearted and confident manner failed to inspire courage, she knew that all must be in vain.
