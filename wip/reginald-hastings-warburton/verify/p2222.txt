[[cont]]the face of Hezekiah Doom, ghastly, supernaturally pale. His right shoulder seemed to be dislocated, and he could not move the arm that he had always held wrapped in his cloak; it was now extended helplessly, and at the end of it was—no hand! a bare stump—on which the black scorching bars of the executioner’s iron remained still visible,—was there instead.

The wretched man appeared almost insensible, and Arnold motioned to me to carry him by the cloak towards our hut. This seemed necessary, for a storm was gathering on Mont Blanc, and if it burst upon us where we stood, the swollen torrent would soon sweep away the very ground on which we trod. Therefore, we bore him along carefully and laboriously, and at length laid him on the bed of leaves from which I had lately risen.
