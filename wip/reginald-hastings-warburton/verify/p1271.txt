Then he would rush at a large oak chest, and tumble out all the old linen to look for sheets, shirts, every thing that I could want. And at length when I was fairly seated at a savoury mess of stewed rabbits, and he had nothing else to do, he took down his well-battered carbine, and began to look it over, as if it must come into immediate use. He seemed to think that I was there for the sole purpose of storming the Manor House and restoring everything to its old footing.
