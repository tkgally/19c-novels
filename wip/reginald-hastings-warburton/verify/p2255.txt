He had not mentioned his niece; perhaps he shrank from doing so, as I was to be his messenger. But I learned afterwards, that his will contained the tenderest messages, the wisest counsels, the most anxious directions for her whom he loved as his own child. It appeared also that he had acquired considerable wealth in the course of his industrious career, and that he had bequeathed it all to her.
