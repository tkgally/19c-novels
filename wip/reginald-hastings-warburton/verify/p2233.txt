[[cont]]spirit of Zillah seemed to float among those pure and lofty solitudes—to hover over my head, and to bless me with an intensity of happiness and hope.

Beneath me, in many a mingled maze of dim and shadowy beauty, lay a wide-spread landscape, receding downwards, onwards, towards the lake, that gleamed, star-studded, in the distance. Beyond it lay the humble cottage, so long the shelter of my mournful exile. But what strange shuddering, and presage of undefined mysterious ill rose up from that far spot to mar my new-found happiness, and chill me in the first warm glow of triumph?
