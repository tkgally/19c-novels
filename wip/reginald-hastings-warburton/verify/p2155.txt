The galliot bounded, like a startled deer, over the dark water, and the oars swept wide and strong, for the electrical impulse of danger nerved the sailors’ arms. Two boats full of armed men shot out rapidly from the bridge stairs; one paused a while to pick up the discomfited preacher from the water, while the other sped swiftly after the fugitives. A volley of small arms rattled through the air, but the men, unsteadied by the jerking oars, could take no aim. Many vessels lay anchored here and there in the river, and amongst them the skipper steered his little craft cleverly, so as to have always one of them between the pursuers and himself.
