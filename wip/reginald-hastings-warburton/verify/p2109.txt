He had worshipped after his own fashion at Jerusalem, in defiance of Jew, Turk, and Papist; and he had rescued a Protestant friend from the gripe of the Spanish Inquisition. Switzerland was the country on which he dwelt with most pleasure, declaring that if England became untenable for an honest man, that he would only exchange the banks of the Thames for those of the Lake of Geneva. That chance expression has determined my lot.
