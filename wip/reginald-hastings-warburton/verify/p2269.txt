The assassin became silent, and his countenance assumed an expression, such as it might have worn when the knife was weltering in the rich veins of Buckingham. Conflicting passions were raging in his memory, as if but just awakened; his teeth were set; his eyes rolled wildly, and the very hair upon his head was stirred, and seemed to creep; whilst his emotion gave him momentary strength, and he sat upright on his pallet—a fearful sight!

The village surgeon had left some soothing medicine, which I now offered to the unhappy man. He dashed it from him, exclaiming fiercely:
