He now only waited for an opportunity to abandon the castle, of which he had been so vain; and he would gladly have exchanged it all, from cellar to topmost turret, for a safe berth on board of the meanest fishing smacks, at good gunshot from the shore. A vessel had been actually chartered for him at Hull, but somehow those whom this cautious gentleman employed, were never earnest in his service.
