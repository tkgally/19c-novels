“Ask no questions until thou mayest do so from the maiden’s own lips. Thou wilt find her in much fear: draw near unto her and whisper thy name; tell her, if need be, to claim thy protection as her near kinsman. Speak no other word, but let thine ears and eyes be open to my signs.”

So saying, Hezekiah drew near a handsome house, near which he paused, until he heard a door opened into Charing Street, and footsteps retiring; he then knocked, and was deferentially admitted with his companion by a very prim-looking waiting-woman. They passed into a richly-adorned room, with an inlaid floor, over which was laid a carpet so soft that they moved noiselessly into the room beyond. There every luxury seemed collected; the richest furniture, the brightest flowers, the most fragrant perfumes, the rarest pictures; every sense was cultivated almost to sensuality.
