# Chapter IV.

<verse>
Our gayness and our gilt are all besmirched
With rainy marching in the painful field;
There’s not a piece of feather in our host,
And time hath sworn us into slovenry.
</verse>
[[right]] <sc>Shakespere.</sc>

T<sc>he</sc> time that I have passed over lightly, I need not say was full of incident and adventure; but in autobiography the interest of nations shrinks into insignificance when compared with the struggles and experiences of our own hearts.
