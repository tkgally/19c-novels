Such was not the will of Providence. But the hour of the fight was come; and so busily had I been occupied with our men, that I had scarcely time to glance along our line, before the battle-smoke concealed it from my eyes. I could only observe that our force was ordered precisely in the same manner as that of our enemies; the cavalry on either wing, flanked by Aston’s and Heydon’s artillery, and the infantry in the centre, where the stout and true Earl of Lindsay fought among his Lincoln volunteers.

I heard a few guns upon the left, then Prince Rupert rushed to the front of our column, and shouted to the trumpeters to sound a charge.
