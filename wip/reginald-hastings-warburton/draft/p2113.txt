“You arrive at a singular conjuncture,” said he, “as regards Sir Janus and his daughters; but first, I must presume you ignorant of all that has happened here. I will also take the liberty to inform you that I am aware of the deep interest you take in this family; of your early intimacy with them; of your flight to the Hague, of the pillage of your house, and the alienation of your estates. This Hezekiah, as he is called, has been long known to me. He is a man of singular character, such as these strange times alone could have produced or brought to light.
