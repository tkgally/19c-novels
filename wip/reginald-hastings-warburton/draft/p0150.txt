“Hallo! hallo! host of the Horns—where art thou? Is it seemly usage for a noble Cavalier to be left here, sweltering in your foul streets like a popinjay in a July sun? ’Sdeath and wounds! will nobody answer; have we a Roundhead ambuscado here, under the beard of sacred majesty, that refuses to admit his officer? So then, here’s for an assault—<i>vade retro, Sathanas</i>!”

So saying, the Cavalier wheeled round a powerful and coal black steed, and backing him against the closed door, had the address to make him kick right into it, and that with such violence that the mark of the iron shoes remains there still.

“Saints defend us!” exclaimed my landlady, “here’s another; and another of the right sort, too, I warrant me.” And straightway the fearless woman opened her beleaguered door, just as the heels of Sathanas were about to do so more unceremoniously.
