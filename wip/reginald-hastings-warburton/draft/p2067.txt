I knew, too, that in London, as it then appeared, she would find democracy triumphant in manners as well as in politics; and I hoped, from what I heard of the populace, that her views would be changed still more in regard to their fitness for supreme legislation.[[fnref *]]

I pitied poor Phœbe, however, with unmitigated pity, and I feared for her too, under such guardianship as that she was about to experience.
