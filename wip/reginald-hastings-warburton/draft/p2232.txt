# Chapter XI.
[[subtitle]] Pesame de vos el Conde.

<verse>
Count, and thou art called to die,
O! it grieves my soul to see:—
Thou hast erred—but yet I thought
Pardon might be found for thee;
That the error love commits,
For love’s sake might pardoned be.
</verse>
