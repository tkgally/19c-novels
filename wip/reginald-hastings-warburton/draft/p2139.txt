We must escape from it to France, here alone he can find no agent, for he will have no followers. Madame de Coligny, our mother’s kinswoman, will surely afford us an asylum, where we may rest until this tyranny be overpast, and happier times allow us to return and lay us down to rest where our fathers sleep. What think you, my sister?”

Phœbe joyfully acquiesced; and for the last few days, active, but secret preparations had been making for their departure. This very afternoon, to their glad surprise, the merchant had decided that his wife and daughter should accompany them.
