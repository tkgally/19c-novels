It was high noon when I reached my destination. I found Arnold at the hut. He received me with great civility, and offered to share his meal with me. As soon as we were refreshed, he proposed to start.

“If your lowland nerves do not fail you,” he added, “we may take a short path through scenes of grave sublimity and beauty. But resolve you well, for deadly peril may befal you, if you even tremble.”

I replied to his taunting tone very quietly: I had feelings struggling within me that left no room for a thought of personal fear.
