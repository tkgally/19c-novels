# Chapter XIV.

<verse>
And its hame, and its hame, and its hame we fain would be,
Though the cloud is in the lift, and the wind is on the lea;
For the sun, through the mirk, looking blithly on mine ’ee,
Says, I’ll shine on ye yet in your ain countree.
</verse>
