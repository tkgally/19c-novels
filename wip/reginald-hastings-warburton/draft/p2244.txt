Well! that poor village girl is all that remains to me on earth to love, to care for, to protect. Since her father died, she has been to me as my own child—my comfort, hope, and pride. In an evil hour, I left Switzerland to toil for her, and not vainly, in foreign lands. Returning with wealth that might have made her a bride for princes, I found her, as I thought, the pastime of a masquerading outlaw. Of him there was but one question to be asked, and his answer involved life and honour.
