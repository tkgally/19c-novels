The little dwarf, however, gave no time for explanations; he impatiently caught Hugo by the corner of his Puritan cloak, and dragged him forward. The women followed, and in a few minutes the little party arrived at Whitehall Stairs, where a boat and two watermen seemed waiting for them. The dwarf impatiently signed to his companions to take their places in the boat, and they were soon swiftly rowing down the river.

All preserved a profound silence, except Hugo and Phœbe, who, wrapped in the same cloak, conversed eagerly until they reached the Tower. When there, they shot in under a dark archway, and were soon admitted through an iron grating into a subterranean passage; there they were received by an official of the Tower; he read by the light of his lanthern a paper handed to him by the dwarf, and then motioned to our adventurers to disembark.
