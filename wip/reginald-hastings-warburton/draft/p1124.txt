He is most unlike what I am in every respect; he seems made for, if not by, these times that are destroying me; wasting my heart away, until every hope I once held is merged in a longing for peace—peace—peace!”

The manner in which this wise and good man pronounced that word was profoundly touching: the echo of them seemed to linger on the midnight; and long after the lips that uttered them were cold, fancy brought back their sound. The word Peace, I hope and believe, might be carved upon his tomb as his best epitaph.

I did not dare to interrupt his reverie, but it was soon broken by a messenger from the King: he came in hot haste to seek Lord Falkland.

“Farewell!” said he, extending his hand and smiling a most sad sweet smile, “you see the Secretary for War cannot be long spared!”
