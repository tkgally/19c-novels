That defenceless girl looked very grand, and almost Pythonic, while she spoke; but poor Phœbe’s heroics were no match for the cold sarcastic, subtle person she addressed. Menaces, punishment, the scaffold itself, cannot extinguish enthusiasm, but may oftimes fan its flame. Ridicule or apathy are far more fatal to its energies.

Pym only smiled indulgently as Phœbe spoke, and though his eyes looked warm admiration, his voice was free from all emotion as he replied, “Very comely art thou thus—thy words are pretty, but their spirit altogether uncommendable. Now, thou knowest, thou art free as the wind that wanders where it listeth; but it is not seemly that a maiden so fair and young should wander through our crowded streets like a roe among the mountains.
