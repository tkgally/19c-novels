We soon found our way to the stables, each man girding himself up as best he might, on his way down stairs; some hastily buckled on a gorget or a haquetin, others mounted in their doublets. I found poor Satan just made comfortable, but he seemed eager for action, and was almost the first steed that was bestrode. It was reported to us that the skirmish could not be far off, as the firing was plainly heard, though the flashing of the fire-arms could not be seen through the darkness and the vapours of the night. As we rode apace, however, we soon heard confused noises, and at length met our Cavaliers with three or four prisoners captured in the brief skirmish.
