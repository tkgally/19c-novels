Bryan and I had assumed the gravest dresses we could procure, but we instantly drew all eyes upon us. All the other guests had their hair clipped, according to the most orthodox “Committee cut,” as it was called; ours still floated down our shoulders, and the very plumes in our hats seemed to wave more jauntily, as if in defiance; at least, I am sure that Bryan’s did.

Finding that my host could or would furnish me with but little news, I prepared to depart, and was leaving the room, when my attention was drawn to my reckless comrade, who was in high conversation with a pretty bar-maid.

“Good wine, by my faith!” said he to her; “none of your lively company can dissent to that matter, anyhow.”
