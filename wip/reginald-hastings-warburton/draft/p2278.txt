My obduracy at length moved the anger of the sisterhood; I perceived that I could no longer remain in peace among the Ursulines, and I was meditating how or whither I should depart from there, when I was sullenly summoned to the grille once more.

“My summons was accompanied by a request from the abbess that this visitor might be my last, for she felt that scandal might befal her convent, owing to the number of men, heretics, too, who presented themselves for admittance at its gates. I went thither with reluctance. It was, however, with unspeakable joy that I then found our old Chaplain, poor Phœbe’s earliest friend. He had been forced by the Puritans to leave our old home.
