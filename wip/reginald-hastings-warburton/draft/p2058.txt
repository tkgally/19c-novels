The poor dwarf sped on, holding up his hands imploringly where there was none to save, and uttering fearful screams, but they were suddenly hushed; the mob, headed by a few half-starved, half-drunken, howling beldames, had overtaken their prey, and all was over, except the tustling, deadened sounds of people struggling and straining amongst themselves. The preacher, meanwhile, finding it impossible to penetrate the crowd, after desperate efforts to do so, had paused when the cries of pursuit had ceased.
