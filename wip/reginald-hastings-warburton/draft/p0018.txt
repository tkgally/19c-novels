I gradually detected faint sounds and sights in the apparent blank and silence that surrounded me; I could hear the rustle of the sentry’s weather-beaten doublet, and the trail of his pike along the leads of the castle. Once or twice, too, I thought I heard something stirring under the stony floor, but that must be imagination. I approach the window to catch a breath of fresh air through a broken pane, and I perceive some scratches on the glass that appear to me like letters. They have been cut with a keen diamond, but if they have any meaning it is undecipherable, and yet—

I had written thus far when the gaoler entered with my dinner, and at once changed the current of my thoughts.
