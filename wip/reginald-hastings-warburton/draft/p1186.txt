That the Governor was very anxious to have me exchanged or removed, but that some secret and strong influence in London had prevailed against me. Finally, that he himself had had a quarrel with Harrison, who commanded his former regiment, and had applied for leave to retire to his own country, Scotland; but that Colonel Hutchinson, a mild, good man, had prevailed upon him to give his services to the garrison at Nottingham. “And now, farewell,” he concluded. “I shall not be pairmitted to visit ye again, for ye require mine aid nae mair, and the commands of the Governor are vera strict. So far ye weel.”
