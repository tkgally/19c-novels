“The rebels are in motion,’ he whispered; “and I believe they have left the Manor already. Well, we’ve one-and-twenty firelocks here, and I doubt not we can beat back five score of such fellows as come yonder. Nevertheless, to save risk, we must be silent. The ladies are all on board, and the shallop will be back by this.”

So saying, he moved away at a rapid pace; I followed closely, and the volunteers brought up the rear. I had vainly entreated them to disperse to their homes; they replied that they dared not, if they would ever wish to show their faces amongst their kin again.
