Her zealous mind experienced a reaction proportioned to her overstrained enthusiasm: she now feared Puritanism more even than Papistry, and would have taken refuge from democracy in despotism itself.

Nor was she singular in thus rushing to extremes. The mind of the multitude in England was similarly changing; and from time to time, vague rumours of returning loyalty reached even to our exile. Booth’s insurrection had nearly recalled me to my native country, but the sinking health of the old chaplain, and reluctance to leave my wife alone among strangers, delayed my departure until too late.
