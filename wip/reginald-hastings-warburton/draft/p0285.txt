# Chapter XVII.

<verse>
Away, away, thou traitor strong!
Out o’ my sight thou soon shalt be!
I grantit never a traitor’s life,
And now I’ll not begin wi’ thee.
</verse>

I <sc>must</sc> now interrupt the narrative of my personal adventures, in order to describe what was passing round me.

It had long transpired that Lady d’Aubigny was about to leave Oxford, and it was whispered that her expedition to London involved in it some object of a political nature.
