Arnold listened to me with eyes of piercing and stern inquiry; then, turning away his face, he remained silent for many minutes. Making, at length, a strong effort of self-control, he extended to me his hand and said, hoarsely:

“It is true. I see it is true; though the strangest of all truths. Hadst thou faltered in a single word—had thy cheek changed colour—thou hadst died; ay, though my body were blent with thine in one bloody mass—below there—in that icy gulf. Thy trial is now ended: I offer thee my hand in proof of my belief, but not of my forgiveness. I respect—but I shall loathe thee whilst I live.
