[[cont]]for a long time. He was very dear, having been like a father to her. He had first taught her to read and think on subjects different from those that occupied the village mind; and he always brought her books and foreign presents when he returned from his travels. For some undefined reason, I was not pleased to hear of this arrival. I felt that by some means or other it would interfere with my newly-discovered source of enjoyment; and, what was worse, I felt that it was well for Marguerite that it should be so!

[[break]]

<i>Oct.</i> 1649.—A month has elapsed since last I wrote, and brought with it momentous changes in my mind. How altered have all external things become at the same time!—But I must force myself to resume the thread of my story.
