At the same moment, the young man looked up from his boat and beckoned kindly, but as hastily, to the poor little creature, to follow him. The dwarf paused, as it seemed, in painful doubt; then, yielding to the stronger impulse, he put his hands before his face and rushed blindly towards the preacher.

“A sorcerer—a sorcerer!” now burst in fearful yells from the mob, “see, his familiar comes!”
