So it was settled and so done. Our jovial and generous Cavaliers of Newark did everything that kindness could dictate: more than one of them was anxious to accompany me, but of course I declined their offer.

It was on a fine frosty afternoon, on Christmas Eve that we began our expedition. Bryan was dressed as when he entered Nottingham; I, in a forester’s rough garb, which was well-suited to the weather and our walk.
