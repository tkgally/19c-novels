Having thus spoken, the preacher strode to the door, knocked three times, and was answered by the grunting voice of the dwarf, who turned the key and flung open the door. Hugo followed at a sign, and almost immediately afterwards found himself ushered into the room by which he had stood sentry; the dwarf entering with him, and crouching at the door as soon as he had closed it.
