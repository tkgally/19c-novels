“To the Lord Hastings at Oxford, these; haste, post haste.

“My dear Brother,

“As regards the chief business that I came upon, I have sped prosperously beyond my hopes. I was only once obliged to use my pass, and Bryan was only <i>once</i> taken prisoner on the road. I need scarcely tell you that he escaped; and I actually found him here before me. Wherefore I was welcomed with blazing bonfires, and happy looks, and loud greetings, and good cheer. I need not now talk of some saddened faces that came, while they welcomed my return, to inquire how those died who shall return to them no more; and how earnestly they listened to each almost forgotten word or deed relating to their dead that I could then recal.
