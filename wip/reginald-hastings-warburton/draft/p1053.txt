At length the Puritan spoke:—“Thou hast heard,” he said, “what the vulgar speech is concerning me—poor simple worm that I am—what thinkest <i>thou</i>?”

“I think of you,” replied Hugo, “only as one who saved my life long ago, and who this day conferred a yet greater favour on me. But, before I say more, let me entreat you to use your great influence that this worthy Sub-Lieutenant do not suffer through his kindness to me: he is still faithful to your service.”

“It is known that he is so,” said Hezekiah, drily; “nevertheless he is discharged from his present office, to command a company of volunteers in the field; but he hath a hundred pieces given him for refusing to surrender his trust to those traitors of the plot. Now for thine own case: he hath well said that thy place will be empty on the morrow, for thou wilt be free.
