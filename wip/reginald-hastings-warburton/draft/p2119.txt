As we left our host’s superb mansion by the unpretending entrance, two tall strong men, with swords by their sides, and staves in their hands, stepped from the house, and followed their master at a respectful distance.

It will readily be believed that I did not stop at my inn. We proceeded to Westminster without interruption; all the noise, and strife, and struggle of the day was hushed. The silence of a crowded city is very impressive, but most of all when we are approaching the chambers of death: it seems to be no place to die in.
