I grasped eagerly at his offer, and stifled the regret I felt at leaving my pleasant evenings with Marguerite. I had long desired, however, to visit the snowy mountains that he spoke of, and hunting was still a passion with me sufficiently strong to have survived all my misfortunes. We parted, therefore, agreeing to cross the Lake on the following morning; and, during our long conversation, the name of poor Marguerite had never been once mentioned.
