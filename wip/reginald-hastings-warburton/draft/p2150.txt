“I can’t afford to be taken,” he added, with a quiet smile.

“Good!” was Bryan’s only rejoinder, expressed with hearty satisfaction.

They now listened anxiously, straining their eyes along the faintly star-lit stream, and at length they heard the sound of oars; a dark object loomed between one of the arches of the bridge, and then the expected barge was alongside. From the bow of the galliot, Bryan watched the embarkation with a beating heart: there were six female figures and <i>two</i> men.
