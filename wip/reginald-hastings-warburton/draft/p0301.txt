The Puritan had evidently been carried away by his feelings beyond the usual boundaries of self-command. It was evident, at all events, that such interference was an unusual assumption on his part, for Zillah gazed on him with a mixture of surprise and proud displeasure. But her nature was too commanding not to be able to command itself—the more so when most stirred.

“Sir,” said she, with a calm, quiet air, that was in itself the best reply to Hezekiah’s intemperate address; “Sir, there is here no matter for such discourse; I had intended to seek counsel from you concerning my sister, who is treated as a prisoner by our own authorities.
