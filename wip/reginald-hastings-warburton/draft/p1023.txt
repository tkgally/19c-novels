T<sc>he</sc> second morning of Hugo’s captivity passed slowly and without event, until about the hour of noon. Then his door was suddenly thrown open, and without any announcement Hezekiah Doom strode into the chamber; darkening the grated little window’s feeble light with his tall form, and for some minutes taking no notice of Hugo, who had risen instinctively to receive his visitor.
