[[cont]]Judges, but I had been self-condemned before—by my own heart. I offered the right hand that had committed the dark deed, as a free-will offering; but the stern Justice, that stood by English law, forbade it. My life, and nothing but my life, would formal justice have; and she had it not. The people were with me; my guards were with me; the very executioner and the wretches who were to share my fate—all hated Buckingham. The felon about to close his eyes on England by England’s laws, still hated the minion Buckingham.
