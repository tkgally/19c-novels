“At length I entered the house in order to break my fast, and had scarcely done so when I was disturbed by the sound of armed men. Hastening to the window, I recognized with joy and pride, some hundreds of our young yeomen, each as well mounted and equipped as those that followed me hence a year ago, and the whole far better disciplined. I was so moved at this unexpected proof of loyalty and devotion to our cause, that my voice faltered when I attempted to address them. But it mattered not, for gallant cheers and well-blown trumpets would have drowned the voice of Stentor.
