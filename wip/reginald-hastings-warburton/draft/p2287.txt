[[cont]]crop. I have turned into something stranger still—a man of business! Yes! I have been up in the North, trying to disinherit myself, and, thank Heaven, I have succeeded. I got a ‘pass’ into Lincolnshire; and by using half as much ingenuity to be honest as that d——d Hezekiah did to serve his master the devil, I have succeeded. I have proved that humbugging deed to be a forgery—and so you’re welcome, my Lord, to your own home whenever you please to return and can get possession of it.
