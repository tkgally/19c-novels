I did not even look at you; I saw only Kate d’Aubigny’s look of triumph as you came. I soon felt that you had passed her by, for I saw a cold, dark shadow cross her face, and the next moment our hands had met. I could have thrown myself on my knees and wept for very thankfulness.

“Just then Lord Digby entered. His eyes sought in Kate d’Aubigny’s countenance and mine the history of the last few minutes. He was the last person I should have wished to read my feelings. I received him as usual, even when he ostentatiously claimed my confidence and detained me in the window whilst he explained why he could not, for the moment, procure for me my father’s papers.
