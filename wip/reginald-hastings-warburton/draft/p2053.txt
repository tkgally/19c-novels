With some difficulty I discovered the appointed house and procured a reception for my horse, but Jan Guelders was out, and his household sorely affrighted at his absence.

I could not restrain my impatience, and was about to sally forth in the direction of the late tumult, when Jan entered hastily; he was deadly pale; he passed me without notice, and flinging himself into a chair by the kitchen fire, he looked round as if he wanted something. His wife quickly and without even speaking, poured out a large glassful of some clear liquor from a certain jar, and then stood with folded arms, waiting to hear his news.
