“Stick to the old one, then, my dear kinsman, for I’m the most miserable man on earth. Read that,” he continued, presenting me with a rosy-scented little note; “you may spell it over, if you like, for you never saw the hand-writing before. Just as I was mounting I received it, in reply to a world of serenades, and love-tokens, and whispered compliments, not to mention a dozen of gallons or so of sack consumed in drinking to her health. That Oxford is a ruinous place, doubtless, to one’s heart; what between beauty and wine, and squabbles and jealousies, one’s mind is always in a diseased state, ready to receive every tender infection.
