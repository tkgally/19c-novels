He was a man of prompt decision, and he had at once made every preparation for the sacrifice of all his household happiness.

The tide was to turn soon after midnigh and already a swift barge lay at the river’s side, prepared to bear away everything that made life dear to him. He could not leave his post.

This was the subject of Zillah’s story, which she ended with a kind farewell.
