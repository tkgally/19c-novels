“And behold the consequence!” cried Zillah, clasping her hands in dread, but still clinging to her argument.

As she spoke, I saw a tall ship unmanageably flying before the wind, her torn sails streamed wildly in the blast; and then, soon after, she became visible above the waves, she struck upon the sands. The wind, blowing right on shore brought to our ears the hoarse commands of the stout captain, the shrieks of women, the confused and struggling sounds of hardy men struggling for their lives. Two boats were hastily lowered, filled with living beings, and almost instantly overwhelmed. Still the waves ran higher, and seemed more ravenous for their prey as the good ship stood at bay, and bravely, for awhile, resisted every wave.
