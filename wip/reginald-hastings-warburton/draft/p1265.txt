So ended Bryan’s story. I then told him everything relating to my altered circumstances, and concluded by informing him that for good or ill, I was determined to revisit that which was once my home. Bryan declared that such was his best wish, too, and that he never would return to Oxford until I did; come weal or woe.

“Nothing is easier,” he exclaimed with animation “than to get home, at all events. I’ve got the knack of the people’s ear now, with this old harp and some psalmody that’s growing almost as dear to me as to them, only I’m obliged to put it a little through my nose when I sing the words.
