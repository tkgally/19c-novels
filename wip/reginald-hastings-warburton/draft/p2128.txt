He had none of that dangerous enthusiasm, which, if it sometimes wins, far more often loses, woman, because she has always more of it than we have. On his finely-cut formed mouth, sate a supercilious expression, that varied only from mockery to sarcasm as his humour changed. He was still comparatively young, but he had anticipated much of the experience and self-possession of age; though not its caution,—for reckless, scornful courage looked out from his large grey eyes.
