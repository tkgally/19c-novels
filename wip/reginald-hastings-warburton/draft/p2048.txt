At Brielle there are many English, (it was once your own town), and you will be safe. There you can wait for our friend, who, I presume, will not be long after you. And here, lad, take this trifle from a brother soldier; you’ll want it amongst our sailors, and you’ll yet repay me with interest, I doubt not.”

Bryan was on the generous Dutchman’s horse in a moment, but he put aside the proffered purse.
