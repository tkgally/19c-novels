It was very touching to hear the mournful accents of this boy-soldier as he spoke of his past life—pure and innocent though it had been. Indeed he might have stood for the picture of one of the angel warriors in the old Christian stories, as he leant pensively on his sword, his long auburn hair curling down his cuirass, and his tearful blue eyes fixed steadily on heaven, as if there lay his only hope.
