She soon found out that I was an Englishman, and she immediately applied the active powers of her intelligence to learn everything relating to my hapless country that her narrow means of information afforded. She knew that we had been at war, and she immediately concluded that I was a great warrior, and a defeated one. She knew that our King had been slaughtered, and she concluded that I must have been his friend. This conviction seemed, strange to say, to give her pleasure; for her quick woman’s instinct had probably apprehended that my deep forlorn sorrow was owing to some more tender cause.
