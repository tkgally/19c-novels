My gaoler might have gone on much longer to such a ready listener as he found in me, but that he was alarmed by the sound of running steps along the gallery, and a hasty knocking at the door. A man-at-arms had been sent to desire him to bring me with all speed before this redoubtable Colonel, and I followed him with some interest to the well-remembered hall.
