“Meanwhile I fought with the strength, because I had the pride, of a dozen men in me; and with the corner, and my sword broken short, I had greatly the advantage of their long pikes. But my heart failed me when I heard a foot behind me, and I was thrust aside, and there was Sir Hugo, as he had been for the last hour, breast to breast with the pikemen. Just then a loud voice shouted:
