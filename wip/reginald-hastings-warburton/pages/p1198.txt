No sooner had he withdrawn than I was on my knees, eagerly striving to raise the hearth-stone, my portal of escape. At length I succeeded, by means of a small piece of iron that had been left to stir the fire. As I anticipated, a very rugged, but practicable passage then discovered itself. Descending cautiously and holding my cresset lamp steadily, I could not but admire the patience of the poor prisoner who had made this passage; he must have each day carried back with him so much of the earth and rubbish he had excavated as could be disposed of without notice in his fire-place, or elsewhere, until removed away by his [[corr goaler|gaoler]].

After a few yards I found my way open out into a sort of dry well; and I found I could reach the bottom with my feet.
