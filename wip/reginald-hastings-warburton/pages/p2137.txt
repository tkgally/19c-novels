As his character thus appeared to change, he seemed daily to lose ground with the political leaders; or, perhaps, they had become so strong as no longer to recognize his assistance. He followed his ministration, however, with accustomed zeal, and was still enthusiastically heard in the pulpit; though latterly he had never attempted to resume his functions as chaplain to Sir Janus.
