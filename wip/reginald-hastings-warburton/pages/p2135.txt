We had scarcely seated ourselves in the withdrawing-room, when a gentle knock at the opposite door was heard, and the old man rose at the signal. After many good wishes for our journey and our cause, he walked towards the door; it opened, and for a moment Fay’s form was visible, but she evidently thought that Goring was in the room, and closed the door suddenly behind her.

We had not been long alone, when our hostess entered and beckoned me to follow her. I was soon in Zillah’s presence. Phœbe too, was there, anxious and wistful; they were both dressed as for a journey. In answer to my eager questions, Zillah replied:
