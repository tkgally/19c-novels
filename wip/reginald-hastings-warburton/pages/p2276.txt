Indeed, I should have done so, but that he at length proposed to me to return to England—and with him! I at once retired from the <i>grille</i>, and was somewhat disturbed to find that the abbess had been a listener to our conversation, and she was an Englishwoman. From that day forth she persecuted me with her attentions, her arguments, and her controversy. I attempted to take refuge with poor Phœbe; but at length I was forbidden to see her; she was under some ‘soul-saving discipline,’ as they expressed it.
