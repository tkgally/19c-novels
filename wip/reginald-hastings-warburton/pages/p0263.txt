[[cont]]my brother met this morning in your sister’s presence. Lord Digby then received so marked a preference that Hugo retired in sorrow and despair. I feared the consequences for him, procured his immediate dispatch upon military business, and during his absence became involved in a quarrel with one whom I must now call his rival. As you will only receive this letter in case that quarrel should have a fatal result, I thus write to you without reserve.

“Your own judgment will dictate to you what is best to be done with respect to your sister. If I should fall, Digby will probably have to fly; but the King is more than merciful to those whom he loves, and doubtless this dangerous courtier will soon be recalled. If your sister remain at Oxford, he will then be more dangerous than ever to her peace, as she will have suffered anxiety and pain on his account.
