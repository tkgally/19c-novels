“‘He’s been here—he’s been here,’ he cried, ‘and I can tell thee all about it. Oh! but the preceptor’s a wonderful man, and what a loss it is to be parted from him! Well, thou had’st scarce turned the corner when I felt the room creep, and I knew that the preceptor was within the walls, and I stole out, and hid myself in the closet behind the faggots; and I heard him say to Sir Janus that he was astonished at his running away like a guilty man, when the Parliament was ready to receive him with open arms, and to accept a loan upon his own terms, and to make a Lord of him, and to take off the sequestration; “for,” said he, “thou hast not a rood of land at this moment to call thine own.
