They had scarcely proceeded a hundred yards, when a breeze took the sail aback, and almost laid the boat on her beam-ends; she began to fill, and cries of terror from the shore echoed back screams of distress from the little party on the lake.

Throwing off my coat, I plunged in and swam towards the boat: in a few minutes I found myself alongside, and scrambled aboard, putting down the helm, I soon brought her head to wind, making the young men bale out the water with their caps, while I trimmed the sail, and got sufficient way upon the boat to enable me to tack; then putting her about, I ran for shore, and soon had the satisfaction to see the frightened girls restored to the arms of their friends,
