[[cont]]her present struggle continues. Our Queen is very kind to us, and often sends us news from England. She lives in great poverty though the royal family of France are as generous as they can afford to be, since their own supplies are stopped. I have had the happiness of contributing to her necessities, and it is almost the only pleasure I have enjoyed of late years.

“I do not presume to murmur at the sorrows and anxieties that have so long been my lot. I would not abate one of them, in the belief that they are but another form of mercy. If it be only through darkness and suffering that our path may lead into light at last, I would not that my fate—or yours, were ever brightened by one earthly joy.”
