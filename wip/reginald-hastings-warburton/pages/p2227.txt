The mysterious ways of Providence are justified; the darkness leadeth into light. Poor blinded worm that I was, I can see that I have wronged thee; but verily thou art avenged!”

He ceased to speak, and a few minutes afterwards, Arnold re-entered the hut with the village pastor; the latter a fine hale old man, carrying sundry vials in his scrip, and attended by four stout villagers with a rude sort of litter. With skilful and gentle hands, the reverend mountaineer removed the sufferer’s garments, one by one, and cautiously examined his bruised body.
