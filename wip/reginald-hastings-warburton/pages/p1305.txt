I was deeply gratified to observe that Zillah now avoided as much as possible the Puritanical form of speech, which used to be as it were another language;—a Babel by which Hezekiah had contrived to confound our once happy intercourse.

“Thank Heaven!” I exclaimed, “amongst all we leave behind us, that dark-browed fanatic is also left, upon the soil he has so ably assisted to distract.”
