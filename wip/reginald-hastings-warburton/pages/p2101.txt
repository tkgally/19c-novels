This accomplished person now returned me the letter I had sent to him, and asked if I desired to have the money at once. I replied that I had not yet earned it, but would bring him a deed on the following day to witness, and to keep for my cousin. He then pleaded pressing business, and said he should feel highly honoured if, with my friend, I would dine with him on the following day. I accepted the invitation with pleasure, and apologised for detaining him while I asked if he knew where Sir Janus Demiroy was living, and whether I could safely obtain access to him.
