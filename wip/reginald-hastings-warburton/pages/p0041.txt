At first my father’s face flushed with bitter, if not angry disappointment; but he soon read something in Hugo’s look that changed his mood. “I see it all, my generous boy,” he whispered, as he pressed the young scholar’s hand; “I see it all, and I thank you from my soul for the decision that you made; one such victory over yourself is worth a thousand over others.” Hugo returned the pressure of his father’s hand, but, from that time forth, no word or sign escaped him that could tarnish his young rival’s triumph.
