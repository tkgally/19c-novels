A grove of willows now rose upon our view, and thither the stag struggled, evidently with failing heart and foot; he now moved close along the ground over which he first had seemed to soar; his bright red skin was blackened with sweat, and his wide-spread antlers rested almost on his neck; and now he has reached his shelter, and is lost to our view. But the hounds sweep on unweariedly; they never raise their heads, or trust themselves to look upon their game, but with noses close to the ground, they scour along—not wildly fast, but inevitable; now they, too, disappear among the willows, and their cry grows louder and more angry.
