He saluted me very courteously, regretted my long imprisonment, hoped that it was now nearly terminated, and late as it was, (near eight o’clock) that I would join his family at supper and evening prayer. I accepted his proposal with as much pleasure as if I had not heard before of my improved fortunes, and descended the stone stairs with a sensation of delight.

In the circle collected round the high fireplace in the hall, I perceived Zillah and the governor’s wife, whom I had seen in the chapel some two hours before. To the latter, I was formally presented, but my long acquaintance with the former was well known to these cousins of whom I had often heard.
