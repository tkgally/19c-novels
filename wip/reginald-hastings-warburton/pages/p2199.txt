From that day forward life has had a new interest for me. Whenever the labours of the day are done, I am sure to find the soft-eyed Marguerite on the walk by the waterside, and she does not dissemble the innocent pleasure with which she greets my punctual appearance there. My cottage looks lonely when I return to it, and though I guard myself carefully from betraying any such thoughts to her, I sometimes think that in her pure and artless love and sympathy, and in a life and death of obscurity amongst the mountains, I might perchance find repose for my wounded spirit, and as much happiness as my heart is yet capable of.
