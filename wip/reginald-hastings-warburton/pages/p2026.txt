He sat up, and demanded what hour it was; and when informed, he sprang off the bed, flung himself upon the floor, and grovelled there with all the symptoms of despair.

Bryan was absolutely touched by his grief, and even endeavoured to comfort him; he tried to raise him up, and that failing, he sat down on the rush-strewn floor beside him; he offered him his can of wine: the dwarf shrank from it with horror. Finally, as much to make an honourable retreat as in any hope of soothing sorrow, he took up his harp, and began to play one of his sweet mournful native melodies.
