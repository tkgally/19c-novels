He was pale and languid; the crimson scarf that he usually wore in battle as his badge, now supported his wounded arm as a sling, lending a strong contrast to his white dress and pallid skin.

He welcomed me with a smile, and spoke of the scene acted in the King’s presence, as if it had been some masque in which we had played a part together. When I attempted to thank him for his opportune appearance, he stopped me, and declared that it was Bryan who deserved all the credit of it.
