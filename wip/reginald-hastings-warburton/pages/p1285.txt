Phœbe had now started to her feet, and was likewise listening, with all the feverish eagerness of long-suppressed and returning hope.

“To me,” she said, “there is nothing doleful—but absolute cheer in those wild strains. Ah! Zillah, do you not recognize them now?”

Zillah replied by opening the casement. The music ceased, but the rustling of boughs was heard beneath the window, and soon the active form of Bryan was seen clinging
