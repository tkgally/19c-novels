No one could so well adapt himself to all men’s tastes: he was master of every mood, and acquainted with the vagaries of every passion. It was especially in a scene like the present that the versatility and exquisite pliancy of his talents was displayed: by some well applied word, or look, or smile, he had already established a certain intimacy with every man at table, old or young, gay or grave, grim veteran or smooth-faced youth, chivalrous aspirant or sensual debauchee.

As soon as his eye caught mine, he exclaimed, “A place! a place! for our new recruit!
