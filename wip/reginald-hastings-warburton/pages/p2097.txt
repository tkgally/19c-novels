After this little adventure, we proceeded without further interruption, and by aid of a link-boy, at last reached the dwelling of Master Crisp.

We were directed to a narrow entrance opening into a court-yard, where we found a porter. By him we were ushered into a small plain, oak-panelled apartment, in which a fine fire blazed and roared cheerily.
