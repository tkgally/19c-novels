Bryan and I looked at each other in at least equal surprise; but before we had time to make any observation, our host entered.

He was a noble-looking fellow, tall in person, frank and open in countenance, with eyes like those of an eagle, and a genial, fearless manner, that [[corr inspire|inspired]] me at once with esteem and confidence. He bade me heartily welcome, spoke very complimentarily of the knowledge he already possessed of me through public report, and then desired to know if there was any manner in which he could serve me.
