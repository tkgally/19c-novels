On one such evening, I had not long taken up my favourite position, when I observed some of the village youths and maidens entering a small sailing-boat, and endeavouring to persuade a timid girl to enter too. It was the daughter of the widowed landlady of the little inn, the <i>belle</i> of the surrounding country; and I was greatly struck by the natural and unaffected but exquisite grace of her appearance.
