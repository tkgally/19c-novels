# Chapter XIV.

<verse>
Triumph and art ride sparkling in her eyes,
Mistrusting what they look on—and her wit
Values itself so highly, that to her
All matter else seems weak. She cannot love,
Nor take no shape nor prospect of affection.
She is so self-endeared.
</verse>
