I confess I was not sorry that my advances were thus met, but I hastened to Digby, to do what I could in favour of the prisoners. I found that he was gone to the castle, where the Royal Council was sitting. I repaired thither, and on being announced, was requested to enter the large old hall wherein they were assembled.
