“Well, what says the imp of darkness? will Van Beest receive us, and has he found the old Judas?”

“The poor dwarf is not here,” was the calm reply; “I fear me the rude boers have injured him.”

“I tell thee what, John,” returned the unknown with angry impatience, “thou hast fooled me, not to say thyself, by trusting to this abortion of humanity. S’death, man, could’st thou not have trusted me with thine errand?”
