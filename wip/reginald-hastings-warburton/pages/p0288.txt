[[cont]]scending to the court-yard after my trial, I was noisily welcomed and congratulated by my cousin Hotspur, and many of the wilder Cavaliers; while Carnarvon, Southampton, and others, with whom I more associated, also met me with kind greetings. Thus, in a sort of triumph, I proceeded down the High Street to my lodgings; the Prince still leaning on my arm, and giving me instructions for my expedition to London.

Finally, he desired me to mark the rebel quarters well: “As soon as you return,” said he; “I shall beat them up, and show Essex that his strategy is no match for the ‘Mad Prince,’ as his knaves are pleased to call me. And, hark ye, I have settled with the King that your fair Phœbe is to accompany you, or rather Falkland has arranged that she is to accompany Kate d’Aubigny. This is no place for girls of that sort, and we have ascertained that her old double-tongued father is now at Reading, carrying on this business of Waller’s, so that she will be at once at home there.”

I thanked the Prince warmly for his intelligence
