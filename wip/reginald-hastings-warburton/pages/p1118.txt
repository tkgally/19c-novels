# Chapter VIII.

<verse>
What joy can human things to us afford,
When we see perish thus, by sad events,
Ill men and bloody accidents,
The best cause, and best man that ever drew a sword.
</verse>
[[right]] <sc>cowley.</sc>

O<sc>n</sc> the evening of that day we lay at [[corr Newburg|Newbury]]; the enemy were strongly posted on an adjoining heath. At night Falkland proposed to me to visit the outposts with him, and as we had been lately somewhat estranged, I accepted his invitation with pleasure. His reserved and almost mournful temper was suited well with mine, and for some time we walked along in contented silence.
