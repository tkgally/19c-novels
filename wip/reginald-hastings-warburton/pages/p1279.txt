An indescribable, harmonious grace surrounds her, from the rich dark hair bound round her marble brows, to the hem of the dark robe that swept the inlaid floor. Her cheeks were wondrous pale, and the light of her large lustrous eyes with the shadow of their long drooping lashes, increased the spiritual character of the countenance. But then there was a roseate mouth and dimpled chin that were sweetly mortal and which, though saddened now, could surely bring forth delighting and delighted smiles for sunny hours.
