Thy hand is hardened by honest work, and thy brow is darkened by the sweat of noble labour. Yea, thou hast wrestled bravely with the angel of thy fate, and won from Him his blessing.—For me, who hated thee, and triumphed over thee, I have no such retrospect, as thou shalt learn. Why should I not tell thee all? What have I to seek shelter in but humiliation and self-abasement, and what sterner penance can I perform than to make what the Papists call my shrift, to thee?
