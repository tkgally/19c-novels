He seemed to have put off the merchant with his travelling-dress, and now, in his hunter’s simple garb, with an eagle’s eye, and a mountaineer’s bold bearing, he looked as unlike the man of cities as possible. His only weapons were a carbine and a hunting-knife, but he carried a long pole, shod with iron, to assist him over the glaciers. A case-bottle of spirits, and a sort of satchel slung crosswise over his shoulders, completed his equipment.

He addressed me very courteously, and welcomed me to a new sport, with which he appeared to be perfectly familiar.
