[[cont]]clash of swords. I tried to collect myself—to take in the position of the battle, and lead my scanty troops where they were most needed, but in vain; my brain reeled, and it was only by a glimmering instinct that I led—still at a furious gallop—my willing men against the first body of orange scarfs that I could reach. My troops cheered bravely as we dashed in among the enemy, and I can remember no more.
