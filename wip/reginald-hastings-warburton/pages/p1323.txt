“Be it so,” said Rabshekah, condescendingly; “I love not the company of wine-bibbers or riotous eaters of flesh, in ordinary; nevertheless, as good company may perchance profit thy unhappy and malignant soul, I will even accompany you; yea, and look patiently upon the wine though it be red within the cup.”

In a short time this singular pair were seated by a table, engaged in confidential conversation; Bryan’s face, and merry but keen eyes, sobered down into not altogether mock interest in his companion’s discourse. But ever and anon he passed the sparkling wine across to him, and pledged his health often, to stimulate his growing conviviality.
