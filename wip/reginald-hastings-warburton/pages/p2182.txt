Zillah!—to think that thy name, so long associated with purity and nobleness, should be now a word that I fear to hear spoken by the scoffers against womankind! To think, that whilst I was struggling, through toil, and suffering, and danger, to preserve that honour of which you were ever the guiding star; to believe that even then, you were sinking beneath the arts of a thrice-proven villain! And <i>now</i>, there was no excuse; your father was sheltered in his happy grave from the false Puritan’s power, and you were left free—too free!
