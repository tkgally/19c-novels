But he has so confounded his politics with his faith, and is so far a fanatic in both, that he is constantly outraging his own naturally noble character. Added to all these, there may be another influence that we will not speak of now, of which he is himself perhaps unconscious, but which yet farther unsettles his disturbed faculties, and tends to blind his true perception of good and evil.
