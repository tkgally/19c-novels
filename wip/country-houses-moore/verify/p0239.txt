“Oh, for heaven’s sake, tell me who has got them!”

“Fitz Gibbon, Lord Tintern gave them to him, that he might make a novel out of them.”

“Do, thou best of men, go and ask him for them that I may look at them.”

“That I am sorry to say is quite impossible; he is, as you see, busy, writing in a corner, beyond all noise, and I know he is altering his prologue, and poets will not be disturbed, and indeed I do not think he will relinquish his prize for a moment, though he may perhaps show you the miniatures.”

“I shall never rest till I see these letters, I do think I could make a novel out of them as well, if not better, than he can.”

“I have no doubt, if you chose it, you could make one without any such assistance.”

“Do you really think so? I have had a great mind to do so, and bring in all my acquaintance, and tell their adventures, and all their little private histories.
