“Well, Frederic, what is your worship’s pleasure? you don’t seem to have any inclination to consult mine.”

“Upon second thoughts,” said Frederic, “a day’s hunting is not worth coming from Cambridge for, I’ll give you a <i>week</i> at Melton, standing all expenses, if you will give into my charge the <i>model</i> you have got to send to Miss Grey’s little cousins.”

“You bribe high, Frederic, but before I am tempted to commit a <i>breach of trust</i>, let me know what you mean to do with this same model.”

‘Why simply <i>exchange</i> it, and you know exchange is no robbery, I will send the little boy the most beautiful toy in Laurie’s shop. ‘A coach and six if you please. Only let <i>me</i> have the model.”

“Well! Well! I’ll consider of it, and let you know in the morning.”

“That will not do, I must go out of town very early in the morning.”

“Where?”

‘To Melton, I believe.”
