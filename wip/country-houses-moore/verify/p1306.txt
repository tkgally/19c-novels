“I don’t think, Frederic, you can be aware of the importance—the necessity—of your appearance at Carlisle on an occasion when so large a part of the county will be assembled, and where you must be introduced as intending to offer yourself a candidate for the county, when a general election takes place.”

“I had hoped, my dear father, that I had fully explained myself in my letter from Milan, and that you would have entirely given up all idea of proposing me to represent the county; a situation I never can bring my mind to hold. I detest the House of Commons.”

“Detesting Parliament in a hereditary member, is rather unfortunate, as you <i>must</i> one day take your seat in the House of Lords.”
