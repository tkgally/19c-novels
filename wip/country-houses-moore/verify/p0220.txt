“I am sure,” said Emma, with a sigh, “I wish he had any body better to bestow his attentions on, for I am very unworthy.”

“Oh, my dear, don’t make a sorrow of what most young women in the world of your age would think a great source of amusement, quite a good joke; and though I would not suspect him of infidelity to you, yet, as the house fills, which it will begin to do to-day, he will have more subjects for his flattery, and you may escape what you seem to consider a persecution—I won’t promise you so much for your other admirer, Lord John, he is such a determined swain, he never can exist without being desperately in love, or fancying himself so—but you must not mind him either, for he means nothing but conquest; that is his passion.
