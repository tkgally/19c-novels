# Chapter VI.

W<sc>hen</sc> seated the next day at her breakfast table, Lady Honiton sent a servant to enquire after Miss O’Brian; the answer was returned by the lady in person.

“Well, Juliet, how are you?” all exclaimed at the same moment.

“You must ask my shoes how they do, for they are the greatest sufferers, and they were quite a new pair.”

“Oh, never mind your shoes,” cried Lady Louisa, “we will all subscribe to give you others.”
