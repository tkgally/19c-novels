# Chapter IV.

A<sc>n</sc> accession to the <i>corps dramatique</i>, as well as the party, was expected at dinner—but came not! Young men are never to be depended on—punctuality is not in their catalogue of virtues. But, as he handed round the tea, the groom of the chambers said, “Lord John Leslie and Mr. Duprée are arrived my Lord—they are dressing, and would be down immediately.”

They accounted for their non-arrival in time for dinner—they had been upset, killed a post horse, and almost a post boy, and gave a most ridiculous description of their disaster—just one of the sort of things young men are always meeting with!
