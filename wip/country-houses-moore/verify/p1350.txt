Lady Honiton, whose ideas, when her own family were concerned, took the superlative degree, immediately thought of all the splendour she had heard Colonel Blackburn describe as belonging to the lady of the Governor-General of India, and her considerate lord did not undeceive her.

At dinner, Miss O’Brian said, “I am sure, Lord Ottery, Dixon need not have prescribed abstinence to you; I wish I could fast as you do, and yet you get no credit for it.”

Lord Ottery coloured and laughed, but it was forced.

Apropos to nothing, said Lady Honiton, “I am glad to tell you, Juliet O’Brian, that General Clayton will be with us in a fortnight.”
