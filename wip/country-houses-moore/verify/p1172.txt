What a transition from the Opera and Almacks, to dock yards and arsenals! but novelty has charms! The invitation was accepted; and the intervening day spent in practising archery according to Mr. Dallas’ instruction; and no one enjoyed the amusement more than the little boy, who ran to fetch the discharged arrows.

Lady Louisa made some pretty sketches of shipping, to be finished when she returned to Inglewood. The launch was magnificent, and the breakfast on board the Heckla very pleasant; these Lady Honiton met another old acquaintance in the Governor of Greenwich Hospital, he too was anxious to shew his lions, and Lady Honiton and her daughters proposed to go next day to attend the service at the Hospital Chapel, and see the establishment afterwards; in the evening they returned to town, leaving Lady Caroline better for society and exertion.
