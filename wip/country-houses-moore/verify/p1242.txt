# Chapter XI.

H<sc>ow</sc> little does the careless guard of a mail coach know or feel that his horn is “the herald of a noisy world,” and that the box on which he frequently dozes contains “news from all nations,” whilst he, “light-hearted wretch, cold, yet cheerful messenger of grief perhaps to thousands, and of joy to some, to him indifferent whether grief or joy.”
