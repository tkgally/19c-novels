“Hold! hold! Gordon,” cried Mr. Blake, “you are over-doing it now, I am not quite so bad as you paint me.”

“Well then,” said Mr. Gordon “if I have outstripped the mark, take care to convince us I have done so, and I’ll retract any day with the greatest pleasure, but let us enquire for what those at the other end of the boat are sending forth such <i>grand éclats de rire</i>.”
