We have been assured by a daughter of one of the first commoners in the kingdom, whose large mansion is distinguished for its elegance and hospitality, that being questioned at one of these seminaries, by her school-fellows of how many staircases there were at H****? answered <i>thirteen</i>, and for ever afterwards she was called <i>Miss Staircase</i>, and when called on to name the number of her father’s servants, having recourse to her fingers, to reckon them by their employments, enumerated a <i>brewer</i>, a very necessary person in a large country establishment; whenever she returned to school after the holidays, the first question asked her was, How does the <i>brewer</i> do?—and this is <i>fashionable</i> and refined <sc>education</sc>!!!

The variety of new feelings that crowded on Emma’s mind, when she shut her door at night, induced her to throw herself into a chair, to think over them.
