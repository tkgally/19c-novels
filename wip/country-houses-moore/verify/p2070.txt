# The Conclusion.

G<sc>entle</sc> R<sc>eader</sc>,

I<sc>f</sc> thou hast had patience with us so far, “bear with us a little longer.” In olden times a fable with a <i>moral</i> was the mode of instruction. In Holy writ a <i>parable</i>, according to the Eastern usage. We live in a different era; but a novel or a tale, however slight, without a <i>moral</i>, is time wasted to its readers.

Allow us an epilogue to explain the moral of these trifling pages.
