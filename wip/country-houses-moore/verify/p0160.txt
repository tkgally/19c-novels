But to avoid Scylla, he nearly split on Charibdis, by an imprudent marriage. But a sincere attachment carried them through much more than establishment-hunting young ladies have any idea of being within the scope of possibility. His young bride cheerfully surmounted all the difficulties attending his first diplomatic missions—had children born at various courts; but, happy in each other, years glided quickly on, till a godfather of Mr. Silvester’s, a capricious old man, died, and left him a considerable fortune, on condition that he purchased Tintern abbey, for which he had some unaccountable predilection.
