This extraordinary disease, unlike the plague, or small-pox, chiefly affects the <i>imagination</i>, its severest suffering is from the apprehension of its attack. In short, <i>to be bored</i> is so very dreadful, there is nothing one can encounter, <i>short of hanging</i>, that is not preferable.

That Frederic was a good deal under the influence of this baneful disease the reader need not be told, but he could not make it out, and fancied he was the most ill-used, devoted person in the world, because he was always <i>bored</i>.
