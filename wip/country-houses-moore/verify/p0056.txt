# Chapter III.

[[subtitle]] Christmas Day.

W<sc>hatever</sc> may be said of the balmy air of Italy, or the exhilirating climate of Paris, there is nothing so suited to the English constitution as a bright, clear, frosty day; nor any thing that heightens the native complexion of Englishwomen to so brilliant a point as a walk when the ground is covered with snow: the contrast and reflection have some power “not dreamt of in our philosophy.”
