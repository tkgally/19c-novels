All the morning Frederic was occupied giving directions and inspecting the progress of turning the canoe into a sledge, Count Wszebor had been most obliging in making accurate drawings of every part; and his servant Plosko was of no small service; all prospered, and just at dusk Arthur returned with an ostler leading the skewbald.

He had made a capital bargain, and Frederic returned to the house, a little tired with his morning’s exertions, and yet acknowledging the time had gone very quickly. He requested Arthur to stay and inspect the last part of the work, as the workmen were new to the principle on which sledges were constructed. Whilst so doing, he overheard the following conversation between Tom Brown, Frederic’s groom, and one of the postillions.
