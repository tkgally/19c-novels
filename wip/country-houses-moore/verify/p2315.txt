It is not under such circumstances, that the deep grief, usually felt for the loss of a parent, can affect the mind; it was a great shock to her, hearing of his death without any preparation of his illness; but he had been dead some months, and she would see nothing of the paraphernalia of death, nor had she undergone any of the previous agitation of hopes and fears; it was a blow, it struck, and it stunned her, for a moment; but if she did not immediately recover her usual degree of cheerfulness, she did her tranquillity: there was a painful suspense till they heard again, as to Mr. Shirley’s affairs, and their disposition.

This came in the course of some days, he had, Mr. Graham said, divided his property between the children of his first and second marriage equally, leaving his personals to his widow, and the interest of <i>her</i> daughter’s property during her life.
