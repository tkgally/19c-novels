But Miss Briggs is not your daughter, or your niece; she is only your humble friend, or dependant. I won’t tell you the name she is called by, but I do assure you she is a stumbling stone in your way. Why can’t you get Crayon to marry her? he is a widower, with two small children, and very small earnings; give her an annuity, and he will be too glad to take her off your hands.”

“But I cannot do without her, she is my right hand, and my left, too.”

“Why cannot your niece do all she does; write your notes, cast up your house-keeper’s and steward’s books?”

“Simply because she does not know how; and moreover, because she is going to India with the Autumnal fleet, to her father.”
