Oh, how heartless, how selfish, is the system of the present day! how calculated to destroy the best feelings, the dearest and most valuable affections of the human heart! those from which all the social duties spring; all the tender links that bear us through the severe and painful struggles of domestic duties; those feelings which make a beloved object dearer than life itself!

Under this system, this refined code of honour, or rather dishonour, men allow themselves to go every length in <i>gaining</i> a heart they mean to <i>desert</i> the moment they have gained it: the pursuit is the pleasure; the difficulty makes the zest; the unfortunate young woman is left to break her heart, and her false lover gains the distinguishing title of a lady-killer.

“It was not so when we were young.

<verse>
‘Love then was honest genuine passion,
And manly gallantry the fashion.’
</verse>
