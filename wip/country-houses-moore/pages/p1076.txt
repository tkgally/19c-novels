[[cont]]Lord Honiton. He certainly <i>did</i> wish his two eldest daughters well married; for he heard so much of it, he was quite tired of the subject. But Lady Clara was his favourite, and he hoped to keep her a little longer as his riding and walking companion; in truth he never liked to look to the period when he might lose her as such, and so long as no matrimonial offer was agitated for <i>her</i>, he the more readily gave in to those for her sisters; and, like the larks in the fable, he felt himself safe, till Lady Adelaide and Lady Louisa were disposed of.
