“You are quite right, Lady Clara,” said Mr. Gordon, “I wish you could laugh Blake out of his misanthropy, do try.”

“By the bye, Mr. Blake,” said Lady Clara, “suppose you were to give us the history of this wretched life of your’s, I am sure it would be most tender and pathetic; then, perhaps, I might cry outright, and this is just the party for such a narrative; come, begin, do, there’s a good creature, we want something deeply interesting.”

Mr. Blake shrugged his refusal.

“Well then,” said Mr. Gordon, “if Blake will not tell his tale, and be the hero of it, I will for him.
