But Louisa declared she never preached, she only taught.

“But she must occasionally <i>lecture</i>, that was nearly the same thing.”

“Very seldom, and perhaps to very little purpose,” she replied.

“Then your auditors must have leathern ears, and cold hearts.”

“Oh! too much must not be expected from children, especially those of the poor, whose minds are never cultivated at home.”

“Then you own,” asked Frederic, “that it is cutting blocks with a razor to attempt them;” adding “the respect and admiration he felt for those who could devote themselves to such a hopeless labour, and more especially like it.”
