Oh, how unlike the young men my father asks to his house—it will be difficult to endure <i>them</i> when I go back; there are several here that I should like to know better, Mr. Duprée particularly; but Lord John always comes in the way whenever he is going to speak to me, he must be my <i>evil genius</i>; and, come to that determination, Emma sought her pillow:—over that neither her evil genius hovered, nor under it a Cobra de Capella hid.

There is a feeling of pleasure, or rather of satisfaction, in all minds at having, on however trifling an occasion come to a decision, and more especially this is the case with the young, to whom decision is generally a new feeling. Emma had decided in her own mind that young men of fashion are infinitely superior in their manners, and possess in a much greater degree the art of pleasing, than those of a lower grade, even if the latter they have received what is called a good education; but that they are infinitely more dangerous. At this moment Emma did not venture to carry the definition farther, or the comparison to individuals, it was only the class she thought so well of.
