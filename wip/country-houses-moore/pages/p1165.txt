“Alas! Lady Honiton, my purse is too exhausted for me to add to my wardrobe.”

“Never mind, vamp up the old ones; men are no judges whether a thing is new or old; if it looks gay, you know, without flattery, that, when you look your best, you might pass for thirty.”

“But, dear Lady Honiton, there may be another, and, as I have often experienced a <i>fatal</i> bar, you know how my dear Church is reviled in the country.”

“Have no fears, the General has lived so much in Pagan countries; he is very tolerant—and pardon me, in your case, the stipulations need not extend beyond yourself; there is no doubt they will be allowed so far.”
