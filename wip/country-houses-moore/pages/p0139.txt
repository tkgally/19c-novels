“Don’t be a fool, Arthur,” replied Frederic, [[corr only|“only]] if you really wish to escape being thrown out at the window, and picked up by the watchman, tell me <i>all</i> you know of those infernal contrivances which you call <i>fun</i>, and which that <i>she Fiend</i>, Grace Latour, was at the head of?”

“Speak more respectfully of our worthy confraternity, or I will tell you nothing.”

Arthur said this with so determined an air, that Frederic relaxed his hold on his arm, and, after some silence, on Arthur shewing impatience, and resolution to go, Frederic said,

“Now, Arthur, be a good fellow, tell me all you know, and I will give you a day’s hunting at Melton, and mount you capitally.”

“Well, since you are reasonable, and generous to boot, the letter you got that morning with the Brighton post mark, was fabricated amongst <i>us</i>, and copied either by Jane Sedley, or Grace Latour, I forget which.”

“The devil it was! And how came you to imagine its extraordinary contents?”
