The European troops, and the Seypoys in British pay were carefully kept out of sight. The native Princes had assembled in Calcutta, or encamped in its neighbourhood, and the procession, taking a winding direction, appeared even longer than it really was; though it overspread several miles. It began with the lowest Rajah, Ali Cawn, I think, who is little better than a chief of smugglers and thieves; but he and his son contrived to make a very shewy, and, till others came, an imposing appearance. You, Lady Louisa, who interest yourself in Indian affairs, can have no idea how insignificant the English Presidency appeared amongst this assemblage, and I have no hesitation in saying it would not have been half as imposing, if the people had not been <i>black</i>, it contrasted with all their gilding, and their picturesque dresses.
