# Chapter VIII.

W<sc>here</sc> a man is an heriditary legislator, he may be tempted to try to make a figure in the House of Commons, before he votes by proxy in the Lords. All baronet’s sons are not in parliament, and Frederic was not, neither he, nor Sir Thomas particularly wished it—it brought with it county quarrels.

Tired of his room, Frederic took a stroll in the park, where he met Henry Sedley, returning from shooting, loaded with wild fowl.
