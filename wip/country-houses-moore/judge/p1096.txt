This supper, which, at all events, I depend upon <i>you</i> for, will make some amends to Adelaide and Louisa, for the torment of her society.”

“Doubt not my zeal, my kind friend, I will stir heaven and earth in my little way, indeed, I have no doubt of success.”

“Well, then, be a dear good creature, and answer all those notes. But, before you do so, one word on the subject of all others, at this moment nearest my heart: prepare yourself for a surprise! aye! open your eyes! and your ears to hear and believe! that Lord Honiton will not hear of Adelaide having any-thing to say to Lord St. Leonard, even if he <i>does</i> propose; he calls him an idiot, and says she wants somebody to guide and direct her; as if the girls of the present day will be guided and directed by any body! one would really think <i>my</i> lord had lived before the flood, all his ideas are so antediluvian.”

“Wonderful, to be sure! but what do you mean to do—stop in your career?”
