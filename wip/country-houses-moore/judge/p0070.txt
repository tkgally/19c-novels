Thus far, all was as could be wished; but it was very difficult to turn this tandem sledge into the stable yard, and, in doing so, it came in contact with a post, and received a sufficient shock to throw out Frederic and Arthur, but they were not hurt, and had no witnesses to the disaster, but some of the stable servants, so nothing was said about it.

Plosko, who acted as master of the ceremonies, declared nothing was wanting but a peculiar sort of plume, which he described, on the heads of the horses.
