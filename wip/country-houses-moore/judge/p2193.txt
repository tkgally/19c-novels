“I beg your pardon,” said Mrs. Beaumont, “I believe Canovo sold most of his best things to English noblemen, and could not supply their orders; and our first portrait painter went all over Europe, to paint the great men who figured in the late war, and for the King of England.”

“True! ma’am, I was thinking of my friend the Swede, but perhaps his turn is to come; have you ever been in Sweden, ma’am.”

“Never.”

“Oh, you would like it, a fine country! charming people! not so well calculated for English ladies, bad roads, bad accommodations!”

Turning short round to the window, he said, “A <i>camellia involuta</i>, I declare, are you fond of flowers? I see you are. Are there any you wish to have from the Horticultural Gardens? I belong to them, and should be too happy to get any thing, we are anxiously watching some seeds from the banks of the Swan River.”
