“And oh, my dear niece, why should you not give him that chance? you have <i>not</i>, at least I don’t know that you have, any prior engagement.”

“No, indeed, I <i>haae</i> none.”

“Then let him, at least, try to fill a place that is vacant. Is it that you have any—personal dislike to him?”

“Oh no, as a friend, as a brother, I could have the sincerest regard for him; but I cannot—I cannot—be in love with him.”

“Now,” replied Miss Shirley, “I see our ground; you do not feel that romantic preference for Mr. Graham that would make you go with him, or for him, to the extremity of the globe, and therefore you think you cannot love him sufficiently to become his wife. Believe me, Caroline, conjugal love, in its purest and happiest state, is only the most exalted degree of <i>friendship</i>.
