We hear that the Earl of G—— is soon to lead to the Hymeneal altar the beautiful and accomplished Miss H—nsh—we. Great preparations are making for these nuptials. The six bride’s-maids are to be dressed in white satin, and <i>tulle</i>, trimmed with roses. But, as making one heart <i>blessed</i>, a score will be <i>broken</i>, it has been proposed that the rejected lovers of the lady shall act as bridegroom’s men, dressed in <i>sad</i> coloured cloaks, and crape hat-bands: this doleful corps is to be headed by the son of a worthy baronet of ——shire, as chief mourner.

“Capital! capital! how sorry I am that Mr. Vernon is not here,” said Grace, “he could have filled up these blanks, for he knows the parties.”
