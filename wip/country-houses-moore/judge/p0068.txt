# Chapter IV.

T<sc>he</sc> following day was a busy one, Frederic had turned over in his mind the project of painting the horse, and though he did not wish it to be made a joke of, till it was all over, he should like to take Mr. Sedley in, who pretended to be a great judge of horses.
