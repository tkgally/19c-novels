She then ventured to look at herself in the glass; she had often as high a colour from exercise, but she did not feel her face burn so, was it that horrid <i>rouge</i>, or the consciousness of it? she did not discover it was both combined, but the sensation was so disagreeable, it made her venture to rub some of it off, and she felt the happier when she had done so, if she could have dared to rub it entirely off, she would have been <i>quite</i> happy; but she tried, as it was, to think she was obeying her father, at least.
