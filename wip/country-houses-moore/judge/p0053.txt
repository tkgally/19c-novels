Count Wszebor had belonged to other countries; he had anecdotes of Turks and Cossacks, and various parts of the extensive Russian dominions, which would have afforded ample information and amusement; but then Frederic would not have been the <i>first</i> person, and nothing else suited <i>him</i>; besides it was vulgar to talk of travels, every body travelled, as they ate <i>thin</i> bread and butter at breakfast; and to be pleased with any thing that did not administer <i>exclusively</i> to his vanity and egotism was impossible; and yet that same self-love was very much like an injured muscle, if torpid, friction would do it good, but if irritated, friction only increased the evil, the more it was rubbed the more it smarted—it wanted soothing and pampering, the two things most calculated to increase the disease.
