A most ingenious mechanist has invented a small, and almost portable, steam engine for the use of private families, upon a variety of scales according to the size of the establishment: this engine will clean plate and knives; wash glasses, lay a cloth, and even change plates, and remove dishes at dinner; in short, do the work of a butler and three footmen, who have only to look on, and occasionally draw back a slider in the wall; and this, besides saving all their labour, gives them the opportunity of listening to, and profiting by, all the table talk of their master and his guests. Its use in the laundry will be equally complete, and in future no dandy nor exquisite will wear any breast-plate that is not Gephlogistically plaited.
