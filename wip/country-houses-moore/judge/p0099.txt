It was a pretty toy, just to take a turn in the park, but there would be some difficulty in getting it safe to Brighton; and when there, after a drive along the Steyne and up the West cliff, for a shew off, he should wish to annihilate it, to drive it into the sea, but as that could not be done without his taking the leap with it, and that would not answer, nor like Phaeton’s equipage would it vanish in air or fire. No! he must drive it back again into Brighton, and Wentworth and Hawker and George Neville, and half an hundred more such fellows, would get round him the moment he stopped; and would, in a second, detect the old canoe and the <i>painted</i> horse. Brighton was not to be thought of, he had had his amusement, the only thing was to back out of the business as well as he could.
