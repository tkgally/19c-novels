As the men were sauntering about till the first bell rung, Frederic Thornton, with a clap on the shoulder, said,

“Well, Dallas, fortune favours you.”

“What do you mean?” he replied.

“I like that,” said Edward Nesbit, “as if you did not know; but, however, we will be merciful, and say nothing about your looking as pale as Miss Willoughby; there must have been some extraordinay sympathy between you, or else you have a very <i>tender heart</i>.”

“The devil take your mercy and forbearance, is no one free from your quizzing?”

“Oh, my good fellow,” returned the other, “as only ‘the galled jade winces,’ and I don’t wish to give you an opportunity of putting a bullet or two in me, I shall merely wish you joy and success, only remember you give us a little shooting at Middleden some day.”

Mr. Dallas could not stand this, and he walked away into the house.
