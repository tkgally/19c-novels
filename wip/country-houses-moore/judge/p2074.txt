There was no need to make our path more thorny, for “God help us!” those who cater for the public know too well what a variety of palates they have to please! there is but <i>one</i> overpowering <i>Ude</i> in our species of literature who succeeds equally well in all its various beginnings and endings. But for us humbler souls, who dare not be servile imitators, no untrodden path is left.

The poet may indulge his imagination to its most romantic extent, in descriptions of climate and scenery, such as those who never travelled beyond their fire-sides, can hardly have ever dreamt of.
