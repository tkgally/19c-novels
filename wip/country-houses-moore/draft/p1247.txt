Colonel Neville readily acceded to this arrangement—was it possible that he had been an unobservant witness of Lady Louisa?—we leave those similarly situated to determine this delicate point.

The Miss Williamsons and Miss Hughes were knowing and practised toxopholites; the latter had won the prize at Blyfield Archery, and at the Winstay meeting also; on enquiries being made respecting ground and distance, it was proposed that such good judges ought to reconnoitre the ground before the meeting took place.
