# Chapter XIV.

S<sc>unday</sc> all the party attended the parish church, excepting the Duchess of Dartmoor, who was languid, and breakfasted in her own apartment, and Miss O’Brian, who, driven by Monseiur Dujeu, and accompanied by Florence Cormac, went to Greystock Chapel.

When they were all assembled at luncheon, Lady Clara, who was opposite an end window of the room, exclaimed, “Oh, there is Ottery; I am sure it is a foreign carriage I see coming through the wood.”

“Where! where!” was the cry, no carriage could be seen.

“Why, Clara, you must be out of your senses.”
