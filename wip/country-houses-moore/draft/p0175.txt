He spent, as usual, the rest of his morning in his office with his clerks. But when he joined his daughter at dinner, he seemed so cheerful, rubbed his hands, and occasionally chuckled with glee, that she could not help saying,

“You must have had something particularly pleasant this morning, dear papa, I am delighted to see you so happy!”

“Thank you, Emma, I have had a very pleasant ride to Fullerton, and found our good friend, Mrs. Graham, and her daughter, quite well.”

“I am doubly glad,” replied Emma, “if what pleases you is also a source of pleasure or satisfaction to them.”
