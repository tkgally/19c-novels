# Chapter III.

<sc>It</sc> is really quite wonderful how much preparation and how much trouble must be taken to get up a little play. But then this depends on <i>how</i> it is to be done. As a family affair, and an impromptu, it is easily managed. Private theatricals have been admirably managed, and excellently performed. No one likes to have a failure; therefore this was intended by its projectress to be super-excellent.
