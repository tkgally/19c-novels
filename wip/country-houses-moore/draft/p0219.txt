“Well, now I am convinced I am right, though there has been great disputing whether your hair should, or should not, remain in those pretty natural ringlets; the men are all against me, but they always fancy nature is never to be counteracted; but, for the part of Cathos, you must be dressed <i>a la mode Françoise</i>, and I have no doubt when they see you, I shall have them all of my opinion.”

Emma coloured deeply at the idea of having been a subject of conversation and dispute.
