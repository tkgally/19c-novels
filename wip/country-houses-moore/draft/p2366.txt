This act of kindness was at the same time communicated to both the young ladies; as Mrs. Beaumont had not mentioned either her kind intentions, or the object of her visit to Emily, when her nieces attempted to express their thanks, she took them alternately in her arms, saying,

“Don’t thank me, for this is by far the happiest day of my life! I never knew before the value of wealth as a source of happiness; I now feel it, as a means of making others happy.”

Their visit was not long; Caroline had suffered a good deal from revulsion in feeling, and kept all the evening in her own apartment.
