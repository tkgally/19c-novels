But to return to Mr. Graham, Caroline, what passed between you?”

“I will tell as well as I can recollect, but it flurried me a good deal. The little boys were running joyously before us at a little distance, and Williams and Felix were behind: I said, How happy those dear little boys are, how much they seem to enjoy our English climate, and the power it gives them of exercise! I wonder how children in India live; I wonder what I did myself, when there; I think I must have been very unhappy.
