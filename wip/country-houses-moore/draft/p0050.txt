Frederic could not conceal from himself that there were <i>le pour et le contre</i> as to Brighton. Lady Henshawe and her charming daughter were there. Lady Henshawe was one of the most captivating women in the world, insinuating in address, irrestible in manner, and her dinners! they were not select, they were <i>exclusive</i>, <i>recherchée</i>, EXQUISITE!

Her husband had been ambassador at various courts, she had accompanied him even to Constantinople, and therefore knew how to combine Eastern and Northern luxuries.
