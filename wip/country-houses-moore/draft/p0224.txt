# Chapter VI.

L<sc>eft</sc> to herself, Emma felt the full force of Lady Tintern’s kindness, and the value of her advice, and protection; and, aided by that, she now, almost thought she could stand against even flattery and love; she felt some fear of this Lady Anne, but yet if she would keep Lord John’s eyes from her, and prevent those little soft whisperings, those nothings, which he took every opportunity of pouring into her ears—she wondered why he <i>whispered</i> what might as well be spoken aloud, and were not, after all, half so flattering as what the Count addressed to her.
