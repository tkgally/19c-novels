But, my dear Mrs. Villiars, you are tired, and why should we prolong a conversation so uninteresting to you?”

The parties separated for the night, but Caroline going as was usual, into her aunt’s dressing room, found her in deep meditation, with her head leaning on her hand; she hesitated a moment at the door, but on Miss Shirley’s lifting up her head, and saying,

“Come in, dear Caroline, I want to speak to you,” she entered and seated herself by the fire opposite her aunt: whosaid,
