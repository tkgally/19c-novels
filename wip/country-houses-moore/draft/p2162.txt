“You are quite right, sir,” said Miss Shirley, “my afflicted friend was left a young widow, and her only consolation was excessive indulgence and almost idolatory of her children; her son had a long minority, and, when he came of age, he wished his mother to continue at Elstree Park, and let him be her occasional guest; but she fancied he would enjoy himself more, without the restraint of an old woman; but I believe now, she may have too much reason to regret this arrangement; for I understand, he only keeps up a few rooms for himself, and his friends, as a hunting and a shooting place; and I should suspect, it is from some of the orgies kept there that this unfortunate accident has resulted.”

“You are too near the truth, Madam,” replied Col.
