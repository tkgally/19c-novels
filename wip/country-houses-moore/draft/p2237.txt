“I don’t mean to distress you, my dear, but I think you might put a little confidence in such a circle of friends as we are.”

Miss Neville seemed choking, and without the power of articulation. Miss Shirley tried to turn the conversation by complaining of the heat of the room; she got up, and at the same time Miss Neville also, to open the door into another room; when their hands were on the lock, Miss Neville, said,

“Do come for a moment with me into this room.”

There she explained the whole mystery, and begged Miss Shirley would communicate to her aunt Colonel Wetheral’s proposal, and she escaped to her own apartments.

Mrs. Villiars was delighted at her niece’s prospects, which were, in a pecuniary point of view, better than she could have expected for her. Colonel Wetheral had a very good income now, and would eventually have a much larger.
