No! no! it must be <i>select</i> and <i>exclusive</i>. Now what think you of my vigilance?”

Mrs. Beaumont had lent a patient, but languid, ear, to all this breathless harangue; and to the question answered, “I will do any thing <i>you</i> wish, to promote any plan of <i>your’s</i>; but I grow tired of <i>literature</i>, indeed tired of every thing in this smoky town; I don’t think it agrees with my health; I have some thoughts of running away from it directly; and if not going into Warwickshire, to look after my gardens, or to ruralize, as you would call it, I must get, for a short time, a villa, near Richmond.”
