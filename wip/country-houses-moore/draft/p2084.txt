Miss Briggs bustled about, and tried to shove people together. Miss Talbot talked loud, but it would not do, and I am sure every body went away, saying to themselves, “What a horrid, stupid evening!””

Caroline had hardly finished her account, and given her aunt a second dish of tea, when Mr. Melbourne was announced; he came to inform Miss Shirley that a vessel would sail for Calcutta the next day, to ask for her letters, and to beg one of recommendation to her brother, for a young friend of his own.

Miss Shirley soon after left the room to make up her own and Caroline’s despatch, and write the requested letter to her brother, and desired her niece to entertain Mr. Melbourne till her return, adding, “You must exert yourself, Caroline, and be very agreeable, that our friend may not think my absence unconscionably long.”
