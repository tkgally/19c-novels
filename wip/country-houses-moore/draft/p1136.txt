# Chapter V.

M<sc>r</sc>. D<sc>allas</sc> had been summoned to fill the place which Lord St. Leonard seemed determined not to return to, and this place was opposite to Miss O’Brian, who held Mr. Dallas in extreme aversion, and for this dislike we must account.

He was a rising young man, without family pretensions; his father was an attorney in Dublin, but having been useful to successive Lord Lieutenants, and particularly versed in electioneering business, he had realized a large fortune, and without loss of character.
