“I am so glad,” he observed to her, “that you are of my way of thinking, about albums and charade books, for I see you have neither.”

“Oh, don’t be too sure of that, for perhaps I may combine both evils in one; and have a more abominable thing called a <i>scrap</i> book, for I take in <i>every thing</i>.”

“Well, perhaps that is more tolerable; for at any rate, there is no display in your’s, and that is what I most hate; I will look out for fugitive pieces for you, and as I cannot contribute in any other way, I will cut out of newspapers every thing likely to suit you; depend on me!”

This, though said in rather an under tone, was not lost upon Grace Latour.
