“Nothing! I confess,” replied Mr. Blake.

“I’ll tell you what Blake likes,” said Mr. Gordon, “and let him contradict me if I am wrong. In summer a house at Sorrento, with a felucca always in readiness for a sail to Ischia; winter spent at Naples; the carnival at Venice, now and then a little of Paris, England never, except to raise money.”

“Why,” answered Mr. Blake, “one might endure that, and I am going to try if I can improve on such a plan, by taking a little of Greece or Asia into it.”

“Oh,” cried Miss Fanny Williams, “what have you done, Mr. Blake, with that dear, darling poodle of your’s, from whom, I thought, you were never separated?”

“Pray, pray, don’t ask me! he is dead! every thing I love, I lose. I never loved any thing but him, and a charming, intelligent monkey, and both are dead!”
