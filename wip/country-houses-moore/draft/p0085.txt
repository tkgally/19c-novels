Somehow, he thought Sunday came round <i>oftener</i> than any other day—it shined no sabbath-day to him—there had been one already this week: not that Frederic doubted the truths of that religion in which he had been educated; but its <i>practice</i> encroached on his habits of self-indulgence.

There are many occasions on which we feel we must do as others do, even if we would not, were we left to our own decision, he therefore joined the party to church, but left it as soon as the service was over.

His sister and Louisa Grey were to be busy at the school, distributing the prizes gained in the year; and Miss Sedley, who was well versed in such business, offered her assistance. She, her father, and sister, were to have returned home after church, but the business of the quarter sessions did not finish on Saturday, and the two worthy baronets were obliged to attend it again on Monday. The party was so agreeable, that the young ladies obtained permission to stay till it broke up, and Mr. Sedley also found some attractions he was not inclined to forego.
