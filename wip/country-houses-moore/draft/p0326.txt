She knew Lord John joined the party, but it was composed of several persons; if Emma was a tolerable manager, she might keep him from any particularity of conduct; she could not attend to every thing, she had given Emma all the caution and advice in her power, if she would not mind her, she must take the consequence, and get a little heart-wounded.
