When it began, there was a rage for dancing amongst young men of fashion, and, though all could not be Worcester’s, or St. Aldegande’s, they were imitators. Ten years has numbered out their ‘dancing days,’ and their places are supplied only by Guardsmen, whose first regimentals have not lost their gloss; or Lancers, whose utmost efforts have not been able to raise nine hairs by way of mustache on their upper lips.

Then the Opera is a failure! the men are so occupied with the favourite singer or dancer, that Venus herself, drawn by her doves, would be neglected, if <i>Malibran</i> sang, or <i>Taglioni</i> danced.

And those abominable Clubs!!! all who are <i>bons-partis</i> only dine where there is a capital cook! but ‘they don’t dance,’ and therefore file off to the whist and écarté of their respective clubs.
