After church, she and Emily Vernon were to preside at a dinner of roast beef and plumb-pudding, given to the children of Lady Vernon’s school; and at the school-house were also the aged, who had been clothed by the same benevolent person, and who were looking forward to their beef and ale at home, her gift also. Of course the gentlemen who were of the party to church, which did not include the foreign guests, took no part in this exhibition, but accepted Sir Thomas’s invitation; together with the Miss Latours and Mrs. Elenor, to see some improvement he was planning, by throwing down hedges, and adding his park; and he had a particular pleasure not only in consulting his son’s inclination, but giving his taste the credit of every judicious alteration, so that Frederic had a gratification of his self-love he did not deserve; but he took it, notwithstanding, and was accordingly in great good humour with himself and those around him, more particularly so, as by Miss Lator not coming down before the party went into dinner, be was at liberty to take in, and place himself next to, Louisa Grey.
