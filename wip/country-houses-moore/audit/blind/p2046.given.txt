Miss O’Brian was deep in discussion or wholesome diet, and previous to dinner, had been presented by General clayton with a box of charcoal bon bons, the first she took stuck in her throat, and nearly choked her through the first course.

During the evening Lady Adelaide received Sir Godfrey’s marked attentions with such decided approbation that her displeased mother could not endure witnessing what she could not prevent, and retired to her own apartment.
