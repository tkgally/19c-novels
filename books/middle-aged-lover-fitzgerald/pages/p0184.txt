Mr. Doughty was duly installed, and very soon the horny sounds of fiddles proclaimed to Brickford that “Music, heavenly maid,” was on active service. Anon the sounds of a piano were heard, and a German gentleman or two, with Corinna presiding at the grand, discoursed that most refined and dignified of earthly performances, a chamber trio or quartet.
