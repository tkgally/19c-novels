# Chapter XVI.
[[subtitle]] Confusion Worse Confounded.

HERE was the irrepressible Lady Duke come off, hot foot, to begin operations, now that “the ground was cleared.” She looked a little disconcerted when she saw that Mrs. Gardiner was already “on the ground” before her; and the two ladies sniffed a reciprocal hostility.

Mr. Doughty seemed to overflow with bitterness, and a sort of quiet fury; while his eyes sparkled with eagerness.
