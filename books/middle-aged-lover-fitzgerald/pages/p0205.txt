But Corinna turned on the speaker with something like indignation.

“Mr. Duke hardly deserved that speech; you know very well it was not his fault if he could not keep his engagement,” she said, quietly.

This was all; but the speech had a curious effect on those listening. Over Mr. Doughty’s face passed an expression of pain and positive anguish; over that of Lady Duke one of anxiety. Mr. Duke was triumphant. In short, it was the presence of jealousy, dislike, and the feeling of success.

When he was alone after the scenes of this day, alone in his large music-room with his grand piano, Mr. Doughty paced about restlessly.
