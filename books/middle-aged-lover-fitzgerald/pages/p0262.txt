# Chapter XIX.

[[subtitle]] “Dear Brothers.”

THUS then had ended this part of this little episode. Mr. Doughty, living in a world of music, simple, shrewd, retired, and solitary, yet perfectly familiar with the world, and the characters found in the world, had a very sensitive temper, which in time had reached to a pitch that was almost morbid.
