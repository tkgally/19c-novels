[[cont]]You’ve ‘snaffled’ him at last? Give a kiss to your father, my future Mrs. D.!”

“I have no news of the kind you mean, papa,” she answered coldly; “nor is it likely you will ever hear any of that sort.”

“No news,” he repeated in wonder. “You’re joking, Miss. Why, the man’s in love with you; the whole town sees it. What nonsense you do go on with.”

“A delusion,” said she; “one more added to the many that have done us so much mischief in our course through life. I tell you, papa, he does not think of me, nor I of him. You must put the whole idea aside.”

“Ridiculous! Preposterous! Fiddle-dedee! Put it aside, indeed! How easy you talk of it!” were the only incoherent words that suggested themselves to Mr. Nagle.
