“Why not, pray?” said Mr. Doughty sternly. “I hate to hear that nonsense about ‘blue blood’ and such stuff. There is no blue blood, in England at least, and the real question is, is he worthy, not of the affections of that young girl, which he has won already, but of her hand?”

“Exactly,” said the clergyman, enthusiastically, “that’s the <i>true</i> way to put it! She might marry anybody!”

Will Gardiner glanced at his brother, and with a certain warmth, replied:

“That’s all very romantic, but still people do make remarks; and say what you like, it’s not exactly the custom.”
