“And <i>you</i> say this. How happy it makes me. To tell you the truth I was not thinking of the words, but of something else. That was what gave the idea of such feeling to you. Oh, Corinna——”

“Halloo, you sir!” the conventional testy father would have exclaimed. But it must be remembered that the young warriors of our day receive the most prodigious encouragement from the admiring demoiselles with whom they consort, so that in a single night a fair maid has been known to be wooed and won. Competition is so brisk, and the market so—though this seems rather a disrespectful strain. In short, the young gentlemen are privileged, more or less, to say what they please.
