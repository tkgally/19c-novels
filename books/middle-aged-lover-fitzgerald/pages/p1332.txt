That gentleman advanced, smiling.

“My poor Doughty,” he said, “in what a way I find you! I received your telegram,” he went on, “and the amazing news it contained. Why, it is like a romance. I met Miss Nagle this morning, and she is coming here.”

“Look here, gentlemen,” said Doctor Spooner, with a sort of dogged fury in his eyes. “There is some understanding here among you all. And I suppose you have planned this meeting. But, let me tell you, we are not to be put off from carrying out our purpose. We are authorised to do so by the proper parties, and are acting legally. I warn you, we have assistance here, and will tolerate no interference.”
