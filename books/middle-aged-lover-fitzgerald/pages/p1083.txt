“Which of the two do you mean?” said he eagerly, tortured by this mysterious denunciation. “Why every man, woman, and child in the place saw that you cared for that Duke.”

“It is no matter now, as all is over. I must leave this place at once, as I will not be forced to sink lower than I have done. Very fortunately I am enabled to do so, as only a few days ago an opening occurred. That opera manager is good enough to say that he has confidence in my gifts; that he is sure he will not lose. I do not believe he will, and I have accepted.”
