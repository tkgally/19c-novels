“Fine! Regular Italian-looking! A something now that reminds me of Grimani, who came when we were at Brighton. He used to sing with the royal people sometimes. One of those odd women’s trebles, which seem a little foreign to our English ways.”

“Revolting, sir,” said William, with disgust. “By the way, we must get our handsome Miss Corinna a beau. Here, Master Duke,” he added, in one of his loud whispers, “I want to introduce you to a deuced fine girl.”
