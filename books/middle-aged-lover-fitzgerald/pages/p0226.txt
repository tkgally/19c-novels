“Something has occurred that has made you happy—very happy?” he went on.

“No, no,” said Corinna.

“I thought so,” he answered, with an effort. “And of course your kindly and well-meant caution to me is connected with such an event. Come,” he again added almost fiercely, “I am surely entitled to some answer from you on these points; it is the least, after all, you can do for me.”

“You quite misjudge me,” said Corinna. “If you have any confidence in me, you will not assume anything now.”

“Oh, I understand! Now, then,” he said, “listen to me, and weigh carefully, I conjure you, what I am going to say. Do not suppose there is any selfish motive.
