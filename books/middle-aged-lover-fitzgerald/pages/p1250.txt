Mr. Doughty started when he saw him.

“What do you want here?” he cried; “who allowed you to come in?”

“I just came in to see how you were getting on,” said the other, confused, and in his mildest way.

“To see how I was getting on, on the road to incapacity and idiotcy. Do you all find that I am not going fast enough? Are you getting impatient?”

Much taken back at this speech, Will Gardiner could only falter out that he was delighted to see that he was better.
