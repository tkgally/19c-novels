“Pardon me,” said the other, standing in the doorway; “I cannot permit it—at least not now. I have no doubt that he will see you later, and if he express the least desire, I can assure you you shall be admitted.”

“I shall be admitted! Thank ye, sir! Well, and who gave you the right to set yourself up as captain or commander-in-chief here? I see your game, sir; you want to kick down the ladder on which you scrambled up.”
