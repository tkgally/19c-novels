“I came for my son, madam,” said the visitor, trembling with rage; “but I do not choose to enter on any discussion of the matter with you. So you must excuse me, please. Now, sir, perhaps you will give me your arm.”

Young Mr. Duke cast an imploring and helpless look at Corinna. But he was awed. Without a word, he did as he was bid, gave his mother his arm, and left the room with her.
