Lady Duke was crushed; her pleasure at the withdrawal of such a dangerous rival was overpowered in her wonder at what “the girl could be at.” She could only rise and murmur:

“Well, this is really wonderful, and shows a very proper feeling. It is quite Spartan of you, and indeed I must say that you have behaved very fairly through the affair.”
