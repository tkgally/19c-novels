Loudest among the laughers was Will Gardiner, who was holding the General—a placid, grey-moustached gentleman—by both arms, and telling him “as comic a thing as he ever heard in his whole life.” In a moment, however, Mr. Gardiner had caught sight of the tall gentleman in the next room, and had rushed noisily to greet him.

“How <i>are</i> you, Nagle? Very glad to see you here. Where’s the wife and child? I hope you brought them.”
