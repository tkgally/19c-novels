“Ladies and gentlemen, it has become necessary to solicit your indulgence for a trifling change in our programme. We shall, with your kind permission, take the ballad now, and postpone the duet, thus transposing the two pieces. I trust that this little departure from the order set down will not be visited with your disapprobation.”

All of course applauded loudly. But Mr. Nagle did not then retire. He had a few more words to add.
