“You thought,” he said, hesitatingly, “that your motives would be misconceived—by me, I mean; that your father’s position, your own, and mine—the ‘great millionaire,’ as they called me——excluded everything from the matter but self-interest. Yours was too lofty a nature to endure the suspicion of being made a mere instrument for securing money and fortune. And so you left this place, and went out into the world. I did not see this then, so clearly as I ought to have done; but what you have done to-night has revealed it all to me.”

She looked at him gratefully.
