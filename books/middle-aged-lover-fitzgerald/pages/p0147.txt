Give your daughter some chance of distinguishing herself, and don’t profane her noble voice and herself by such things. Do something for art, and don’t quite turn it into a tradesman’s business.”

“Oh, my good sir,” said Mr. Nagle, impatiently, “don’t teach me, pray, at my time of life; I am a little beyond that. No, we really can make no alteration in the programme. It must stand.”

“But you know it is altered already, and does not stand,” said the other quietly.

Corinna’s eyes flashed, she drew herself up. Mr. Doughty saw what he had done, and actually coloured.

“I did not mean——” he said, his voice actually faltering.
