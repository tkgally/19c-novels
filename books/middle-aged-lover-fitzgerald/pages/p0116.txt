Mr. Nagle started and coloured. The friend and pupil of the imperishable Braham to be classed with common strollers! Yet he did not know what to answer.

“It is simply ridiculous—not to be thought of. The people here, I find, are talking of this intimacy, against which I set my face. It is right to let you know at once that nothing can come of it. If you are wise, you will let the thing alone. You are all very clever, no doubt, in your profession, but this sort of thing never does. Neither I nor his father will tolerate it. And as for having a son of mine exhibited for money, such a thing is not to be endured a moment!”
