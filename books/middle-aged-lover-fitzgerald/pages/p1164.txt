Then she turned to greater interests.

Lady Duke and one of the conspirators met that night, Doctor Spooner having come over to see her.

“Birkenshaw,” he said, with something like consternation, “has gone away, taking with him the heads of the instructions. The deed will be ready for execution to-morrow.”

“What is to be done?” asked the lady.

“I really think that he believes he will not live,” said the other.

“And are those Nagles to have it all? Surely we are not going to accept such a state of things.”
