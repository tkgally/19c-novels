Lady Duke made no answer, but gathered her shawl about her. It was evident that the answer had satisfied her one way or the other, as indeed her companion seemed to read in her face. As she was going out of the room he stopped her.

“Just pardon me. Do you think he loves her enough to do that, to brave all—his relatives, friends, the world, poverty, everything—sooner than give her up?”
