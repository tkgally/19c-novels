This old gentleman was a little eccentric: lived in a quiet, unostentatious way, and among persons who knew nothing about him. He had no relations. Though he had a rich friend, a musical amateur also, whom everybody considered sure of succeeding to the inheritance, not one of the friends who “fiddled” with him dreamed that he possessed so enormous a fortune. And when his death came, no one was so amazed or confounded as Old Doughty.
