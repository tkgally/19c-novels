But already he saw, with some confusion, that his lady had taken the pleasing office on herself, and was “sympathising” with Corinna. Faces, curious and malicious both, converged to hers as to a focus, to see “how she took it.” Even the most delicate could not resist. The situation was delicious. There she was, “left in the lurch;” where two stools were concerned, as the doggerel bard had sung, the result was nearly always invariable.
