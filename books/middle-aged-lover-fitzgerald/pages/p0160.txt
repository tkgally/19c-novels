Mr. Nagle interposed somewhat roughly. “My good sir, you seem to me to be interfering. You are going a little too far. This gentleman is a most particular and warm friend of ours, who has behaved in the most chivalric style. I really can’t suffer these sneers. It is not becoming.”

“Quite right,” said the other; “I had no business to interfere. I have brought this all on myself. Perhaps you may be a little sorry for this. It is not for me, of course, to say so, but you may see your mistake; it may be, before the night is over.”
