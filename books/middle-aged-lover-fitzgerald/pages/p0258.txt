But the fair Corinna herself never dreamed of such a thing. I swear to you she didn’t. Come, my dear Nagle, you and I have a regard for each other and tastes in common. Many a friendship and pleasant prospect has been spoilt by some such misapprehension as this. So leave me out of the question, and dismiss the idea from your mind altogether.”

In answer to this rather “brutal” way of putting the matter, Mr. Nagle felt he had nothing to offer. Moreover, being a person of ambitious views, he generally considered his daughter’s interests as completely secondary to his own. He looked ruefully at Mr. Doughty, gave a sigh, and thus accepted the situation.
