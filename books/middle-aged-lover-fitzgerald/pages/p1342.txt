Mr. Doughty, still pleasant over the matter, answered:

“Well, I am not going to force my company on them. But really, after this eagerness of weeks, and the general anxiety about me, it is a little mortifying to find myself reduced to the position of a mere cypher. I am afraid that nobody cares about me now, or what becomes of me.”

The two men retired, but Lady Duke held her ground. That proud lady was determined not to slink off in company with her defeated emissaries, but would hold her ground until some more creditable way of retiring offered. She trusted to the chances of events. But there was more mortification in store for her.

Mr. Doughty had gone several times to the window with some anxiety.
