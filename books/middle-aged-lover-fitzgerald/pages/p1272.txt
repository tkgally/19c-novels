They went up-stairs, and found the owner sitting at his desk, busy with his papers. His friend Will Gardiner was in the room with him. A few moments later arrived the general, who entered not a little fluttered. There was a look of good-humoured toleration on Mr. Doughty’s face, and he did not seem in the least disturbed by this odd visit and the gathering of people.

“What a large party,” he said, as they entered. “To what do I owe this? Are you all so anxious about my health? I am much better, I assure you—all but restored to health.”
