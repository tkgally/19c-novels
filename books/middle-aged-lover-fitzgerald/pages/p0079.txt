# Chapter VI.
[[subtitle]] Old Doughty.

OLD DOUGHTY, were his family documents, certificates, &c., duly scrutinised, would be found to be not so very old after all. He was, in fact, neither grey nor stooped, and not very old-fashioned in dress or manner, but more old and “dry” in mind than in body; a cold, grey-eyed bachelor, who lived by himself, and at a club.
