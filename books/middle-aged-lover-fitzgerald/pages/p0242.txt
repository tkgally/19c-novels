[[cont]]young gentleman was so accustomed to have his own way in the world, to be petted by the young damsels with whom he consorted in garrison towns, that any opposition annoyed him exceedingly. It must be again said that, if not actually “in love” with Corinna, he had a violent inclination towards that young person, such as gentlemen of the garrison often experience for the young ladies of the district where they are quartered, and he longed to show that he had conquered the prettiest girl in Brickford.

He was walking down to the club, rather proud of his last night’s proceedings, and of the victory he had gained, of which, no doubt, all the ladies in the place were talking, or would be talking.
