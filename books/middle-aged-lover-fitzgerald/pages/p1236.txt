“We must let him be seen by another doctor. The opinion of two physicians will be necessary. I will get Craggs—a man,” added Doctor Spooner, slowly, “who has had a very great deal of experience in these more delicate cases, and who is independent of all parties here.”

“Then what must be the first step?” asked Lady Duke.
