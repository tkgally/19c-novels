“More fool you; and what have you to endure now? To be laughed at by all the envious women of the place.”

“Well, it all must end, father. We must give up these wretched plans, which, indeed, I have only tolerated for your sake. I wish now to work for my bread, to confine myself to our own proper station.”

Had the immortal Braham risen through a trap-door suddenly and stood beside him, Mr. Nagle could not have been more astonished. “Why this is all moonshine and—and——” the word would not come, so he had to use his favourite one—“all fiddle-de-dee!”
