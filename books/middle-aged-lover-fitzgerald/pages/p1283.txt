[[cont]]most hopeless and discouraging being in the world is the wistful creature, male or female, who wants to teach the world French, German, the piano, the violin, or the guitar, or the formation of the human voice. This operation always seems to have the air of a benefaction, or of some charitable work; but it is, in truth, a charity intended solely for the teacher, a wish, not so much to teach, as to be supported. The piteous appeal runs not so much “<i>Do</i> let yourself be taught,” but “Support <i>me!</i>” In fact, the number of would-be teachers far exceeds the number of those capable of being taught.
