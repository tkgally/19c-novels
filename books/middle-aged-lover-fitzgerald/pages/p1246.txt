# Chapter XXI.
[[subtitle]] A Conversion.

MR. DOUGHTY, when his medical adviser left him, fell into a strange state of excitement. It was now revealed to him in what peril he was standing.

“Good heavens,” he thought, “if they should take any such step! I have read of such dreadful things! I am a poor helpless creature, here, quite at their mercy. God help me!”
