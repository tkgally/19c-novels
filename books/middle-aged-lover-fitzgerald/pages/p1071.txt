Mrs. Gardiner carried something in her hand, and the two had sympathising faces. Lady Duke and Will Gardiner looked at them significantly; but the new arrivals were wholly unfitted for taking part in such a struggle, having neither the boldness nor the shiftiness necessary to hold their ground, or push their advance.

“What, you here?” said Will, scoffingly. “Oh! I declare this is too good!”

“Oh, yes,” said his brother; “it is dreadful, isn’t it, and so sudden too. He dined with us only so lately. He must have had it on him then.”
