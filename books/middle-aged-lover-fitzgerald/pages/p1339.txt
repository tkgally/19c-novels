# Chapter XXVIII.
[[subtitle]] Last Scene of All.

A SORT of stupor had settled on the confederates.

Mr. Dodd inspected the document that was handed to him, with due gravity, and said:

“Well, I must say I had expected this, and, in truth, was a little astonished when I heard that another had been chosen. I am very, very sorry for you, Doughty.”
