As a beginning, Lady Duke, do you see that heap of torn-up papers? There is the draft of my will. But now I must take care of you all. First, tell me,” he said to Lady Duke, “has your son quitted England as yet?”

“No,” said that lady. “But I had a letter from him this morning. I believe the sailing of his vessel has been postponed.”

He started up.

“I knew it! It is now all as clear as daylight. Of course, he did not write to you that she had gone to meet him. Was there ever such an instance of an overpowering passion!”
