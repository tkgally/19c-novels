This rather selfish and ornamental young officer had one grand aim in life, which was to avoid being “annoyed” or “worried;” and was very well content to bestow his affections on the enchanting Corinna, provided matters went very much as they would on some long night at a ball, where all was music and dancing, bright and flashing light, beauty, youth, and entertaining talk.
