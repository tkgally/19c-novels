He was looking at her with a shy and curious interest that really spread a light over his face.

“Oh,” said Corinna, in her most natural way, “it was so kind of you to let me come, though I hear you are such a severe judge.”

“Dear me, no! What nonsense the people fill their heads with! Indeed I confess to liking good old music, and some of the new, and can fairly judge of that; but as for pronouncing on the style of singing and playing pursued by the young ladies of the day, I confess I am utterly unfit for it. I know nothing about it. They neglected my education.”

Corinna’s speaking face told that she was a little mortified. He saw it. “Not that I doubt that you are one that I shall like.
