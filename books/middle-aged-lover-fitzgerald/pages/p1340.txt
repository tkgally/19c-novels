He seemed to delight in keeping up the comedy of the situation.

“They have been at a vast deal of trouble, attending and watching me. Lady Duke, here, has been like a perfect sister of charity. They are so concerned for my state that they have brought their people, and carriage, and everything. We ought not to detain them longer.”

Lady Duke was looking at him darkly.
