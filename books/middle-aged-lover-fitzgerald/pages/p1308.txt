Still, there was a good deal of sympathy expressed; for he was a charitable, gentle-tempered man, who had done good and won popularity. There was, however, a certain class of business men, who, by a sort of instinct, arrived at the true conclusion, namely, that it was an awkward affair, and that there was some plotting at the bottom. It was, in short, talked of a great deal. As Will Gardiner was not very restrained in his speech, and his wife said everywhere that the proceedings taken had been at the instigation of “those Dukes,” it was not surprising that rumours should have begun to swell, and that indignation should be expressed at the treatment of the “unfortunate gentleman.”
