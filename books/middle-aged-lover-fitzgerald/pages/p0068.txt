The young man listened, wondering and delighted.

“Somehow they never seemed to care much for my musical gifts at home,” he said; “and to tell you the truth, I never considered the matter much; but if you really think that I have a voice, I do wish you would take it in hand. My father and mother go away to-morrow: but I shall stay on here for some time. I find it a very pleasant place.”
