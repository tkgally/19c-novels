Between her and her good-natured, complacent husband, unusual scenes of altercation and reproach took place. But there was an agreement in one essential point, that “something must be done,” and that very quickly, or the card house in which they were living would come toppling down about their ears.

Of Lady Duke’s position we have already spoken. She herself was pressed and harassed a good deal by the same forces. But with people of fashion, and “of a certain position,” and who have some sort of an estate, the billows of pecuniary distress are slow in rising, and may beat for years against the stone breakwater without serious damage.
