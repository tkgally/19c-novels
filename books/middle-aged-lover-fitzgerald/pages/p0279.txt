“Why,” cried Will, noisily, “I declare, here he comes to answer for himself!”

Mr. Duke started back when he saw the room so full, and found himself greeted by the obstreperous Will.

“Ha, ha, ha! Just talking of you, Master Duke,” he said, seizing him by the shoulders. “Do you know it has been passed nem. con. by all Brickford that you are to come forward gallantly forthwith and do your duty like a man! No shirking to be allowed!”

The young man impatiently shook himself free.
