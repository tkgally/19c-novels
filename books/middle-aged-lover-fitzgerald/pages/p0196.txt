[[cont]]“You may say that, but I declare to you it is the only place in which I have found health. What with Doctor Meiklejohn and the air, I really quite forget my neuralgia.”

Mr. Doughty laughed good-humouredly. “After this,” he said, “physicians will be ordering their patients to smoky Brickford, as they would to Nice or Arcachon.”

His eyes had followed Corinna, who had moved disdainfully away to the window, where Mr. Duke had attended her.

“And your son,” he added, in a hard tone, “does <i>he</i> also suffer from neuralgia?”

Lady Duke laughed loudly.
