# Chapter IX.
[[subtitle]] Lady Duke in Council.

OF all this little band of conspirators—if that be not an inappropriate term for persons who were seeking to carry out their own ends, independently of each other—Lady Duke was perhaps the most experienced and skilful. She was a woman of the world, in a higher sphere of life than the others, and had, besides, been tolerably successful in various schemes, which had presented far greater difficulties than the one now on hand. Thus a great deal was in her favour.
