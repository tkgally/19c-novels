“Your daughter, I fear,” she went on, reading his face, “thinks I was a little hasty the other day. I own it. But you must consider a mamma’s feelings, and poor Alfred, you know, would have made a sad exhibition of himself. A mother, you know, could not bear to see her son break down.”

Delighted Mr. Nagle called out enthusiastically to his daughter:

“Corinna, come over here. Lady Duke wishes to speak to you and explain——”
