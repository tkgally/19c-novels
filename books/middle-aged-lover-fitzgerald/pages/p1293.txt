“Perhaps so,” said the patron; “and I admire you for taking his part. Forgive me if I say I know the whole of your little history, at least, all but the latter part of it. For to say the truth, after what I heard, I am a little surprised to find you here. Don’t be angry,” he added, hastily. “I ask no questions. I wish to be your friend.”

“I am not angry, indeed,” said Corinna, “and I do believe that you wish to be the friend of one who has no friends. Why should you not ask me questions? I am willing to answer them.
