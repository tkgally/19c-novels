Mr. Nagle was ushering in the great London doctor, and led him to a desk where he was to write prescriptions, Doctor Spooner following deferentially. Lady Duke flew over to Mr. Nagle, and caught at his wrist, her favourite confidential mode.

“Now, how is he? Any danger? What does the doctor say?”

Mr. Nagle’s head was in a sort of azure empyrean, while the rest of his person moved in the fogs of earth. He answered somewhat brusquely:

“My good madam, we can’t attend to you now. I must really beg of you to—Oh, Gardiner! you’re there?”

“Well, what’s the news?” said that gentleman, in his natural voice—a loud one.
