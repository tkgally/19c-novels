About one o’clock the party broke up, and all went home. When the sound of clattering carriages had died away, the owner of the place was left alone in the great waste of his great music-room, alone with the long and disordered piles of chairs, and the music and instruments scattered about. After all those sounds there was a stillness. A sort of fury came into his pale worn face.
