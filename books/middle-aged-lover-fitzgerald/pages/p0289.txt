“Stonebreaker” is not at all an unsuitable description of such professional gentlemen as Dobson and his brethren, who abound in every town, and who, honest souls! are the very hodmen of music. They thump at their pianos with a resolution worthy of those who are breaking stones on the road-side; bring out the human voice, and teach singing, much as a drill-sergeant will work at the arms and legs of his recruits.
