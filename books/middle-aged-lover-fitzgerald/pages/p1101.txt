Your kindness and attention I am sure he ought never to forget should he recover; but as you have such an interest in him, you will feel that not a chance can be allowed to go by. Now that we have a nurse in the house, it seems scarcely necessary that you should harass yourself by sitting up with him.”

“My good fellow,” said Mr. Nagle, his brows lowering, “what do ye mean? There’s no harassing in the business. What are you driving at, sir?”

“Your health is equally valuable to your family, and it is, I say, superfluous that you should go through all this trouble. Everything will be looked after just as well if you sleep at home in your own comfortable bed. In fact, I have sent back the stretcher that you so kindly sent up.”
