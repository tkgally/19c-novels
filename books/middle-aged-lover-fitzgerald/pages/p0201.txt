“My lady was kind enough to mention that—er—little affair the other day, and to appreciate the step she was compelled to take. It is most good of her ladyship, Corinna——”

“What, on the day she entered our house, and spoke to you—to us,” said she, with flashing eyes, “as if you were the meanest serf in the world——”

“Oh, hush! I say nonsense, girl!” said Mr. Nagle, excitedly. “You don’t understand. Serf indeed!”

“With all my heart,” said Corinna. “Be it so. But let no more be said of that scene, for it crushes me with humiliation even to think of it.” And she swept away across the room.

Mr. Doughty was listening with wonder and unconcealed admiration.
