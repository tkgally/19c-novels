“Oh, Miss Spooner, this is all so dreadful, isn’t it? So sorry I couldn’t go and call on you before, but I have been so taken up with the children. There was poor little Harry in the measles, and I assure you I went nearly distracted.”

“And why should you call on me?” said the other, coldly. “I have no wish that my brother’s friends should trouble themselves about me.”

“No, of course, quite right of you,” said the visitor, not seeing that she was coinciding in a rather uncomplimentary way. Then hurriedly changing the topic; “And how is he going on? Tell me about him. You mustn’t overwork yourself, dear Miss Spooner. Promise me that you won’t. You might break down, you know. Do you know what William and I were planning last night?
