This artful speech had its effect.

“What inclinations?” he said, half rising, and then falling back from exhaustion; “no, she could not be so artful, so cruelly artful. It would be devilish. She might have waited for decency’s sake until the grave had closed over me. It would not be for long.”

“Yes, and then she could follow her own affections, and enjoy love and money together.”

“Never!” he said, snatching at some papers that were on the table. “See, here is the deed I was childish enough to have drawn out. It is only the draft.
