# Chapter XVII.

[[subtitle]] A Warning.

ALFRED DUKE had a secret dislike to the cold and rather mysterious being whose guest he had been, who seemed to gauge perfectly all the uncertainties of his weak soul, and to be actually acquainted with all the crooked passages and little cabins of his mind.
