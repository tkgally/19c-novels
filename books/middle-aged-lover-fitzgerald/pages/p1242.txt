As he was hurrying along on his way to the house, and had just come to the corner of the street in which the victim lived, he came upon two figures, whose heads were laid close together, and who were talking earnestly. They started in a guilty fashion as he came up. It was the music-master and the clerical brother.

“What are you two hatching there?” said Will, suddenly growing hostile. “Contriving how you will get at the goose with the golden eggs?”

“Oh, that will not do,” said his brother. “That comes very well from you. Others are entitled to have their chance. The thing isn’t to be made a monopoly of.”
