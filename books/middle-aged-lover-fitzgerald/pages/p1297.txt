Then followed introductions. The host had to come up with many a “Miss Nagle, Lady Mantower wishes to be introduced to you”—“Miss Nagle, Lord Leader has asked me to present him—a very great amateur, I assure you.”

These noble personages came up simpering and bending; said they were “charmed,” proposed her singing at their parties, or giving lessons to their daughters.

Before the evening came to a close, Mr. Dodd was beside her again, and with much satisfaction, said:

“You will do. You are on the high road to success.”
