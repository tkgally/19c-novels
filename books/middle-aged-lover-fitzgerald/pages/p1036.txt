At all events, here he was now, at an hotel in Southampton, feeling some compunctious twinges, but, it must be confessed, also feeling a great weight off his mind. It would never have done, he said to himself; it would have been misery for the girl herself, this taking her out of her station; and they would have both heartily repented of it before long. They had neither of them a shilling. Why, was it not sheer folly and madness, whatever way you looked at it?
