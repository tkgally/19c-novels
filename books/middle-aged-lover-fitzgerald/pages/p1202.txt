# Chapter XVII.
[[subtitle]] Suspense.

IT may have been noticed that a singular change had come over the band of persons who were now engaged in hunting down Mr. Doughty and his treasure. These, when seen at the little musical party with which this story began, were a group of ladies and gentlemen of the average kind, harmless and indifferent, and in some degree good-natured.
