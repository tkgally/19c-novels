Will Gardiner had never heard his wife speak to him with such energy. She overpowered him, and he felt from that moment that she had a strength of mind and a will that was superior to his own. However, he did not make any open protest; but retired silently, determined to carry out his own little plan. For it happily so fell out, that he could do so according to the letter of his instructions, though not after their spirit.

“I’ll not raise a finger against the poor old fellow—if I can help it,” he said to himself, putting in a useful qualification.
