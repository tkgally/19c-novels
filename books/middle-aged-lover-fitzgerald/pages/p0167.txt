Mr. Nagle noticed during the night that the audience seemed to be not a little distracted from his efforts by something “in the body of the hall,” as he phrased it. One disturbance was of course owing to the presence of that gallant prodigal, Mr. Duke, on whom all eyes and opera-glasses were concentrated. There he sat in a stall in the front row, and when Corinna came forward to sing her song, there were but few who followed the young lady’s performance, most of those present eagerly watching his enthusiastic face.
