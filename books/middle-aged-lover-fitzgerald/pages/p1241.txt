It would, to a certainty, be done by those harpies, “Duke and Co.,” and he was, in reality, doing a rough sort of kindness in saving the poor fellow from them. And then, most potent argument of all, there was the bill of sale, the daily pressure of applications for money, which he knew not how to answer or to put off, to say nothing of domestic pressure at home.
