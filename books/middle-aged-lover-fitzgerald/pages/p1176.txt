“Gone away!” said Mrs. Gardiner, genuinely astonished.

“Yes, gone altogether—for ever, ma’am.”

“And at such a time! Then it’s all over with your chances. Those Dukes will have it their own way now. What infatuation!”

Mrs. Gardiner did not care to remain listening to the dismal laments of the baffled musician, but hurried at once to Mr. Doughty’s.
