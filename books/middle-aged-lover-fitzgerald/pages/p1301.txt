“You didn’t feel for <i>me</i>, I suppose,” he said, in a wounded tone.

“Not in the least,” said Corinna, smiling. “But I felt the talk and gossip of the place—the ill-natured remarks to which you exposed me. They assumed that I was disappointed; that I had set my heart on gaining your affection, and had failed. I fear, from my behaviour, it looked as though I had. But really I am glad that we have met, were it only to assure you I never thought seriously of the matter.”

The young man answered bitterly,

“You are determined to mortify me, it seems. Still, you can hardly wonder that I thought the opposite. You were kind enough to distinguish me with——”
