“Degradation,” said he, “for Miss Corinna Nagle. Who wishes to degrade her, pray? What singular ideas you have.”

“You talk ridiculously, Alfred,” said his mother. “I was only hoping that Miss Nagle would sing at our party.”

“Which Mr. Doughty thinks would be a degradation. Absurd!”

“Don’t misinterpret what I said,” replied the other, his voice slightly trembling. “Perhaps we might have meant that <i>you</i> once thought it a degradation to sing at Mr. Nagle’s. Malicious people in this place may have put that very construction on your refusal. It had all the look of it.”
