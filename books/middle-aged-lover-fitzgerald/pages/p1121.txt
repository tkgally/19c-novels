“That will arise by-and-by, when the part of the work done shall be an earnest for what is to follow. The reward should, of course, be substantial. But the prize is worth it. Neither must it be left to good feeling or generosity, which only produces misconception.”

Lady Duke drew up haughtily.

“Oh, I can enter into no bargains of that kind. That is too dangerous a business.”

“I do not ask you,” he said. “By-and-by you will be pressing and asking me to do so. It will be left to your own choice. You will move in the matter, Lady Duke, never fear. This is merely a little preliminary interchange of ideas. Meanwhile, I have only come to you to ask for advice. I now return to the—I must not say deceased, as you did, Lady Duke—but to the patient.”
