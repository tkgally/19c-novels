# Chapter V.
[[subtitle]] Early Skirmishing Round the Patient.

WHEN he entered the drawing-room of Mr. Doughty’s house, he found Lady Duke waiting there, while a motherly-looking woman was in the hall. The lady went to meet him with a torrent of “Isn’t this terrible?” &c. The London doctor, it seems, was upstairs in consultation with Spooner, and “that Nagle.”
