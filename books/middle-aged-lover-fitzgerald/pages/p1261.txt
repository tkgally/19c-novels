The doctor’s eyes twinkled at these welcome words.

“You hear,” he said. “The whole place is full of these supposed intentions, which are singular enough. But we shall see all about that in good time. Meanwhile, Mr. Gardiner need not threaten. He can stay if he pleases, only he must take the consequences.”

“Cheerfully,” said Will.

The doctor retired. Will Gardiner seized his friend’s hand, and, after a short conversation, left the house. The counter-plot had begun.
