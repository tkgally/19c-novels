“That’s what <i>you</i> always say in these matters. Can’t help it! I always told you that if you showed a firm front the fellow would not have dared to go so far. You are too soft and mealy-mouthed altogether; too good-natured to everybody. He ought to be just taken by the shoulders and put out of the house.”

When Mrs. Gardiner spoke in this rather rough style, calling a spade a spade with the noisiest emphasis, it had the effect of annoying her husband, not so much with her as with himself, and when his eyes were thus roughly opened, he was generally ready to plunge into any course, however opposed to the one which he had been previously pursuing.
