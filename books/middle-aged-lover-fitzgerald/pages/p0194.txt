He seemed to look on music as wholly a subsidiary matter. Meanwhile the enchanting Corinna played away in the great music-room, lighting it up with her presence, filling it with her inspiration, and gradually helping to work that mysterious change in the wealthy owner of the establishment which had caused so much remark in Brickford.
