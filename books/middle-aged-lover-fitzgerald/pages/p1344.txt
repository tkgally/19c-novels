Lady Duke started. All her enemies seemed to be gathering to confront her. And here was the worst mortification of all, that this girl should arrive at such a moment to see her defeat. For Corinna she always entertained a special dislike, that began so far back as that little scene where she had interrupted the composition of the posters. In presence of the lofty character of Corinna she always felt inferior. She, too, showed no awe of the superior lady.

There she stood in the doorway: the enchanting Corinna, the music-master’s daughter, looking round on them all with an expressible air of dignity and nobility.
