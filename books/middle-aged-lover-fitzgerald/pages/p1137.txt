This did strike our professor, and he moderated his tone.

“But my good sir, when I see one woman let in and another excluded—my dear child, Corinna, for whom the man would give his last breath—why should we have this tabooing of some, with the letting in of others by back doors?”

“You are quite right as to your daughter, and have given the precise reason why it would be most objectionable to admit her—as yet, at least. He must not be agitated. Take my advice, come back later.”
