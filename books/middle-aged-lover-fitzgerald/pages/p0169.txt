The gentle heart of Corinna had felt some pangs at the unceremonious way in which this person who had shown such a kind interest in her, had been treated, and answered eagerly:

“Indeed, I have been wishing to tell you how much I feel your kindness, but in all this flutter and excitement I was hasty. You must consider that I have others whose wishes I am obliged to follow.”

“Perhaps so,” he answered, hastily. “But I wish you to know this. Circumstances now may allow me to be of use to you—of great use, perhaps—that is if you would permit it, and—”

At this moment came up Will Gardiner, leading Mr. Nagle.
