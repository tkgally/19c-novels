# Chapter XVIII.
[[subtitle]] The Birkenshaws.

LADY DUKE had also made up her mind to act, and did not lose a moment in carrying out her plans. She had written to her husband to come back at once, believing that his presence with the “K.C.B.” adornments, his military, almost magisterial presence, would lend a sort of weight and glitter to the proceedings.
