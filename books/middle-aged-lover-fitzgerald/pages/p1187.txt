[[cont]]Pray leave me now. Do, I implore of you!”

They all went their way, interchanging some curious looks.

On the stairs they met the doctor.

“He is really very odd,” said Lady Duke. “His mind seems quite unsettled by this shock.”

The doctor shook his head.

“I have long been dreading this,” he said. “The news should have been broken to him gently.”

“Oh,” said Lady Duke, in defence of her new ally, “I am sure Mrs. Gardiner took every care about that. He must have known it sooner or later.”

Mrs. Gardiner gave her a grateful look, and squeezed her hand.
