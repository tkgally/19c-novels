The shopkeeper’s daughter was pining in a sort of discontent, bitterly disappointed at her exclusion from the high society of Brickford, which she had made sure of entering. She heard of the many entertainments that went forward, and looked wistfully from afar off, quite in despair at her exclusion. Her husband, too, who felt that he had been dragged down from his former position, was aggrieved by this neglect, and eager to be revenged on the Brickfordians for their slights.
