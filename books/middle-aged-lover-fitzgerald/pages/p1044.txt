“Are you serious? Not care for you?” he repeated.

“No. She is such a mysterious girl, and so full of delicacy about her position and all that, I really believe she is afraid of letting it be seen for whom she cares. My mother, who is a woman of the world, has said so all along.”

A delightful suspicion of the truth began to fill his hearer’s soul. But he was determined to be chivalrous to the end, to keep to that reserve in the matter which he had maintained from the beginning. Every step in advance which he gained should be independent of that dreadful money with which he was so cruelly weighted.
