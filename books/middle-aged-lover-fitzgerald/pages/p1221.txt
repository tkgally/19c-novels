Lady Duke, as we have seen, was a woman of the world, and knew how to avoid being patronising where she meant to be gracious. She was most agreeable, hinted at her own private influence in Brickford, and said there were numbers of her friends who would be delighted to know Mrs. Birkenshaw. Then she turned the conversation on the prevailing topic of the moment. Here she showed some adroitness; for she had taken care not to make a single allusion to the matter down in the office. Business in the drawing-room loses the official air of business.
