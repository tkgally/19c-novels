# Chapter VIII.
[[subtitle]] Peachum and Lockit.

THE fever which had attacked Mr. Doughty had settled on his lungs, oppressing him with a terrible weight and causing him serious exhaustion. For many days he supported a terrible struggle with death. It was presently known that his senses had returned, but that it was doubtful whether he would recover. There was much regret in Brickford when this became known, as he was charitable and generous.
