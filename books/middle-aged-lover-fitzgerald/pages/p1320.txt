Corinna coloured a little, her eyes were cast down. She answered:

“Offended! I fear if we speak of that—but all that is past now. We must save you from these wretches—<i>I</i> shall do it, if all the rest be wanting.”

“<i>You!</i>” he said, with an eager look. “But have you thought of the difficulties? And what can even you do? They are all against me, every one. I am quite helpless here.”

“No matter for that! I have an instinct within which tells me I shall find means and strength. They will not dare to oppose me. I have a certain confidence that I shall save you.”
