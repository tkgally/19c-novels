It’ll make him as good as your husband; for when he gets better, and begins to try and recollect who gave him his medicine, who smoothed his pillow, and did the ministering angel, and all that, he’ll be bound to come forward. The man loves you; surely you can see that with half an eye, and now that that skulking snapjacker has taken himself off, there’s no let or impediment. Come, come,” added Mr. Nagle, with sudden excitement, for he saw a stern opposition in Corinna’s eyes, “I’ll have no disputation about it. I owe money enough in this place, and we can’t afford to be doing romance.”

“But,” said Corinna, now speaking with a deliberate coldness that alarmed him, “as you are calculating all the chances of the case, suppose that this poor patient should be sick to death and not recover.”
