But see, we are losing all the fine music; people will be wondering what has become of us, and will be settling, as I believe some of the gossips have settled already, that some very serious matters are being arranged. Let us go back, or rather let me conduct you to your favoured cavalier, for whom, as I must conclude, you are making this grand sacrifice of a career. Now one word more. As you have made me your confidant in this matter, I am bound to aid you to the utmost, and you may really depend on me. I have been singularly unlucky in the one or two attachments I have had in my life, so I must indemnify myself by forwarding the interests of others.
