“You are interrupting us. I can help no one. No one has ever helped me through life.”

“That’s good from you,” said the intruder. “Where would your fiddle be but for the help you are getting from our friend there? Do lay it down a minute and listen to me. It’s really a very hard case.”

In a very simple, natural way Will Gardiner began to pour out Corinna’s story. He described the mortification, the humiliation of the poor girl, and the shabby, contemptible fashion in which her admirer had “left her in the lurch.” She had the noblest voice.
