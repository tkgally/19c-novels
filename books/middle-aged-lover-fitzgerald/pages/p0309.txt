“To see something satisfactory. Well, we must only wish her plenty of valentines—to-day is Saint Valentine’s Day, you know. Have the young men of Brickford been pouring in verses and odes?—No?”

“My dear friend, there it is. Girls are such utter fools with their fiddle-de-dee. I know well the man that I believe her heart to be set upon. There’s some unlucky malon tongdew somewhere, and things won’t go straight.”

“You must only try and straighten them,” said Mr. Doughty, indifferently. “That’s all. Good-bye.”
