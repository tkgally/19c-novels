“Really, Lord Frederic,” said Miss O’Brian, “I do believe you are turned saint, and mean to set up methodist preacher; I am sure you must have been taking a lesson from Irving, or some such enthusiast, for I never heard such a homily, I hope we shall all be duly edified by it.”

“I am neither a saint, nor a preacher, I assure you, Miss O’Brian, I am only an unfortunate younger brother, and for that reason condemmned to be a bachelor, but I do not go about the world with my eyes shut.”

“Oh! I dare say not, and you know bachelor’s wives are regulated to a proverb.”

“Equally with old maids’ children,” replied Lord Frederic, a little maliciously, “but if you like wise laws, ‘lookers on, see the most of the game,’ and I believe we unhampered folks are the least prejudiced judges of what we <i>see</i>, though perhaps we might not act wiser if we were noosed.”
