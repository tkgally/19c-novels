Yet [[corr situations|situation]], and the unlucky fashion of the day, had spoilt, or at least obscured, his better nature. An entailed estate, and three daughters born before him, had made him anxiously wished for; and, though Sir Thomas had neither threatened, nor determined, to drown his fourth daughter, his son was most welcome, and rendered doubly precious by Lady Vernon’s ill health, which followed his birth, giving little reason to hope for another son.
