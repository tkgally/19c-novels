I have not seen a great deal of her since her marriage, but my intimacy with her mother has always, from her childhood, given me the privilege of openly speaking my mind to her. I have heard much of her conduct that has given me concern; her insatiable love of admiration is her bane; she cannot exist without incessant sacrifices at the altar of her vanity.”

“What sort of man is General Forester?”
