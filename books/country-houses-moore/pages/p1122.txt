Imagine all the jewels you have ever seen, Lady Louisa, at a drawing room, collected and covering this gridle, and the pistols and dagger belonging to it, and a turban of the finest shawl, surrounded by a tiara of the same precious stones as the girdle, and also a sort of feather or aigrette; but his pearls were beyond all price: row after row, hanging down like what I believe you ladies call a negligé, and as large nearly as the eggs of small birds; the figure of the Great Mogul on a pack of cards will give you some idea of him, only you must imagine the clear atmosphere of India brightening the lustre of these jewels: what struck me most was his bearers, their bright polished skins, their turbans covered with gold, a gold scarf and waistcloth all their clothing; but the ends of the poles of the palanquin representing golden wings, rested on their black shoulders; and produced an effect I cannot describe, the Nabob only made a bow to the Governor General without stopping as he passed, the others who preceded him had done the same.”

Colonel Blackburn stopped, saying, “Now, Lady Louisa, you must find another <i>raconteur</i>, you have exhausted me.”
