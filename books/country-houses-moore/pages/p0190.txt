“It will never, never do, take my honest word for it,” said Mrs. De Cameron: and as Lady Tintern did not return, she determined to take a walk, and the volume of <i>Moliere</i> with her, that she might perfect herself in the part she fancied <i>must</i> of necessity fall to her share.
