How could he help thinking of himself, if every body was thinking of him? Alas! that the situation meant as the most favoured of Providence (that which a late king said comprehended <i>every</i> thing a man could desire,) should be a snare to its possessor; and engender that egotism which is not only incompatible with the happiness of the individual, but equally contrary to the self-denying precepts of religion.
