Retired to her room, and alone, Emma gave free way to her tears, repeating to herself a thousand times, all is over!!! When this burst of grief was past, her pride came to her aid, and she determined to drive from her mind, by every possible effort, one who had so cruelly trifled with her feelings, with her heart!!! This resolution was more easily made than carried into execution, but every struggle she made produced additional strength.

Mrs. Graham, with her usual good sense, forbore to touch directly on the tender subject; she knew “that it is <i>suspense</i> and <i>hope</i> that make the food of misery,” that <i>certainty</i> is always endured, because known to be past resistance, and felt to give defiance to struggling. She was too judicious to inveigh on all occasions against the perfidy of men, or even to hint at her suspicions of the full delinquency of Emma’s quondam lover; she knew nothing was so likely to raise her up as his defender, however she might herself feel his base desertion; the less of his conduct that was revived the better, forgetfulness was the only plan.
