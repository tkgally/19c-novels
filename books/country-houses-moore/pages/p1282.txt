Going through a gate, which was held open by a groom of Sir Godfrey Blisset’s, the ladies had all passed through, when the groom’s horse, which was fidgetty, struck his head against the handle of the gate, and gave so sudden a start, as to throw the man back, and compelled him to let go the gate, in falling back it brushed the flank of Sir Henry Willoughby’s famous mare, on which his vanity to shew her off, and his persuasion of her gentleness, had induced him, for the first time, to mount his daughter.
