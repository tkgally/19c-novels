# Chapter XIII.

I<sc>t</sc> is always difficult to find morning amusement for a mixed party in a country house, especially when there is neither shooting nor hunting. The stable, the news-papers, and billiards, are soon over; happy therefore is it when there is a <i>show place</i> in the neighbourhood, a celebrated ruin, or even a manufactory. In a county so abounding in natural beauties as Cumberland, this evil was less felt than almost any where.
