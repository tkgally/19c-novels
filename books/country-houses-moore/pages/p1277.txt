As Miss O’Brian knew that Florence was a fertile source of information, she prolonged her toilette, and said, “is that all your news, tell me something nearer truth, if you can.”

“Why and ye would not sure think I am telling you what is not true, for I am sure and certain it comes from the best authority, but they say Mr. Dallas, that gentleman as you dislike so ma’am, wants to get Miss Willoughby, and they say she has thousands and tens of thousands a year when Sir Henry dies, but his man says, there’ll be two words to that bargain, and he asked me, as his country-woman, if Mr. Dallas was of a great family?
