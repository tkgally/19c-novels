Lord Honiton smiled at the impetuosity and despair of a young man in love, but told Lady Louisa she might take till the next day seriously to consider the matter, and give him her answer, exhorting her to weigh well, if she could give up her country and her friends for ten years.

As the deliberations of a young lady in favour of a highly-prized lover are not very dispassionate, Lord Honiton pointed out to his daughter that she would have to encounter the disadvantages of an unhealthy climate, a long voyage; and, after ten years of sub-regal state, return to a much more moderate income at home.

But it was, she felt, with the man she loved, and had loved long before she was aware of it; and of whose worth, and in whose honour, she had the highest confidence!
