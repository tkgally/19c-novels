Just as the first dinner-bell rang, and some of the party had retired to dress, the Marquis of St. Leonard arrived, and was rapturously received by Lady Honiton; the Duchess, and Lady Adelaide lingered a little with him before dressing, and he told the latter he had brought <i>Le Noir</i>, and she was to command where his clarionet was to be played.

When the whole party assembled before dinner, proper introductions took place, and Lord St. Leonard joined Lady Clara and Miss Helen Willoughby in a bay window, and had some laughter.
