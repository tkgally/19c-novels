Miss O’Brian laughed heartily at the idea; poor Lady Honiton was too deeply lacerated to enjoy even her own joke.

But she observed at dinner, that Miss O’Brian referred to General Clayton as to the wholesomeness of every dish, and he rivalled Sancho Panza’s physician in his prohibitions. Lady Clara not only enjoyed the scene, but promoted it, by temptations in opposition to this system, well knowing Miss O’Brian’s taste for French dishes and high cookery.
