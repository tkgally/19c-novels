She never married; and having on her father’s death refused to claim his personal property, which he had left her, his estates going to the heir at law, Lady Gertrude succeeded to what her niece refused to put in her claim for.

The latter part of the Comtesse’s life was occupied in founding, with the Pope’s sanction, a religious house for twelve ladies, and she had the pleasure of seeing the Chanoiness Reinebie the first head of <i>Rosenberg Priory</i>.
