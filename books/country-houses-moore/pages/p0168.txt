He mounted his horse early, and arrived at Mrs. Graham’s soon after his breakfast; he found her alone, but his whole appearance was of a man hurried in body and mind, beyond “his wont:” in general, his deportment was that of a quiet methodical man of business; and as the affairs persons of his profession transact are not their own, they occasion no overwhelming anxiety for their subsequent event. It has been said, and believed, of Mr. Pitt, that, arduous as was the task of prime minister in such turbulent times, he never lost a night’s rest by his official anxieties.
