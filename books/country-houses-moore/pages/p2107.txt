“This you may think explains the mystery, but I own it makes me quite sick to be baffled by such a set; but I know there is no contending against professionals, so I will go down to Beaumont Abbey directly, and forget it all amongst my roses.”

“Fye! fye!” cried Miss Talbot, “don’t be so poltroon: with your means, you may do any thing, and every thing; recollect this is only your first year and it takes three years in the most ordinary walk of good society, to get up a London acquaintance: go on, and you’ll prosper.”

“No, no! the dream is over!”
