“I own it was an absurd observation, but he gave me the idea of a ‘pigeon-winged head’ in Ansty’s Bath Guide; but I will maintain in the more obvious sense you have taken it, they are <i>pigeoning</i> him by making all they can out of his lungs. Though these observations were made in a sort of whisper, Lord John would, without any scruple, have said it all aloud, but as he did not, it served to confirm Lady Tintern’s impression of the nature of his conversation.
