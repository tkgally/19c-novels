“Oh, do not call it <i>admiration</i>, dearest Lady Tintern, you had better call it <i>persecution</i>.”

Lady Tintern smiled at the simplicity and earnestness with which this was said, but as she wished Emma good night, the latter burst into tears.

“What is the matter, my dear,” said Lady Tintern, in a most tender and feeling voice, “are you unhappy?”

“No indeed, Madam, I have every thing to make me happy—only—Lord John will——”

“What my dear?”

“Nothing ma’am, only—talk to me.”
