“At least, my dear mother,” said Mrs. Forester, “you can not accuse yourself of committing <i>me</i> in this way; perhaps,” she added, with a sigh, “it would have been better if you had made the choice for me.”

“My dear child,” said Mrs. Villiars, “I always wished you and your brother to please yourselves, do not <i>you</i> give me reason, as I fear he has done, to regret my indulgence.”
