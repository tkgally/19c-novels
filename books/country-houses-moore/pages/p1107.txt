Passing the Tower just as the elephant was restored to its casket, Lady Clara, exclaimed,

“What is that great building? Is it Newgate?”

“It has often been a prison,” replied Colonel Blackburn, “though built as a place of defence, it is the Tower; look, Lady Clara, at that turret where Anna Boleyn passed the night before her exectiuon.”

The lately admired elephant produced a concatenation of ideas in Miss Fanny Williams’s head, who asked Lady Clara,

“If she had ever seen the Tower, or the animals kept there?”

Lord St. Leonard, who loved an impromptu of that sort, immediately proposed their landing <i>now</i> to see it, as Lady Clara expressed a great desire to do so; this proposal was seconded by Miss Fanny Williams, who said,
