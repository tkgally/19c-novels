“I hope her mother’s influence, and yours, my dear madam, in a scene of sickness, may make some impression on her: she idolizes her brother; perhaps it might break through connexions, and conduct, so little creditable to herself, and her family.”

“Alas!” replied Miss Shirley, “her mother’s indulgence and blindness to the faults of her children, give me little hopes from her; but I will not shrink from the painful task, if I can do any good.”

After communicating the name of the person who was now “lord of the ascendant,” as well as told those who had previously been so favoured, Mr. Melbourne took his leave.
