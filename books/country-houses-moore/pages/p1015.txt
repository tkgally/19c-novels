# Chapter XII.

As much of the duty of the toilette was to be dispensed with on the day of the play, as the ladies were to change their dresses immediately after dinner for their theatrical costume; Emma therefore ventured, when she reached her room, to throw herself into a chair, and think over her conduct for the last hour, and consider what it ought to be for the two next; but in the multitude of recollections that rushed on her mind, she could decide on nothing beyond behaving cool to Lord John; she had scarcely fortified herself in this resolution, and without considering how she was to practise it, the most important point, when the maid who usually attended her, came to say that dinner was serving up.
