I never shall forget him! he came fresh from the review, I don’t think he had washed the dust off his face, and he had not been in my rooms five minutes, when all crowded round him: he raved like a madman, described all the quarrels he had on the ground with people who unavoidably pushed up against him, and declared he had at least six duels to fight before breakfast next day, and at last darted off like a sky-rocket, to find seconds.” The company laughed at this description.

“I dare say I can name the man,” said Mr. Dallas, “Terrence Mac Dermot.”

“Oh,” replied Mrs. Gordon, “I believe he had some such detestable name.”
