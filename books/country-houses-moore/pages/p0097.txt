Whilst contemplating this basilisk, under whose eyes, like that of the fabled monster, its victims seemed to cower, and almost fall into its mouth; an unlucky mouse ran across the room, it roused Vixen, and by the noise and bustle she made in catching her wretched prey, completely awoke Frederic.
