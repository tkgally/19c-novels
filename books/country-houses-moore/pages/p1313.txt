These young ladies, <i>new</i> to the trade of recommending their wares, were rather nervous and diffident, but the Duchess’s kind admiration was encouraging, and she asked for two chairs, saying, she and Lady Adelaide would help sell the things.

Amongst other articles was a black velvet waistcoat embroidered with a border of foxes in gold. The Duchess asked what price was put on it, and when told five guineas,

Exclaimed, Five guineas!

Miss Dowbiggin very modestly said, “If your Grace thinks it too much, we will lower it, but the materials are expensive, and the work troublesome, and we thought, as it was for a charity, we might go a little beyond the shop price.”

“Oh dear!” said the Duchess, “I do not mean it is too <i>much</i>, it is a vast deal too <i>little</i>, it must be twenty guineas.”

Miss Dowbiggin timidly said, “she was afraid that would be far beyond anything they could get.”
