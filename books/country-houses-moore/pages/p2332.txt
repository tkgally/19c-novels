Caroline looked rather flushed, and on Miss Shirley saying,

“Who have you been with?”

Did not answer the question. Her aunt repeated it, and Caroline did not seem to comprehend what she was saying, and answered

“I don’t know.”

“My dear Caroline, what has happened to you? You do not seem to know what I have been saying, or what you have been saying yourself.”

This roused her: going so close to her aunt that Mrs. Villiars, who, was very busy trying to take up a fallen stitch in her knitting, could not hear her, she said:

“I will tell you all when we are alone.”
