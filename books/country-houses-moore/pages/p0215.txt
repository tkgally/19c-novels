A flood of tears followed this soliloquy, they always bring relief in vexation, as in sorrow, and looking for the key of her work-box did her good also; she began, before she had found this precious paper, to fancy that she was a little tired and nervous with all the exertions of the day, and therefore disposed to see all <i>en noir</i>. The paper found, she kissed it, and felt it had some power of protection. We must edify our readers by transcribing its contents.

Mem., No. 1.—To attend in every thing to Lord and Lady Tintern’s wishes, particularly to those of the latter.

Mem. 2.—If you want advice, and cannot get it from them, apply to the governess.
