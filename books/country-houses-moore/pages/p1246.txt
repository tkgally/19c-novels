When Lord Honiton had read the letter which was taken to him by Lady Louisa, he said,

“Oh, by all means ask Sir Charles and Lady Crofton, and offer them beds; they are old friends of mine, though it is long since we met; I shall be very glad to see [[corr them.|them.”]]

Immediately after the present party had finished a breakfast which sometimes in such houses is <i>unlimited</i>, and may last from ten to one, the Dean of Carlisle and his two daughters (Miss Williamsons), his niece, Miss Hughes, and her brother, Captain Hughes, arrived; and whilst the greeting with the ladies was going on, Col. Neville asked Lord Honiton how late he might catch the mail at Sandlegate?

“Not after half past three,” was the answer, “but must you go to town to-day?”

“I am afraid I cannot delay it,” replied Col. Neville.
