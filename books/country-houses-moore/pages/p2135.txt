“Yes, and is of most infinite use: you have no idea of the applications I have; look at that pile of letters; they are all begging letters, or applications for subscriptions; not a church in the kingdom is to be built, or a school established, but I am sent to: but my excellent friend understands the business of the poor so well, I leave all to her; and I cannot be ungrateful for all her long attentions to me.”

“And has not her <i>sejour</i> with you, and the position you have given her by it, had its reward? Don’t be so scrupulous; get your set of society, and let her get her’s: nothing, nothing else will do, believe me; so think upon it—and <i>act</i> upon it—and fare ye well!”
