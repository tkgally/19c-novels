“My dear Juliet,” exclaimed Lady Honiton, “how glad I am to see you, you are the very person whose assistance I want, and I was just going to send George for you.”

“Too happy Lady Honiton to be devoted to you, but what is the matter? for you really look worried to death, I never saw any body so nervous.”

“There is reason enough! do my dear creature get me my salts from that table, and then you shall hear all; for I know I shall have your pity.
