It has been said that time only <i>aggravates</i> and does not <i>cure</i> anything; but certainly grief and suspense may be <i>alleviated</i> by time.

Lord Honiton had frequent letters from General Clayton, and though no regular accounts had come of Colonel Neville’s being out of danger, yet so eagerly do people look out for promotion, that if no regular mail, or official account had been received from Malta, there had been private letters, and no mention made of Colonel Neville’s <i>death</i> by those who might have profited by it; he certainly therefore was not dead, and those fevers are life, or death, so that his hopes were reviving, and General Clayton said, when he had good news to send, he should soon follow it.
