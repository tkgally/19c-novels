The subject would bear no farther discussion, and both parties made a pause; when Lady Honiton simply asked,

“May I tell the girls that you consent to take Clara, and leave us?”

“Yes.”

“And may we hope you intend to keep your promise of giving them an Archery meeting, at Inglewood?”

“Certainly, they may depend on that: it is a county affair, and may be useful to Ottery’s interest; I only bargain that they do not desire to have it before his arrival, which I hope will be in August.”

As Lord Honiton put his hand on the lock of the door, he added,
