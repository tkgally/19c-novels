[[corr There|“There]] is, I must confess, one place, that no asking will gain admittance to, that is so exclusive, I mean B—— house; the D—— has drawn his line, and not even his nearest relation can venture to ask to have it extended.”

“His line is a good long one, for it always encircles five hundred persons; but if I was in his place,” said Mrs. Beaumont, “I should do as he does, I would not be intruded on.”
