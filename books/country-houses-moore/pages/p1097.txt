When you have answered all these notes, give them to George, and he will take them.”

“But, Lady Honiton, though I know who will <i>not</i> be of your party, you have not told me who <i>will</i>, and I should like to know, and to settle our plans of co-operation.”

“I can soon tell you that. The boat holds twenty, but to get twenty, I have asked between forty and fifty; more than half always disappoint. First, for the women: Mrs. Murray Williams and her two daughters; they are quiet harmless souls—only attract what others leave.
