“There can only,” replied Lord Ottery, “be one road to life eternal; if protestants wander from it, it is at their own peril—so large a body of mankind have lived and died in the catholic faith, there is more reason to think the <i>many</i> are right, than the few.”

“We are,” said Lord Honiton, “forbid in scripture, ‘to follow the multitude to do evil,’ so that number is no proof of right; and the ignorance in which all catholics, excepting the priests, are kept, deprives them of the power of deciding for themselves between right and wrong. The book of knowledge, the <i>Word of God</i>, is a sealed book to them.”

“Is it not safer,” said Lord Ottery, “that a book with so many doubtful passages, should be withheld from a general perusal?”
