As Lady Clara came down prepared for her ride, she met her mother, who said,

“I don’t know what we shall do with all these odd people we are to have on Saturday, could not you get up some <i>charades</i> or <i>des tableaux vivants</i>, if you really will play no tricks on Mrs. De Clifford, I give you leave to consult her and Mr. Blinco about this; they are quite <i>au fait</i> to the thing. Oh, here is Louisa! I shall give you into her charge, that you may not commit me or yourself.”

Lady Clara returned, charmed with the success of her visit, but sworn to secresy as to Mrs. De Clifford’s <i>fancy dress</i>, and the <i>des tableaux vivant</i>, but Lady Louisa assured her mother there would be nothing extravagant or absurd in either.
