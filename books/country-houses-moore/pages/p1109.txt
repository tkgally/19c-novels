“Let us sail under orders,” said Sir Henry Littledale, “no steering right else.”

The project for seeing the Tower being negatived, Colonel Blackburn said,

“I am glad it is so determined, for I never like to see those miserable beasts in their unnatural and languid state of existence; after having so often seen them in all their native grandeur and triumph, and I always long to knock those so exhibited at head, and put them out of their misery.”

“By the bye, Blackburn,” said Lord St. Leonard, “I suppose you have often gone elephant and tiger hunting in India?”

“If you mean, my lord, that I have hunted elephants as well as tigers, I never have been on any party in pursuit of wild elephants; but I have hunted tigers on the back of an elephant, frequently.”

“Oh! oh! I did not know the difference,” replied Lord St. Leonard, “but I suppose tiger-hunting must be fine sport, only perched up on the back of such a monstrous creature and shut in a sort of box, as I have seen in pictures, must be tantalizing work.”
