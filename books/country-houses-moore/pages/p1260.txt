# Chapter XII.

T<sc>here</sc> are various ways of doing the same thing; some more troublesome than others. In London, a lady may open her house to her “five hundred, or five thousand” friends, and prove she, “hates their coming,” by not noticing a tenth part of them; if she appears near the door of one of the rooms, she is distinguished by those to whom she wishes to show herself as the mistress of the house, but it is not necessary all her company <i>should</i> see her. Nay, we have known instances of persons of high rank giving an <i>At Home</i>, and falsifying it by going out themselves; but their company knew it not, and were perfectly satisfied with their entertainment.
