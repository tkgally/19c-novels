“Well, Miss Emma,” said he, “so you have been playing truant, and something else, too, I hear; how did you get on with all those lords and ladies? devilish proud some of them—you had the old Earl of Downham and his daughter, hadn’t you?”

“And some blades of fashion,” said Mr. James Martin, “I wonder if you had any of the steeple chacers?”

Emma did not know.
