But in return to those who found a place at their table, or in their carriage, or who lent her a footman, she did a thousand little offices that belonged to no one, and which no one liked to undertake: her kindest friend was Lady Honiton, to her discomforts she was a ready listener, and in her difficulties a useful coadjutor.

There are many such hangers on the skirts of rank and fashion; and as Miss O’Brian had extensive connexions she had constant employment, and fancied herself a most important personage, trusted with <i>delicate</i> commissions which no common skill could execute, and charged with secret negotiations no common head could carry.
