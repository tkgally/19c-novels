“Don’t interrupt me, for I am in haste,—Mrs. Beaumont and Miss Phillips.”

“Bad enough!”

“Major Mac Venom: will he please you better?”

“Yes, if he is in a tolerably good humour, and has neither bile nor gout about him.”

“Your Father has asked General Dorozenski, who, with a Polish Count, is making a tour of the Manufacturing district, to take us in their way; but they are uncertain as to time.”

“Oh, pretty well! Mac Venom is a regular humbug, he will make something of the Russian bear, if the Pole is impracticable.”

“Sir Thomas asked him on purpose to explain what they had been seeing, as he is so great a mechanist, and some young men have been asked also, but I don’t exactly recollect who comes; and I had forgot Mrs. Elenor Latour and Miss Grey.”
