# Chapter XI.

T<sc>he</sc> intimacy between Mr. Graham and Caroline imperceptibly increased, as they do in such a place of frequent meeting, as that they were now in.

Returning one day from an airing with Mrs. Villiars, as Miss Shirley was getting out of the carriage, some boys, passing in the street, frightened the horses, they moved, and she fell on to the pavement; the servant at the carriage door broke her fall, but she fell with her foot under her, and got up in the greatest pain; a surgeon, who was sent for, feared a small bone in the ancle was broke, and pronounced it would be some weeks before she would be able to walk.
