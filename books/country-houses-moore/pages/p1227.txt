“You consider yourself at least, a very important person at Carden,” said Lady Honiton, “but all I beg is, that your protegée is not made so ridiculous we shall be ashamed to acknowledge her as an acquaintance; and if you promise me to be discreet in this respect, you may, from me, invite Mr. and Mrs. De Clifford and Mr. Blinco to dine here on Saturday; and we must have the Bishop of Carlisle, he is such an old friend of Sir Henry Willoughby’s. I heartily wish these foolish people at Carlisle had not fixed on Monday for their ball; though, indeed, I don’t see how they could have done otherwise, when they learnt the day <i>I</i> had chosen for the archery.
