These worthies having settled the point, that neither envied Mr. Dallas, went in to dress, though both gave a specimen of the ruling passion of the present day amongst young men, <i>avarice</i>, and the most selfish species of avarice; they covet what will contribute to their own <i>individual</i> gratification; it may be less sordid than that of the miser, who heaps up his store without a heart to spend it, but it is not one jot a better passion, and leads to many vices which even the other is exempt from, for it has generally gaming added to all other sorts of dissipation in its train.
