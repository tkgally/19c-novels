# Chapter VI.

[[subtitle]] Sunday.

S<sc>ir</sc> Thomas and Lady Vernon were amongst the number of old fashioned people who lament that the habits of the present day often make evening family prayers <i>impossible</i>, for half the under servants in a large [[corr etsablishment|establishment]] are gone to bed before the dining room is cleared. But still they kept to morning prayers.
