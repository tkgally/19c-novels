# Chapter VII.

T<sc>he</sc> following morning Colonel Wetheral accompanied the surgeon, or rather brought him in his gig; the former breakfasted with Mrs. Villiars and her family, whilst the latter remained with his patient, and he gratified his hostess very much by repeating the anxious enquiries made after her son, not only by all his brother officers, but by many of the privates in his company, by whom he was greatly beloved; he also brought a spaniel belonging to Captain Villiars, who he said was so wretched without his master, he knew not what to do with him.
