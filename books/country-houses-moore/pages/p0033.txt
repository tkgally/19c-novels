The dinner was devoured and discussed in the usual way, General Dorozenski ate, drank, talked and laughed; gave accounts of the magnificent, yet peculiar, way of life amongst the great manufacturers of Manchester, Preston, and Liverpool; and told some good stories of the cotton spinners. Dessert passed, and Frederic had only remarked of his neighbour, Miss Latour, that she had good taste in the wine she chose after fish, that her voice was certainly melliffluous, and, as the cook had done his best, things rather improved with him. There is nothing like a capital dinner for putting people in a good humour, even if they are not professed epicures. “Lie lightly on him earth who” reduced Gastronomy to a science, theoretical, practical, experimental, and conversational, for how many, otherwise <i>dull</i>, dinners does that make interesting and agreeable!
