# Chapter XV.

<sc>There</sc> was a sort of irregular breakfast before the party set off for Rose Castle, there they found a most hospitable one.

Lord Ottery joined neither, but his father depended on his appearing at Carlisle.

Accompanied by the Dean’s family, they all set off for the town hall, where the bazaar was held, and arrived when the room was more than half full. Great pains had been taken, and a good deal of taste displayed, in decorating the respective counters with pink, yellow, and blue calico, and a number of very pretty and ingenious articles were displayed.
