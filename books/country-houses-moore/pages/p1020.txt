Emma was exactly at that point, she felt the force of both sarcasms, but luckily also, she did not feel forlorn; Mr. Duprée seemed in some degree implicated in them, and he was able to defend both her and himself, so the scale turned on the laughing side, though her laugh was almost hysterical, yet still it was a laugh, and one that touched Lord John to the quick.

“That girl’s a thorough coquette,” said he to himself, “like the rest of her sex. I thought her above that, but I’ll care no more about her.”
