In short, what else could the Brownites say, now that the star of Smith was so decidedly on the ascendant—the whole family in the height of prosperity, and a daughter about to be married to the very hero whom the Brownites thought all their own?

Then it began to be whispered about that not only had Mr. Brown lost his all, but that he had debts, and ominous looks were exchanged, and many individuals who had reckoned to the last on the solvency of William Brown, began to feel very uncomfortable.
