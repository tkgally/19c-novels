And, accordingly, she did get over it wonderfully; she extolled the Christian virtues of her husband and son, and magnified the constancy and generosity of Edgar. Of old Mr. Brown she said nothing; but in speaking of Hannah she echoed the sentiments of Miss Richards, adding, that she was amazingly clever, and might have made a fortune as an artist; and that, for her part, though Hannah had not the style of her own girls, she thought her very pretty.
