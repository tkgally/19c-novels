And so the flirtation with Miss Westcote, which had languished during the absence of Hannah Brown, perhaps on account of the hot weather, began again afresh with renewed vigour: and in a short time, as Miss Wellby’s news grew stale, had superseded the tale of the Welsh clergyman in public interest. Everybody expected every day to hear that it was going to be a match, and Mrs. Smith and her daughters were plied with many hints and questions.
