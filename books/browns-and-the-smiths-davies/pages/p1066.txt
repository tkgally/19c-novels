“If Mr. Edwards himself, or anyone on his behalf, has spoken to you on this subject, will you, papa, tell them to drop it for ever. I will never marry Mr. Edwards.” And Hannah, as she finished speaking, rushed out of the room.

Mr. Brown’s head fell on his breast, and he uttered a deep groan.

Hannah, poor girl, in the meantime was bolted into her own bedroom, crying bitterly. She had not shed tears before, since her trip into Wales. She felt both sad and bitter, and she thought, “How little he understands the sacrifice I made for him! How little any of them understand! Men are all alike, but they should not judge a woman’s feelings by their own.”
