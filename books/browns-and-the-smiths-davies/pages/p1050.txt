But possibly it had an influence, for at Mrs. Westcote’s he was certainly very chatty and agreeable, and entirely effaced from Miss Westcote’s mind any unfavourable impression he might have made the previous evening. Of course, Hannah Brown and the Welsh clergyman were talked of, and their engagement seemed to be considered a settled thing. Again the two elder Miss Smiths went into raptures over his personal appearance, and Laura Victoria expressed a desire to see him, as, from what her sisters said, he must be just the man she should admire.
