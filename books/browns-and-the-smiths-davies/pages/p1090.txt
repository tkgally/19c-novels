And yet, if Hannah had reflected, she must have remembered her father had not so acted himself. His own wife had been long dead, but he had brought home no step-mother to Hannah. Were she and her father more like one another than she had ever imagined?

It had never struck her that they were like one another, and it did not strike her now.

To her great relief Mr. Brown did not that evening renew the subject of Mr. Edwards, and yet she should have liked to have known if her father had seen him and had told him her feelings. She should have liked to have known how he had borne it. She wondered if she should see him again, and if she did, what he would say? Up
