He was a proper hero for the kind of romances Miss Clara read and loved, and in the days of which I write, these were the modern romances. But though those days are not very ancient, they are not modern now. We live so fast now, and our fashions revolve so quickly, that even the fashions of the celebrated “Minerva Press,” which were the jest of our younger days, seem to have come uppermost again.

Gallant soldiers, interesting clergymen, eccentric and kind-hearted misanthropes, have had their day, like the fair orphans, the noble-minded heiresses, the intellectual, not very pretty women, it was the fashion to mate them with.
