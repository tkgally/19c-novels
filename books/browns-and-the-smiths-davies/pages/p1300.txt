And, if at any time these appeared peculiarly trying, a glance at Hannah’s imploring face would check him at once, and he would think how the possession of that treasure overpaid him for all petty trials, and how he could bear anything rather than to bring a cloud on that face. For Edgar Smith, with all his faults and foibles, was a gentleman, in the best sense of the word. Thus, though he and Hannah were happy, they had their share of mortal cares. Among these might be mentioned the little grudges and affronts which were frequently manifested by the Tudor Lodge party, more especially by Mrs. Smith, senior.
