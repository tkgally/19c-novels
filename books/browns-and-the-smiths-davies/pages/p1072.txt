“Oh, my dear sir, I wish you had only told me first! Men do not know how to manage these things.”

Poor Mr. Brown’s face got longer and longer. He had come for comfort, and some how or other it seemed that he deserved blame. But he could not quite see this himself.

“I don’t know what you mean about managing, Clara. The girl either likes the young man, or she does not, and she says she does not.”
