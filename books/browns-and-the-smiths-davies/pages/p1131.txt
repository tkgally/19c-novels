Where do young lawyers and new firms get all that furniture, so old and shabby,—yet in that exact stage of antiquity which stops short of decay?

But, leaving this question as an insoluble mystery to the uninitiated, I am going to astonish you, my dear young lady, with a further assertion of my belief:—not only have these attorneys once been romantic, but some of them are romantic still.
