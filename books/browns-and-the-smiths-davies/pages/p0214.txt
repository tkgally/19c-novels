Here Edgar turned fiercely round, and confronted Mr. Brown with angry eyes. Edgar, though he was not quite blind to family faults, like all the Smiths, had strong family affections. But if he intended to speak, Hannah prevented him.

“Father,” she said, firmly, “you are mistaken. He would have loved me all the same, if I had not had sixpence. I know he would.”

“Thank you, Hannah—thank you for ever,” said the lover, earnestly.

“You have great confidence in your own charms, it appears,” said Mr. Brown.

“No, father; but I have great confidence in Mr. Smith’s sincerity.”
