# Chapter VII.
[[subtitle]] Miss Richards’ Tea-Party.

H<sc>annah</sc> B<sc>rown</sc>’s general health had quite recovered its ordinary tone before the day of Miss Richards’ tea-party. Her arm was still in a sling, but Mr. Splint said it would soon be quite well. It was a fine evening late in June, so that Hannah and Miss Wellby, who were to go together, decided on walking.
