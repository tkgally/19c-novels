Poor Hannah felt miserably depressed; but first hopes are not, after all, so easily crushed. Hannah began to think of what Miss Richards was reported to have said about Christian forgiveness. Were forgiveness and good sense so incompatible? And if forgiveness did not mean reconciliation and mutual oblivion of offence, what did it mean?

Hannah was not naturally a very bold thinker, but she did begin to suspect that common sense was either a misnomer or a different thing from good sense.
