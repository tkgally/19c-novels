“Hannah Brown, perhaps,” said Mary, pettishly.

“Hannah Brown!” was Mrs. Westcote’s contemptuous exclamation.

“Well, mamma! you know when I told you long ago how attentive he was to Hannah Brown, you would not believe me, and I was not so far wrong then.”

But though Mary Westcote said this, she did not quite actually believe it. “No, he never could think of Hannah Brown, so poor and so dowdy; and when she had refused him, too!”

For, though Mary would not allow it in public, she had a latent belief that he had been rejected by her rival.
