“I will take two of these,” said Edgar Smith—“Goslingford and Barley Bridge—at five guineas each. I think them cheap; and, Mr. Bradley, as Miss Brown has not told her acquaintances in her own neighbourhood, I should be obliged to you if you did not mention who was the purchaser.”

Good Mr. Bradley’s face brightened with pleasure.

“I shall gladly do as you wish,” he said, “and I am much obliged to you. Where shall I send the drawings?”
