I have met them all before.” And there was again in Hannah a touch of pride. “Indeed, lately, and especially to-day, I have had so much to feel and to think about, that all these things seem to have gone far back into the past. I feel getting quite old, Aunt Clara. It will be quite pleasant to settle down into the calm of life.”

“Nonsense!” cried Miss Clara, with angry emphasis, “you’ll have plenty of lovers yet, Hannah, and be married, too.”
