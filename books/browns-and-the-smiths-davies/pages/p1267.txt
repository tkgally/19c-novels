“How cross he is,” said Venetia, “about being teased! Can he and Mary Westcote have had a quarrel?”

“Oh, no!” cried Julietta, “I daresay it is the heat, and because mamma was annoyed about the salmon, and the trimmings of her dress.”

“I don’t think it can be quite settled, or he would have told us,” said Venetia, “though I don’t quite understand why it is not, as, I am sure, Mary is ready enough.”

“I think it is settled,” said her sister, “and that he will not tell us because we laugh at him. Well, I like the match on the whole, though Mary will never set the Thames on fire.”
