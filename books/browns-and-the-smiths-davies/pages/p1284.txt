“I was so certain, only this morning,” said Hannah, “that you were going to marry Mary Westcote.”

To which Edgar made some answer, much more flattering to Hannah than to the lady to whom she had referred.

“But how much you have been with her! I—I hope she is not disappointed.”

“If she should be, Hannah, will you act the part of a generous-minded heroine, and give me up to her?”

Hannah could not help smiling, though she did not quite approve.
