“It is well for you, Harriet, to be able to make such presents,” said Miss Wellby, who, though much pleased, could never resist an opportunity of a hit at her old friend, and was moreover slightly jealous that she had been able to do more for Hannah than she could afford herself. “Do you intend to be as liberal to all the young people married in Goslingford?”

“No, Clara, I do not; but I wish to testify both to the Browns and the Smiths my approbation of this match. The Browns behaved most honourably at the time of poor Mr. Brown’s losses. The Smiths now are acting the part of true Christians, as I have always thought them. I do not know a finer natural character than Hannah Brown, and I rejoice to think she is so soon to become one of a Christian family.”
