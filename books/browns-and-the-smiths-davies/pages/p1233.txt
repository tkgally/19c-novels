[[cont]]Bradley added, the gain might not be great, perhaps nothing; but that, at any rate, it would make her known, and might sell the originals.

Hannah was much pleased by this letter. It was pleasant to her to think that she could do something—to feel that the old nightmare sensation of incompetence was passing away. Then perhaps she might make a little money, or it might lead to her making a little at some other time.
