“I am hurt that you should say so, Miss Brown; you then will never think of these to me happy evenings?”

“Yes I shall; but I meant, you must have memories of much deeper interest.”

“You do not believe then in consolation?”

She was saved the difficulty of answering by the appearance of Miss Clara, who, impatient of solitude, had climbed part of the way to meet them.
