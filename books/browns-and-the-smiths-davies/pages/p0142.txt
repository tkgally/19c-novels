[[cont]]Mr. Brown, Miss Wellby, and their party; and Miss Clara, who had always courage equal to the occasion, attacked Mr Greenfield for his want of churchmanship. He stoutly denied the charge.

“We have two enemies to oppose,” said he, “sin and schism; but as I believe schism to be a mere offshoot of sin, I think it best to attack the enemy at head-quarters. Depend upon it, Miss Wellby, when there is no sin, there will be no schism.”

And years after, when Miss Wellby was congratulating him on the accession of a whole family to the church-communion, he said, “You see, Miss Wellby, I have not been such a bad churchman after all.”
