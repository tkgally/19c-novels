“On the contrary, I think it very amusing.”

“Oh, Clara! Clara! But I know you better, and how truthful you are.”

“That is the very reason I am amused. But about Hannah. I tell you, Harriet, because I know you can keep a secret, that I am sure this young man will propose to her, and I hope she will take him.”

“So do I; as he is a good young man. Is he a member of the Evangelical Alliance?”

“Oh, dear me, no. He is rather a High Churchman.”

Miss Richards’ countenance fell, then rallied a little.

“It is not a subject to jest on, Clara. I hope you are only saying so to tease me.”
