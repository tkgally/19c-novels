And thus father and daughter lived and loved—yes, loved; for either would have died for the other. But their mutual love added little to the happiness of either. In an economico-affectionate point of view, it was a sheer waste of the precious material, and no “Moral Wealth of Nations” had shown them how it might be turned to account.
