If Mr. Brown had not refused his consent, then Hannah’s conduct had really been that of a heartless jilt—quite infamous; and on all sides arose to this effect quite a Smithite chorus—almost the only Smithites who did not join in it being Mrs. Westcote and her daughter. Mary Westcote was very certain Hannah Brown would have given her eyes for Edgar Smith. <i>She</i> refuse him of her own free will! “Don’t tell her.” Miss Westcote did not give herself much concern about being consistent.
