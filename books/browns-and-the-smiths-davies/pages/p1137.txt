Mr. Smith proposed he should take pot-luck with them, and the invitation being warmly seconded by all the ladies, he was nothing loath to accept it. The outer world was so miserable, and his solitary fire-side at Halton so dull. He felt it an additional advantage, too, that Mr. Edgar Smith was not to be at home. He was in London on a short holiday.

Mr. Edwards was surprised, on getting home to Halton, at night, to find how late it was; and also to find the letter he had written to Mr. Martin, in the morning, still lying on his chimney-piece, whence, in his absence of mind, he had forgotten to take it to post.
