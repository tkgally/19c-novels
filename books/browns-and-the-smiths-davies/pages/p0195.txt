Hannah Brown, in the meantime, was dressing for the ball. Aunt Clara arrived to superintend the completion of her toilet, and to act as her <i>chaperone</i>, for in spite of her annoyance of the previous day, and her dread of what the evening might bring forth, a ball toilet, and Hannah Brown’s ball toilet, was a subject of too engrossing interest not for the moment to supersede all other considerations.
