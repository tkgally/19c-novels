No wonder, when the struggle to keep up during the day was succeeded, as soon as she laid her head on her pillow at night, by bursts of tears and hours of wakeful misery.

It must not be supposed that Hannah had no religion. She did pray, poor girl! and with fervour, and she was comforted often by the hope of a world where there would be no cruel quarrels, no blank uselessness; but, in the meantime, life seemed so long, so tedious!
