To be in love yourself generally lessens your chance of making another in love with you, perhaps because we all prefer that which is difficult of attainment. A woman without a heart is the most likely woman to have a dozen lovers—a dozen rivals for that which does not exist.

Fortunately for himself, Mr. Edwards hitherto had not had much opportunity for exciting the admiration of the other sex. He was a younger son of a Welsh squire of decayed fortunes, and had been educated in retirement almost as great as that which was now his lot.
