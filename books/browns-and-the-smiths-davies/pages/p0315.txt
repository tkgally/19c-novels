“Now, Hannah, don’t be a goose, and throw away the substance for the shadow, like the dog in the fable.”

“Mr. Edwards’ memory is shorter than mine,” said Hannah, again returning to the old idea.

“You mean, my dear, Mr. Edwards is more submissive to the will of Providence, and more ready to make the best of things.”

Hannah said nothing; but Miss Clara’s was, to say the least, a new way of putting it, and Hannah, like most of Miss Wellby’s friends, was obliged to acknowledge, at times, that that lady’s random shots went as straight to the mark as if they had been aimed with the utmost precision.
