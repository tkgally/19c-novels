“I am sure I don’t know.”

“But I know, Hannah, if this is not put a stop to, it will break your heart. You have not the spirit of some other people. And it went hard enough with them, too. But that is an old story, gone and done long ago.”

“But what am I to do, Aunt Clara?”

“Don’t encourage him.”

“I will not; but I cannot be uncivil to him. No one else has ever been so kind to me.”

“Some kindness is no kindness, Hannah. And now remember; you are warned!”
