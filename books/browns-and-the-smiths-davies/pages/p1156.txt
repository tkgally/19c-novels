“No, not at all. I am so accustomed to the girls being admired, that it never strikes me, as a consequence, that they are going to be married. I thought, too, Edwards’ affections were engaged elsewhere. All the world said so.” Edgar spoke in a firm, unmoved tone.

“The world, I suppose, spoke nonsense, as it often does,” said Mrs. Smith, colouring slightly, and in a half petulant tone.

“I must confess, then, I took the same view as the rest of the world; and I rather wonder that Laura can be quite satisfied with being second best.”

“Second best! Upon my word, Edgar, a more devoted young man—but you think nobody can have a lover but Hannah Brown.”
