It was once more the summer time, the “leafy month of June,” and very warm for the season. The engravings from Hannah’s drawings were in progress, though the matter was still kept quiet in Goslingford. Little consultations with Mr. Bradley and Mr. Wilkins, the president of the Archæological Society, occasionally, though not very often, took Hannah to Buttonborough. Hannah had set off one morning after breakfast, and reached Buttonborough early. She had a good deal of shopping to do in that part of the town near the station, and as Mr. Bradley’s was at some distance, she did her other errands first.
