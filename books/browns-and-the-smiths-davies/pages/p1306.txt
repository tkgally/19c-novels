Then, even where the united families are not Browns and Smiths, how, in a world where the inhabitants are not angels, can they be expected to see eye to eye or feel heart to heart? Mrs. Jones will ever be of opinion that her son is more attentive to his wife’s family than to his own; and Mrs. Robinson, that her daughter has got into the foolish, extravagant ways of the Joneses.
