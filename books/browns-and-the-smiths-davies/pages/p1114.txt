Hannah heard now. There was something in the subject and something in the manner which alarmed her. She turned round quickly with a flushed and anxious face.

“But I will not speak on the subject if it annoys you, Miss Brown.”

“Oh, pray don’t.”

“I only wish to ask if I may be allowed to have the privilege of seeing you occasionally. I know time is required; but if I may only try, if I have only but a spark of hope, half a lifetime——”

“Oh, Mr. Edwards! I thought my father had told you—you must think of me no longer.”

“I have not seen Mr. Brown since—since——”
