And then Hannah began to speak, and to join warmly in the admiration; for that ancient church, and those venerable trees, had early impressed the girl’s imagination, and she tasted with joy this first draught of sympathy in feelings as old as her memory. Clara, too, admired the church, but it was because it was the church; and, in the presence of a dissenter, more especially, she felt a sort of personal triumph in the acknowledged superiority of its architecture and antiquity.

The triumph was, perhaps, a little spoiled by the fact that Edgar did not seem altogether to regard it as a triumph, but as a matter of fact universally acknowledged.
