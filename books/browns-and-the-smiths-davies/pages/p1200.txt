# Chapter X.
[[subtitle]] Mr. and Mrs. Edward Edwards.

A<sc>n</sc> hour had not passed after the departure of Mr. Greenfield and Mr. Splint, ere Miss Clara Wellby arrived at the Old Red House. She had not been able to wait another instant.

How excited she was, and how fervently she embraced Hannah and shook hands with Mr. Brown may be imagined by those who have known Miss Wellby.
