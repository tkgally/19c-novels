“You have no objection, have you, father?” said Hannah, “for it is not too late yet to put a stop to the business.”

“Objection! my dear; I can never have an objection to anything you wish to do. If you can make a little money in any way, after giving me all you possessed, how can I be otherwise than glad? I had no idea you were so clever, and you make no display.”
