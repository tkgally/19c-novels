“I declare, Edgar, it’s no use asking you anything. You seem to have left your wits at Buttonborough. Did you see anybody there you knew?”

“Mrs. and Miss Westcote, I met.”

Mrs. Smith’s face relaxed into a smile.

“Ah!” she said; “well, as that was the case, I suppose I must forgive you forgetting to order the salmon, and to buy those things at Slade’s, though how my dress is to be made without them I am sure I don’t know.”
