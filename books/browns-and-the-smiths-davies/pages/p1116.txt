# Chapter VI.

[[subtitle]] Miss Wellby’s Trials.

I <sc>shall</sc> not attempt to describe Miss Wellby’s sensations when she received a melancholy epistle from Mr. Edwards, that same evening, telling her of his final rejection by Hannah Brown, and of how impossible even he felt it to hope any longer.
