“I cannot see what good end it would answer if you could, and I am sure you cannot in this hot sun, and with your bruises, for you must be bruised, though excitement has hitherto prevented your feeling it.”

Poor Hannah looked so anxious and so distressed that her companion good-naturedly began to think how he could help [[corr her|her.]]

“Stay,” he said, “there is a farmhouse about half a mile from here, and the farmer keeps some sort of vehicle. Perhaps he will lend it to us—shall I go and ask him?”

“Oh, thank you, thank you! How kind you are! You see I am the only one left.”

“Then come here, and wait till I come back.”
