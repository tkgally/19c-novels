She hardly dared to say what she thought; but, accustomed to bring the highest motives into play on the minutest occasions, she said, with the same kind of courage which would have led her to martyrdom, but in as soothing a manner as she was capable—

“My dear Clara! I do not quite see. Are you angry with Edgar Smith for saving a child’s life at the risk of his own?”

Now, this was a sort of unconscious <i>reductio ad absurdum</i>, which only made Miss Wellby more angry.
