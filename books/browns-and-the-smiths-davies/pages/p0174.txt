Old Brown had for many years—ever since Mrs. Brown’s death—given up promiscuous party-giving and party-going. About once a-year he entertained his own old friends, and year by year these were diminishing in number. Often Miss Wellby dropped in to tea; and occasionally she and the Greenfield family were more formally invited to the same old-fashioned meal; but with these exceptions, the Browns did not see any society.
