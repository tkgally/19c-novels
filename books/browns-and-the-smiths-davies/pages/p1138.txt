The following morning saw Mr. Edwards again at Tudor Lodge. Mrs. Smith had never read Thomas à Kempis, and Mr. Edwards had promised he would lend her the work. It was a bright frosty day after the storm of the preceding evening, and whether or not it was owing to the favourable change in the weather, there was a great improvement in the young clergyman’s spirits.
