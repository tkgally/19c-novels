Miss Wellby was the daughter of a former Rector of Goslingford,—Miss Richards owned the more humble parentage of an auctioneer and land-agent. When young their families had moved in rather different “spheres,” but Clara Wellby—handsome, lively, admired, and sought after even by the county magnates—was not the woman to disown the friendship of her plain, timid, humble school-fellow, whom she had always patronized and protected.
