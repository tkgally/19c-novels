Miss Clara was, as we know, a very orderly person, and so she got over her unpacking, and her setting to rights, and her scolding of her maid Susan—to whom, however, she was much attached—with wonderful celerity, and, the day being fine, she donned a silk dress and her best bonnet—such a pretty bonnet—and set out, first of all, to call on some of the Smithites. Why she called on the Smithites first, rather than on the Brownites, with whom she had more in common, I will leave my readers to guess.
