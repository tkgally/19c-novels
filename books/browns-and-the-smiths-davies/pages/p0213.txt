Hannah could just find words to answer—“Yes, father.” She trembled excessively; but she was beginning to get less nervous, though more alarmed.

“Hannah,” he said, and there was grief and severity in his tone; “had I not heard this from your own lips, I could not have believed it. Are you aware that this gentleman is one of a family who would do anything—anything not in the world’s opinion dishonest—to better themselves, that they even avow such principles?
