“Neat, that is not ragged. It is taste you object to. If a thing is ugly, it is all right.”

And so Miss Richards, as usual, was silenced, and the Westcotes were not invited—an omission which they never quite forgave.

Everybody knew Miss Clara liked to be punctual, and as, for some reason I will not stop to analyse, everybody liked to please her, all arrived in good time. Mrs. Edwards, the principal attraction of the evening, entered, leaning on the arm of her handsome husband. She was beautifully dressed, as a bride, and looked blooming, happy, and proud.
