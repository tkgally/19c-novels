But he turned from them all, and for a second, whether intentionally or not I cannot take upon myself to say, looked full at Hannah Brown, as he passed her towards the cliff. Her face as it met his was blanched, and her eyes wild with fear. And she said, in a tone almost of agony,—

“Oh! take care.”

“If I perish, will it be anything to you?” asked Edgar, in a quick whisper, which all were too much occupied to notice, and the more especially that he hardly looked at her as he spoke, and was occupied in disembarrassing himself of his heavy great coat.

“Oh!” said Hannah, and it was all she said.
