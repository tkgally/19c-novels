Old William Brown, in a garden-chair, was being wheeled about by a man whose business it was to render that office to his master, as well as to superintend and work in the garden. Little Hannah, in a great straw hat, was trotting about near him, and the elder Hannah walking by his side, looking pleased and happy, but as if she felt the exertion on the warm summer day.
