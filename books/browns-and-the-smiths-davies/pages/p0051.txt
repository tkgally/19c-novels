At twenty years of age, Hannah Brown had never even had, as far as she knew, so much as the ghost of a lover. It was rather mortifying.

There were not very many eligible beaux in Goslingford, still several of the girls had lovers; and at evening parties all of them had more partners than Hannah Brown.

It was very—very mortifying. Hannah knew that a superior mind would have been above being mortified for such a cause; but she <i>was</i> mortified, <i>ergo</i>, she could not have a superior mind.
