There are, however, a few individuals, with Miss Wellby at their head, who persist in considering all that, in their opinion, superfluous hair, a proof, not of the advancement, but of the degeneracy of the times. And when Miss Richards, who naturally takes the unbecoming side of the question, happens to suggest that they are healthy, and keep off consumption, her friend and adversary will reply triumphantly—

“Healthy! Dirty, you mean, Harriet. I am an old-fashioned person, and believe that cleanliness is akin to godliness; but you don’t, I see, so we’ll say no more about it.”
