But this is a long digression, and all <i>àpropos</i> of vindicating poor Mr. Edwards from being a type of the most modern novel representation of a hero, when in fact, I meant only quasi-modern.

And so poor Mr. Edwards left Miss Wellby, after his <i>tête-à-tête</i> tea-drinking, almost as hopeful as he had come.

What though Hannah Brown “had loved before”—had he not done the same? He had not quite liked it at first, but now, when he thought it over, it almost seemed an additional point of sympathy. He understood now much that had seemed capricious in her conduct. He would give her time, even if he were obliged to return to Llan Gwdd with his happiness uncompleted.
