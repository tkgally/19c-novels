“I assure you, Venetia, I don’t admire blue eyes and fair hair at all.”

“How very polite!” said Venetia, looking in the glass at her own.

“If you will force me into speaking plainly, I cannot help it.”

“Lovers are not generally so touchy about a little teasing.”

“How can I tell you more plainly, I am not Miss Westcote’s lover.”

“Well, well, we will say no more about it;” and the sisters smiled to one another with provoking incredulity.
