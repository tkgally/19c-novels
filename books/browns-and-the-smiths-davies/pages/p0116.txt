She would have liked to know if any of the Smiths were to be there, but she did not like to ask Miss Wellby in direct terms. She had very rarely, except at large parties, been asked to meet the Smiths anywhere (her father never), but Miss Richards was very intimate with them, for as Mr. Brown had caustically remarked, “You might be sure that Thomas Smith would be intimate with anybody who was rich.” So Hannah merely asked if it was to be a large party at Miss Richards’s.

But Clara was not in the humour to know anything about it; and after having wrapped Hannah’s feet in a shawl, shaken her pillows, strained her barley-water, kissed her, and scolded her again for sundry misdemeanours, she took leave.
