“Good morning now, Harriet, we won’t quarrel; we are far too old friends for that.” And for the moment, excited and triumphant, Miss Wellby departed.

She had not been gone half-an-hour when Miss Richards’ anger was utterly overcome by her sorrow.

“Poor Clara!” she thought, “how I wish I could do something for her!”

For twenty years this had been one of the most earnest wishes of this good creature; but all her attempts had ended something in the manner of the above. Clara always came off triumphant and affronted. Fortunately the affront never lasted long. On the present occasion it only lasted till the next day.
