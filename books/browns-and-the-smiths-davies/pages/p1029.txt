# Chapter II.
[[subtitle]] Mr. Edgar Smith’s Rival.

H<sc>annah</sc> B<sc>rown</sc> got bravely through the season of expected depression, with how much bitter memory, how much heart ache and struggle, we will not inquire; but the strength with which she had been enabled to maintain the conflict had been an encouragement to herself, and seemed to afford hope for the future.
