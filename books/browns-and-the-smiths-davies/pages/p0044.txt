She copied the ancient arches, the picturesque tower, and the old porch of the church with photographic accuracy, and she made sketches of all the trees and all the decaying stumps in the neighbourhood. She even tried her hand at bead mats, at wax flowers, at potichomanie. But somehow or other all her own performances seemed to herself to be failures—not like other people’s. And certainly they were not very like other people’s—in Goslingford, at least.
