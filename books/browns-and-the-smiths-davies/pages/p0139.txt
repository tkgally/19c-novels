Mary Westcote bore this better than could have been expected. In fact, both she and her mother felt that Mr. Edgar Smith was coming home. He could not devote himself to his own sisters, and he was undoubtedly the great matrimonial prize of Goslingford. Miss Westcote had met Mr. Edgar Smith in the street since his return, and had secretly formed an opinion that his moustache was very becoming.
