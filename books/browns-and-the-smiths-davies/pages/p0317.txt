What sort of sermons would these be, whose doctrine corresponded with the doings of the best of men? It is my belief that the best preachers are always most powerful when they preach on their own weak points.

Hannah, as we know, had a humble mind, and, though Miss Wellby’s speech had no effect—what speeches ever have?—in arguing her out of her own feelings, it made her think a little more charitably of Mr. Edwards, and feel a little remorseful towards him. And then she said to herself—

“It is no matter, I shall never see him again. Men cannot feel like women. After all, he cannot help being a man.”
