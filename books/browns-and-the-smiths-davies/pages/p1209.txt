The long procession of bridesmaids was immediately followed by Mrs. Smith and her prayer-book, as above described. She was leaning on the arm of her son. As they passed opposite the Old Red House, Edgar looked up—looked straight at Hannah’s window—and did not take his eyes off till they were passed. It was too far off to see the expression of his face; but Hannah’s heart beat as she noticed the gesture.
