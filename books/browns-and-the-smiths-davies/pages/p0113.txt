Now, Hannah, at that moment, felt very low. She had been excited all the day after the accident; but now the reaction had come, and, though she was touched by Miss Wellby’s kindness, it had somewhat worn her out. She answered, wearily—

“I am sure I don’t much care. Miss Richards’s parties are generally dull.”

“Dull!” cried Clara, who had given utterance to the same sentiment dozens of times herself. “I am sure poor Harriet does her best to make everybody happy; but young people now-a-days must have excitement.”
