“My good old friend,” said Mr. Greenfield, “do not trouble yourself to speak. You will receive this mark of the good-will of your friends, and the high sense they entertain of your own and your daughter’s honour and probity, in the spirit in which it is meant. May we tell them so?”

Mr. Brown took the Rector’s outstretched hand, and pressed it warmly, and they left the father and daughter alone together.

And then stern, undemonstrative old William Brown opened wide his arms, and Hannah sobbed on her father’s heart.
