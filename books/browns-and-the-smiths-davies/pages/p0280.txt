“What do you mean?”

“I mean that she who was the partner of all my cares, the angel of my life, lies under the turf there outside—the chancel I was going to say—but I mean where the chancel ought to be. Her baby, too, is with her, and I have nothing left but to go on my work alone. I visit her grave every day.”

“Then you are very wrong. That kind of sorrow is not healthy. I don’t approve of such sentimentalism.”
