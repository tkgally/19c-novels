“But suppose it is not, Clara. What right have we to lead on this young man merely to serve our own purposes, and to run the risk of disappointing him in the end?”

“But he won’t be disappointed.”

“We hope not, but we cannot be so sure of that. It would not be right, Clara,” said Mr. Brown, so decidedly, that Clara could venture no opposition. But, after a few minutes’ discomfiture, she rallied again.
