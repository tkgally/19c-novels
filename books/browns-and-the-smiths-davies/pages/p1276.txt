“I hope—I hope Miss Brown is quite well?”—with a gasp.

“My daughter is quite well, thank you.”

And there was a slight change in his tone.

Edgar gasped again. Why would his heart thump so loud? But he must speak now, or never!

“I would give all I possess in the world, Mr. Brown, for your permission to call upon Miss Brown. My feelings to her nothing can ever change; but if you disapprove of them as you once did, I will never mention them again.”
