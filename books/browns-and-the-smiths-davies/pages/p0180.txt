It was only their proportions that gave them a tinge of absurdity. Now, the doings of dukes, and countesses, and statesmen, and authors on the theatre of the great world, are, after all, animated by the same motives, and stimulated by the same ambition, as those of the Goslingford people on their little country-town theatre. The difference is in the size of the stage, not in the passions of its actors.
