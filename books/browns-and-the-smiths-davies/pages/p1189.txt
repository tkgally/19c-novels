[[cont]]House. Neither father nor daughter ever spoke to each other of this trial. Hannah could only guess what such a removal would be, at her father’s age, from the home where he had spent his boyhood, where he had brought his bride, where his children had played, and from which he had followed them to the grave. Then the Old Red House seemed identified with the very existence and respectability of the Brown family. Many people thought it ugly, old-fashioned, and gloomy; but to William Brown it was a very nonpareil of respectability and comfort; and to leave it, was, as it were, the consummation and seal of the fall of his race.
