But, as we all know,

<verse>
“A man convinced against his will,
Is of the same opinion still.”
</verse>

And so is a woman. Miss Richards was not to be induced to think like Miss Wellby. Nor was any Smithite persuaded to become a Brownite, nor any Brownite converted to Smithism, as far as I know. All the arguing and disputing, and gossiping and counter-gossiping, went for nothing, or, rather, tended only to confirm the separate parties more strongly in their own opinions.
