“Edgar!” said his father, with an absolute frown, a rare thing on the face of Mr. Thomas Smith, who was really a good-tempered man. Edgar made no answer. He had had his little bit of revenge for being forced to accept Miss Richards’s invitation, and was contented with his triumph. His sisters said nothing. They dared not, for their very lives, have supported their brother, but, privately, they admired his hardihood. It is a trite saying, that there is a crook in every lot.
