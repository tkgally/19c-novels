Mr. Edwards was the first to recover his self-possession, perhaps because he put a flattering interpretation on Hannah’s confusion. He seated himself beside her, and everyone saw he was devoted to her, not vulgarly or exclusively; but spontaneously. Nobody doubted Miss Clara’s Welsh romance now, and she herself was in the highest spirits.
