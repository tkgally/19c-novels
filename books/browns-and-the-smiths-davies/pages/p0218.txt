“I think, sir,” said Mr. Brown, “this interview had better terminate. It must be highly painful to my daughter, and, as a gentleman, you must wish to spare her.”

“God knows I would have given my life for her,” said the young man, bitterly. “Farewell, Hannah—farewell, for ever—since so you wish it to be.”

She made no answer in words, only her eyes, for one moment, sought his, with a mute, deprecating appeal. He took no notice of it then, but in after-days that look often haunted him painfully.

And so it was all over!
