“Oh, Clara! a Tractarian!” cried Miss Richards, with the face with which she might have said, a murderer! “Then I hope Hannah Brown is too well-principled ever to think of such a thing. ‘Be not unequally yoked with unbelievers, for what communion hath light with darkness? what concord hath Christ with Belial?’”

“My dear! Mr. Edwards is no more like Belial than you are. A better Christian I have seldom seen; if visiting the fatherless and the widow have anything to do with it. He is all quite right, too, about the creeds, and as to his nonsense about crosses and bowings, I don’t know that it is greater nonsense than yours about dingy ribbons and no bonnet flowers.”
