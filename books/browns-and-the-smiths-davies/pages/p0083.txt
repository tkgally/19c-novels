It was much easier and more amusing just then to look what promise there was of marrow-fats, and how the nectarines and peaches on the south-wall were coming on. Then Mr. Brown sat down in the moss-house at the end of the middle walk (a long alley formed by espaliers, and bordered by double daisies, at the upper end of which was a sun-dial), and admired the garden in general. And it had a kind of beauty, though Hannah did not like it—at least not at that period of her life;—it was so associated in her mind with long, tedious days, a sinking heart, and a longing, mortified spirit.
