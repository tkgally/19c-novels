And now the time drew near for the sale of the Old Red House. The purchaser was not to enter into possession for a month after the sale, and during that period such of the furniture as Hannah and her father were unlikely to require in the smaller abode to which they intended to remove, was also to be sold. Hannah was now very much occupied in looking for a house, but it was no easy task to find a suitable one. At first Miss Wellby had entered with zeal into the quest.
