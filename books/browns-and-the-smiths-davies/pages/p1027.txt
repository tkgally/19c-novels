And the Goslingford world stared in demi-incredulity—all but Mr. Edgar Smith; and as that oracle gave no sign on the subject, it was supposed that he shared the incredulity, which became thereby confirmed.

“What a pity it is,” said Mrs. Westcote, “that Miss Wellby should go about making such a fool of herself about that girl Hannah Brown. Mrs. Smith may well say that all Miss Wellby’s geese are swans. And as to the Welsh clergyman, I told you he would never come, so something else must be invented to keep up the fuss about Hannah Brown.”
