Now the truth of the matter was, it was the dread of an accident which had so much disturbed Mr. Brown, though he would not have acknowledged it even to himself, and he was one of those whom anxiety always makes cross.

“Ill news travels fast.” As the messenger from the train reached Dustwhirl Road there chanced to be a butcher’s boy riding by the station on his way to Goslingford. After having heard the news he rode off at a double pace, and in about an hour afterwards it was all over Goslingford that there had been a dreadful accident to the mail train between Buttonborough and Dustwhirl Road, fifteen people killed, and a great many injured.
