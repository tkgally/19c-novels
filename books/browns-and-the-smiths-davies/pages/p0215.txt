“Fine words, and the folly of a lovesick girl,” said Mr. Brown, “will not alter facts, will not excuse you, Mr. Smith, for clandestinely stealing my daughter’s heart, when you must have known such an alliance would be distasteful to me.”

“Sir, I have not clandestinely stolen your daughter’s heart. I have sought her in the face of the world, and the moment I have known my affection was returned, I have sought your countenance. I knew there were grudges between the families, but I hoped this union might have been the means of terminating feelings which are neither right nor wise.”
