And what said Hannah Brown to all this? Not much, certainly. Nothing except to Miss Clara Wellby, who was the only person that spoke to her on the subject, though others had mentioned it in her presence.

“You see, Hannah,” said Clara, “how unworthy he has been of you, to turn so soon to Mary Westcote.”

“I thought you wanted me to do the same.”
