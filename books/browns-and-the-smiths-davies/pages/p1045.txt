“Oh, no, Mr. Edgar, I never saw you looking better; mamma was saying, yesterday, how very well you were looking.”

But Mary Westcote failed to win, on this occasion, the reward she sought for. Not only the Miss Smiths, but the whole party now rose to take leave, and Edgar Smith heard Mr. Edwards’ entreaty that he might have the pleasure of seeing Hannah home. He did not stop to hear her refusal, but hurried his sisters away, over the frozen roads, listening in moody silence as they talked over the events of the night—the arrival of Mr. Edwards, his good looks, and his devotion to Hannah Brown.
