“Shall I go and see? You are not afraid to be left alone for a few minutes?”

“Oh no. I should like to know the worst, or if I can do anything.”

He was a long time in returning—at least it seemed a long time to Hannah. She looked eagerly down the long train of carriages, and saw many taken out; but she was not near enough to see in what condition they were, and she did not like to go herself, lest she should be in the way. She became very lonely and uncomfortable, and longed for the return of her companion. At last he came.

“Good news!” he said, “no one killed, or even in danger. A few broken limbs, and a great many bruises, that is all.”
