Stopping for an instant in front of an engraver and picture-dealer’s shop, she then opened the door and, entering, inquired for the master. He came in a few minutes—an intelligent looking man, with very good manners. Hannah gave him her card, and in a shy, stammering manner asked to speak with him. He looked at her card, and asked if she were the daughter of Mr. Brown, the solicitor in Goslingford. Hannah replied that she was, and with much politeness and respect, he rejoined that it would give him much pleasure if he could serve a lady so highly spoken of.
