[[cont]]Mr. Smith should be so fond of money at his age. And covetousness, you know, is idolatry, just all the same, Harriet, as if he told his beads to the Virgin.”

“Ah! but his views—his views are so sound—his foundation so different from——”

“So much the worse for him, if he knows what is right, and does not do it. What is the use of a foundation if it has no superstructure?”

“Oh! Clara, surely you must know what I mean by——”

“Indeed, Harriet, I never know what you mean, except that everybody of your way of thinking is right, and everybody of any other way wrong, no matter what their actions.” And Miss Clara laughed her triumphant laugh
