Hannah, with but slight hesitation, offered hers. He took it gratefully, and shook it warmly, but without speaking.

Hannah felt now that she understood him, and that he understood her. She had not felt so happy for a long time. She was as happy, though in a much less agitated way, than after the scene on the ice. She had begun, as I have already said, once more to have rather a better opinion of the other sex.
