You all, too, know the moral of faded flowers and fallen leaves, so why should I allude to it? There is nothing new under the sun; and even this is a hackneyed lesson which Nature herself annually repeats to us; and yet, does it ever seem stale?—or, rather, each time that it is repeated, does it not appeal to us with increased strength—with deeper pathos? Are there not moments when it seems almost sad to think that our very sorrows have passed away into the dim region of forgetfulness?
