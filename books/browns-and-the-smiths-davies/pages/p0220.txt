She was thankful when, at last, Clara Wellby came—Clara, whose company she could often have dispensed with, though she was, at all times, grateful to her for her kindness. Clara saw it all at a glance, and her talkative sympathy, at this period, was not out of place. Hannah had not yet arrived at the time when sorrow seeks silence, and shrinks with dread from any infringement of it. Clara proposed that Hannah should go out with her, and make a few calls, and Hannah acceded. Anything to escape from her own recollections!
