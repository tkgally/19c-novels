That is what you mean by Christian principles. And, let me tell you, people who marry from such motives rarely marry as happily as when they follow the guide God has given them in their own hearts.”

“But, dear Clara, you were just accusing Hannah of following her own inclinations.”

There was no denying this; and almost for the first time in their mutual lives, Clara was for a moment silenced by Miss Richards. But she did not bear her defeat so meekly as her friend, perhaps because she was not so much accustomed to it.
