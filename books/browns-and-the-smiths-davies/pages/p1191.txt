From the time of Mr. Brown’s loss, up to the present moment, Miss Clara Wellby had been in an even unusual state of fuss and fidget, running about from house to house, and calling incessantly on Hannah with offers of advice and assistance; and then telling everybody how well she behaved, and how sensible she was, though she had several times been on the point of quarreling with her because she would not he guided by her counsel.
