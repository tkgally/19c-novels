# Chapter V.

[[subtitle]] On the Ice.

M<sc>iss</sc> C<sc>lara’s</sc> whole thoughts were now occupied in what she considered the good work of bringing about frequent meetings between the two young people. It was sometimes, however, a little difficult to contrive. Winter is not so favourable a season as summer for accidental meetings, and it would not do constantly to invite them to meet each other at her own house at tea.
