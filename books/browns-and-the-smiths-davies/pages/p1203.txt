[[cont]]next month, Hannah, and the Smiths are making such a fuss about it. You would think nobody in the world had ever been married before. There are to be ten bridesmaids—such folly! and so worldly in a clergyman’s wife. And such a trousseau—silks and satins and velvets. Much use they will be of at Llan Gwdd. And they are going to Paris, and to visit some of his relations. And then they are coming back here, for a little triumph, I suppose. And everybody is putting off their parties; and I understand the dissenting minister is in a dreadful way about it, but he dare not say much, as he might affront Mr. Smith, and there would be a loss to the chapel it would never get over. It is a foolish business. I don’t think I shall entertain them when they come.”
