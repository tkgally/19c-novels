“Well, say no more about it, then; and I won’t blame Mary Westcote either, if you don’t like to hear it.”

“I don’t like you to think that——”

“Oh! don’t be afraid I shall be too severe upon her,” said Mrs. Smith, rising and laughing, with a tone of incredulity, which made her son follow her. But as he reached the door, he apparently thought better of it, and, opening it for her without any further attempt at remonstrance, he shut it as soon as she was gone, and returned quietly to the table at which his father was still seated.
