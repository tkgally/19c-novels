In fact it would have required an immense counterbalancing advantage to himself and to his own family, to have induced him to do so. Then another important question was—“What would Mrs. Smith say?”

Now Mr. Smith was by no means a henpecked husband; still to have the wife of his bosom in a state of indignation both at himself and his son, and a state of indignation by no means utterly unjust, was far from a pleasant prospect.
