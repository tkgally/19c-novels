“Oh, it does not tease me at all, I assure you.”

“My darling! Do you think Edgar Smith has no eyes?”

Mary Westcote at last condescended to smile, and a glance in the mirror, at her own great blue orbs, inclined her to take her mother’s view of the question. But she had still sufficient misgiving to invest the affair with the interest of uncertainty.
