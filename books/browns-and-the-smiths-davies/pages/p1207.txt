With all her pride, she has not been too proud to be glad of Hannah Brown’s leavings.”

“I look upon this marriage altogether,” said Mr. Silk, the draper, and a sound Churchman, “as a severe—a very severe—blow to dissent in Goslingford. Winter is looking quite blue about it. The next generation of the Tudor Lodge family will all go to church.”
