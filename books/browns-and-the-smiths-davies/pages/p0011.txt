And then, the hackneyed one has the advantage of being understood by everybody; even by those—if in these days of universal knowledge there be any such—who have only heard of the Montagues and Capulets by way of reference.

Now, the Browns and the Smiths were the Montagues and Capulets of the Borough of Goslingford.
