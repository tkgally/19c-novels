Mr. Edwards smiled in the conscious superiority of a churchman’s motives and temper, and agreed at once to petition Mrs. Smith. A favourable moment presented itself immediately. Mrs. Smith came into the drawing-room and took up her work. Mr. Edwards looked for her thimble and her spectacles.
