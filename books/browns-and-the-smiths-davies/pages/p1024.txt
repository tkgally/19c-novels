“That was a very different thing.”

“I cannot see the difference.”

“Don’t you? I see a great difference between Mr. Edwards and Miss Westcote,” said Clara, who always would have the last word. Hannah did not answer.

Hannah’s feelings were very different from what they had been before her trip to Wales. If her heart was unchanged, her body was stronger, and her animal spirits more vigorous. In spite, too, of all her disapprobation of Mr. Edwards, perhaps the feeling that she had again inspired an attachment, was not altogether disagreeable to her; though it confirmed her in her opinion that man’s affection was lightly won and lightly lost.
