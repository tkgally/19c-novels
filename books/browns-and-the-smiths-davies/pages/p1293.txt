The Browns had, in fact, “knuckled down,” as it were, to the Smiths—quite a different thing; and, this being the case, Mrs. Smith found it much easier to forgive them their former offences. It is so easy to be magnanimous when one’s pride has been propitiated. Harriet Richards, too, approved of it, and her husband defended it—and last, not least, it was Edgar’s own doing, and Edgar surely must do right.
