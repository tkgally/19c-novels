# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Matrimonial.

I<sc>t</sc> was Mr. Greenfield’s custom to have a parish tea-drinking once a year in the school-room, generally about or shortly after Christmas time. The chief expense of this party was borne by Mr. Greenfield himself, Miss Richards, and the smaller subscriptions of a few others among the better-off of the Goslingford church-people.
