“My father!” she cried, “if he should hear of this accident before I return. Oh, what will become of him? How can I go home?”

“A man has walked on to Dustwhirl Road, and the engine has gone back to Buttonborough for help, and to stop the next train. Probably the Goslingford omnibus will come on here, and after it has helped to convey the wounded, it will probably take us home.”

“Oh, that will be so long,” cried Hannah, too much pre-occupied to remark that her companion had spoken of Goslingford as his home too. “I wonder if I could walk to Dustwhirl Road?”
