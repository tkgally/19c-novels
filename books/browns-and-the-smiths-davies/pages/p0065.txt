But this was an event which rarely occurred. Poor Hannah’s shyness was generally catching to her companions. There are, however, some individuals whose constitutions are proof against certain diseases, even when exposed to infection in the most unguarded manner. Hannah’s present fellow-traveller had an idiosyncrasy of this nature. A female Marlow would not have daunted him, any more than the male one did Miss Hardcastle.
