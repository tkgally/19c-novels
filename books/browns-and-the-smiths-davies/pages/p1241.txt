Inconsistent Hannah!

Both of them had looked round as she opened the door, and Mary Westcote, with a triumphant, patronising smile on her face, had turned towards her, while Edgar Smith, becoming suddenly grave, bowed with evident embarrassment.

“How do you do, Miss Brown?” cried Mary Westcote. “Dear me! how dreadfully pale and tired you look! Are you not well?”

“I am very tired,” said Hannah; “the heat——”
