“It was the monkish spirit, my dear,” said Clara, who, like most people, when she said she liked old ways and old thoughts, meant only the ways and the thoughts of her own youth, and who viewed the people of remote times, as of remote countries, with a species of contempt. “If you want to know about it, I have a very nice abridged History of England; but, though they did build fine churches, they were such fools then, they are not worth reading about.”
