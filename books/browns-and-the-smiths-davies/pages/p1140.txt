Tudor Lodge, as we know, lay on the road to Halton, and as young ladies do not generally walk quite so quick as young gentlemen, it was natural to suppose that Mr. Edwards would overtake the Miss Smiths on their way home, and that they should continue their walk together.

Both Miss Venetia and Miss Laura Victoria took occasion, as was quite true, to say what a treat it was to them to go to church, and how they wished their parents were not dissenters. Of course Mr. Edwards was ready to furnish all the sympathy which could be desired on such a subject, and he and the two Miss Smiths parted at the gate of Tudor Lodge in the most friendly manner.
