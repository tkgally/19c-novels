Mr. Bradley’s countenance fell a little. He said, “It was very difficult to dispose of such things, even when they were very much beyond the average of amateur productions in point of ability—that he could hardly encourage her to hope much—that he feared she would be disappointed.”

And poor Hannah, though she said nothing, did look terribly disappointed. She held the portfolio nervously, and was moving as if to take leave, when Mr. Bradley said kindly, but without much encouragement in his tone:

“May I look at your drawings?”
