“Nor I,” said Mrs. Splint, warmly. “She has too much feeling.”

So, even among the Smithites—for there is some justice, after all, in the world—Hannah was not without advocates. Mrs. Smith said no more, but only coloured up. She would not have liked to have offended Miss Richards. But Mrs. Westcote continued to whisper—

“Our dear, excellent friend, Miss Richards, knows so little of the world, and she has the strangest infatuation about Hannah Brown. Depend upon it, we shall never see Mr. Edwards here.”
