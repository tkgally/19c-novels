In idea he belonged to the times of our grandfathers. He was a man professing religion; but his religion was not of the shape prevailing just at present. It had considerable resemblance to that of Miss Clara Wellby. As I have said, he was a Churchman, but he almost equally repudiated Tractarianism and Evangelicalism, and he would cordially echo Miss Wellby’s opinion that people were quite as good when there were no such things.
