Hannah felt immediately what a shadow she was beside her in her grave, middle-aged dress; what a contrast altogether in prospects and position, and all that makes up the fulness of life and youth; and yet Hannah might at that very moment have been in her place. Was the thought one of regret? If it was, it was but for a moment.

The Miss Smiths and their brother were about half-an-hour after the rest of the party, as the carriage had to return for them. They were all in bridal finery, and Edgar had a face as if he were much amused at something or other.
