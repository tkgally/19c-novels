“You don’t seem to admire the view, Hannah. Dear me! I should have expected you to fall into raptures with these hills, and those fine clouds there in the west. Even I, who do not pretend to have such a turn for these things as you have, say a great deal more about them than you do. I should have thought you would have exhausted the dictionary in praising them, and hardly have been able to find words to express your admiration.”

“I cannot find words, Aunt Clara,” said Hannah, in mild exculpation, but her meaning was lost on her companion;
