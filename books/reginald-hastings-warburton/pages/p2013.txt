[[cont]]<i>feel</i> the sound of approaching footsteps, as they echoed on the frost-bound road; yes, though as yet far off and cautious, he could perceive that they approached him. An indescribable nervous sensation stole over him; he instinctively felt for his pistols in his belt, and their familiar touch reassured him. He groped about him to ascertain his position, and he found there was a niche left in the archway, in order to allow the driver room, as his horses passed by, on the narrow path by the canal. In this niche Bryan ensconced himself, and soon afterwards he heard that firm, heavy tread that could belong but to Hezekiah Doom.
