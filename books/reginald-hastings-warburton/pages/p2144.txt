It was past eleven. In less than two hours, Zillah was to embark; but long before then, I must be past the Roundheads’ city-guard, or forfeit my parole. We rode rapidly through the silent streets until we came to where the Strand begins to open out upon the country behind Charing Cross. Near here was the last outpost of the enemy.

Bryan reined up his horse, and asked me solemnly if I would forgive his leaving me. “I do not speak of leaving the standard,” said he, “for I may rejoin it in a week; and if I never hear it rustle again in a battle-field, I shall be little missed. I have fought hard for it, and been rewarded grudgingly, and almost with scorn; nevertheless, my heart is still as true to my King as the dial to the sun; rather than draw a sword against him, I would cut my right arm off.
