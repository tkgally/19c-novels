This amiable and accomplished lady instantly adapted herself to her husband’s manner towards me; and with grave sweetness hoped that as a guest I should be able to forget that I had been their visitor in any other light. I returned to her my sincere and hearty thanks for all the courtesies that I had received, but especially for the great kindness which I felt had been extended towards me in my illness. Mrs. Hutchinson glanced involuntarily towards Zillah, and declared that she done but little—only a small part of the duty that she owed to her Christian profession, and that she was, after all, but an unprofitable servant.
