There we paused. My eyes were dry; for my grief was too solemn for tears, as I looked out upon the scenes where I was now a trespasser. The spot where my mother lay tranquilly at rest; the place where my brave brother had fallen in defending what was to him his home!

Looking up to the right, my eyes wandered over the steep woods, and rested on the castle tower where Zillah slept,—slept, I hoped, then forgot her earthly sorrows in her heavenly dreams.

I looked at Bryan, he was on his knees; his eyes were full of tears, and he seemed praying heartily. He was a Papist, poor fellow, and able to comfort himself by praying for the souls of the departed.
