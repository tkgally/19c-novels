“Pity it is, that the world—such as we have made it—should darken over a bright soul like that. I was jesting with her just now and amusing myself with her surprise, and yet she has wakened in my own heart more thoughtfulness than my present critical position could inspire. But we are coming, I presume, too near our border village to waste time on sentiment; I will therefore briefly satisfy your very natural curiosity as to how I became acquainted with yon fair damsel.

“You know she has been on a visit to our reigning wit and beauty, Lady Carlisle. There I saw her, but she saw me not—at least she saw no Pedlar. My Lady Countess was delighted to have possession of such an attraction for the men as her beautiful young kinswoman; and Mistress Phœbe, though a mere child in years, soon found out her value, and the influence over her patroness that she might pretend to.
