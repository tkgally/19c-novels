“If I had set an example of anything wise or worthy, your Lordship would have been as slow to notice as to follow it.”

Digby’s arrowy retorts had always Parthian activity, even in retreat.

“Your Highness’ high nature is ever proposing to yourself what seems impossible to others,” he replied, with a complimentary bow and curl of the lip.

“Come, Prince,” said the Duchess, interposing, “and let me present to you a member of your garrison, who, if she prove as formidable to the public as to private peace, may prove a very Helen to our Troy.” So saying, she drew over to her the rein of a lady’s horse, and gracefully throwing off the veil of its rider, discovered, to my surprise, the lovely, blushing face of Phœbe Demiroy.
