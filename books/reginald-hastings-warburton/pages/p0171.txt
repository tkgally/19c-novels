I then repaired to the Antlers, and long before I reached that hostel I could hear joyous voices resounding in loud laughter or wild chorus through the open window of the inn-parlour.

Such scenes as I was about to witness were new to me, and I paused for a moment on the threshold; but hearing the governor of the castle’s voice high above the din, I entered, and for the first time beheld assembled the chief officers on whom the King and kingdom depended for their safety!

As I before mentioned, a massy oaken table ran from side to side of the long low parlour, and this was attended by two long benches of equal length; at the upper end was a huge chair, in which Digby sate installed as president. The benches were closely occupied by carousing Cavaliers; not a place was vacant, and not a voice was silent as the health of the new governor was pledged in the best wine the inn afforded.
