[[cont]]woods ceased suddenly to screen our hasty march; and a long narrow strip of gravel only intervened between the sea and an overhanging range of cliffs. Along this we now moved swiftly and noiselessly; the night was almost quite dark; and the moaning of the waves, before they dashed upon the shore, drowned the noise of our footsteps. A few faint stars shone out between the driving clouds, and showed us at length the Black Rock; but there was no boat to be seen there. Still on we went; when suddenly the cliffs above us blazed with a volley of musketry, and two of my poor fellows dropped beside me.

“On, on,” shouted Blount, “there’s shelter nigh.”
