“I will look out,” I said, “be it man or fiend that makes that fearful noise. Nothing can be worse than watching for it thus.”

And so saying, I rose and groped my way towards where I imagined the door to be. My hands met something warm and stirring, from which I recoiled with a shudder. It was only Arnold,—who, however, had evidently no more thoughts than I had, of resuming his rest.

“Where is your boasted English courage, now?” he demanded almost scornfully.

“You may yet live to prove it;” I replied, now forcing my way towards the entrance.

“I may yet <i>die</i> to prove it, I presume you mean;” said my companion giving way; “well, it is just possible,” he added in a graver tone.
