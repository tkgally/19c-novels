The faintest streak of dawn was already touching the mountain tops with a delicate rose-colour, but all the rest of that magnificent tract of snow, looked pale blue in the starry gloaming. At a short distance beneath us, was a forest of pines, but above, to the very skies, all was smooth, unsullied snow or jagged glacier. That glimpse of dawn at once restored me to myself, and like a good angel, dispelled all the dark visions that had haunted me. I stepped back into the hut, flung myself on my bed of leaves once more, and was soundly asleep in a trice.
