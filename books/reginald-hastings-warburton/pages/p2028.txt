“And so you shall, my poor lad;” said Bryan. “You shall leave that gloomy tyrant of yours, and find a crust and a lesson from me, as long as I have a sword to win the one, or a finger to teach the other. So now to your grace, and say your prayers, and creep into bed; I believe, as my Lord says, that you’ve got a soul to be saved after all.”

The dwarf instinctively obeyed his new master, though, as soon as the spell of the music had died away, he began to sob again as if his heart would break. More than once, during the night, Bryan heard him rise softly to approach the door, as if to escape, but, at the slightest sound, the poor creature crept back again, and resigned himself to all the torment of unrest.
