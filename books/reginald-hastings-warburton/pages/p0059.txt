I <i>would</i> not waken from that happy trance, until I saw poor Hugo’s face bent over mine in agonized suspense, and then I started up, and in a few minutes was myself again. But Zillah and Phœbe (who was there too) were hastened away by fears of alarming the castle, for it was now late: as Zillah turned away, her parting glance stirred my heart with the almost only proud pleasure it was destined to receive.

Although much bruised, the poor fellow I had dragged ashore was little injured, and after a night’s rest was able to render an account of himself. He was an orphan, he believed; born, as well as he knew, in Ireland; of his parents he knew almost nothing, but he maintained that his father had been some Irish king. He himself had been lost or stolen when an infant, and brought up by pirates, whose vessel some years after had been captured by a royal frigate.
