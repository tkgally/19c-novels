Will ye listen to the pale-livered who preach and pray that the heart of Agag may be touched; touch his heart—forsooth! hath he not a fifth rib to be struck? What means the prophet when he commands to bind our Kings with chains, and our nobles with links of iron?”

And in this wild manner he was proceeding when Bryan, after struggling through the crowd, at length got one foot on the edge of the tub, and leaned upon it as if excited by the sermon; as, indeed, he was. Just as the preacher had collected all his breath for a furious explosion against the Gentiles, Bryan’s foot pressed too heavily on the edge of the tub, and sent its occupant spinning over the heads of his hearers.
