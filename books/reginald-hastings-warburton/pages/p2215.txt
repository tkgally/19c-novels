Once, indeed, there came a sound so wild and unearthly, that it penetrated into my sleeping ear; and I opened my eyes, half ashamed of seeming alarmed in the presence of my companion. But, by the clear star-light shining in through the open casement, I saw that he, too, was awake; and in an attitude half erect, was gazing at me with such a stern, if not fierce expression of countenance, that I involuntary rose, as though to encounter him.
