As he spoke, in tones that lent horror to his words, the same terrible sound came quivering to our ears, and seemed to spread itself, slowly, far away over the mountains. It was more like a despairing moan, wrung from some agonized heart, than any cry that wild beasts could make. I asked Arnold in a low voice, whether he did not think it might proceed from some fellow-creature in distress.
