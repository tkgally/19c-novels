I was too happy to be allowed to remain, even in that probationary state, and I thought with bitterness on the fast-approaching hour that was to recal me to England and my loyal duty.

So it fared with me for the first few days after our arrival at the Hague. Sir Janus had not sufficient energy; I had not sufficient inducement to leave the house. Bryan alone was indefatigably restless; never weary of examining the strange people and their customs and places of business, and haunts of pleasure. In a wonderfully short space of time he had learned some of their language, and made for himself so many acquaintances that he never used the table of the inn.
