I thought of poor Bryan’s feelings with anguish. His reluctance to leave me; his responsibility to the precious freight he had on board; his remaining perhaps too long, and finding his boat stranded, while the pinnace would be left with no second hand to guide her when we had fallen; as these thoughts flashed rapidly through my mind, I saw the shallop shoot from behind the Black Rock, as if to show itself, and then retire.
