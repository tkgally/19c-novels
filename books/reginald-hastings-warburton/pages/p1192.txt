For, even as I write, both time and clime combine to render this beautiful region most beautiful. A balmy evening of a happy southern spring is approaching; the golden sunshine is still pouring down upon the snowy mountains, and trickling through the pine-forest of the hills. Lausanne is lighted up by its last rays—all except yon sombre castle on the heights; Vevay comes out from its rich vineyards, and then the day’s broad light is lost among the woods of Clarens. But a lovelier light succeeds, tenderly transparent over the emerald fields round Chillon, but gorgeous in violet and purple, as it passes away into the valley of the Rhone.
