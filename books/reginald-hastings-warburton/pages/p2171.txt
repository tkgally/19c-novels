I much fear, however, for Phœbe’s sake, that we have ill-chosen our retreat, for she at once strove to soothe her sorrows by penances; and I fear she may at last shelter her poor heart beneath the veil.

“You know we had hoped to find Madame de Coligny here; she is a Protestant and a woman of high character, but she had, unfortunately, left Paris for La Vendée, and we were afraid to follow her through this distracted country. We then appeared to have no other refuge open to us than this convent, for Fay and her mother intend to proceed to Venice as soon as they can procure an opportunity.
