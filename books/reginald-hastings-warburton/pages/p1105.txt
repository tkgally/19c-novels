“It is now midnight: I am about to make up this long letter, in order that the bearer may be safely out of the Hall before our enemies surround us. I wanted to send Bryan, but he refused flatly. The messenger brings such moneys as he could conveniently carry. He will remain hidden in the woods until the fate of to-morrow’s fight is decided: if it goes against us, he will depart alone; if we succeed, he will be soon followed by yours in life and death,
