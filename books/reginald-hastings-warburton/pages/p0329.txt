At length a low and piteous voice whispered in my ear, “Master, master dear, it’s light, and soon God’s sun will rise and our’s must set,—Ohone and sorrow!”

“My poor Bryan!” I exclaimed, “now, indeed, I feel the anguish of this hour. My poor boy, well may’st <i>thou</i> shrink from sharing the doom which to me has scarce a pang.”

The boy dashed away the tears from his eyes, and exclaimed reproachfully—almost angrily: “And did you think it was a thought for myself that could make a woman of me? Oh no!—no—no!—it’s little that matters what becomes of the life you saved, when the pride and the hope of my heart’s gone from me.—And to think I could not save you from being murdered, after all!”
