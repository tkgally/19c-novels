Marguerite’s old kinsman arrived, as she had expected, and I was scarcely surprised, instead of his young relative, to find him at our usual place of meeting; I concluded at once that the tall, grave-looking person whom I there encountered must be the man. He seemed to expect me, for he saluted me courteously, though formally, as I approached, and without any preface, entered into conversation with me in very tolerable English. We spoke of our respective countries; and as we continued to walk to and fro, his serious manner seemed to give way to some more pleasant impressions.
