I afterwards learned that the attempt at our rescue, by a body of Newark horse, had failed; we had been carried into Nottingham Castle and given up to the charge of Colonel Hutchinson; or rather of his wife, for the Colonel was then absent.

[[break]]

Thus far have I completed the task I set myself. A full month has elapsed since I began it; a month unmarked, except by the memory of the scenes I have recalled, and noted down each day, as if they had been re-enacted. During all this time I have received honourable, and even kind treatment; but I have seen no living creature, except the Caliban, my gaoler. On him I have tried all kindness, and even bribes in vain.
