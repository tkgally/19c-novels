The former elders had quite passed away; some few, they said, had died of broken hearts, when the King was slain. The name of Blount was scarcely then remembered; though now it is carved upon a marble monument, raised by the sea-side, in memory of as faithful a service as ever was performed. Rosine had watched over her husband’s father till his death, and had then wandered away, none knew whither. I never could discover her.
