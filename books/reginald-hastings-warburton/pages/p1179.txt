As this conversation proceeded, we had been moving along slowly by a broad and quiet stream, which I soon recognised as the Trent. Our only escort, that we could see, consisted of a couple of pikemen, who walked close behind the waggon; but I thought I could hear the tramp of troopers, both in front and rear. Suddenly there seemed to be some alarm given to our captors, for our two guards tossed their pikes into the waggon, and taking up their matchlocks, began to blow their match. At the same time, a body of horse galloped back to join their comrades in the rear.
