He described to me all that he had seen, and much that he had felt, with almost scriptural terseness and simplicity; and he concluded by informing me, that he had “sought me far and near without ceasing, for that Phœbe was wounded in spirit, and sick unto death, refusing to be comforted until she had obtained my pardon and forgiveness.”

But this was not the only purport of his mission. He brought some considerable sums of money, voluntary gifts they might be called, from my poor harassed people, and some information that was far more valuable.
