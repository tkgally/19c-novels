“Ah, me, yes,” replied her Majesty; “I did hear that he was hang very often; but dese Irish are used to dat.”

Prince Rupert had meant well; yet I doubt however whether Bryan will owe much promotion to his support on this occasion. The King, to my great relief, interrupted his Highness’s further rejoinder, and amiably endeavoured to smooth the argument.

“We claim to have some Irish blood in our own veins,” said he, smiling, “so we must speak of it respectfully; and you need not fear, my Lord Hastings, that I shall take good care of my young kinsman. You will soon return, I trust, to our court, and be yourself my witness in this matter. But here comes Ned Hyde with his budget of daily miseries, and I will detain you no longer from your journey.”
