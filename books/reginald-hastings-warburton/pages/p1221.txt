“I accepted this unpalatable declaration with as good a grace as I could command, and then drew near to Zillah, while the Governor went to summon my gaoler, and Mrs. Hutchinson thoughtfully withdrew to speak to the disguised harper. I hastily asked Zillah who was this new personage, this Cromwell, of whom they all stood in awe.

“He is one,” said she, “as yet little regarded, except by those who know him well: but with them, his will is imperative, and the Committee never questions what it pleases him to do. Moreover, I suspect he means to sell you your liberty at the price of your estates, for his ingenuity and unscrupulousness in raising money are notorious.”
