But that one, (whom they knew to be Sir Hugo, from his tall white plume,) was always to be seen, as he moved about among his followers. Then the maiden whom he had loved, hid her face once more and sobbed aloud.

“‘Father,’ she said to her old chaplain; ‘can it be that we have two souls within our one small heart? I have told thee, that I love another—Ah! too dearly! and yet, methinks, the love I bear that doomed and gallant youth is stronger still, though greatly differing. Oh! I would give a thousand years of life for one brief moment—to throw myself on his breast and protect him with my own from the death that is hovering round him.
