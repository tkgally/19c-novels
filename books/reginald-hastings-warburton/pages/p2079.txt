[[cont]]found ourselves entering London by the Reading road.[[fnref *]] We stopped at the first house of entertainment, in order to learn the news, and to procure some refreshment. It was the Piccadilly, which I had visited when a boy, under far different circumstances. It was then frequented by the gayest, as well as the most influential men about the court; and there I had seen Hyde in familiar conversation with Essex, while Digby was apparently amusing himself at bowls with Lord Holland.
