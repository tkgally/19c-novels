“Your pardon, Sir Janus,” said the young man, “but I had business of such importance with you, that I thought I’d venture to intrude; and indeed it won’t be long before the rebels—the gentlemen I mean at the Manor down there—will be paying you a more unceremonious visit. In short, Sir Janus, they hear that you’ve secreted a whole pot of gold; and, as all things are given to the saints, they think that all that you’ve kept from them is wrong and robbery. Now you see, my Lord and I are going to escape in the pinnace below there, which nobody else seems inclined to make use of; but as you paid for her, I thought it would be only courteous to inquire if you had any commands to make, or any little valuable to send to a place of safety.”
