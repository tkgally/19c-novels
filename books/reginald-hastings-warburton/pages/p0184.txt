# Chapter XII.

<verse>
The field all iron cast a gleaming brown,
Nor wanted clouds of foot; nor, on each horn
Cuirassiers, all in steel for standing fight.
* * * * *
Such and so glorious was their chivalry.
</verse>
[[right]] <sc>Milton.</sc>

I <sc>pass</sc> over the raising of the standard, and the consequent events that are fresh in every recollection, and not easy to be obliterated. Never seemed a nation more reluctant than was England to go to war; but when once thoroughly uproused, her efforts were prodigious.
