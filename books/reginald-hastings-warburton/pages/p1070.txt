Even now, she said, she was in anxiety lest counter-orders should arrive before dawn to stay his departure.

“Now, how shall I tell you, dear Hugo, what next she said? You must look away and unclasp my hand, and I will try to think of you as when we were children long ago and I used to tell you everything.”

“Yes, yes!” exclaimed Hugo, “let us have that long-ago back again, when I used to call you my little wife, and no one smiled or looked the graver for it.”
