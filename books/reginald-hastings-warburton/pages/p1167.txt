Bryan was still unable to be moved, but he was in high spirits, for the Prince had appointed him cornet and standard-bearer in his highness’s own regiment. I furnished him with all suitable appointments, and left to his care and use my good steed Satan, being as unfit for work at present as the young cornet.

It was with a light heart, that having made these arrangements, I prepared to depart. The King granted me an audience to take leave, at which the Queen and Prince Rupert were present. His Majesty was very gracious, the more so when he found that my only request was in favour of Bryan—for his preferment if he should so deserve.

“Ah,” said her Majesty who valued herself on knowing everything. “Is not he de young Irish king, dat my Lord Digby speaks of? Jermyn saith it is but base blood after all.”
