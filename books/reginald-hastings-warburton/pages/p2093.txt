[[cont]]I called out to inquire whether Bryan was gone out, as it was already getting dusk: my call was answered by the stiffest and most prim-looking Puritan I had yet seen. As he stood between me and the fading light, he might have passed for an impersonation of the Gentleman in Black; tall, angular, gloomy; without curls, but with a huge rapier that appeared like a tail between his legs.

“What would’st thou, son of confusion?” he demanded with a severely nasal twang.

“My friend, who came with me just now,” I replied.

“Lo! he is even here,” returned the nasal voice, “though, verily, he is somewhat changed.”
