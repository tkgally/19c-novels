[[cont]]Lord; may I have charge of any message to console him for this sad day?’

“Just then steps were heard along the gallery; he sprang to the window, but paused a little space for her reply. She motioned him away as the door opened, and the brave lad was gone.”

The old man proceeded to say that his young pupil was long sick, almost to death; refusing to be comforted, and raving oftentimes of her lost ‘brother.’ That she had forsworn life and all that it could bring, and had implored her old chaplain to permit her to take the veil, promising that she would still be a true churchwoman in all else. But he forbid her to think of such a living suicide; he told her that the only true retirement was to be found in her own heart, and the only true discipline was that of a chastened spirit.
