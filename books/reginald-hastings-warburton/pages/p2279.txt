“At length he had reached that city, whereto all wanderers, sooner or later, are surely attracted. He had heard of my poor father’s death. He had traced us painfully to St. Germains, and now he stood before me, jaded and faint, and in beggar’s weeds. When I told him of Phœbe’s fate, the faithful old man was well-nigh overcome.

“‘I have lost her,’ he exclaimed; ‘she who was the star of my evening—my only hope on earth—my child, my child!’”

“And, so saying, he lifted up his voice and wept. I tried to soothe him. I claimed his assistance—I prayed him to accept me instead of her who was lost to him.
