On landing from the boat, we struck off the road abruptly through the mountains; and Arnold seemed resolved to put my pedestrian powers to the test, for he strode away at a pace that few but those accustomed to such exercise from early youth could have sustained. As we ascended into pure and more elevated regions, he stepped out still more energetically, and only halted at mid-day, when hunger reminded him of the hour.
