Here, seated by the side of Marguerite (for so she was called), I yielded myself with only too much facility to the charm of an intercourse to which I had been long a stranger. Marguerite was by no means ill-informed, though all her information had the attraction of novelty and originality to me. She displayed an acquaintance, that surprised me, with the history and brave actions of her countrymen; her imagination, as well as her memory, was enriched with a thousand legends appertaining to her beautiful lake, and its shores, and its mountains.
