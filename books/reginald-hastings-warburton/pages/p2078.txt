The Treaty of Uxbridge was now attempted, and gave our garrison at Oxford both amusement and comparative freedom. I had little expectation of its success from the first, and therefore the more eagerly availed myself of its brief interval, to procure a pass to London. My supposed complicity in Waller’s plot was now forgotten; and as Essex alone was to be consulted in the matter, I obtained his order, without difficulty, for myself and Bryan.
