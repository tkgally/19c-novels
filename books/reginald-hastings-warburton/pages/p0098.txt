“He is a pedlar,” said my father, “who deals in dangerous wares. In a word, he comes with despatches from the Queen, and Her Majesty’s communications, with reverence be it spoken, are seldom of good omen to our country. My visitor brings solemn tidings too, which you shall hear from our own mouth, as far as he chooses to reveal them. I shall only give you one caution: imitate our friend, Sir Janus, for once, and avoid committing yourself prematurely to our guest. I am bound by my promise to keep his secret, and by my honour as a servant of the King, to obey his directions; but I would rather his Majesty had chosen any other messenger, both for his own royal sake and mine.”
