Astonished as I was by such duplicity on Digby’s part, I was too much interested in the former part of Falkland’s story to dwell upon what merely related to myself: it seemed to me, moreover, to be the slightest matter in the world to call Lord Digby to account for his conduct; and with this resolution I dismissed the subject from my thoughts, and turned to that in which I felt more interest.
