But I soon discovered my mistake; I saw she was perfectly ignorant of her hostess’s true character, and that her own purity had kept her unconscious of the viciousness that surrounded her. I was touched, I scarcely know why, by her situation; and I resolved to rescue her, in an impulse of romantic feeling, for which few of our fine ladies would credit me. I watched her closely, and soon found my opportunity.
