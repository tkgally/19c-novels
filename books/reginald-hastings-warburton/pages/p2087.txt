Just before my departure, I had received a formal legal notice from a lawyer, in Hotspur’s employment, to resign and abandon “all that and those, &c. the estates of Beaumanoir, and to refund all rents that had been received therefrom, during the last forty years.” I could scarcely believe that this proceeding had been sanctioned by my cousin; yet, there, too truly, was his signature, unmistakeable in its wide bold writing.

I could not deny the justice of such an application, but it appeared to me to savour of wanton cruelty to make such a demand upon me at such a time.
