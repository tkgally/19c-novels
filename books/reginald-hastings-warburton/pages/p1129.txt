“A good many friends are missing there,” exclaimed my cousin, pointing to Byron’s regiment. “But here’s my Lord Falkland, trim and gallant as a bridegroom, as if he had dressed his ruffles and his smiles for the occasion. And there, by Jove! is that traitor Holland, looking as blandly loyal, as if he was a gentleman. I would give all the plunder of yonder rebels that he were in their ranks, and I within sword’s length of him this day. There goes Sunderland, a true Cavalier, though he has some womanish notions about wine, <i>et cetera</i>; and there, the good Carnarvon, a modern crusader, and a right noble gentleman.
