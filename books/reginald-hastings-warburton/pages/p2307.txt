“D— your diminutive oaths!” exclaimed Buckingham. “If you must swear, swear handsomely; swear by the loud thundering and eternal immortal Ju—”

“Swear not at all!” interrupted a solemn voice, which appeared to be recognised, but not by many; for the garb of the speaker was that of a Dominican friar, and his voice was the voice of Goring, Earl of Norwich! I might multiply these strange incidents innumerably, in speaking of this extraordinary court, but my conscience warns me not to trespass further on the reader’s patience.
