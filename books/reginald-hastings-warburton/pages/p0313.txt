My arms still hung without sensation by my side, yet I could rapturously feel the poor heart that I so adored as it beat against my bosom in its frantic grief. Yet—who will believe it?—by some irrepressible impulse, even at that moment I raised my eyes to where Hezekiah stood, or had stood, for another form seemed to occupy his place: dilated, quivering in every limb, his lurid eyes blazing up into fierce fire; and yet there was over all such an expression of unutterable woe, that, rapt as I was in the greatest happiness I had ever known, I still could pity him. All this passed with the rapidity of thought, for, with a groan of pain, rather than an exclamation, the Puritan laid a heavy hand on Zillah’s shoulder.
