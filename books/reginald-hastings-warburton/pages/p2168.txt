Fay blushed deeply, and paused for a little while as if she was making up her mind to some great decision. Then she raised her beautiful head proudly, and sang those noble words of Lovelace with a noble air:

<verse>
“I could not love thee, love, so much,
Loved I not honour more.”
</verse>

Bryan listened with grave pleasure; then solemnly took her hand, pressed it reverentially to his lips and departed. As soon afterwards as land and sea would permit, he was by my side, riding under the old banner, to the siege of Leicester.
