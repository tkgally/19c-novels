A few words explained all. He had gone at nine o’clock to the appointed place. He had waited impatiently, and began to fear that Hezekiah was too ill to come. “But then,” he continued, “I knew he wasn’t the sort of man to be ill, and I began to suspect something else. I hastened back to the inn, and just as I entered the town I was seized by a guard of soldiers; they dragged me away to a filthy guard-room, and all that I could make out was, that I was suspected of having drowned a man the night before in the canal.
