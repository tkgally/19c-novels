Who is there that is proof to the delicate flattery of such unconscious admiration? Bryan was not, but continued to charm and soothe his poor little companion who had never been ravished by such sounds before. Music, for which it appeared that he had a fine sensibility, was the undiscovered passage to his soul. He became mild, cheerful, and at length so touched, that he burst into tears, and clasped his mis-shapen hands in a sort of tender transport.

“So you like that?” observed Bryan to him, as he laid down his harp.
