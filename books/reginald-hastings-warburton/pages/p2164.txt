I pass over the various transactions that succeeded, and that are still fresh in every memory. Bristol was taken by the enemy, and Prince Rupert deprived of his command, at the instigation of Digby. The Southern army dissolved, and Goring fled to France. The King retreated to the North; returned to Oxford; gave himself up to the false Scots, and found himself their prisoner. Prince Rupert himself at length left the kingdom and the last loyal garrison was surrendered by the King’s order.
