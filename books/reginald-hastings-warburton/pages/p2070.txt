Thenceforth, all was a series of misfortunes, errors and defeats. Once more we found ourselves at Newbury, (in October), and there left behind us, in the ground, the best part of the bravest blood still remaining in our army. In November, we once again entered Oxford for winter quarters, and found time to rest. So ended the year 1644.
