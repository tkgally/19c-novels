“Now, Zillah, farewell! With you is associated all that my young life has ever known of happiness and hope. You first taught me to aspire to a life of purity—and to feel the degradation of a mere life of pleasure. Through the bright medium of your mind, I felt how beautiful was the world that seemed unmeaning to other men. In your sweet keeping were concentrated all the stray desires and thoughts of love that ever haunt young hearts for good or ill. Even my resolution wore your form, and if in this most miserable war I have won some honour—the praise be yours;—yours, though gained upon the party you espouse.
