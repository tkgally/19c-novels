Phœbe is the very damsel to form the love-dream of a young Cavalier, and keep him from all the gross realities that deform so many of our comrades’ lives. Right glad am I to find that since she must needs mix in this fold of tainted sheep, there is some one to come between her and that wolf Digby.”

I hastened to undeceive my companion, saying: “But you are mistaken, my dear Lord; she you speak of is an old and dear play-mate of mine,—we were children together, but nothing more; and as to Lord Digby, I know nothing of him that deserves such an epithet as you apply to him. I have ever found him a kind and warm friend.”

“Ay, ay,” resumed Falkland, “fair and false, fair and false. I know him better than you do; but though I like him not, I would scorn to disparage him, if it were not necessary for you, young and inexperienced as you are, to beware of him.
