The shades of night gathered over us; profound silence wrapped all around, and the fitful gleaming of the fire alone threw its light upon us—the imaginary speaker, and the eager watcher for a word that might throw light upon his future life. Hitherto, with a clear articulate voice, had the Puritan spoken of himself; now that he was about to speak of Zillah, his voice failed him, faltered, and was gone.
