This day passed slowly away, it was the eve of that appointed for the setting up of the standard, and though no person expected that appointment would be kept, a sort of feverish suspense almost universally prevailed. In the afternoon, however, my brother Hugo arrived at the head of as goodly a troop of horse as ever armed for the King. I had thenceforth sufficient occupation all the remainder of the evening in providing for their wants, to prevent my time from passing slowly. I had also much to learn from my brother, brief as had been his stay after my departure.
