I present to you a worthy son of the noble House of Hastings, to whose conduct and bravery I owe my safety here, and who has seized the opportunity of our entertainment to encounter the vanguard of the rebels.”

While Digby continued in this strain, I was cordially greeted by the Cavalier whom I have previously mentioned; the owner of the steed Satanic that had carried me so well in my late encounter.
