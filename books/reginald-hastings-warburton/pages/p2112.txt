[[cont]]it, he led it on into such a strain as seemed to require song to support it, and song came; came in such thrilling melody and power, that my host bowed his eyes between his hands as if to exclude every other thought; the old man sate up almost erect in his chair, and his lids trembled over their sightless eyes with pleasure. But Fay looked like one inspired. She had never heard a fine voice before, and now it entered into her very soul. Her eyes no longer seemed to shrink from every object as soon as they had shone there; but, on the contrary, they rested on the young minstrel, with an expression of delight, and almost of awe.
