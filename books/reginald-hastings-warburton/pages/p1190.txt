I am living in a land of liberty. How could men be otherwise than free amid such god-like scenery? Those Alpine summits, raising their stainless and transparent snows to heaven, offer a fit theme for freedom. No slave can skulk, no tyrant trample there. The very winds that circle round them are pure and strong, redolent of noble thoughts and strenuous deeds. Hypocrisy or baseness could not breathe that air and live.

Heroes of Sempach and Morgarten, ye flung back a usurping protector from your sacred soil; but who would have dared to name to you the murder of a lawful King? Patriots of Schwitz and Unterwalden, no impious act of yours ever violated the great council of your nation! Reformers of Zurich and Geneva, your great labours were never consummated by cant, or defiled by blasphemous fanatics![[fnref *]]
