Those are mistaken who assert that two great passions are incompatible in the same breast; nay, they rage the more fiercely from their contact.”

This speech that was meant to soothe, alarmed me. The mention of this Puritan, too, was almost as distasteful to me as his presence. Every man, I presume, is haunted by some other who is sure to encounter him at every trying juncture of his life, and Hezekiah seemed the incarnation of my evil destiny. I replied to Falkland with some asperity, I fear, for he rejoined in a more determined tone:

“No, the influence that this man possesses has <i>not</i> been obtained by unlawful arts. His is simply the power that strong minds (which are few) possess over weaker ones (which are many).
