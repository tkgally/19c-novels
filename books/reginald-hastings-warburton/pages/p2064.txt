As the muddy waters closed over them and bubbled up, Bryan appeared quite affected, and remained some time after in silence, which I did not care to interrupt. At last, with a deep sigh, he seemed to cast the sorrow from his mind, as he had cast the cause of it from the boat’s side; and turning round, he saluted me as if we had only just met.

I told him I had heard the story of his late adventures. “A sad business!” he said thoughtfully, “but now the horror of his ending is over, it’s well for him. Poor creature! what was life to him but a scorn and a pain.”
