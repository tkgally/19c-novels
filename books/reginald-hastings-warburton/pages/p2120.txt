I was introduced into the room; to the bed-side, by which Phœbe was kneeling, and weeping. Zillah stood by her father, supporting his head. I met her eyes but for a moment; they had been dry and dim with long watching, but now they were filled with half-suppressed tears; and, for a moment, they turned kindly upon me, and then again fixed themselves on the pale, gaunt face that leaned against her bosom.
