Verily, this wine is good, and thy demeanour pleaseth me, young man.”

“It is too much honour for me,” Bryan replied, “to keep company with the confidant of—”

“Never mind, never mind,” exclaimed the dwarf, looking nervously all round him; “it is better not to name any, for verily names do oftimes seem as if they conjured up those spoken of.”

“’Tis a pity,” observed Bryan, “that a spirit such as yours was thrown away among those canting Roundheads: you would have made a right roaring Cavalier surely.”
