“I offer you,” said he, “all that you have left me, in frank and cordial pledge that you shall henceforth find me your true friend; and, lo! here is our Hotspur for my witness.”

My cousin reappeared through the hedge, in high glee at having sent our pursuers on the wrong scent, and he was still more pleased with the issue of the fight. To say the truth, though brave, and generous, and truthful himself, he was a warm friend of Digby’s, and looked upon his faults with a very lenient eye.
