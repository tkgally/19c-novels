[[cont]]far from intruding, his object had been to save them from molestation, and therefore he had come alone; but two armed boats waited near to enforce the orders of Parliament, and prevent clandestine departures from the realm.

“<i>You</i> shall go free, lady, nevertheless,” he continued; “but there are on board two wards of the Parliament, whom their guardians reclaim by my hands. One of them especially is under grave suspicions of treason against the State and of Popish practices, which this unseemly flight but too much seems to countenance. I do assure you, that all honourable treatment awaits them; but the power of the State may not be braved with impunity, and I now demand their return to their true friends. You, lady, and these honest men, may then proceed upon your voyage.”
