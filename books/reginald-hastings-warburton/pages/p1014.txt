It seemed, as when some bridge has given way in the midst of a populous city, many of the inhabitants found themselves on the wrong side of the river, but were fain to stay there until they had some safe means of crossing over to their own.

Among these was the sub-lieutenant of the Tower, who had been handed over to the Roundhead Governor, with old guns, and pikes, and bows, as part of its armament. He discharged his duties honestly towards his new masters, as long as those duties did not appear to him to interfere directly with his allegiance to his old one. He was altogether a soldier of fortune, and serve he must; but he would have preferred to serve the King.
