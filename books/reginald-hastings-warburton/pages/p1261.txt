He took my hand and pressed it to his lips.

“But one thing, they used to vex me about at Beaumanoir, and that was about my Irishry, as they used to call it; and they used to pretend they wondered I hadn’t got a tail, and a long knife, or a harp at least.”[[fnref *]]
