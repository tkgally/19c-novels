“Dog of a rebel!” exclaimed an old woman in the crowd, when she saw him demean himself with such brave insolence.

The young Puritan turned upon her a look of lofty and unutterable scorn, and fell dead![[fnref *]] A dozen eager servants hastily removed the offending corpse, but its late possessor had triumphed: the incident had spoiled the scene; the King retired, and the remainder of the procession had no spectator belonging to the Court.

I will not dwell longer on the minor details of this mournful war. All that my imagination had pictured of grandeur and proud pleasure in a military career, gave way before the sad and stern reality of experience.
