“They will not long be needed,” he replied solemnly, “my pilgrimage is nearly accomplished; my most wretched existence is drawing near its close. Wonderful has been the guiding that led me to thy roof for my last shelter—that appointed thy hand to close my weary eyes—thine ears to receive my last and only confession. Yes—Reginald Hastings, thou hast heaped coals of fire on my head, and they have melted my stubborn heart. I always, even when I hated thee, respected thy bravery, and would have loved, whilst I pitied, the gentleness of thy heart. For my hate—it has never harmed thee as it has harmed me. No—though it has left thee houseless, landless, and alone in a land of exile; still thou hast acted according to thy light, dimly as it shone for thee.
