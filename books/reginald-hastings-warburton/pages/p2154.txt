Bryan thought he observed symptoms of wavering in the face of the sailors, who had, in truth, the strongest reasons for wishing themselves well away. Hezekiah also, perhaps, thought he had moved them, for he resumed, addressing himself to the skipper:

“Decide, and you shall not be molested; the fugitives can return without noise in my own boat. Refuse—and there are those at hand who await but a sign.”

“Here it is, then, Jonas!” exclaimed Bryan, as, with a violent jerk from below, he flung the preacher overboard; at the same moment he cut the rope that held the galliot to the bridge, and shouted to the crew: [[corr ‘Now|“Now]] you’ve no help for it, so pull for your lives, my hearties, whilst I cast loose the sails.
