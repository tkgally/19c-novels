There we found our gallant quarry, with his back protected by two old trees; his branching antlers making wide sweep all round him, and the fire of despair gleaming in his eyes, though tears were dropping from them, large and fast. The storm had now burst over his head, and the wind roared among the trees in wild harmony with the howling of the hounds.
