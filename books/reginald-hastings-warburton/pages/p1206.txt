“I marvel not,” he exclaimed, “that a soul like thine can enjoy the fierce raging of these elements. These are sights and sounds to stir the spirit from its depths, and reveal its powers and its mysteries to kindred natures. Oh, would that I could do so—that I could reach thine inner ear—that thy heart, as well as thine understanding, were open to the ineffable happiness—the high intercourse—”

“A maiden’s heart, Sir, is no fit matter for conversation here,” said Zillah, in a voice so stern that I scarcely recognised it. “The last time that your words wandered thus, I forbade you ever to approach me but in the presence of others, and where your holy calling was sure to be remembered better than your distempered fancies. Once more I have permitted your presence in private; prove to me that such conference was not idly sought for, or—depart.”
