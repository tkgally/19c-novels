# Chapter XXI.

<blockquote>
That I neither know how she should be loved, or how she should be worthy, is the opinion that fire cannot melt out of me. I will die in it.

D<sc>ays</sc> passed on; Essex had retired his army from our immediate neighbourhood; Bryan was still under the surgeon’s hands, but rapidly recovering. With this exception, all outward traces of my late adventure had passed away; but my thoughts were sorely troubled.
