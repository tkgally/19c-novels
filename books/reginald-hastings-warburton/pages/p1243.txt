I was about to commence my operations on the hearth-stone, when a thought struck me that the Governor, or even Hezekiah, might visit me, and this thought kept me quiet until all was still within the castle. Then I cautiously explored my former track, closing the hearth-stone like a tomb above me, as I descended into darkness. With some difficulty I gained entrance into the old chapel, and at length discovered the mock tombstone that opened into Mortimer’s Hole. Thence I found my way, guided by the noise, to where the guard was set. Close to the light of their watch-fire I crept, and found a crevice that opened into the natural cave below. Then I groped my way into a broad meadow and stood free. In that exulting and glorious consciousness, I forgot all my sorrows past, and dangers to come.
