There—as the lightning flashes in upon us, those carved faces grin out horribly, as if roused from their stony sleep. And they say that these are the images of rival monks; verily, that they that made them are like unto them.”

He ceased, and Zillah spoke: “Methinks, Sir, it would be more profitable, if my solitude must be broken into, that we should speak of what you said concerned me so much to know; for to-morrow I depart betimes from hence, and thou, as I understand, abidest here.”

But Hezekiah appeared to think there was no necessity for haste. He too had been looking out upon the storm with stern delight; he seemed to have tasted of it, and become inspired with its spirit.
