He prayed me to let him tuck my hair under my hat, and to arrange my neck-cloth more in the fashion of a band. He then sedulously pulled my doublet out of shape, and set my boots in a different fashion. When he considered my arrangement completed, he requested that for the future I would only behold in him Aminadab Sparerib, and showed me a letter which he had not only written, but addressed to himself under that title, in case of any difficulty. We then set forth in search of Master Crisp in Threadneedle Street.
