I must not here omit a characteristic conversation that passed between the King and Sir Bryan previous to the knighthood. His Majesty seemed desirous to impress on the Irishman’s mind, that it was as a favour to Prince Rupert that he knighted him—an Irish adventurer. Bryan was equally anxious to maintain that the honour was his due. As he had the weakness (if such it be) to claim royal descent, he had, at least, the good sense to maintain his claim with unswerving pertinacity:
