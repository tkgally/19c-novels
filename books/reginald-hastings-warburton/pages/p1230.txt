After a few minutes’ silence and scrutiny, Cromwell, to my surprise, turned away from me, and thus addressed his officers, aloud:

“Behold! this is the sort of man with whom we have to deal; this is the work put upon us. Yea, here is a brave gentleman—one of good report; honourable and just moreover in his generation, and well-beloved by the dwellers on his lands. One whom we have marked as not swift to shed blood, or greedy of gain, or profane, or a wine-bibber.” He paused, and I began to feel nervous about the conclusion of this most unexpected eulogium, though I had not <i>then</i> known his panegyric, and swiftly following denunciation of the brave Lord Capel.
