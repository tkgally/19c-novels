If it had been my brother that lay there I could not have tended him more anxiously, wiped his moistened brow more tenderly, or prayed for him more earnestly. At length, he seemed to hear my prayer: he turned round his poor bruised frame, and gazed upon me with a look of wonder in his eyes; he appeared to listen with astonishment; perhaps some dark and cherished prejudice was giving way, for at last he spoke, in a subdued and hollow voice:
