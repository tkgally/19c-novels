I returned the compliment, and inquired whether we should find any game that day.

“Scarcely,” he replied. “You see yonder black speck upon the verge of the snow? Well, that is a châlet, where we shall rest for the night, and by the first dawn you will be on our hunting-ground, and we shall test your aim.”

When we resumed our march, my companion became still more communicative and agreeable, and I could not imagine what Marguerite could have thought it necessary to warn me against, in a companion so brave, and frank, and kindly.
