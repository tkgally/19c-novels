“I have got some bruises,” he answered: “but those clodhoppers let blood enough too, to keep the fever down; and if it please God, I may yet see the poor woman that owns me after all!”

I had never heard of this personage before; I observed, therefore, as soon as I could get sufficient breath, “So you have a wife, then?”

“Why, not exactly, my Lord, for the poor woman died whilst I was away with your father, at the Isle of Rhé. But when I was prisoner there with a Frenchwoman—(a Protestant, though she spoke French)—I got married to her in a sort of manner, for it was service only read in French gibberish.
