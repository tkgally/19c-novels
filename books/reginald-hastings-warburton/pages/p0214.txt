But there’s a beckon for me, so I must say good bye. Come and see me at Merton College; you will find me over the porter’s lodge, where I have usurped some banished student’s rooms. Lady d’Aubigny <i>hangs out</i> (I believe our fellow gownsmen call it), in the same apartment, and dislikes as much as I do being disturbed by those Court creatures.”

So saying, Phœbe cantered forward to pursue the Duchess and her sport, and left me to myself. I instantly sought out Hugo: I knew too well where to find him; in the very apartments that his first love had pointed out.
