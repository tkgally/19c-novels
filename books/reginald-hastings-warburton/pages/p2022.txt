[[cont]]now deadly still. The voice of Hezekiah had gathered strength as it proceeded, and Bryan held his breath to listen for the answer. It sounded—but was voiceless; a heavy stroke, and a faint cry, and the crackling of the ice, with the plash of water, was all that Bryan heard; his feelings had been wrought up to intensity, as the dialogue proceeded, and he would gladly have flung either, or both of the disputants into the water, with his own hands; but when one became the victim of the other, every feeling was merged in the mere impulse of humanity—and retribution too.
