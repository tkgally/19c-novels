That we should both dwell in the same inn and even have arrived in the same vessel, appeared little extraordinary to the tolerant and phlegmatic Hollanders. Wonderful anecdotes concerning our departure from England, had gradually risen from our pilot, through the fishermen of Schevening, up to the Court. Bryan, who was by far the most accessible of our party, amused himself with enhancing and embarrassing these marvels; and at length the interest of both Court and Exchange, became concentrated on our inn.
