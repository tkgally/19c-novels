My contingent of men and money for the King shall soon follow with his brother; unless, indeed, I myself may be allowed and able to wait on my sovereign in person. I seem to have grown stronger since I have been thus roused from my sad lethargy, and it may yet please Heaven to permit me to accompany the royal standard to the field. But now time presses: you, my Lord, will need some refreshment after your long and perilous adventure; you will find it in the adjoining chamber, and while you are so employed, I will tell my son in a few words what it is most necessary for him to know. Then he shall be ready to attend you.”

So saying, he led Lord Digby into the antechamber, where the wondering but silent butler had prepared a meal but little in accordance with our visitor’s assumed character.
