“Phœbe,” said he, “do you not love Lord Digby?”

That home question needed no audible answer; her countenance replied: no timid blush, no eye-lids suddenly let fall to veil the tell-tale eyes, but a full, bright, child-like look of surprise and innocence was there.

“Love Lord Digby!” she repeated. “Love that man, of whom, ignorant as I was, I told you to warn your brother long ago! Alas! if those we love are so ready to suspect and become prepossessed against us, how can we wonder that our rivals are ready and prone to take the darker view of our conduct, if there be room for doubt.”

“But then,” rejoined Hugo, “you admit that there was room for doubt?”
