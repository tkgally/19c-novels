B<sc>y</sc> the Prince’s favour I was a prisoner in my own apartments, and my first care was to look for my letter to Zillah, and to burn it. It was gone! A white flag to Essex, on occasion of an exchange of prisoners, had afforded an opportunity for sending letters to London, and this was as eagerly seized on by the garrison at Oxford as by mariners at sea who find a homeward-bound ship.
