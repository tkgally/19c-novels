On the following day we dined there again; and we saw Fay, in all her beauty, rendered more touching by the shade of sorrow that sympathy had cast over her bright countenance. Her Italian mother was likewise there, somewhat like her daughter, but only as autumn to the spring: in the shape of her features and figure, and the tone of her voice, she resembled her child; but in the latter, the blue eyes of the north had stolen in under the dark brow and lashes of the sunny south, and the raven tresses of the mother been softened into rich dark brown. Bryan’s warm heart had at once surrendered itself utterly and unresistingly to Fay, in sweet captivity; and he became thenceforth comparatively useless for all the practical purposes of life.
