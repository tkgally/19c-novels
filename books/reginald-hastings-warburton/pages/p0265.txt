I only left a message for my brother, and some orders for Bryan, as if I expected to return soon. I was resolved to do so, if alive, and to meet my trial, whatever the penalty might be; and I determined not to take Digby’s life under any circumstances.

Sustained by this resolution, and by the indifference to death that long familiarity with danger had taught me, I rode away almost cheerfully to the place of meeting. It was a secluded little meadow, enclosed on three sides by a thicket, on the fourth by a high hedge. As I pushed aside the branches, to force an entrance through the hedge, the scene of action stood revealed, and there I found Digby, thoughtfully leaning on his sword.
