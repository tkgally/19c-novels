With a profoundly deferential air he asked leave to recal the song, and having first taken hold of the instrument as delicately as if it would crumble at a touch, he finally seized it like a conqueror, grasped it firmly, and swept his hand over the strings with proud mastery.

I believe his very hair rose up in his excitement, for the black Genevese cap in which he had hidden it, fell off, and his curls rolled out, and down his shoulder in most malignant fashion. He was no longer a disguised Puritan, or Cavalier adventurer—he was no longer anything but—the passionate minstrel in the presence of all-conquering beauty.
