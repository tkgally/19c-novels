If they stopped our Pedlar, he was to evade them civilly, if possible; if not, we were to fight for it, and he was determined not to be taken alive, under any circumstances. We had about forty miles to travel, however, and as it was of great importance to push on as fast as possible during the night, I dismounted my page, and requested Lord Digby to take his place.

“Are you then going to leave the boy behind?” he demanded.

“No,” I said, “I am about to send him in advance, to reconnoitre, and I promise you that no mortal man shall approach without your having sufficient warning to dismount, and assume your disguise.”
