Bentinck at length said to me:

“Of course you want to follow them; you may find them at Brielle, where our pilots always make delay. In ten minutes you shall have a horse that will satisfy you; give him, and use my name, to one Jan Guelders, at the corner house of the Hoeg Straat; he will do all you bid him. Farewell! I hope in happier times that we may meet.”
