“It was thine own will that sent thee on this errand;” replied the other evasively. “It was well known that these deputies were not men to be trusted with the cause, and thou did’st offer, of thine own will, to observe and report—yea, to be a spy upon them. Now, thou art an able penman, no doubt, but hast too much of that other world in thee. Though thou art a match, I will say that for thee, for any living man in argument or courage; but thou art no match for a hypocrite.
