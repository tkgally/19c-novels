“By the tongue of Rabshekah and the life of Absalom!” exclaimed the dwarf, “you must forego that vanity, that becometh rather the tire of a harlot than the head of the righteous.”

So saying, he produced an enormous pair of scissors, leaped up upon a chair, and in a moment seized a handful of the obnoxious ornament in his left hand. Poor Hugo! he would have borne much rather than retard his important mission for a moment; but still to be shorn, and as if in mockery, was very trying. While he hesitated, Hezekiah looked up, and his face changed into a scowl so terrific, that the dwarf shrank down as if blasted.

“Finish what thou hast begun, fool, without impertinence,” muttered his master, “and let me find thee in half an hour where thou knowest.”
