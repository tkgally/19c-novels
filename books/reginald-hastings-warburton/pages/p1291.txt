Nay, I will abide with them, and finish my pilgrimage where I have begun it. There is no fear for me, dear Lady: alas! I am not worthy to be admitted to martyrdom. There was no time for further parley between Bryan and me. When the castle clock tolled nine, we were to embark, and Bryan had first to repair to the pinnace to make final arrangements with her [[corr pilo|pilot]] and to bring her little shallop to the appointed place.
