So saying, she flung a small piece of paper with some weight attached to it from the window, and listened eagerly. There was no sound audible, however, but the creaking of the chapel-door, which now opened and scarcely left me time to withdraw behind a pillar. A very earnest remonstrance followed from the voice of Mrs. Hutchinson, and Zillah with her friend withdrew.

It was long before I regained my cell, having, in the first instance, watched anxiously at the window for that which Zillah strove to hear. I then ascended to my prison as fast as I was able.
