# Chapter VI.

<verse>
Ritter, treue Schwesterliebe
Widmet euch dieß Herz;
Fordert keine andere Liebe
Denn es macht mir Schmerz.

* * * * *

Eurer Augen stilles weinen
Kann ich nicht ersehen.
</verse>

T<sc>he</sc> fortnight of which the Prince and I spoke so lightly, is gone by! I am sitting alone in my desolate chamber. A letter of many pages is in my hand: the writing is that of my dearly loved and noble-hearted brother. It is characteristically buoyant-looking and bold, yet delicate withal, and finely, though rapidly executed.
