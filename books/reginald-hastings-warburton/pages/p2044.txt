[[cont]]but first I thought I’d try the bars of my dungeon.

“‘These easy-going fellows,’ thought I, ‘are not accustomed to an Irish prisoner. The poor creatures they have in limbo wouldn’t give themselves the trouble to escape, if they could!’ Well, the bars were a little tough, but they were very old, and with the leg of a stool, I contrived to force them wide enough to squeeze through. In a minute I was out on the top of a low roof; in another minute, I dropped down into a back lane, and ran for the life of me to the inn; I tore up stairs and into the little parlour—ah! there was only a shred or two of threads and things, and bits of packing on the floor, and the ladies’ room was wide open!
