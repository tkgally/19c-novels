# Chapter IX.

<verse>
Sir, he’s a fellow,
To take the devil by the sinister horn,
And twirl him round like a top.
</verse>
[[right]] <sc>Barry Cornwall.</sc>

I<sc>f</sc> I have lingered too long on these recollections of my earlier youth, I may be excused by those who can value the innocent happiness [[corr f|of]] their own; to others I have no apology to plead, and little interest to offer. I now enter, however, on very different scenes, in which my own experience and sensations are to occupy only a subordinate part, until my story reaches to my present imprisonment. Were I abroad and stirring in the world, I might write very differently, and far less tamely than now I do;
