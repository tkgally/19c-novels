By this time we had arrived at the tavern in question. Mine host was a grave, anxious-looking personage of immense bulk, who waddled to the door to receive us, casting an inquiring look upon our guide before he did so. I know not what secret sign he received, but he straightway ushered us into a large upper chamber, where the royal arms were carved above the ample fire-place. Here he told us we might feel ourselves secure from both King and Parliament, if either of them could have ill-will against two such goodly gentlefolks. In a few minutes our horse-bags were brought up-stairs, and at my desire I was left to myself to ponder upon my future movements.
