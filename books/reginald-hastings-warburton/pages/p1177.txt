The day was now dawning; the reviving influence of sunshine, together with the exquisite pain that the jolting of the waggon caused to my wounds, recalled me to a dreamy consciousness. I hoped, rather than believed, that my good Blount was my companion, and I pronounced his name.

“Body o’ me!” exclaimed that faithful follower, “but I’m the happiest man in England at this blessed hour to hear you speak once more;” and at the same time, he pressed both his hands to that poor mangled frame he had evoked, whether to stifle his grinding pains, or to help himself to suppress emotion, I know [[corr not|not.]] “We’ve lost the day once more, my Lord,” he continued, “and surely the prayers of the poor must have been about your honour, or we’d have lost our lives too in that base farm yard. I wonder shall we ever win a battle?”

I inquired if he was badly wounded.
