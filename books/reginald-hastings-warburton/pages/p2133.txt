[[cont]]but she took her grandfather’s hands and placed herself between him and her father, maintaining throughout the meal an air of reserve and dignity that surprised and charmed me.

Goring meanwhile drank deeply, and exercised his wit unsparingly on every topic that arose. He soon began to express his admiration of our host’s daughter in such unmeasured terms, that she pleaded an excuse of illness and left the table. Poor Bryan’s look, as her beautiful eyes spoke timidly her last adieu, was one that she must have long remembered. Fear of frightening her, or of annoying his host, had forced him to repress his rising indignation; he expected, too, that Goring would of necessity, join our party to Oxford, and he flattered himself that he could then quarrel with him at his ease.
