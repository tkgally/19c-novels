[[cont]]and once more mounted on my black steed, I looked my last upon that lovely scenery, and all its haunted woods, and paths, and <sc>graves</sc>.

I soon found myself at Geneva, and the old goldsmith, whom I had before employed, had letters for me. One was from Bryan, and to the following effect: it was dated from the well-remembered house in London, where we had so long been guests.

The young Cavalier had taken to his new profession with characteristic enthusiasm and energy; he had been proportionably successful—successful beyond his hopes. After two years’ absence he returned to Venice, where he found our friend the merchant, with his daughter; the mother had died soon after she had reached her native city.
