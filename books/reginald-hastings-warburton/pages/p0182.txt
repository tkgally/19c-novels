“Fancy that light-hearted, laughing girl,” he said, “giving me sage counsel with tearful eyes, and in a voice so earnest, it might have penetrated a tougher heart than mine. Amongst other matters that surprised me, she told me to beware, and to warn you, of that most accomplished and clever person whom she met under your escort. She also told me she had seen your page Bryan in long conference with Zillah, who had appeared sadder and more stern than ever after your last interview. Poor Phœbe! her prospects are dismal enough, for one like her; prisoned in that gloomy castle, which has assumed a decidedly puritanic caste since the Parliament waxed so strong.
