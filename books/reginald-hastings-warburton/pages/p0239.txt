I pledged myself that very night, in the House of Parliament, to call the prelates to account, and you know I kept my word. I was rewarded for my impetuous and perhaps rash declaration, by a look of approval from the fair listener, and that look haunts me still. I sought her acquaintance; I found in her a perfect sympathy with every better impulse of my nature, whilst every narrow or sectarian feeling seemed, like bats, to shrink from the clear sunshine of her presence.
