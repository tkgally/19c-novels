I saw a veiled novice kneeling at a gorgeous altar: I heard solemn music, and priests chaunting joyfully: the abbess approached the poor victim of the Church, removed her white veil, and cut off the rich auburn hair that clustered round her lowly-bended head; then, like an eclipse, a black veil descended on the new-made nun, and all was over. One more triumphant hallelujah pealed through the chapel, and the sister turned to take her place among those who have left the world for ever: I then saw my sister!

“Oh! she was so pale and spirit-like in those shroud-like robes, that I scarcely believed, scarcely hoped, that she was alive! She was——alas! dead to me.
