Alas! there it must all end; my hope and faith in Zillah; my life’s love, my heart’s pride—all was blighted and destroyed for ever.

[[break]]

I was still young, however, and without the means to live; it was necessary for mind and body that I should do something. I loathed the very name of war; its honours and its objects had always been associated in my thoughts with Zillah. I had a scorn for those who hired out their blood, as Goring and others had lately done, to foreign Powers. Yet, something I must do. I was as poor in the world’s wealth as in all higher sources of enjoyment and of life. I had heard much of Switzerland; of its honest industry; of its poverty, dignified by independence; of its reformed faith and free exercise of religion.
