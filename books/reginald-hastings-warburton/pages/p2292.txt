But the chaplain never seemed infected with this home-sickness. He felt so near his final home, that he thought little of any resting-place upon the road; and never was mortal pilgrim more gently borne towards his goal. His last hours, too, were watched over by Zillah as by a daughter, and his spirit rejoiced in her return to the services of his beloved Church. For my wife now numbered the vague doctrines of the Puritans among the disappointing aspirations of her younger days.
