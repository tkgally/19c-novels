For a few gasps of breath she paused, and then continued more hurriedly, “I sought and obtained one favour, to see you once more; and f possible, once more to waken in you the pure thoughts of our happy childhood, and prepare you for the higher comfort that some godly minister may, through grace, be permitted to impart.—Oh! do not interrupt me, or the few minutes that remain to us—to me I mean—will be lost. Oh! Reginald! on this dread moment may hang all the hopes of a happy eternity—Forget! blot out from your soul the evil and profligate experiences of the camp, that have so dishonoured your once noble nature. Remember the day when we sat hand in hand, gazing on the sunlit sea, and beholding God visible in everything that was beautiful. Remember the time when the death of heroes seemed to us so glorious, and a shameful death impossible.”
