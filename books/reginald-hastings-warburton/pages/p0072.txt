My strength and powers seemed multiplied supernaturally, I shot through the waters by mere volition, and my eyes detected every object on the sands. But once I raised my head, to measure the distance from the shore, and then again diving, I beheld a sight that will never, never leave my memory.

Hugo’s body lay stretched among some rocks, half mantled with sea-weed, from which his arms emerged, stretched towards the child that seemed just torn from his grasp: its poor little body lay, as if softly sleeping, upon the smooth yellow sands, its little hands extended towards the bright world above, from which it had been snatched so suddenly. For a moment I hovered in suspense over those two forms, and then, in pity to poor Hugo, judging for him by myself, I first caught the child in my arms, rose to the surface, and struck out for the shore with desperate energy.
