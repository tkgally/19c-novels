Again, a doubt came over me, and I laid down that tempting pen. To whom am I about to unveil the secrets of my heart—the secrets of my friends?

Who will be the readers and the critics of what I am about to record?

Will even those who have known me find interest in the re-awakened memories of scenes that we have shared? Will those, to whom the writer is but a name, bestow their sympathies upon my joys and sorrows, for the sake of joys and sorrows of their own which my narration may recal?

It may be that the former are grown too old, or cold, or changed, and that the latter will be too little touched by the strangeness of my story to lend to it a willing ear.
