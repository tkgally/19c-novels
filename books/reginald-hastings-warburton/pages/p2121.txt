I gave him mine, and he seemed to seek for another to clasp with it; he found one ready; he laid it softly on mine, and gave a sad smile. He perhaps thought it was his daughter’s; but it was the cold hand of Hezekiah that met mine!

The effort of Sir Janus had been his last; the smile still hovered round his lips, but he was gone. The Merchant, with quiet, thoughtful care, summoned the nurses and the serving women, and led the orphan daughters gently away. The door closed behind them and their woe: we left the house, accompanied by the physicians, but Hezekiah was no where to be seen.
