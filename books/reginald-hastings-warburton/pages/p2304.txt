This was discouraging intelligence, but I soon made my way to the Chancellor himself, who confirmed it.

“Any property,” said he, “that has been confiscated, may perhaps be restored; but the amnesty exempts all those who have meantime enjoyed it, from being obliged to make retribution, and property that has been legally bought and paid for, must so remain.”

I was silenced. I determined, however, to press for an audience of the King from a sort of sentiment. I had long time to wait, and my suspense was the more disagreeable
