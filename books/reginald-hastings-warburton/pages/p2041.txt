Not so my gallant enemy, however; I had used my knife with effect, and he now turned on his side, and babbled out his last breath among the breakers. I then dragged him easily ashore, and when I had done so, and received compliments and congratulations on my feat, I was almost ashamed of it: I could have wished myself far away, and my victim once more roaming free among his native forests.

I have dwelt upon this hunting adventure longer than I should have done, but that I shrink from what succeeds.
