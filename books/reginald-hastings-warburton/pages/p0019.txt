The turnkey was a short, stout, bow-legged varlet, with broad stooping shoulders, and a neck that might puzzle the hangman to discover, through the tangles of his lank red hair and grizzly beard. A certain stamp of men seems to be produced by nature to fill certain situations, and this man had evidently found his destined place; his small malevolent eyes appeared just adapted to scrutinize a dungeon, and to examine fetters; his mouth was full of bitterness, and there was room for a good deal of it within his huge jaws and wolfish teeth; a greyish sandy beard bristled on his pointed chin, and reached half way up his face. Yet was his presence heartily welcome to me, independently of his savoury mission; he was the only fellow-creature I was destined to see for many a day, and I would have liked him if I could.
