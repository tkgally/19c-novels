“H<sc>ere’s</sc> a funny letter I’ve got for you,” said Arthur Treleaven to his mother as he entered the house; “Mr. Frost gave it to me as I passed the post-office. Just look at the address: ‘Mistess Treleaven, widow woman, Pendruthan. N.B.—If she’s gone, send it to her.’ Whoever can it be from?”
