“There’s one thing, father,” said Mark persuasively, “if she brings nothing with her, she knows how to take care of what she comes into.”

“Ah! well, there’s something in that,” said Jeremiah in a somewhat more resigned tone. “Keep well is better than get well, and a penny saved up is as good as a threepenny-piece picked up. But make the best of it, and ’tis a wisht job then. But is it all settled?”
