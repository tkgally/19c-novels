“P’raps,” said Mrs. Trewhella, “instead of inquiring what they do up to heaven, we had better teeake a little trouble to git there. I’m sure we arn’t none of us too good.”

“No, that we arn’t,” assented Mrs. Craddock, “and other people es a passel wuss. Look at the people who do live boath sides of me, what a set they be. I’m sure I often say like David, ‘My sawl es among lions, and their tongues are drawn swords.’ A basely owld cat wan of ’em do keep es the pleeague of my life.
