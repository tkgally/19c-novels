“Well, I hail from the South Pacific now,” the man replied, “but when I’m at home I hail from this very parish. This is St. Enodoc, isn’t it?”

“Yes, this is St. Enodoc; but who in the world are you? I seem to know your face. Why, as I am alive, you are Treleaven, who was drowned three or four years ago.”

“Yes, I am Arthur Treleaven; but I wasn’t drowned, for here I am.”

“My eyes!” exclaimed the coastguard, as something seemed to flash across his mind, “better you had been drowned and ha’ done with it.”

“What do you mean?” asked Arthur.

“Mean? why I mean that the t’other chap is marrying your sweetheart, that’s what I mean. They’re in the church this very minute, I ’spect, for I heard they were going to be married at eight o’clock, and ’tis just eight by me.”
