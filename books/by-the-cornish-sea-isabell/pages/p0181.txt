“I’ss sure, sir,” was the answer, “I’ll dig it for ’ee. I beeant perticler whether tes pertaties or greeaves or geeate-postes I’m diggin’ so long as tes a job. Are I ’customed to diggin, ded ’ee ax, sir? Iss, I reckon I be. I’ve a buried a purty many people in our churchyard in my day, sir, and I hope to bury a passel more yet. Nobody ceeant say as I wornt never ready to bury ’em. Do ’ee want un digged up altogether, sir, or what?”

“No, not altogether, ’twill be sufficient to clear away the earth this side so that I may see what I think was the front of the cross.”
