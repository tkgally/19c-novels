A dead silence reigned as Mr. Josiah sat down, for even Captain Penbole could not frame his lips to say “Hear, hear,” and Mrs. Craddock was so taken aback at finding that the old bachelor had gone over to the enemy, that she sat for the first time in her life deprived of the power of speech.

Mrs. Dunstable had insisted that the wedding trip should not be shorter than a week, and that she should bear all the expense. Before the speech-making and tea-drinking was fairly over, the little gig which was to take them away drove up to the door, and in a few minutes they were seated and their modest luggage secured, and, waving their hands to their friends, they drove away.
