Jenifer had a dim foreboding that soon the death-bell would tell the world that she too had passed away, she felt so weary and sick at heart.

Looking up from her reverie she saw that Mark was watching her from the adjoining garden. Seeing that he was observed, he greeted her warmly, receiving in return a quiet but cordial smile. After a few words about nothing in particular, Mark said,—

“You don’t know what I am come home for, Jenifer, I suppose?”

“No,” she answered, “is it business or pleasure?”
