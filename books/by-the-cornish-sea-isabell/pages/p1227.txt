“I’ve said nothing that I need be ashamed of,” said Mark, somewhat sobered. “Arthur was not your husband, and even if he were, he has been lost a long time now, and no woman is bound to remain a perpetual widow. If you dislike me, say so, but don’t say that I have done anything dishonourable.”

Jenifer saw that her feelings had caused her to be unjust. A proposal of marriage by a free man to an unfettered woman of equal birth could scarcely be called base, whatever else might be said about it. Her eyes filled with tears as she held out her hand, saying,—
