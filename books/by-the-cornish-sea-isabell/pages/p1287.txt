The wedding-day soon came—a day of blue sky and bright sunshine, and spring flowers and the music of birds. Love gave away the bride, and the very heavens seemed to smile upon the union, for, as they stood at the altar, the sun streamed through the coloured windows and lighted up their faces with a golden radiance.
