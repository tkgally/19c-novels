“When Uncle Peter was got aboard he says to the captain, says he, ‘I’ll serve he a trick for that,’ and a trick he did serve un, sure ’nough. He got a big square piece of fat pork and hollowed it out like a box, leaving a piece for a cover. Into this he put a small canister of gunpowder with a fuse all ready for lighting, sticking out through, and fastened down the cover so that it looked like nothing but a piece of pork same as before.
