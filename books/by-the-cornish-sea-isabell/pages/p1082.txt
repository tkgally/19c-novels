Mr. Burlington, having faithfully copied Uncle Anthony and shaken all the dust from his feet, entered the parlour, and the old man went in search of Mrs. Treleaven and Arthur.

“How do you do, madam?” said Mr. Burlington, politely bowing as they entered; “how do you do, my young friend? I was very pleased to hear of the event of last night—I mean, of course, the rescue, not the danger—and I thought I would call this morning to show my appreciation. Will you oblige me, my young friend, by accepting a couple of sovereigns as a mark of my approbation of your conduct?”

“I hope you won’t be offended, sir, but I would rather not take them,” said Arthur.
