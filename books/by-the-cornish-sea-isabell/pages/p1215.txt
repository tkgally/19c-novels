When Jeremiah had got out of hearing George remarked to Mark, with a sly grin,—

“How about the poor beauty now, old fellow? I fancy papa has got grander ideas in his head. Jenifer and a couple of thousand might do, but Jenifer and nothing is not to be thought of for a moment.”

“If I could get Jenifer to say ‘yes,’” observed Mark, “I am not afraid but that in time I could bring round my worthy sire. I flatter myself I know how to manage him.”
