When the seals were on board the anchor was weighed and the boat headed for St. Enodoc. The wind had risen in accordance with Daniel’s prognostications, and a slight roll was coming in. The seasoned sailors and fishermen took no notice of it, but it had a disturbing effect upon Mr. Burlington. The first symptom of future trouble was a series of yawns. Daniel knew what this foreboded, but he winked to the captain, who was steering, and said,—

“Would ’ee like to lie down for a nap, sir, while we’re pullin’ the boat in? you got up early this mornin’.”

“No, thank you,” was the reply, “I don’t feel sleepy, but yet I can’t keep from yawning somehow. I suppose it is the fresh sea air.”
