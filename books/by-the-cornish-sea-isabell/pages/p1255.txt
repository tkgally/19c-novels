# Chapter XIX.

[[subtitle]] “Wilt Thou Have This Man?”

<blockquote>
He that will enter into Paradise must have a good key.
[[right]] <i>Proverb.</i>
</blockquote>

I<sc>t</sc> was the first morning of a new year. The heaven was black with clouds, and a raging wind swept the sea and the land, driving the former before it in fleecy foam, and making the great trees on the latter reel and stagger under its fierce blows. What had induced Mr. Carnanton to venture out in such wild weather? He had seen many winters, as his feeble gait and the white hairs which peeped out from under his hat abundantly testified.
