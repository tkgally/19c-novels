“Yes, I know I did, but that was my fault, not the fault of the prayers. I stitched it badly, and then I was vexed to have to do it over again when I wanted to get home early. But I shan’t be persuaded to give up my prayers, although I nearly did when I was staying at Liskeard last summer with uncle Wilton. Ada and Annie, my two cousins, you know, used to laugh at me every night when I knelt down, and sometimes they would pull my hair and throw the pillows at me; but I promised mother before I went that I would always say them, and so I did, although very often I felt ashamed.”
