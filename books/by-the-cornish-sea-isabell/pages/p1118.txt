For an hour silence reigned in the room, then Uncle Anthony spoke again,—

“Jane, everything I lave behind is yours, except my watch—’tes an owld one, but he do keep good time—he’s for Arthur. When you’re wearing that watch think of Uncle Anthony sometimes, my son; he was very fond of you. And doant forgit mother, Arthur; teeake ceeare of her and respect her, for she’s been a good mother to you. Put something more on my feet, Jane, I’m very cowld.”

Arthur was sobbing aloud now, strong young man as he was, and he could only answer,—

“I shall never forget you, uncle, and I’ll take care of mother.”
