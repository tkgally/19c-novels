M<sc>r.</sc> B<sc>urlington</sc> had somewhat recovered by the afternoon, and sent a message to Daniel that he was to care for the rescued sailor at Mr. Burlington’s expense, and that the contents of the hamper were at Daniel’s disposal. The answer was returned that everything possible should be done for Nash, who was recovering rapidly from his exhaustion.
