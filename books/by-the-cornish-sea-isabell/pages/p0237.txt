Jeremiah’s wife, Maria, was good-natured in her way, but in all matters of finance was under the absolute domination of her husband. Only where one person was concerned did she ever venture to assume an antagonistic attitude. On behalf of her son Mark she was ready to do battle with all comers, Jeremiah included. In so far as Mrs. Rundle possessed any practical religion at all, Mark was her presiding deity.
