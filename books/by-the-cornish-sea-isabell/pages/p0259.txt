As the merchant bought them he placed them on a hard beach in long piles, called “parks,” until a cargo was completed, when the “smack” grounded, and was loaded between the going out and the coming in of the tide. It was when the oysters were lying in these parks that the watchman was needed. Oysters were valuable, and the parks were a mile from any human habitation.
