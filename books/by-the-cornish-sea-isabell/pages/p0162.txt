If he suffered for anybody’s sins it must have been for the sins of some ancestor, who, unfortunately, strictly entailed the gout as well as his broad acres. Mr. Treleigh was a well-informed man with a taste for poetry, a keen politician, a straightforward and fearless magistrate, a liberal landlord, and a good master. To say that he was universally loved in St. Enodoc would be an exaggeration, for ill-disposed persons are always to be found ready to grumble at perfection itself; but all St. Enodoc, whose opinion was worth having, declared that there was not a better nor a kinder man than Mr. Treleigh in the county of Cornwall.
