“The first thing to settle upon es the house,” resumed Mrs. Trewhella, who had manifested signs of impatience during the recital of Kezia’s melancholy love-story. “Now there’s that house in the Broad Street that Meeaster Bilkey comed out of.”

“Damp,” murmured Kezia.
