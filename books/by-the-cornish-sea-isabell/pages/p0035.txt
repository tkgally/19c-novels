From that time the recovery of Arthur was assured, and it was not long before he won back again all his former health and strength.

In spite of Dr. Anderton’s rebuke to Mrs. Treleaven, Betsy spread far and wide her part in the turning of the fever, and no inconsiderable portion of the community accepted it as a confirmation of their old faith in her as a sick-nurse and attendant worth all the doctors within a circle of fifty miles round.
