[[cont]]Mind you don’t start before I come back, Jenifer.”

As soon as Esther had disappeared, Mark said,—

“Come Jenny, I’m going to start at once.”

“No, Mark,” she remonstrated, “perhaps you had better wait; she’ll be dreadfully put out if you don’t.”

“Well, let her be put out,” answered Mark wrathfully. “I’m determined that she shan’t go fishing with me to-night.”

“But haven’t we as good as promised?”

“Promised? no: she only said, ‘Don’t start before I come back.’ Jump aboard, Jenny, I won’t wait another minute.”
