At this juncture a loud peal of laughter was heard from behind a low hedge to the left of the path, followed by a volley of “tabs,” or pieces of fresh turf. The “tabs” were well aimed, as Mr. Burlington’s garments testified, and the recipient of these gifts came hastily to the conclusion that it would not be well to wait for explanations, and therefore sprang to his feet and rushed down the valley with all the speed he could command. Unfortunately the hedge lay in the same direction, and the individuals who so ruthlessly interrupted his love-scene pursued Mr. Burlington with “tabs” so far as the next bridge.
