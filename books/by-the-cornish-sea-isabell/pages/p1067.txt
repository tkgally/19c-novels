[[cont]]“Tedn so substantial,” said Uncle Anthony. “Why when I sailed in the <i>Jolly Briton</i>—that’s fifty years agone—we had some beef, sure ’nough. The ship was more’n ninety year owld, and some of the casks had gone every voyage in her. ’Twas that hard it would a’most turn the edge of your knife when you tried to cut it. One piece the cook used, so he said, for a block to cut up sticks upon. Ah! that was something like beef, it wodn a bite and a clunk, but was something to chew the cud upon. But my teeth arn’t so good as they was then.”
