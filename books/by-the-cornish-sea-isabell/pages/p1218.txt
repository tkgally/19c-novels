“This is a pretty state of things,” he was saying to himself, when the door was opened and Mr. Josiah appeared. He had heard the noisy conference at the door, and the final bang, and had come to see the cause.

“Well, Mark,” he said, when they were seated in the little parlour, “is your fortune made yet!”

“Not quite,” was Mark’s laughing answer.

“But you are making haste to be rich, I hear,” said Mr. Josiah. “I suppose your salary and three per cent. is not sufficient for an ambitious young gentleman like you?”

“Hardly,” replied Mark. “That did very well for the past generation, but we move a little faster nowadays.”
