Now Mr. Potts, like Francis Bacon, had found “a handsome maiden to his liking,” and to secure her found it necessary to exchange short commons for Doctors’ Commons, and the Education Department for the Home Office.

For a week or two there was an interregnum, a state of things not much relished by parents who had to calculate carefully the cost of boots and jackets, but appreciated greatly by all the boys, whose naughtiness had been restrained by Mr. Potts. At last another Pharaoh arose who was to appear before the boys on the following Monday. Stimulated by curiosity the boys took their places with unwonted punctuality on the eventful morning.
