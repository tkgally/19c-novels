“But I must talk of it,” protested Mr. Burlington; “you might as well try to stop the waterfall as try to stop the expression of my love for you, it must come out.”

“Please don’t talk like this,” said Jenifer, “or I shall not go on.”

“Oh, do listen to me,” pleaded the antiquary, sinking on his knees and seizing her hand; “what if I am in a different social position? I am rich enough to disregard society, and I can make you a happy lady if you will accept my love. Please do listen to me. Perhaps you might come to love me after we are married if you don’t now.”
