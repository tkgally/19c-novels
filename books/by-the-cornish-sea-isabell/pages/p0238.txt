Aunt Charity, the only other adult present, was Mrs. Rundle’s sister, an old maid, but, like most old maids, a most invaluable personage. It was she who was called upon in any emergency, sickness, or trouble, or death, and she never failed to respond to the call. She was always ready to advise or to work, to love or to suffer. People might sometimes suggest that she was not over-wise, but nobody ever accused Aunt Chatty of an unkind heart.
