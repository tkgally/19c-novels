Accordingly, invitations were issued to Mrs. Trewhella and Jenifer, and Mrs. Treleaven and Uncle Anthony, to meet the redoubtable seal-hunters on board the <i>Clara</i> at one o’clock precisely on the following day. Jemmy Nash was already on board. Daniel would have housed the poor fellow but for lack of room, and the captain, who did not come behind his friend in generous feeling for the distressed, insisted that he should make the <i>Clara</i> his home until he was able to proceed to Liverpool to join his ship.

Mrs. Trewhella offered to assist the captain in arranging the table and cooking the necessary vegetables, but he protested energetically that the ladies should not put a single finger to the preparation—they were to be honoured guests, not waiters.
