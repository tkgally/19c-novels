“Come here, you three,” he shouted in a tone which filled the breasts of the delinquents with terror. They knew well that Mr. Potts never invited any of his flock to meet him without giving them something to keep in remembrance of him.

“Hold out your hands,” said Mr. Potts. Three unclean little hands were stretched out.

“Hydrogen, nitrogen, oxygen,” said Mr. Potts as he brought down his cane in turn on the three hands.
