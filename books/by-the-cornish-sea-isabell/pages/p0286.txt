“That’ll do, Mark,” said Jenifer when he had minutely looked at every puncture made by the spikes; “that’ll do, ’tis nothing of consequence; let go my hand.”

“I should like to look at that prick in your forefinger again; the spikes are poisonous, you know.”

“Don’t be foolish, Mark,” remonstrated Jenifer. “I won’t be held like this; the people in the other boats will see you.”
