Poor Mark was in bitter distress. It was bad enough to lose his sister, but the thought that it was his own carelessness which was causing her death was almost more than his little heart could bear.

Jessie did not seem troubled when they told her that she was going away from them. She only said,—

“I want Jenifer, send for Jenifer.”

As soon as Jenifer came Jessie took her hands, saying,—

“Jenny, mammy says I’m going away.”

“Going, where?” asked Jenifer, somewhat dazed by her grief.

“I’m going to heaven,” answered Jessie gravely; “won’t you come, too, Jenny, as soon as you can?”
