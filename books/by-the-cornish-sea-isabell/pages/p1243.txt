# Chapter XVIII.

[[subtitle]] To-morrow.

<verse>
This year I slept and woke with pain,
I almost wish’d no more to wake,
And that my hold on life would break
Before I heard those bells again.
</verse>
[[right]] <i>In Memoriam.</i>

“W<sc>hat</sc> are ’ee crying for?” asked Rebecca Trewhella, noticing the tears on Jenifer’s pale face.

“Oh, it is nothing of consequence, mother,” was the answer.

“’Tes somefin’ young Mr. Mark said to you,” returned Rebecca. “I seed un talkin’ to ’ee ovver the wall. You haven’t falled out again, have ’ee?”
