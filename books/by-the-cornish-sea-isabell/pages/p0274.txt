“It’s so refreshing, my dear,” she was wont to say; “it’s so refreshing as washing your feeace, not that mine is washed very often, thenk the Lord; it doant want it. There’s only wan thing more refreshing than a pinch of snuff, and that’s a drap of gin.” “Thenky, my dear,” she said when Arthur made his offering; “thenky, my dear. I allays said there’s no such cheeld as thee in Pendruthan, and I’ll stick to it.
