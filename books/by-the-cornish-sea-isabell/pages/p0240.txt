“Jeremiah,” said the old man solemnly, “I’m often carr’d away, I knaw, by my thoughts, and sometimes I do see strange sights, and feel that I am like the Apostle on the island, looking at beautiful sights of angels, and hearing lovely singing, and harpers harping with their harps, but when I come to myself I find I’m still on airth, and that I’m getting blind and deef, and the world is nothing to me.
