This inward pain so increased that Arthur was unable to follow the rector further than the second lesson, and Mr. Potts was devoutly following the reverend gentleman when he was scandalized by the sight of one of his boys darting out of church like an arrow from a bow before his very eyes. Without waiting for the usual “Here endeth the second lesson,” the little gentleman started in pursuit. If this sort of thing goes on, he thought, canes had better be abolished by an Act of Parliament.
