But the strength of the child ebbed away, and to-night the king of terrors was looking his intended victim full in the face.

Betsy Polsue was old, and withered, and brown. Her hair had long since disappeared, and a wig intended for brown, but with a decided inclination to brick-red, usurped its place. A couple of yellow fangs represented her erewhile ivory teeth; and when I say that Betsy was the owner of a well-defined squint, and that her ancient wig was usually set on awry, it will be easily understood that her personal appearance would be to few people a powerful recommendation. But notwithstanding her unpleasing aspect, Betsy was an extremely popular member of the community.
