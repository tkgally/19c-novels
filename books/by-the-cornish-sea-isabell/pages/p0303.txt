“How very kind of you, uncle, to interest yourself about Arthur in this way,” said Mrs. Treleaven.

“Aw, that’s no consekence,” replied Uncle Anthony; “Capen Shortridge es very friendly, ’cause I keep a heye to his office when he’s out and his clerk edn there. Tedn ’zactly a gove’ment office, but he’s the Counsel, and wan thing and t’other, so o’ course as I belong to the service, why, when I’m about I’m on duty, and must keep a heye to things.
