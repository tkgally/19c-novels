“What is the matter, Captain Penbole? is my husband hurt?”

“Well, I am afraid he is, rather seriously; the fact is, the life-boat has been upset, and some of the men have gone down.”

“Is my husband one of them?” inquired Mrs. Treleaven, calm now by reason of her great agony of suspense.

“Yes, I’m afraid he is,” answered the captain; “the men saw it from the point: they were watching with their glasses, and they knew John because he was the only one who hadn’t a red cap on, and the boat that picked up some of the men brought the news that five are drowned altogether. Try to bear it, Mrs. Treleaven, try to bear it,” continued the honest old tar, not knowing what better to say.

“O God! O God!” exclaimed Mrs. Treleaven, wringing her hands, “then I am a widow, and my poor boy is fatherless.”
