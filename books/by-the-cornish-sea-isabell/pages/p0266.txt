Towards the end of the oyster season he had a change of occupation.

“A plenty of mackerel have been seen in the bay,” said Mrs. Treleaven to her son one forenoon, as he came downstairs from his bed, where he had lain later than usual to make up for his vigil the night before. “Captain Vingoe has been here to say that the seine is going out after dinner, and he wants you to go.”

Arthur was delighted to hear this. Mackerel-fishing was new to him, and the price of mackerel was high, so that a good catch would be very profitable. In the afternoon the seine <i>Good Hope</i> started for the fishing-ground.
