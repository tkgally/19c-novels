The doctor being called immediately gave it as his opinion that it would probably develope into brain fever, the result of the excessive mental strain, accelerated by exposure.

Soon after the doctor left Mark called to see Jenifer. Both were greatly embarrassed. Mark began by inquiring if she had recovered from her faintness, to which she replied that she was better, but still felt weak and shaken. He wanted to ask all the time, although he dreaded the answer, when the wedding could be proceeded with. With considerable hesitation he began,—

“I suppose, Jenny, this affair will not make any difference in our relations to each other?”

Jenifer remained silent.
