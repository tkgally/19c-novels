Mrs. Treleaven was ill able to express much gratification at this fortunate result of her son’s labour. She was greatly troubled about Uncle Anthony. The old man, who had been ailing for several days, had gone to bed early, complaining that nothing would give him heat.

“Come up and see him,” said Mrs. Treleaven to her son.

Arthur obeyed, and found his uncle lying on his back with his hands crossed over his breast. At first he feared he was dead, but the sick man opened his eyes when Arthur spoke to him.

“What’s the matter, uncle?”
