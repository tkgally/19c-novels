“Won’t you speak to me, Jenifer?” he asked, recovering himself. “You needn’t shun me as if I were a snake or a toad, or some other noxious animal.”

“I never shunned you,” she said simply, “I only moved out of your way.”

“But you can’t deny that you dislike me?” he persisted.

“I have no feeling of dislike towards you,” she answered; “I have no feeling one way or the other. You have passed away altogether out of my life, that is all.”

“And yet we were once great friends, if not something more,” he said. “Why should there be such a separation?”
