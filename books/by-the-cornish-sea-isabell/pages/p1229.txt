A<sc>nother</sc> six months passed away without bringing prosperity to the fisherfolk of St. Enodoc. Now and then a little money was made by succouring disabled vessels, but this, added to what they could earn by their nets, barely held the wolf at bay, and many a father and mother went hungry to bed that the cry of the little ones might be stilled.
