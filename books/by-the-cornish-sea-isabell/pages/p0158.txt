“You struck me unawares, you struck me unawares!”

“Get along,” said Nash, “’twas fair fighting, and you were licked. Did you want Treleaven to stand waiting for you all night? Well done, Arthur,” he continued, patting the victor over the back. “Whenever you fight, my boy, always hit ’em as hard as you can across the nose, it makes ’em see blazes in quick time.”

Arthur’s explanation of his dishevelled appearance to his father and mother when he reached home was received in different ways.

“Don’t go fighting, my dear,” said the mother, “’tis very wrong, and you’ll get hurt by great rough boys.”
