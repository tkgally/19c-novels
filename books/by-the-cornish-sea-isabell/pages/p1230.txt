It was Jenifer’s exertions which saved them from utter destitution; it was Jenifer’s needle which earned their bread. Mrs. Dunstable, knowing something of the state of affairs, employed her to some considerable extent, and Miss Jink retiring from business on account of advanced age, a portion of her work fell to her late assistant.

Rebecca Trewhella’s chief trouble was the evident fact that this constant attention to her work was telling upon her daughter’s health.
