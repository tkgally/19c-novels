“Well, father,” said Rebecca, “what have ’ee got agin Mr. Mark?”

“I haven’ got nothin’ agin un,” rejoined Daniel; “oh dear no: quite the contrairy.”

“I should be thankful ef you was married and comfortable,” said Rebecca, turning to Jenifer. “I’m worriting myself to see ’ee weeastin’ away like before my eyes. And to think that we ceeant do nothin’ but set down and look at ’ee. Here’s father ony gittin’ a shellen’ now and then, and beginnin’ to look so owld and waik and wisht like. ’Tes wisht to think about, and we was doin’ so well before that bank brokke. ’Tes the Almighty’s doin’s and I knaw we mustn’t murmur agin’ He, but ’tes hard to set down and starve.”
