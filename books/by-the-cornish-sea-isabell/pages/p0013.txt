She is a large barque flying the English flag. Unable to stand out to sea, she has become embayed on this pitiless coast, and has, after parting her cables, drifted broadside on to a reef of rocks about three hundred yards from the shore. Even while they look a mighty wave dashes her with such force on the rocks that she parts asunder amidships, and her main and mizen-masts go over the side with a mighty crash.
