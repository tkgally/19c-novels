Deduct from this the money required to keep a respectable coat on the master’s back and to keep a fair supply of canes in pickle for refractory pupils, and it is evident that the unfortunate individual would become extremely attenuated in a short time were not some liberal hand held out to keep the meat-tub supplied.

Pendruthan National School was not a very imposing edifice, nor was it fitted up with great elegance, but it inspired great awe in the mind of Arthur Treleaven, accustomed only to the lesser glories of Mrs. Warder’s school.
