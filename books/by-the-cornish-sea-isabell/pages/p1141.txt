“My dear, I’m very glad,” said the mother; “I thought Mark would ha’ bin the wan, but I would rather marry ’ee to Arthur than to he or anybody else. He’s the dearest young fellow I knaw, and he’s so kind and loving to his mother as ever a young chap could be. I always believe in a young man who is kind to his mother.”

“There’s something else he said, mother. He asked if it could be soon.”

“Ef what could be soon?” inquired Rebecca.

“Why,” said Jenifer, hesitating and blushing, “why, mother, you know.”

“Do ’ee mane married?”

“Yes, that was what Arthur meant,” replied Jenifer.
