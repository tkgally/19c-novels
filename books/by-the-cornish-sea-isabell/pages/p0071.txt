“Mercy me, Measter Josiah,” said Mrs. Craddock, “you are ’nough to frighten a woman out of her wits; why I sheeant slape be night for draming about it. Dost a think the world es a goin’ to be burnt up soon? Our praicher towld us t’other day that ’twas time for us to git ready for it, and I think so too, ef the fire es got so fur up as that.”

“My good woman, you misunderstand me,” said Mr. Josiah, “I’m using a figure of speech.”
