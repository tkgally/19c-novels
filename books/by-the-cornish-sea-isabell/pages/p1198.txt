From the appearance of the boat it was not improbable that the <i>Annie Lee</i> had been in collision with some other ship, but there was nothing to show what had become of the two crews. They might have escaped in one or both the ships or in boats.

Daniel heard this piece of news from the group of men on the quay as he landed from his boat one forenoon, and after ascertaining that the intelligence was correct—that is to say, that it was in the newspaper—went home with a heavy heart to tell his wife and daughter.
