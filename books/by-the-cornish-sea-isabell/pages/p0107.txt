“You don’t want to go, Jessie, and leave poor mammy,” said Mrs. Rundle, through her tears.

“No,” she answered, “I don’t want to leave you, but I’m tired, and teacher told me that Jesus do carry the little tired children in His arms. Carry me, please, carry me,” she continued, looking up and stretching out her arms.

For a moment or two she remained in this attitude and then her arms sank by her side, her blue eyes closed, and saying softly, “I’m sleepy,” she fell asleep, never more to wake in this world of pain and weariness.

Jenifer had found her first great trouble, and her mimic wedding was turned into a real and sorrowful funeral.
