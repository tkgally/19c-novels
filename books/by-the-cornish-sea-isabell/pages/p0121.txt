# Chapter XI.

[[subtitle]] On Heaven.

<verse>
Alleluia we sing, like the children bright,
With their harps of gold and their raiment white,
As they follow their Shepherd with loving eyes,
Through the beautiful valleys of Paradise.
</verse>
[[right]] <i>Hymn.</i>

Two years had passed since Jessie Rundle died, but her image was still fresh in Jenifer’s memory. It was easy for a boy like Mark, thrown more into the society of his school-fellows, as he grew older to forget gradually the one great grief of his life.
