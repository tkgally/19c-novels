The dinner was a great success. The cooking defied even Mrs. Craddock’s critical eyes and palate to find fault, and the rest of the company were in no fault-finding humour. Captain Penbole, in his abounding good temper, essayed to mollify the acid demeanour of Mrs. Craddock, who sat next him, but, it must be confessed, with indifferent success.

“Fine weather lately, ma’am,” he began.

“Iss, too fine, a passel,” she answered; “where’s the barley comin’ from if it stays like this.”

“But we’ve had some nice refreshing showers lately,” said the captain.
