Mrs. Thornlake soon showed that she was able to wield the rod; for, the very first afternoon, turning quickly, she saw Nash with his thumb to his nose and his fingers pointing straight at her. Nash explained that he was merely scratching his nose, which was inclined to itch sometimes.

“Very well,” said Mrs. Thornlake, “probably your back also is itching and wants scratching, and, as it is difficult for you to reach, I will scratch it for you.”

She thereupon scratched the patient’s back to such good effect that it cured not only Nash’s back, but also his nose, for, so far as could be ascertained, the latter never more itched while he was under Mrs. Thornlake’s care.
