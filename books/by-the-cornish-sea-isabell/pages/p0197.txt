When the boats were not more than two hundred yards from the inside man, another poor fellow showed signs of yielding to the cold and exposure. John Treleaven saw this, and, forgetting himself, supported his comrade until a boat approached.

“Pull in Tom first,” he shouted, “he’s nearly done for.”

Two men seized Tom, and dragged him over the side, but before they could perform the same kind office for John Treleaven, a wave dashed him against the side of the boat, breaking with the blow the straps which bound his cork jacket around him.
