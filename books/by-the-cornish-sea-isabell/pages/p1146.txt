[[cont]]Mrs. Trewhella, disregarding Kezia’s explanation, “what we’ve got to do es to put things fitty for them, they’ll forgit half the furniture else. People in love ceeant pay much attention to crocks and pans.”

“Crocks and pans arn’t much good to em, for love do teeak away their appetites,” said Mrs. Sims.

“Hould tha tongue, Kezia,” said her sister; “they must have somethin’ to ait ef they are in love. Ded you live upon hair when you was married?”

“No, my dear, but then you knaw Tom was a very cacentric kind of a man. He had a wisht end fr’all, poor fellow.”

“How did he die?” inquired Mrs. Treleaven.
