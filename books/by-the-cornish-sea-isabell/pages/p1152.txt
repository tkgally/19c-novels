The steward was waited upon and the rent ascertained, and it only remained to obtain the approbation of the high contracting parties. But the critical bump is not usually well developed in young lovers, and therefore no objection to the house was offered on their part. It would just suit them they thought. The house was accordingly taken and the furnishing went on merrily. It was arranged with the people who supplied the goods that they should be paid when the work was completed, for by that time the month’s notice of withdrawal which was given at the bank would have expired.

Mrs. Dunstable, hearing of the approaching marriage, dropped in to see Mrs. Trewhella one morning.
