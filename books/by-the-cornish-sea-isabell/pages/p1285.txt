In answer Jenifer told simply and unreservedly the whole story of Mark’s importunity and her own tardy assent for the sake of her parents.

“Why did Mark give you up?” he asked when she had concluded her recital.

“I cannot tell you that,” she replied.

“Well, I won’t press you to tell me; I don’t know that I have any right to. But what I do want to know is this—could you love me again as in the old days if you tried?”

“I don’t want to try,” she said gently.

“Do you love me now?” he asked.

“I have never ceased to love you. I am quite content to be what I once was, if you will let me.”
