“Won’t you say any more than that?” asked Mark dejectedly.

“No, I had better not.”

“Give me one kiss, Jenny dear,” pleaded Mark, “only one.”

“They will see you from the other boats,” remonstrated Jenifer.

“No, they won’t,” said Mark, “they’re too far away, and the light is going. Will you, Jenny?”

“Yes, if you wish,” said Jenifer, lifting up her beautiful face to her young lover. The kiss was given and taken, with passion on his part, with sweet sisterly affection on hers, and then in silence the anchor was weighed and the boat rowed slowly to land.
