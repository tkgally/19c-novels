The boy absolutely refused to turn informer, and Mrs. Thornlake, seeing that further questioning was useless, gave him three or four sharp blows over the back, and placed him in the corner, with his face to the wall, for the rest of the afternoon.

Master Nash was struck with admiration at this magnanimity, and, when school was dismissed, proposed to the five boys who had joined him in sending Arthur on his unfortunate errand, that they should present him with a testimonial for sparing their hands and backs at the expense of his own.
