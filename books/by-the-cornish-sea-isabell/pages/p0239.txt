Times is very bad, and rope-making isn’t all profit. Now, father, you are the oldest, what’s your opinion on the subject?”

“My opinion?” said the old man, rousing himself from a reverie; “I’m an owld man, my dears, and my opinion edn wuth much, the youngsters knaw more than the owld people nowadays; but ef you ax my opinion, I should say, ‘Jerry, my son, meeake the booy a local praicher.’”

“The poor owld man is getting tooatlish,” whispered Aunt Chatty to her sister.
