“I wish we could all go weth ’ee,” said Mrs. Sims; “’twouldn’ be so lonely, would it? We could mend up your things, and keep ’ee clain and dacent.”

“Hould tha tongue, Kezia,” said Mrs. Craddock; “wha’s the use of a passel of wemmen aboard a ship? They had better stay at home and look after their housen. Besides, wha’s the use of company? No company es the best of company, tha’s what I say. Ef you’re by yourself, you’ve got nobody to fall out weth.
