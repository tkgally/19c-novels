Arthur found that stopping a seine under the supervision of Captain Vingoe was a somewhat trying performance. The captain was irascible at the best of times, and, when excited, would have had considerable difficulty in deciding whether his head or his heels were uppermost.

“Pull!” he shouted to Arthur and Nash, “pull for your lives, or we shall lose every mackerel.”

They pulled with all their might and main, but the two ends of the seine were far apart, and could not be brought together in a moment.

“Drop the rope, Treleaven, and beat down!” screamed the captain.
