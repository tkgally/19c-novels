# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] A Seat of Learning.

<verse>
Infant reason grows apace and calls
For the kind hand of an assiduous care.
Delightful task! to rear the tender thought,
To teach the young idea how to shoot.
</verse>

[[right]] <i>The Seasons.</i>

T<sc>he</sc> time was come when Arthur Treleaven’s education must begin. He was now six and a half years of age, and strong and healthy for his years. His father had wished to send him to school as soon as he was completely recovered from the fever which nearly proved fatal to him, but was overruled by the mother.
