“Marry me before I go,” he whispered; “give me a right to call you mine, darling, so that no one can come between us.”

Jenifer disengaged herself at length, pale and trembling.

“Don’t ask me for my answer at once, Arthur,” she said, “wait a little while.”

“No,” said he, vehemently, “tell me now, yes or no.”

“You know what I should wish,” she replied, “but it would be better to act upon our first resolution, and to wait until you come back.”

“If you loved me as I love you, you would consent to be married now,” he said.
