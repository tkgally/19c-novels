[[cont]]result. Before the boat had left the land a hundred yards behind, they heard loud shouts, and looking, saw Esther waving her fishing-line for them to stop.

“Esther’s come,” said Jenifer to Mark, “won’t you go back for her?”

Mark had seen her before Jenifer, but continued rowing all the same. He now shut his eyes, saying,—

“She may wave that line as long as she likes, I can’t see her. She must pester somebody else if she goes fishing to-night.”

In about half an hour they came to a little group of boats moored abreast of the lighthouse, whose occupants, young men and maidens for the most part, were busily engaged in pulling in the chads, which came to their hooks as soon as the lines were thrown overboard.
