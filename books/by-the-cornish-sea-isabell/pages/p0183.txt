“Well, sir, spoase we do say the seeame price as I do charge for digging a greeave. I’d sooner dig a greeave than a geeat-post, the ground is more freer a bra’ dale.”

“Very good, what do you charge for making a grave?”

“Three and six; and tedn a farden too much: though there be some people to grumble at the odd sixpence.”

“Do they? I suppose you charge the extra sixpence on account of the melancholy associations connected with your calling?”

“Melancholy ’sociations, sir? I doant see no melancholy ’sociations in havin’ a job to do; the moast melancholiest ’sociations, seemin to me, es when nobody weeant die for a month at a stretch. Thank the Lord, that edn very often.”
