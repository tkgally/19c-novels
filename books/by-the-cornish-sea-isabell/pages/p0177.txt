“But there it is,” he continued as they reached the Vicarage gate, “you can examine it at your leisure.”

It consisted of a shaft set in the earth, measuring about seven feet in height, surmounted by a beautiful Greek cross. Mr. Burlington was enraptured. He went round and round it, scrutinizing the tracery and admiring the proportions, and ended by taking careful measurements of its length and breadth and depth and height.
