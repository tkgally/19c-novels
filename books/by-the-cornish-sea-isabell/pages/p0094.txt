“If you do,” returned Master Nash, “I’ll punch you when we get out of school.”

Here was a dilemma, indeed! Which was better, to be thrashed indoors or punched out? I am sorry to say the moral aspect of the case never presented itself to Arthur. He thought not of his duty, but of the consequences. The thrashing he might avoid, the punching he could depend upon if he called out his friend Nash. Whatever Nash’s vices might be, he kept his word—at all events when he had promised to oblige any one with a punching.
