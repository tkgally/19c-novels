“Perhaps you would like something to eat,” suggested Mr. Burlington hospitably.

“No, thenky,” said the captain; “I can’t eat nothing while they’re in there; my nerves arn’t so steady as Dan’s; perhaps ’tis because I’m older. I shall be all of a tremble till they come out again.”

“Listen,” said George. “Arthur said he would let me know when they got on dry land inside.”

A faint, long-drawn “Ahoy! ahoy!” as if from a considerable distance, came through the dark opening.

“They’re in all right!” exclaimed George. “Now then, seals, look out for yourselves, the Philistines are upon you.”
