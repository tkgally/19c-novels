“Aw, dear! you’ve a tookt my breath away,” said Jeremiah in despair. “I’ve a listened to ’ee, my son, and have made a bit of money by it, but you mustn’t expect me to invest much in this here risky specilation, for I’m doubting that you’ll never get enough by it to pay the working expenses.”

“Oh! I’ll chance that,” said Mark, well satisfied that his father offered no stronger opposition. “And now you have given your consent, I’ll tell mother all about it.”
