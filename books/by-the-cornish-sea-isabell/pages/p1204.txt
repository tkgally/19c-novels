After a time intelligence came of the arrival at San Francisco of the ship <i>Ocean Queen</i>, having on board the rescued crew of the <i>Annie Leigh</i>, and it was now all but certain that the <i>Annie Lee</i> and her crew had gone to the bottom.

Mrs. Treleaven, who had been ailing for some time, sank into a profound melancholy which ended in death before the end of the year. For some weeks before the end she ceased to speak to Mrs. Trewhella and Jenifer, who were assiduous in their attentions to the poor widow.
