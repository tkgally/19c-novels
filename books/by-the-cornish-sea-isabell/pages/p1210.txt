“I believe not. There’s a chance for you, old fellow, you used to be sweet upon her you know. It was almost an engagement at one time, wasn’t it?”

“Almost,” replied Mark; “but she took offence at some little attention I paid to Esther Mansell, and then Treleaven came in the way, and I gave her mortal offence by calling him a fishing fellow.”

“A somewhat proud young woman,” suggested George.

“Well, rather,” assented Mark, “but as good as gold—she’s as good as she looks, and that’s saying a great deal.”

“Come, come,” said George in a bantering tone, “I verily believe, my worthy friend, thou hast still some lingering affection for the comely damsel.”
