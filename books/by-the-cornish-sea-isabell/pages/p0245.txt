This suggestion met with general approval, and Mark declared that he thought he should like it very much.

“There’s one difficulty, though,” suggested Jeremiah; “we haven’t been very friendly since my dog Ponto killed that old tom-cat of his. P’raps you wouldn’t mind sounding the old gentleman, Dan; I’m afraid he’ll bite my nose off if I ask any favour from him.”
