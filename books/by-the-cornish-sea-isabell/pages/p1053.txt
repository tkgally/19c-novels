“No, no,” said Dan, grasping the gunwale of the boat, “’tes a dead man I mane, leastways he edn quite dead, for he’s able to grooan a bit.”

“What in the world do you mean, Mr. Daniel?” asked the antiquary.

“Why some poor shipwrecked sailor have a got by some manner of manes into the hawl, and he’s moanin’ and groanin’ as ef he weeant be long for this world,” answered Daniel. “You had better bring the booat abreast the hawl, and throw ovver the anchor, and tie the oars to wan of the lines, so as we can pull ’em in to meeake a raft to put un upon. We must be sharp too, for the tide’ll soon cover the mouth of the hawl.”
