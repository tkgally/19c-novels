Tall and well-made, with black eyes and clustering hair of the same hue, he was, so far as outward appearances go, a young man whom a woman might be pardoned for falling in love with. And, indeed, with the exception of a somewhat large amount of self-satisfaction and self-assertion, there was nothing particular in his character and conduct to find fault with. He was handsome, capable, and prosperous, and, therefore, decidedly eligible from the matrimonial market point of view.
