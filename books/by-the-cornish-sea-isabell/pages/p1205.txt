Jenifer suffered as keenly as the mother, but she was young and healthy, and after a time resumed her work as before, though with a whiter cheek and more subdued mien. As for Daniel, he never ceased to regret the loss of the brave young fellow who had in affection as in relationship been to him almost a son.
