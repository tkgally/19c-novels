“Nothing strong for me, Mr. Daniel, thank you. I myself have become in principle nearly a teetotaler—in principle, I say, for I take a drop of whisky now and then, and some brandy when I feel fatigued at night—not an uncommon circumstance with me; but now, as I see you are drinking tea, I will take a cup, very weak, with no sugar and not much milk.”

This potent beverage having been prepared, Mr. Burlington resumed,—
