Unfortunately for the man, he followed this advice, with the result that the precious gingham was turned inside out at the very first corner and resolutely refused to turn back again. Perhaps contact with the archæological donkey had instilled a stubborn stiffness into its ribs, but kicked and knocked and thumped it refused to bend, and the guilty servant had to deliver it to its owner, looking more like a dilapidated cabbage than a staid and respectable umbrella.
