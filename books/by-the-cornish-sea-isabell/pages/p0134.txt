This wily individual had made sure that the new master was not yet arrived, and then practised upon the credulity of his fellows.

This artifice naturally disgusted the dupes, and half a dozen of the biggest boys, seizing Nash, proceeded to inflict a suitable punishment upon that part of the human frame provided for the purpose, when again the door opened, and in walked the rector, a gentleman in spectacles, and a lady with long drooping curls.

“Boys, boys, what’s all this?” asked the goodnatured rector.
