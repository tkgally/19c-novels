Therefore when Mark insisted that Jenifer should be his bride, and Jessie the parson to tie the knot, the two girls yielded after Jenifer’s ineffectual protest.

“Well, Mark,” said Jenifer, “you must only marry me for ten minutes, I want to play at shows after that.”

“Very well,” said Mark, “but you’ll be my wife when I’m big enough to keep a horse and cart of my own, won’t you, Jenifer?”

“Yes,” answered the bride-elect, “but I don’t want to leave Jessie. Can’t you marry Jessie, too?”
