“I see I have made a mistake in coming here,” said the antiquary; “they were blessed with one valuable relic, and that they wantonly destroyed. But is there nothing else worth looking at?”

“Aw, iss, sir, there’s plenty to look at. There’s the say, and the rocks, and the ships, and the castle—ould King Harry, what had a bra’ many wives, he built he, so they say—and there’s the pilchards. If I was you, sir, I should go to the fish-cellars to see um ‘bulking’ fish; everybody amoast do go.”

“Oh, very well, then I’ll run down, and be back again in time for lunch—say half-past one.”
