“But the Rundles are far better off than we,” said Arthur.

“What if they are?” was the answer; “girls don’t value young men by pounds, shillings, and pence. I can’t think any young woman would prefer Rundle to you—I’m sure I should detest him.”

Arthur was not so sure that Jenifer would reject Mark and choose him. He felt that his mother, with her natural partiality for her son, was hardly a standard by which he could gauge Jenifer’s feelings.
