“And put the parting out of sight?” he asked.

“No, not exactly that,” she answered, “but after the parting there will be the thought of our love for each other to cheer us.”

“Tell me that you love me more than any one else in the world,” he said abruptly, looking into her eyes.

“You don’t want me to tell you that,” was the answer, “you know that already.”

“But tell me,” he persisted.

She looked bashfully but steadily at him, and said slowly,—

“I love you more than anybody else in the world. I love you with my whole heart. I will love you till death.”
