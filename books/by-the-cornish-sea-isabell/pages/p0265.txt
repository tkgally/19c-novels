One night he received a severe fright. It was pitch dark, and he was following the path through the first field as best he could, and congratulating himself that the ghosts were far in his rear, when he became conscious of some presence near. A thundering sound seemed to shake the earth beneath him. He turned cold for a moment, and then the very roots of his hair seemed on fire. Had the ghosts come at last?
