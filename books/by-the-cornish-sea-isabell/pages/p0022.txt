Mrs. Treleaven was a fragile-looking woman of about thirty-five years of age. She was the only daughter of Captain Retallack, a master mariner, trading to the East Indies, and had married, seven years before, the coastguardsman John Treleaven, in spite of the bitter opposition of her father.

Captain Retallack, who was a widower at the time of his daughter’s marriage, died a year after without showing any sign that he had forgiven her, and leaving all his savings to a distant cousin, with whom, in his lifetime, he had held no intercourse whatever.
