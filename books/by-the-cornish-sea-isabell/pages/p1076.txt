As soon as she could speak she exclaimed, “Arthur is drowning! he is under the cliff at Deadman’s Haven! you must go round in a boat, there are no ropes there.”

“How ded a git there?” asked Daniel.

“Getting Esther and me up,” answered Jenifer; “but be quick, father, or the tide will wash him away.”

Daniel, grasping the situation, rushed to the quay, and in a minute or two a six-oared gig, manned by the idlers who were as usual pacing up and down seaman-fashion, was making for Deadman’s Haven with all possible speed.
