During the succeeding months the mother and sweetheart waited anxiously for tidings of Arthur, but no tidings came. Once their hopes were raised by the news that a bottle had been found on the south coast of Ireland containing a paper with this message written upon it: “Brig <i>Annie Leigh</i> going down in the Bay of Biscay after collision—ship bearing down upon us—weather fine—boat coming alongside—saved—James Maclean.”
