# Chapter VII.

[[subtitle]] Billing and Cooing.

<verse>
Live with me, and be my love,
And we will all the pleasures prove
That hills and valleys, dales and fields,
And all the craggy mountains yields.
</verse>
[[right]] <i>Shakespeare.</i>

W<sc>hen</sc> the funeral was over and paid for, it was found that Uncle Anthony had left his relations the sum of a hundred and five pounds, eight shillings, and ninepence, besides the furniture, which was old and of no great value. To this sum Arthur added twenty pounds from the money earned through his fishing, and became the proud joint possessor of a hundred and five pounds, and shillings and pence as aforesaid, invested in the bank at St. Cyrus.
