This was admitted by all, and it only remained to decide upon some particular name. Mary, the name of the barque which brought her to their coast, was first suggested, but was rejected at once.

“We doant want to be always bringing to her mind that bad day, when she graws up,” said Daniel. “Mary weeant do.”

Mrs. Dunstable thought Margaret would be suitable, as the child, like a pearl, was plucked from the sea. Mr. Carnanton liked Ruth, because like Ruth she had come from a far country to dwell among strangers.
