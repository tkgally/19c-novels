In the pale grey hour of early dawn we came along side a large armed vessel. A golden flower pot, with green and yellow roses, was painted on its stern; a flag, blood-red, with the half-moon embroidered on it, hung calmly floating above; and as we came upon the deck, the crew were rising from their rest; and some were combing out the beard; some curling the moustaches; some anointing the shaven head; some lifting water, and performing the prescribed ablutions; and they looked up at us, as we were driven forward, with mocking grins, and cruel eyes, and a contemptuous hate.
