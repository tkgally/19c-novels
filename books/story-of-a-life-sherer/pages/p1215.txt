I looked upon this form of life, and glory—was it indeed to die!—and thus?—and so young?

The dark executioners threw him down on his belly, upon the sand, and with a razor they gashed a deep wound for the impaling stake. Before they had time with their ready paste to staunch the flowing blood, a dozen Greeks, wounded and beaten as they did so, had broken into the still circle, and dipped their handkerchiefs in the stream, to them so naturally sainted; but the Turkish guard instantly threw buckets of water all about to wash up the precious flow, and many of his countrymen were driven back with the blows of staves, and sabres, disappointedly.
