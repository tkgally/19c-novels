How mean—how poor—how base—how utterly despairing I felt as I contrasted my life and my loves with his. Death would not come to me; I bore, as it were, a charmed life. Again I was bitterly tormented by the thought of my apostacy; and yet I asked myself what would this Henry have done, situated as I was? Would he have given up the gentle Fatima? Could he have seen her die? Vain questions—in a course like his, such temptation would never have assailed him; or if it had, a way of escape would have been made for him by that mighty hand, to which he daily looked, on which he daily leaned.
