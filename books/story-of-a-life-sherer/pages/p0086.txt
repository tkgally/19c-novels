It was with joy that I heard the proposal of the Count to leave the city, and pass a few weeks at a quinta of his near Cintra. It seemed as if I had escaped from the tyrannous grasp of polluting fiends, when I stood again alone in the deep shadow of woods—when I heard the murmur of brooks, and the song of birds—when I climbed the naked rock to see the sun rise, or watched him sinking in his ocean bed. I loved to look upon the black cypress-tree, which, in Portugal, grows up of a stately beauty like the cedar of Lebanon.
