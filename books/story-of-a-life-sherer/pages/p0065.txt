Above all things luxury breaks down the strength, if not of our principle, at least of its resisting power.

Here was I, with less experience of the world than a boy trundling a hoop among his playfellows. With an imagination all fire—a heart swelling and impressible—a face, and form, and figure inherited from a father lofty in his stature, strong yet faultless in his symmetry, and having himself the black eye, and prominent nose of manly beauty, to which in me was added the mouth and smile of a mother, whose countenance was ever full of sweetness and expression. Here was I, abandoned to my own weak reason and strong will.
