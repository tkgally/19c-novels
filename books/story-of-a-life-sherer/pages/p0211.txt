At length I reached the <i>Hospice</i>, and was dried, and warmed, and fed; and slept through the long day, and on again through a refreshing night; and, when the sun was high, and shone brightly on the snows, and beamed on the blue lake that mirrors back the high, encircling peaks around, I again set forward. Down to the base of Cenis, amid those sounds and sights the traveller loves, I rapidly descended; falling waters made voices to my ear; all things looked fresh and green; the tree, the bush, the plant, the blade of grass were glistening as they dried their wholesome tears; birds sung, and every passing muleteer was carolling for joy.
