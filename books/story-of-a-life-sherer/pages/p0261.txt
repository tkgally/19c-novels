In wild haste I spoke, and fled away; but I was pursued with humane activity, brought back, made to throw off the poison, and lay, for days, exhausted on my couch. I lost all flesh; my eyes became hollow; my cheeks very wan, and, to crown my misery, my kind landlady became a bankrupt; was forced to leave her house, and go, and take service as a journeywoman in an Italian flower-shop.
