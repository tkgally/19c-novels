[[cont]]those painted coffins, in which the wealthier dead lie embalmed. We lifted one of these lids; the mask painted on it was that of a female. Rude as was the portrait, the artist had contrived to give a roundness, as of youth, to the cheek and chin—a bright blackness to the eye, and to put a smile on the red lip; within there was a strait stiff form; narrow, and shapeless with its hundred linen folds, and the dark mummy mouth was gilded o’er, and the hands gilt. A branch of the sacred sycamore lay on every coffin; perfect it looked, but, at the gentlest touch, fell to a formless dust. Here we passed the long, long day impatiently, and said but little to each other. I thought of death, and of the world when it was young, and of the world to come. Glad was I, when, at the dusk evening hour, they let us out again.
