“‘What! Priyamvada, Vasanta leave me? leave his sister? Oh, it cannot be, we are two wings; one could not fly without the other’s aid. Be less fond of him—how? look at Vasanta; listen to him; then tell me, how?’

“‘Letchima, I know you fear the gods, and love the goddess mother of your name. I tell you that you must wean your heart from this violent affection for your brother Vasanta. It cannot be happy—cannot be innocent.’
