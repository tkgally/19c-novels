It was dark night when he closed his tale. I could not see his features, but from his tone I thought he wept. I asked him “Had he friends?”

“Dear ones.”

“Had he ever loved?”

“Love is a sacred thing—do you think, stranger, that I have laughed and shed tears, like other men, and lived till gray hairs are mingling with these brown locks, and never loved?”

“What, then, is it you fear for me; wherefore have you thus opened out your heart—surely travel is innocent, is profitable.”
