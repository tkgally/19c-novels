I had so planned it, that I felt secure in the concealment of my being a Protestant; but the main facts of my flight from my country, and my family,—of Agatha,—of the wealth acquired—concealing whence it came,—and of my present isolated state, I confessed freely. The priest heard me silently to the close.

“And can your own church give no comfort or counsel,” said the invisible. “Me you have not deceived; but you are awfully deceiving yourself.
