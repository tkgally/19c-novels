[[cont]]and had them removed under my own eye; saw them in clean, and wholesome chambers, and in comfortable beds, and then returned home, and took my lonely meal in quiet thankfulness. When it was dark I went out into my balcony, and sate looking on the stars, and, at times, down on the dark, and mirror-like canals, which reflected, in their still depths, these distant planets, as if they too were lighted up below with the same pale rays of gold, which softly shine above us, and gem the dark sky when night is black around.

Lost in deep musings many hours rolled by. Fewer, and fewer, were the straying lights, which flitting on the waters, and casting on them flashing gleams, marked the late returning gondolas—at last all was motionless and silent.

Was it a phantasy of the o’erwrought brain? No;—dreams are to the dreamer realities; such was this waking dream, this vision of the night to me.
