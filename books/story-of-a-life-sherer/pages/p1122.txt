I was delighted as these wonders of our common nature were thus powerfully presented to my eager mind. I loved those tempests of the soul; the smiling and the weeping of those fierce, bearded, armed men; the brow burning with noble anger, the flesh trembling with a cold fear; and the heart melting with a soft tenderness; and all for a mere phantom of the brain—a creation of the desert bard—wonderful!
