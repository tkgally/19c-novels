“For anything that can serve or save this lady.”

“Then go to your sleeping chamber, and persuade the old people to go to bed; and, at one o’clock, when the moon rises, arm; and when you hear a pebble strike your casement, drop yourself from the window; it is a long fall, but the ground is soft. I have known older than you to leap it down safe.”

It was with difficulty I could prevail on the father and mother to retire to their chamber, and on no account could they be persuaded to do more than lie down, dressed as they were, on mattresses.

It was near midnight when I got to my room, and I lay looking at the window, that I might catch the very first glimmer of the rising moon. The rain fell less heavily, and at length ceased altogether; and the moon rose red, and crescented. I felt down and fixed my pistols firmer in my broad loin-girth, and drew tighter the strap of my sword-belt; and I thought a prayer; and then I felt honored and animated by the enterprise.
