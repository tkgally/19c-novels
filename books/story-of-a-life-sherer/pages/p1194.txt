“I tell you that I have lost her; but I must relate the mournful circumstances as they occurred.

“I wrote to you after my last illness, and then acquainted you with the sad interruption of our happiness, and the cloud that seemed gathering over those enchanting prospects, which I had gazed on with the fond and foolish belief that they would surely and easily be realised. Alas! they have proved like those blue masses of golden-tinged clouds, which take such shapes at eve as mock the form and the solidity of the mountain; but human foot did never yet scale their heaven-lighted summits. The whole but a gorgeous vision that has no stay. Faint image of the promised joy which I would picture—what have suns and mountains to do with the form and heart of her I love?
