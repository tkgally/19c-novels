The sacred fringe, the hallowed phylactery, the frontlets, and the arm amulets inscribed with sentences from the law—all these things the old man venerated; you could see, in the commonest actions of his daily life, that he felt or fancied (it is the same) that an angel stood on his right hand, and another on his left.
