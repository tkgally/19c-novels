You, George, will never speak thus to me. You have seen her, and I have told you that her lovely form but feebly images her lovelier mind. You know, too, that although she did spare me the pain of uttering that mournful, melancholy, and eternal farewel, which was already forming itself on my trembling lip, and which, as my burning brain conceived it, and my heart bled with the cruel wound, shook my whole frame: you know that such would have been my course, when the dread alternative was placed before me. I should have,—I have resigned for ever, those hopes of happiness below, on which my soul fondly hung.
