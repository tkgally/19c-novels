[[corr I,|“I,]] too, have sinned. Like thee, in agony of soul, have mourned, dark and disconsolate.—The shirt of hair, the scourge, the long and wasting fast, the midnight watchings, and naked body, stretched in prostration on the cold marble of the grave; I have tried these things, but I found no peace, no rest, no hope! It came—the blessed light that gave it me—as bright, as soft, as the young early beam of dew-eyed morn in summer. Look there, my son,” and he pointed to the crucifix, “that, spiritually seen; it shone from that; and my guilt was washed away by the clear fountain flowing from that wound, where the blood of expiation was mingled with the sacred and pure waters of everlasting life.
