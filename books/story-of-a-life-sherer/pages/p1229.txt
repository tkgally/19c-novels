A sigh of the night wind burst from between two loosened masses of the huge stones. “[[corr Fool,’ theyseemed|Fool,” they seemed]] to say, “we are the world:—kings swelling in their pride—slaves fainting in their toil, dug us from the dark quarry and reared us here; a hundred thousand of such things as you lived only for this object; for us they earlier waked, they later slept;—they cried over us with bitter tears; the blood of their lashed skins has stained us; they cursed, they groaned, they sickened, and they died; and then he died, for whom they had so laboured—<i>all</i>
