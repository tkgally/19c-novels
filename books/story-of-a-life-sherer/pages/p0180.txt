[[cont]]Osman, reflect—if it is so. Whither can it lead? Where end? A foreigner, a stranger to our altar, you would entail misery on her, on us, on yourself. It can hardly be innocent, such love; but oh! my beloved brother, if it be a love unhallowed, fly the fatal snare; break the ignoble bonds; return to us quickly; you will bring back smiles, and peace, and we shall all be happy in our forest home.

“I have a thousand things to tell you of—above all things, I have treasured up for you every word and look of our departed Edward, during his afflicting illness. Many times he spoke of you with tears, at the sad thought, that it was impossible for him to see you before he died. Come, Osman, come to the gentle light of truth and peace. Somers has contrived to shed this blessing over our bereaved circle; and here your fevered spirit will find repose. I long to embrace you.
