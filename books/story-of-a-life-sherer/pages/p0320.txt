During the day I could get no privacy—no rest. I had no power to think of my situation, and, in the course of it, I witnessed a strange scene of Turkish despotism. There was a sudden tumult, and loud cries, and all hurried off; and they dragged with them the renegade.

He had struck, it seemed, the black cook, and had overset the food, and insulted the serang. Again all was silence, as, amid the hushed crowd, the two accusers told their tale; a muttered something fell from the prisoner, but the dead silence awed him, and he felt fear, and the savage eye looked apprehension.
