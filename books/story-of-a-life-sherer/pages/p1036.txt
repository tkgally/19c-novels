Rachel stood anxiously trembling over her, and Benjamin plucking his beard with nervous fear. But the angel of the Lord came down, in mercy, to that narrow bed, and the aged Deborah sate up, and in sobered feebleness she spoke, and opened her sightless eyes, and they glimmered as struggling to behold.

“My children, an hundred and twenty years have been my days of sorrow. Five generations have I seen; and of the evil under the sun have I tasted, in many wanderings, among strange people; and, for many years have I now lain still, in darkness, and the Lord has heard my prayer. I feel cold, my children, and I die. Swear to me, that you will carry up my bones, and lay them in the cave of my father’s house, over against the wall of Rama, near to the palm-tree.”
