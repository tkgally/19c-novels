At last we reached the wished-for land—saw the white cliffs of England, and ran in. Home was the word on every lip—alas! I had no home, but I loved the very sound, and joyed for others at all the images it brought before their brightening eyes, as in silence they seemed thinking on all dear to them. Thus to me my country became a home—its soil that is, to tread on and be buried in—its people to look upon, and love as brothers. I travelled up in the first instance to London.
