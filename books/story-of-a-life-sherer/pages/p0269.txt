Strangers have got our cottage, and our vineyard; my brothers and sisters are all scattered, I know not where; and I know not where my poor Giuseppe is now wandering, and singing for his daily bread. Till the ruin of this my humble patron, I was wretched indeed, but yet resignedly so. I well knew that with haggard and emaciated features, and a palsied limb, and the ragged veil of poverty, it were vain to hope for the exciting of any sympathy amid the common crowd of the wealthy and self-indulgent. Beauty in weeds and tears, loveliness in poverty and pain, will seldom want assistance; although oftentimes they are succoured by those who look to the harvest of a base, and a compelled reward. Forgive me this unworthy spleen, Senhor; I speak from the experience of a bitter life, and I joy to have been at once relieved, and reproved for my uncharitable feeling, by your noble bounty.
