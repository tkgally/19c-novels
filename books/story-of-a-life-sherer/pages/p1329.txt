“Each night, at the dead mid hour, the small chime gave its weak mournful tinkle, and immediately the many doors opened and shut, and we glided noiselessly to the gloomy church for the midnight service, a service of low melancholy murmurs, and silently we stole back again, looking in our white robes like shrouded forms, which, having walked the night, fled back before the matin to their tombs.
