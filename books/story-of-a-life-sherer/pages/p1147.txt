I met them at the door.

“The girl, the girl,” said the old Arab, “turn her forth—give her up to them.” “For God’s sake,” cried the Italian, “lose no time, they are furious—they will burst the gate—they will take her by force, and we may then be slain in the affray. You cannot save her—turn her out.—Why did she come?—Did you beckon her?—She must be a common worthless creature—turn her out—all will be well—a few purses will make up the matter for you.—<i>You</i> have nothing to fear—you cannot save <i>her</i>—<i>She</i> must die.”

She rose, that youthful being. “I will go—It is my hour—God is merciful.—I have brought you into danger, Christian—forgive;” and she bowed down her veiled head, and touched my feet.
