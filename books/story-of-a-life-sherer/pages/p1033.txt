Such are the Turks, and such was the government of Algiers.

The disturbances and outrages in the city, for many days after the election of the new Dey, were excessive; the exactions heavy, particularly on the poor oppressed Jews; and the aged Benjamin, trembling at the prospect of a turbulent and tyrannical reign, determined to withdraw secretly, and take refuge among his brethren in Egypt. This intention he communicated to me, and promised me, that he would arrange with a captain of a Sicilian vessel, then in the harbour, to smuggle me off when she sailed, and would supply me with money sufficient for my need, until I could communicate with Venice, where he had no doubt I should find my property secure, and untouched.
