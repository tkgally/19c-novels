It was a dark night; there was no lamp; but I saw four gondolieri, and knew it was a bark of the larger class, and designed for flight; so rapid was our course, that we were already seated in a carriage at Fusina, ere I thought myself clear of the city.

“My deliverer carried me to England, and placed me in commodious lodgings, in London.

“In no place, in no country, is the condition of a female, who has yielded, lost, or been robbed of her honour, other than unhappy; but, in England, the true paradise of girls, free, chaste, and fearless; of wives loved, leaned upon, and honoured by noble manly husbands, the lot of the mistress is doubly wretched.
