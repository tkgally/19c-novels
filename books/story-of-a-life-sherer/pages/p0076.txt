The shade of the house was sufficient to conceal and favour the observing gaze, while so near as I sate to the object of it, it could not disappoint me by hiding her charms; nay, it rather threw them out into a fuller, richer relief, as is the case in the portraits of the best and oldest masters. Her form was tall and majestic, but yet femininely so; she had that ample bosom of matronly beauty, where the breasts are widely parted, and swell to a gentle fulness: the white and rounded arm of perfect proportion. Her graceful neck rose stately from fine-falling shoulders, a faint carnation hue just tinged a cheek, the complexion of which was pale and transparent.
