Again I was sent for home, to be present at the anniversary of the marriage, which my father unwisely chose to celebrate, for no blessing had followed on the alliance. They had no child, and the character of my brother had become contemptibly notorious for his general and lawless profligacy. Indulged by my father, he was wasting his substance in riotous living, and expended large sums on a courtezan of the city, of the most daring and shameless character.

We were all staying at the villa without the city; it was the night before the anniversary; we had separated at about midnight, and I was walking in the long gallery alone and sad; not a sound was to be heard; suddenly, a cry, a fearful piercing cry, such as the murdered die with, woke the wide palace.
