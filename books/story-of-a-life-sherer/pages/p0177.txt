[[cont]]milton (who is always talking about you), has been most kind. Old Frankland has shown, in his own odd way, a true neighbourly sympathy, and the girls have been to Harriet quite sisterly. Your mother still keeps her room; she was very much affected, so much so, that it induced a low nervous fever, which is only now beginning to subside. She sends you her fondest love, and bids me say, that she prays every night and morning for your safe return. Somers, who is now sitting in the room, and who, by the way, has been, under God’s blessing, our only stay and true comforter, bids me give you his kindest regards.

“Ever, my dearest boy,
