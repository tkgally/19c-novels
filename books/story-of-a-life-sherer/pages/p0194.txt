“She does; she does. I marked the turning gaze; the beauteous hand that gently pressed upon your willing arm, and the breast that heaved its silent speech of love.”

“Your eye deceived your restless mind.”

“Ah! No.”

He stood for a moment leaning on his unsheathed sword, silent, and very pale; big tears gathered in his eyes, and dropped, heavy, and few; then coming closer, and putting up his sword, he took my hand in his, and holding it very nervously, looked fixedly in my face.

“Be it so. I could have cherished her with a fonder, closer sheltering than mother-bird its unfledged nestling. The light of her smile, the music of her voice, had made this dull world to me a very paradise. It is not to be. Let me look upon you; you are the husband of her choice.
