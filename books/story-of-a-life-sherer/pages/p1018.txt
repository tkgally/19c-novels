“What cause, stranger, hath moved thee to this wish?”

“The love of truth.”

“Knowest thou the strictness of our law?”

“I do.”

“Hast thou any worldly interest to forward by this act?”

“None.”

“Has the love of any of our daughters taken thee?”

“I have loved one of the daughters of Israel, but she is dead.”

“And is it then the indulgence of a vain fancy—is it to cherish a lover’s sorrow, thou wouldst defile our sanctuary?”
