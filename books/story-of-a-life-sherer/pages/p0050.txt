My fear had come—thus early come; and they were mocking.

I clung to the side of the companion with all my steadiness, and strength. The morning came; a reddish glimmer in the east; a dark scud above; the sea heavy and black, with thick far-blown showers of spray, flying wild from the tops of the curling billows.

The ship drove fast before the gale; the rudder torn away; the canvass gone; nothing to hold the furious wind, but the bending, straining masts, and the wailing cordage. The waves broke over the vessel repeatedly. Every sea that struck her I thought would be the last; that she would go to pieces, or suddenly overset and sink.—Still on she drove; though under her bare poles, fast bounded on the stormy billows.
