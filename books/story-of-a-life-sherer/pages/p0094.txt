[[cont]]and I closed by expressing my great pleasure that Providence had enabled me so soon and so agreeably to repay in part the obligation conferred.

He appeared much struck by the animation of my manner, for he said instantly, that he rejoiced at any circumstance that had given him the opportunity of making my acquaintance, and still more to find that I was a neighbour.

As danger was evidently abroad, he insisted that I should return with him, and pass the remainder of the night at the villa. We accordingly prepared to retrace our steps; a wound in the fleshy part of the thigh, which he had not felt at the moment, and in the heat of his late encounter, now became stiff, and painful. I bound it up, and placing him on the baudet, supported his limb with my hand, as we moved slowly back towards the mansion.
