Now, may the God of Jacob swallow you up quick—I have not bowed down, and worshipped your golden calf. With Shadrach, Meshech, and Abednego I have been faithful. Ye would have cast me down into your burning fiery furnace; but, lo! I am delivered out of your hands.”

I moved towards him with an eager, and kind anxiety—

“Back, I say—back to your burning Sodom—surely, my Lord shall destroy you, and rain down fire from Heaven on your head.”

“I am no enemy—I would serve, save you.”

“Are you a Christian?”

“Yes.”

“Then are you to me as the Philistine, and the Canaanite. I spit my hate at thee, though it bring my death stroke.”
