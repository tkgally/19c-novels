“Suddenly the cot swayed as if to overset. Old Dennis in reaching over for some purpose, had fallen into the water; my mother tried to save, and followed him; they immediately drifted away from the cot. In still alarm, yet swift, and anxious, I contrived to row to where my mother was struggling in the water; I reached out, and caught hold of her, but she slipped from my grasp. I lost my presence of mind with the failure of my effort. Wildly I called—cried aloud for help, but I could see no one. Dennis, unable to swim, from lameness, joined in my eager cries, whenever the choking water suffered him. In that awful moment a sound of oars was heard, a vigorous strong pulling; a cot shot round the point; my mother was lifted with strength and skill into it; away towards Dennis, and the like office performed for him, though with a difficulty and motion of the little bark that made me tremble for them all.
