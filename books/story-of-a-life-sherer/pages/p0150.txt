The contrast of your warm affection and my lord’s cold indifference, was trying, very trying to my heart; thus only could your love ever have moved me at all. Thus was it that it did; but restored, amid the late awful scenes, to be the controller of my own actions, I turn back with a full and faithful heart, to my early, my only, my cold, yet cherished love.

“In the hope and belief that it will assuredly dissipate all the illusions which associate me, in your day-dreams, with vain schemes of impossible happiness, I have written out for you my tale of sorrow.

“I shall remember and pray for you.

[[right]] “A<sc>gatha</sc>.”

Again and again I read over this letter, and wept upon it; and though it seemed to tear all hope from my bosom, yet I was to be remembered and prayed for by Agatha.
