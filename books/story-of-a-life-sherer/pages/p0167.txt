Your sister was very weak, and in the lowest spirits. I can assure you that the intelligence of your safety and merciful preservation has revived and refreshed us all. We hope that, on the receipt of this letter, you will immediately leave Lisbon, and return home. Your presence is most anxiously desired, and will be very consoling to all. Family circles, as they become smaller, should draw closer. A very dear member of yours has been suddenly removed—your amiable brother Edward is no more.”

I dropped the letter, covered my face with my hands, and groaned and wept bitterly, as I paced my room with the quick step of agony. Alone I was, yet I covered my face. At last I took up the fatal paper, and read on—
