“To the house of mourning!” I exclaimed, as my tears flooded forth, “to the house of mourning! Oh! Somers, the Osman whom you knew, the brother of the sweet and gentle Edward, I know not where he is. I look within—I cannot find him. The inner Osman is a being of warm, wild, restless wishes, with an aching, unsatisfied heart. One who could neither impart nor receive consolation. Not privileged to weep and grieve in a pure company of mourners. My once happy home would but represent to me the image of lost bliss, no more communicable.”
