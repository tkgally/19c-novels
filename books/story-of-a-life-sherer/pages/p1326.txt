Jealousy, certainly, and yet I never, never breathed a word of love to her. I would excuse my folly to myself, by fancying that I feared her danger, as a beautiful innocent thing, exposed in a large city to temptations, and I especially felt this, if I saw any of the handsome young gallants of the city pass by, and look admiringly at her, or attractively themselves.
