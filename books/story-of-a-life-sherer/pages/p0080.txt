The morning came; again I was early up, and out in my balcony, and gazing at the window, and I saw the lady of my fancied love come forth; but judge my astonishment. She came careless and smiling, with unbraided hair, and an elderly duenna following; and she was gaily throwing back her white arms to catch those long, thick, wanton tresses, and bind them up again.

Her voice I heard—her features I looked upon. No; they were not the tones I listened for. Those eyes and lips had beauties, but not the charms I sought. She no sooner discovered me than she seemed greatly surprised; but soon the expression of her countenance changed to free delight; and, after the custom of the silly, prisoned women of that land, she gave those glances of the sparkling eye, those gestures of the playful hand, that challenge the bold wooer.
