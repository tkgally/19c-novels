[[cont]]Ambrogio, you will scarce know him again; nor he you, in truth. Why, boy, how round your shoulders are, and how your neck stoops. Oh! this comes of study; and your complexion, how changed! why it is pale and dull as if you were a cardinal already—but for a churchman this little matters—it is better thus:—to be sure what a change the tonsure makes; and how close they have clipped off the hair beneath: why you want but a jackass to drive before you, and the brown robe and cowl, to pass for St. Francis himself. Go to your chamber, and try to make yourself look more like a wedding-guest, or you will surprize your noble sister, Lucrezia: she is a girl of high mind, and keen observation. I should not like her to be disappointed with any of my family, even you; though, to be sure, it matters less, for your paths will never lie together.’
