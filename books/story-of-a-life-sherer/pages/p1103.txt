There is no finer restorative for the languid mind than the pure wind of heaven, as it blows freshly over the white breaking crests of the blue waves, and fills the swelling sail, and drives you laughing onwards to the wished-for haven. It seemed, for a time, as if I had left my cares, my sorrows, my disappointments all behind me, chambered among dark city dwellings. Never was a voyage more swift and prosperous than that which bore us to Alexandria.

The mountain, the cliff, the chapel on the rock, are the common objects for the seaman’s land-mark; and hues of black, and slaty blue, and a red-brown, first meet the sight of home-returning men, or eager travellers.
