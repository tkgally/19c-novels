“A great anxiety for the health of this innocent caused me again to write to its father. I stated the expences of my confinement, the debt to my landlady, the illness of my child—of his child; the aid, and comforts, which, from the smallness of my salary as a figurante, I had no means to procure for it.

“I got a cruel reply; denying his child, and vilifying me. It contained a bank-note of small amount, telling me to physic my brat, and myself for once, but to teaze him no more, and never presume to name him as its father.
