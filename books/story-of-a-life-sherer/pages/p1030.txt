“Why, the fellow was only a water-carrier at Ismyr.”

“So I have heard, lords; but he has the green turban.”

“Ah! but he has no right to wear it; they would tear it from his head in Istamboul.”

“That none dare here.”

“Give me wine, thou Jew dog, what dost thou know of men’s daring?”

“They say too he had his fortune predicted to him; and that he is born under a lucky planet. I remember the day he was proclaimed.”

“How fell it out?”
