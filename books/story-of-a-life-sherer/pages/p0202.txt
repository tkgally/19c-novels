[[cont]]were not unfrequently throughout my after life, checquered by others of a joy, which, though tasted by a guilty thing, I can yet look back upon as pure.

Air and light, wood and water, were not cursed to me. They never are to the veriest wretch that lives. The hot brow is cooled; the lonely path is cheered; the cold hand is warmed; the thirsting lip is moistened; and all men—sleep.

[[break]]
