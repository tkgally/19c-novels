I did not, could not, frown upon a girl so beautiful, so young, and taught to be the thing she was;—but here, the thought of broken loves, of her I virtuously loved and lost, came o’er my spirit, and, like a talisman, it changed the scene; chilled the warm blood, and though it made me wretched, kept me pure.
