On such a spot, I well know, you could not willingly remain a single unnecessary hour. Indeed, every association with your present residence must, of necessity, be so melancholy, that we cannot doubt your eagerness to leave it, and you must long to be once more safe at home. The many awful scenes you have lately witnessed, and your merciful preservation throughout them, have no doubt left a salutary and deep impression on your heart. I feel satisfied that my dear Osman will improve it. We have lately had our lesson, and have learned, by the bedside of your poor departed brother, how, with the hope properly fixed, a very child can look steadily upon the king of terrors. We are recovering, although slowly, from the gloom into which his loss had naturally thrown us; for, in the first moments, we were deaf to the voice of consolation, and turned from the comforting words of poor Somers, with the natural impatience of the bereft and the wretched.
