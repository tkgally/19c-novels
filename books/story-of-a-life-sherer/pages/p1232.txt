In this manner we travelled for some days; by day shaping our course in the desert, in the evening coming near again to the inhabited country,—once only did we meet any persons on that waste; and those were only a few swart Moggrebyns, in white cloaks, having white and peaked cowls; and they looked out darkly from them, and uttered the hoarse salaams, as they passed us by.

I shall always remember this strange, silent journey with pleasure; there was a calm about it very sobering to my poor spirit, after all I had gone through. It is thus, that when the couched eye again receives from the skilful leech the power of seeing, for a time the chamber is darkened, that it may not be dazzled, to its injury, by too sudden a glare of bright and attractive objects. So with me: I could not have borne to gaze again, at first, upon the busy world; but the yellow sand, and the grey rock, were objects suited to the mind, just loosed from chains like mine, and from the looking on sights, and listening to sounds, which had well nigh broke it down
