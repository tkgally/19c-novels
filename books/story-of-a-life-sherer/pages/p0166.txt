It was evident that Agatha could never give me one such answering throb of her heart, as would satisfy the warm demand of mine. At first, however, even this thought lightened not my heavy chains; forged by strong passion, and by bitter sorrow, they bound me, as it were, in the dungeon of despair. I shut myself up in hired apartments at Cintra, or wandered forth among the deep shades of that romantic and endeared solitude; one day frantic—the next, disconsolate; every day anxious and unhappy.
