“The remembered details of this year would make a volume, tiresome enough, though not to me.

“The scene was now to change. I was taken to a public school, a little candidate for the dignity of a black gown and a white surplice, and the band, the proud band!

“My heart rose to my very throat, as I wound my way up the stone stair-case to the election chamber, and found myself standing before six black-robed, grave men; and was interrogated with grave hems, by a kind and reverend old warden, and asked if I could sing; and answered with a stammering effort at harmony, ‘All people that on earth do dwell, sing,’ and was smiled upon, and bid go down again.
