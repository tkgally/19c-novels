I could not be mistaken: it was the Lisbon robber—the violator—he that murdered the fair girl.

The prayers were no sooner over than we captives were summoned to the poop. The Rais was a short, thin, pale, cruel looking man. The deck was crowded; but there were no sounds, save alternately his voice, and that of his interpreter, and the trembling replies of a poor Neapolitan taken with me, who spoke the Lingua França, and then, at times, a long pause, and the gurgle of water as the snake-like pipe was slowly updrawn.
