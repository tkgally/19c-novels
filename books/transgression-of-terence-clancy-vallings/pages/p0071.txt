“Are you the secretary, Mr. Doidge? Well, I got this at your office this afternoon.” He spoke with a disarming smile, which had a curious melting effect upon Doidge.

“All right, sir; no offence. But as I dismissed both keepers for idleness t’other day, I’m obliged to do the watching myself just now. Somehow I like this young fellow,” he added to himself; “he’s quite different from that d—d stuck-up swell of a Secretan!”

One sharp, sudden, half-scared, glance Clancy shot at the secretary as he turned away; but no one noticed it, unless it were Mary Pethick, who had looked up from her fern-gathering upon hearing Ezekiel’s voice.
