“Write in a day or two?” exclaimed his wife, indignantly; “wire this instant, you mean! Pray ring the bell, Julius, and don’t be so abominably cool.”

“You’re welcome to get into a fume, Kate, without which no woman’s happiness is complete; but I warn you that you’ll be disappointed. Simon writes calmly, and talks of leaving the country again in a few weeks; and I’ve little doubt that such is his real intention. It’s evident to me that he’s wrapped up in this adopted daughter of his, and thinks of nobody else; that he has no idea of settling among us again and renewing old friendships. I notice an undertone of bitterness in his letter, and——”
