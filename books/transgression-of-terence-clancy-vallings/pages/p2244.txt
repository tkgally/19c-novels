Aunt Mary could never get through a week without having dear Kate over to enliven her, and was always getting up some little dance or other entertainment for dear Kate’s benefit; Captain Saunderson was under strict orders to press his intimacy with Julius; to the squire was assigned the task of keeping Julius thoroughly well bored of home. In a word, between three or four of them, the stubborn bachelor was successfully immeshed.
