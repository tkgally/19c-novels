The breeze of fortune which landed Terence upon this pinnacle of prosperity sent a thrill of excitement, rather than surprise, through the neighbourhood. Mrs. French-Chichester had long since given up her own hopes in favour of Nell and Terence; and other shrewd eyes had seen this climax looming in the distance for some time. Even dull ones, judging by the after-asseverations of their owners, must have had occasional glimpses of it. At any rate, there was little astonishment openly expressed, though a perfect buzz of romantic interest and speculation arose from Chillington and went eddying through half the county.
