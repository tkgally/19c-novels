[[cont]]Mr. Simon, if you were going to have an operation, which might cure, but would far more likely kill, would you put it off, or make the plunge and get it over?”

There was a yearning in the woman’s voice which touched Simon, though he could make no guess at its meaning.

“My dear Joyce, you mystify me. What can I do for you?”

“You can make my few remaining weeks a paradise, and let me die in peace, blessing you,” she answered in a strained heart-whisper.

“Tell me how.”

“No—no; let me dream a little longer. I’ve given over the habit so long that ’tis like a childish treat come back. I won’t tell you—I won’t. If I do tell you, will you promise not to say ‘No’ to-night, so that my hope may dwindle gradually, instead o’ bein’ killed by a single word?”
