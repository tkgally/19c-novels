In fact, I’m a wretched bungler altogether, Simon, and this visit of mine seems likely to be a failure. I came here to try and cheer you, and how can I manage that when you do nothing but entangle me among planets and sun-systems, and things that I know nothing about?”

“I say again that, just as carbon——”

“I don’t want to hear any more about carbon, and don’t believe you understand anything about it. To begin with, you said it was an important element in animal as well as vegetable life. Now, whoever heard such nonsense as that? Do you mean to assert that I’m made of the same stuff as a cabbage?”
