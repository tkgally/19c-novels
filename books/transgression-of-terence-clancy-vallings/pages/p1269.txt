“There’d be not a tittle of evidence agen’ him but your bare word: not one in ten would believe you. Mayhap his wife might, knowing you so well, and gettin’ to know him a bit by now, I should think; and like enough you’d spoil her life—her’s for whose sake you’ve bore this. For I know that ’tis so, though you gave me all your motives but the true un. And, lookee, sir, a vain attempt to set up your innocence would break you worse than you’re broke now.
