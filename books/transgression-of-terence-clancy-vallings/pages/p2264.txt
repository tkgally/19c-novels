“My course will be no course at all!” cried the young widow, flashing another indignant glance after Simon’s retreating figure; “for I will have nothing to do with a man who outrages me so. Kate, I’ll tell you frankly what I had hoped!”

“No fear about the frankness, once you’ve let your tongue loose!” muttered Kate, parenthetically.

“I had hoped that when Simon returned it would be as an old friend; and—and I did hope he would give me some chance of showing my gratitude—and perhaps restoring at last what rightly belongs to him.”

“Quite so—I see. That is pretty much the line I thought you would take, my dear. You want to marry him out of gratitude! So far, so good. Any love to be thrown in, may I ask?”

“I’ve done with all that long ago.”
