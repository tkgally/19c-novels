The whole story was now perfectly well known, for Ezekiel had sent his witness on to Bickington, in accordance with his promise, after the identification of Clancy at the bridge. Immediately thereupon he had himself made straight for Monks Damerel, doubtless possessed by an insane fear lest his revenge should be snatched from him by Captain Rush; and, but for a prolonged fainting-fit at the mill, he must have reached his destination almost as soon as Terence and his two guests.

Upon learning the news of Terence’s identification, Captain Rush had himself galloped over to the hall, hoping to anticipate poor crazy Doidge and save Terence from the disgrace of a public exposure; but he had only arrived to find that all was over.
