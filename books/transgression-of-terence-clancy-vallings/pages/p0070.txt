Meanwhile Mary Pethick had stepped aside to pluck some ferns, and was stooping over them, red and pale by turns, the fronds shaking in the hand that held them.

Ezekiel, too much out of temper to join her, was watching the others. He presently called out rudely—

“Hi! you, sir; have you been fishing without a ticket?”

“Mr. Clancy is my friend,” said Secretan, tranquilly, without troubling to look round.

“But this is Association water, though it runs through your land, and the Association, represented by myself, knows nothing of your private friends, Mr. Secretan.”

Doidge advanced a few steps, quite cheered at the prospect of a controversy with his rival; but Terence hastily interposed, pulling a fishing ticket from his fly-book.
