I’d rather be like such a one than be a crowned king, though maybe the possession of an absolutely clear conscience would be a happiness insupportable by a poor devil like me! Man’s nothing but a doomed creature, struggling over ground honeycombed with pitfalls, into one or other of which he <i>must</i> fall sooner or later; then the devil throws a net over him, and he never fronts the clear sky more. Look, Nell—look where I’m pointing! Do you see how the sunlight falls on that field by the edge of the wood yonder? and do you see that stooping figure on the grass?
