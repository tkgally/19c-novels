Thus Mary never acquired a new sweetheart without straightway parading him before the eyes of those critical aunts; and doubtless one of her minor objections to Ezekiel was the satisfaction her engagement to him gave the Miss Pethicks. Not that they made her pleasant speeches upon the subject, but rather were careful to declare, with the native fatuity of spiteful people, that the wealthy and respected Mr. Doidge was a deal too good for their feather-headed niece.
