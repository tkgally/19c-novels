Indeed he exhibited so few intellectual symptoms that Mrs. French-Chichester—who was nevertheless well-pleased to have him as an ornament to her drawing-room as often as might be—used to say that the taking of his pass degree at Oxford had exhausted his brain for ever.

“The soil of his mind!” she would exclaim, when people praised his farming; “need never trouble him much; it brought forth just once—but there’ll be no rotation of crops <i>there</i> anyway.”
