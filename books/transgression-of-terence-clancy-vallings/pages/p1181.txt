“Is it true, Doctor Syme? Were you there yourself?”

“Yes, I was there. What did Sir Hamo say to the news?”

“He’s cruel bad, sir. I had to send off for Dr. Clancy an hour ago, and he’s with Sir Hamo still.”

“Here’s a scene in high life for the local papers,” thought Syme. “Between the public and the parent our young squire is likely to get it hot, it seems?”

“If ’tis true,” muttered Mrs. Henley between her shut lips, allowing her intense excitement to gleam in her eyes for a moment, “Sir Hamo’ll cut him off with a shillin’, sure as I stand here; ay, and will understand why Miss Nell had to throw him over. Ah! I always thought there was some bad story behind that.”
