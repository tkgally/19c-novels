The slight rod was bent into a hoop. The suspense had become painful, when suddenly the fish moved, sailed off quietly at first, then bolted furiously down stream, Terence splashing through the shallows in headlong chase.

The situation was now a shade too piquant for pure pleasure, for the river narrowed and deepened, and a low wall, which here followed the left bank, forced the angler to follow in the water, or not at all.
