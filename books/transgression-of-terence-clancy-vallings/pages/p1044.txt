“I s’ant!” said the child decisively.

The impassive Baker showed no concern, however, but drove on with a lift of his finger and a “Mornin’, missis.”

Simon, hearing his name mentioned, stared at the mother and child in a dazed sort of way, but could make nothing of them.

“Why, Mr. Simon, don’t you know me, sir?”

“Is it you, Joyce? Where have you sprung from? You must forgive me, but—I was lost in thought,” Simon added, after a pause. The words which had first sprung to his lips were, “you’re such a wreck of your former self, poor girl, that no wonder I didn’t know you.”

“You find me looking rather corpse-like, eh, sir?”

“No, no; a little pale.”

Joyce laughed, and in doing so took Simon back a dozen years in half a second.
