“Now you’ve found yourself out to be mortal,” said Kate, moodily, “and so liable to fever. It is not an uncommon discovery.”

“Oh, I never knew, I never dreamt,” cried Nell, blushing crimson and hiding her face. “I never dreamt until to-day that I was false; yet when they were struggling in the water, I don’t believe I gave Simon a thought. I have neither honour, nor loyalty, nor truth. Do you hear?”

“I am now certain of what I have often suspected”—Kate spoke with knitted brows and a kind of gloomy satisfaction—“namely, that Terence Clancy is no true man.
