Sir Hamo might be said to cease to live, rather than to die, so placid and serene was his final departure. And during those last few peaceful days of his life there did arise in the old man’s breast a certain yearning after his son Simon, who had disgraced him, had gone away out of the country, no one knew whither, had caused him bitter disappointment and pain; but who in old days, despite his tiresome whims and fancies, had certainly loved his father.
