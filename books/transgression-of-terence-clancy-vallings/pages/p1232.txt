# Chapter XI.

THE face of which Kate had caught a glimpse through the glazed door belonged to no casual passer-by, but to a man who had been prowling about the farm and neighbouring woods night after night ever since Secretan’s arrival—one who was, in truth, far more an object of pity than the lonely philosopher whose steps he was dogging.
