“As Nell is really better, I must send a line over to my father; and I shall ask him to pass on the good news to Simon, who will doubtless have heard of, and been anxious about, her illness.”

So said Kate, who was well aware that Simon’s name was tabooed at the White House, but who always made a point of ignoring the whims and weaknesses of other people. She was now surprised, however, by Terence’s cordial acceptance of her proposition.

“Certainly, certainly; Simon should be told of our patient’s improvement. What a fine old fellow he is, old Simon! I wish, Kate, I do wish,” he added with feeling, “that my happiness had been won at some enemy’s expense, instead of good old Simon’s.”
