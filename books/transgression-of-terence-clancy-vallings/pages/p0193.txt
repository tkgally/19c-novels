“Simon, I do wish you would <i>observe</i> more and <i>think</i> less!”

Now, Simon had a combative side as well as Kate; smart skirmishing was not unknown between them, and, the one being fond of disparaging Clancy, while the other was easily heated in a friend’s cause, they now both prepared for the fray.

“I don’t much believe in this popular hero of yours.”

“Substitute ‘appreciate’ for ‘believe,’ Kate. You’re too hard and cynical in your estimate of people, if you’ll allow me to say so.”

“You’re too blindly good-natured and idealistic in <i>your</i> estimation of them, if you’ll allow me to say so.”

“I’m perfectly aware that you don’t perceive Terence’s good qualities.”
