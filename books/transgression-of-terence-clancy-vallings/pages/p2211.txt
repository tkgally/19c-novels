Terence was still lying in the study, on the sofa, where they had first laid him. He had shot himself in the breast, and was mortally wounded. He knew that his end was but a question of hours. As he had flatly declined to be moved upstairs, Jack Syme and the surgeon who had been summoned from Lymport by telegraph had done their best for him, and perforce acquiesced in his wishes.

Towards the two doctors and the servants who had assisted them Terence had shown his old gentleness of manner, praising their skill and thanking them for their care and pains; towards Nell alone he betrayed a bitterness of spirit such as shocked all those who witnessed it.
