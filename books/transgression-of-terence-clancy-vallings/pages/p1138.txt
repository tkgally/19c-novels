To suit this important occasion the hall had been arranged in a special fashion. As the gathering was to be of a friendly, rather than formal character, and as it was thought advisable to allow a certain amount of free discussion among the audience, the chairman was placed, together with his green baize table and bell, about the centre of one side of the long room; while the platform was left unoccupied—an arrangement which put every man on the same level, and tended to promote good-fellowship.
