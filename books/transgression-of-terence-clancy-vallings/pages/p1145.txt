Mr. Tredethlyn rose again.

“All you who agree to accept Mr. Secretan’s offer, hold up your hands.”

The room forthwith sprouted with uplifted arms and hands, but there was no attempt at a cheer—not a voice was raised in friendliness or gratitude.

Lord Bridistow, still on his feet, looked uncomfortable and hesitated. He had expected, as a matter of course, some enthusiasm at this point, something to give promise of a cordial vote of thanks, such as he was now about to move.
