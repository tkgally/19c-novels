“She has your—your——” he began, with a man’s slowness in getting at the points of a child.

“My impudence, Mr. Simon? Ay, and happen she’ll keep it—until she marries. Ah, if I had but a good watchdog for the little one when I’m gone!”

“What has brought the pair of you here from Lymport, Joyce?”

“Why, just this, sir. I’ve had Mary Pethick stayin’ with me for some time, and I wanted to bring her back safe to her father—for I’m not easy in my mind about that young thing, Mr. Simon.”

“What ails her? I haven’t seen her about here for months.”

Joyce looked up with a queer twist in her face, and seemed to be weighing something.
