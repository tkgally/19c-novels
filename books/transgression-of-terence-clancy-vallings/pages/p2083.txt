And one thing more must be touched upon—for now we are upon the subject of that somewhat shattered thing, poor Terence’s conscience, it were well to make a clean breast—there is yet another secret weighing upon the man, and this one pricks the sharper for being of a professional nature.

For, if somewhat false and fickle in other relations, Terence has been hitherto without blemish professionally—or had been up to the time of Doidge’s denunciation of Simon.
