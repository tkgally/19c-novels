It is certain that he resented nothing more than the imputation of laziness, that the occasional passages of temper which took place between him and his elder daughter, Kate, resulted from a suspected disposition on her part to regard her father as a thought too idle, and a shade too much given to pathetic analysis of his dyspeptic symptoms. In truth Mr. Tredethlyn was no idler.
