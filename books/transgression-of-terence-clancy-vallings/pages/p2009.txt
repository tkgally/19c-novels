# Chapter I.

THE trout in the Hollacomb canal had for many years been considered the most exclusive and aristocratic caste of the fish world in this neighbourhood. Cut off from the vulgar struggles and competition of the main stream, they had for generations enjoyed that combination of repose and good living which makes for high-bred serenity.
