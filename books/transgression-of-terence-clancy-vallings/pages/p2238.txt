Briefly, then, it must be explained that Captain Saunderson had by this time so far recovered his health as to feel equal to supporting the rigours of his native climate; and, further, was possessed by a craving, surprising in so delicate a man, to resume the robust sports and pastimes proper to an Englishman. In fact, he was heartily sick of continental lounging, and had joyfully made up his mind to settle at home.
