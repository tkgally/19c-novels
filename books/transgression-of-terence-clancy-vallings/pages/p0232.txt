# Chapter XI.

SIMON SECRETAN and his host were unusually silent over their dinner that night. Each exerted himself spasmodically to contribute a little hilarity to the evening, but the failure of either was about equally conspicuous. Simon was vaguely depressed at Nell’s cold reception of his entreaty, though as yet not seriously anxious about it. After the first disappointment, the habit of idealizing the woman he loved, of regarding her as above and beyond all criticism, of finding in himself the fault, if at any time she fell short of his expectations, had re-asserted itself.
