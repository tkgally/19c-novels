In a few minutes Mr. Tredethlyn rejoined them, and on further pressure from him, Terence cast a glance of such penetrating appeal at Nell that she looked a half-assent in reply—and the thing was done.

Nell had now allowed temptation to close with her, and was in for a bitter wrestle.

For this last move of Mrs. French-Chichester’s was a passing shrewd one. She had felt baffled lately, and angry with Terence for his continued neglect of his opportunities; having conceived him to be a man of spirit, she now found him a tame, nerveless fellow.
