“What mawkish nonsense!” cried Terence impatiently. “Was ever a man so worried and bothered just for trying to do his best and get on in the world? I let you take your own course, and mope at home when you ought to be furthering my chances in society. I never harass you or put the least pressure on you—and yet you’re never contented for an hour.”

“Because I don’t feel <i>honest</i>, Terence; and the more I think of our position the less satisfied I feel about it. I can’t see that we have any right to be standing in Simon’s shoes——”
