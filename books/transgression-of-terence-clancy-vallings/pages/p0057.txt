“I hate him!” she muttered. “I’ve been a hypocrite to pretend the contrary; and he’s too stupid to see through me! What right has a man to be so blind, so utterly infatuated? He ought to see my torture, he ought—— O God, forgive me! What a mean, cold, selfish creature I am!”

Reaction had come again by the time Simon was back with her; the rebound of a generous nature was strong in her as she placed her arm in his, crying—

“Dear old Simon, you care too much for me!”
