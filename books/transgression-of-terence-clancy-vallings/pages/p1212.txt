In that dismal retreat of his Simon had proposed to substitute brain-stuff for heart-stuff, to take to heart mathematical problems in place of human ones. There seemed no need for insane hatred of his fellows, such as befell Athenian Timon: he proposed rather to ignore their existence altogether. He had spent some years of his life in the endeavour to benefit the community of Chillington, and the place had opened its mouth to swallow down his disgrace with a greedy readiness. He had occupied a twelvemonth in trying to win the love of his promised bride, and had succeeded in completely estranging her.
