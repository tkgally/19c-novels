[[cont]]Simon Secretan—easily recognizable by his tall figure and the big iron-grey he rode—and three ladies: to wit, Mrs. French-Chichester, Miss Tredethlyn, and, yes—Miss Nell.

Simon trotted on ahead of his convoy, rode in through the front gate and round the little gravel sweep, and was greeted by Clancy from the steps.

“Terence, I’ve news for you—I hear that the peal are running up the Culmer in shoals. You must get out your fourteen-footer and mount instanter. I’ve been wanting something of this sort, just to celebrate the commencement of your reign at the White House. You can’t begin work to-day; every one knows you’re not half settled down yet. As there’s sport in question, I ought to have left the ladies at home, but they <i>would</i> come, and have promised faithfully not to interfere with us—in fact, to let us alone and look after the lunch.
