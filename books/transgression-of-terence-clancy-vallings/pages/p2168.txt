“Do you see the curl of foam on this breaking wave, Nell?” he now said after a pause. “It is done with one stroke of the palette-knife, but a life-time of labour and study have gone to make that one stroke possible. Now the real art of life is to clutch the success without the labour. I’m going to do just such a perfect stroke to-morrow, and the only toil that has gone to produce it has been that of winning the sweetest wife in England. Ah, ’tis her presence that has kept all but landscapes and sea-scapes from these walls, for no painted head’s worth looking at beside the ‘ripe and real’ beauty that I have always before me!”
