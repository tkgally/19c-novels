“I must see the old fellow alone to-night,” he concluded, “though I can never tell him half that I feel. Speak to him first, I beseech you. Tell him that I have it in me to cry like a woman—that I shall do so if I attempt to say much.”

“Yes, I’ll tell him,” said Nell, with a glad laugh.

“Will ye shake hands with me now?” he cried, in his eager boyish way; “will ye shake hands and conclude the matter so? There, ’tis a bargain.”

Terence laughed too, though his eyes were moist.

“Ah, how like Simon is the whole transaction!”

Her praise gave Terence a pang of jealousy, but he stifled it manfully.
