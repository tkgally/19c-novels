[[cont]]Terence Clancy never wanted for eloquence. I hate a shifty, half-bad man like that worse than a genuine scoundrel!”

“Your sex is somewhat apt to appreciate a genuinely good man less than either, my dear.”

“What did you say to the pair, father?”

“I hardly know, they overwhelmed me so with amazement; but doubtless I said some very bitter things, and certainly I forbade Nell to see or speak to Clancy again.”

“I wish you could remember what you said, and I only hope you stung him to the quick. I would ask no greater happiness at this moment than five minutes of plain speech with Terence Clancy. But that would never do; anything of that sort might alienate Nell for life, for—mark me, father—She’ll marry the man.”

“My dear Kate, surely we can prevent that! I will flatly forbid such a sacrifice. The man is utterly unworthy of Nell.”
