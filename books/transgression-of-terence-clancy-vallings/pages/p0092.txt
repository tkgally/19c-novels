[[cont]]said the old gentleman, with a sigh and a courteous bend of his silver head. “We are expecting Nell and some others to dinner, and so——”

“Yes, yes, I know; and the sky will fall unless everything’s perfect, won’t it?”

“And so I thought you would be so very good as to come over——”

“Of course. I’m only too glad to leave the dismal shades of Hollacomb for a few hours. Why don’t you send Simon over to see me oftener?”

“Oh, Simon’s no good!” retorted the old man querulously. “He’s always immersed in his star-gazing or his absurd philanthropic schemes. He is away now on some mysterious errand in Chillington, getting astronomical drawings copied, or haply designs for a new infant school, or home for decayed gentlewomen.
