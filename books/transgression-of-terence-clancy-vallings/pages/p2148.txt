Whereupon the audience awoke suddenly, as though under a sprinkle of fresh water, and a cheery rapping of sticks began. The eulogy took a little time to get bodied forth, and contained long words enough to make a plain man dizzy; but at length they flailed it to death with gladsome “Hear hears!” and so won the breathing space long craved.

They were in a mood now for something bright and jestful—and they got it.

Terence was in splendid trim; and so bubbled with wit and drollery, that the room was soon full of volleying laughter, which renewed itself almost with his every sentence.
