This was a bad stroke. Nothing could have been better calculated to arouse his opposition than this simple question; for even a good-natured parson is not to be managed by scoffing at his bodily trials. The genial ruddy face became sullen; its owner relapsed into silence.

“I do not believe he is more skilful than the other doctors; and—and you would really do me a great favour by going back to doctor Rose?”

“He is leaving off practice—as you know perfectly well. I suppose you would like me to do without medical attendance altogether?”

“Why not try Mr. Syme? People are beginning to think better of him, I hear.”

No answer. Her father had assumed the dignified silence of injured innocence. Kate’s indifference to his state of health was a grievance of long standing.
