He looked at his watch; it was barely half-past ten. She would as likely as not be walking home along the canal at this hour, as she was wont to leave the town about ten o’clock. At any rate, it was worth a climb up to the canal on the chance of getting a glimpse of her; so without more ado he struck into a path which led upwards from the river.
