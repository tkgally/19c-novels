Mr. Tredethlyn’s looks answered for him; his ruddy face had actually paled at Kate’s announcement. To lose Nell? He had not half made up his mind to such a sacrifice, for another year at least. Nell was a necessity of his existence, the very light of his eyes. His mingled horror and bewilderment caused the visitor to bubble over with laughter, in which Kate joined more from excitement than mirth.
