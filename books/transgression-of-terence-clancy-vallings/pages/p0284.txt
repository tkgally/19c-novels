Julius Rush, with the usual success of a taciturn man when it pleases him to exert himself, easily drew out the beardless Plungers, learning incidentally that one had been at Haileybury, the other at Eton, so that neither had ever seen Clancy before this evening. By the direction of their glances Kate guessed that the Irishman was the subject of their talk, and was tortured with anxious curiosity. She looked almost imploringly at Captain Rush, caught his eye, and bowed; but no use, he returned her bow stiffly, without the faintest symptom of any intention to do more.
