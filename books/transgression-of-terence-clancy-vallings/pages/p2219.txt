She trembled with fear lest this apparent return to her better self should prove but a passing mood; but time went by and it still held good.

When Terence awoke, his expression was changed; he, too, seemed different, as though her spirit had been working upon his, or as though both had passed under some soothing mysterious influence, been softened by some “sweet oblivious antidote.”

“It was terrible to see you in such pain, Terence. I’m thankful to know that it has passed away again.”

Terence’s hand moved a few inches and lay beside hers, touching, though not holding it.
