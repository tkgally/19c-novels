Meanwhile, Chillington wasted much curious speculation upon the question of Sir Simon’s return, until, no sign or hint of such a probability being published, even that topic lost interest, leaving the weather, the crops, and the state of trade once more to reign supreme.

When the spring came round again, however, a slight breeze of interest sprang up from an unexpected quarter—the direction of Bickington Park.
