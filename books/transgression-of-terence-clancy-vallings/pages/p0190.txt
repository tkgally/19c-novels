Nell had been out of sorts and depressed lately, but to-day a change had set in and her spirits were radiant; while, as for Mrs. French-Chichester, she was never one to refuse the challenge of bright weather and genial company. Moreover, she had good reason to be satisfied with things in general this morning, for was not Simon, with the delightful fatuity which always distinguished him in matters which concerned his own interests, playing into her hand, thrusting Nell into the very charmed circle that had been prepared by herself?
