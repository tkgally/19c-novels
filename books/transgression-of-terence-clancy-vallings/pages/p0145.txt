“He <i>means</i> well,” mused Syme. “But Lord help Secretan all the same!”

As Terence sauntered by Chilling water, stopping to make a cast here and there in leisurely fashion, he was flushed with a glow of conscious virtue. He felt that there was genuine nobility in his renunciation of the evening’s pleasure at Moor Gates. Pleasure! That was a mild term for the satisfying happiness of being with—with people who understood and appreciated him. What was <i>she</i> doing at this moment? Practising the second of the new duet they were to have sung to night? Would she be a little disappointed when Simon drove up without his friend?
