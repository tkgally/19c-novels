They say this will be the crack club o’ the county, and perhaps give a big lift to county cricket. Yes, the whole district will be gathered in our town hall to-morrow night, mister,—and I’ll be among them, if I have to be carried there on a shutter!”

This speech sounded a fresh clang of alarm for Terence. Was it, then, just as he had feared? Was this wretched man actually brewing some scheme of vengeance, hugging it close, so as to hit the harder when he did speak? Clancy was absolutely in the dark as to what clue, or fancied clue, the crazy fellow possessed; he might turn and rend any one. It was only clear that he was upon a wrong scent at present.
