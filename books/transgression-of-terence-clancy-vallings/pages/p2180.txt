Ah, Nell, Nell, if I could only start fair again—start fair and straight from the beginning, with you to help me—I might win something better than the sham happiness that’s me lot now!”

He ended with a sob and a broken exclamation, and Nell could only weep with him and pray for him and try in vain to comfort him.
