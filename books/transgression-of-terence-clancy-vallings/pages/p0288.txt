She accepted his meaning frankly, then? He might have foretold as much; no mock modesty or pretence of blindness would be possible with Nell. Her tone said plainly, “Of course we are both miserable, and must be, for duty demands it.”

Terence felt this to be an ominous beginning, and had a chill perception that her very willingness to be alone with him, the suppressed excitement of her manner all the evening, and other symptoms upon which he had been building a handsome edifice of hope, should really have been read as danger signals. Yet this first note of opposition only intensified his stubborn resolve to conquer. If she was, as he now perceived, in the very wrath and ecstasy of renunciation, why he was possessed as vehemently by quite another passion.
