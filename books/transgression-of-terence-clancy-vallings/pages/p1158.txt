“Gentlemen,” he cried in a strong firm voice edged with sarcasm, “your intended captain appears to have no answer to make to this accusation. Perhaps he is too proud to speak—too superior to the common herd of us to care about vindicating himself. If so, I believe I shall only give voice to the general sense of this great assembly if I ask him to step down to our level for a moment and speak his mind. He is accused not only of wronging this girl, but of egging her on to make away with herself in order to screen him, or at any rate of taking no steps to prevent such a climax. I shouldn’t myself care to keep silence under this double indictment; and I think even a gentleman in his high position might condescend to a word or two of explanation.
