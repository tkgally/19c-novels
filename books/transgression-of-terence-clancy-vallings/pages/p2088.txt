Above the roofs towered a tall brick chimney, for Doidge used steam as well as water-power; many wheels were plashing, many stones grinding; whitened men were working busily; clerks coming to and from the office; carts piled with sacks passing in and out of the spacious yard.

As Clancy entered this yard the busy work-a-day aspect of the place impressed him in a wholesome fashion, for in so bustling and cheerful scene there was nothing for morbid fear to fasten upon. Strolling into the office for a moment or two, he further steadied his nerves by a little gossip with Doidge’s foreman; and thereafter marched through the flower-garden to the miller’s front door with a better head of courage than he had been able to muster since leaving the town bridge.
