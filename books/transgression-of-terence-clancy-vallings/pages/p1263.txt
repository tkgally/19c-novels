Presently she halted before the glass door of the book-case, pointing and saying, “Pitty bookee!”

Not having the key about him, Simon offered her other books, only to be rebuffed once more.

The mother regarded the pair with eyes yearning and hopeful, and a smile of deep peace. The child, standing and pointing imperiously, looked very quaint and pretty. Her small face was upturned to the big gentleman, her bright eyes looked eagerly from under the tumbled fair curls, the short, crisp little features were alight with piquant impatience.

The new guardian was of course compelled to find the key, and next to take down one by one a whole row of Mr. Pethick’s science primers. When the last one was down, she suddenly lost all her interest in books, and became athirst for fresh diversion.
