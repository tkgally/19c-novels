Nell greeted him cordially as usual, drawing a chair for the old squire beside her own; and he settled down with a feeling that he was joining a family party.

Little Rose’s prattle quickly disclosed the fact that this game on the bowling-green was, in fine weather, the ordinary sequel to her morning’s lessons; that, her books once laid aside, she and Simon were in the habit of crossing the brook, which separated the two properties on this side, and making their way hither by a steep path through the woods.
