“I know that I could,” she answered proudly. “If there be any money difficulty, do not hesitate to tell me all. We can give up our house, go into a mere cottage, and work harder. I don’t fear poverty; I only fear lest this wretched secret may eat away our love if you persist in withholding from me your confidence. Tell me all, I entreat you.”

“Could you even leave the country with me, and begin life anew in a foreign land?”

“I could do that and more.” Nell’s answer came without hesitation; but her face was getting pale and drawn, she had begun to dread the coming revelation exceedingly.
