Our Lady Bountiful, as it happened, always took especial pleasure in these quiet evenings at Monks Damerel; for Nell’s ill-concealed jealousy of her influence over Terence was an unfailing source of enjoyment on the one hand; and on the other, the training and development of so apt a pupil as Nell’s husband could not fail to be a relishing task.

On this occasion she took more than usual pains to impress upon Terence what a vista of fine possibilities was opening before him, and at the same time gave him precise instructions as to his attitude towards Lord Bridistow on the morrow. Nor was Nell left out in the cold in this matter of advice and encouragement.
