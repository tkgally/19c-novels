But, though he continued to speak of his return to the other side of the world as a settled plan, they steadily persisted in their endeavours to undermine it, each of the party pegging away at him in his or her particular fashion.

Julius, who said little in public, would attack him privately, under the friendly veil of tobacco.

“You and I are too old to make new friends, old fellow,” he would urge, “so we must even settle down within hail of one another. And your old craze, if a trifle subdued, is still in working order, as I know very well.
