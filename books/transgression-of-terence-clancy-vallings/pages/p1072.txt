About midday half Chillington was gathered near the bridge, watching the flood and enjoying the unusual excitement.

“D’ye reckon,” asked one of another, “that the weir’ll hold out long agen thiccy?”

“Like enough her won’t.”

“If her gives, ’twill relieve this mort o’ watter up over, and flood Mr. Secretan’s cottages down along worse nor they be a’ready.”

“So ’twould. And I tell ’ee what—’twould run the canal dry, like enough.”

“How so, mister?” This question was put by half a dozen people at once.
