“But I have perceived nothing of the sort.”

“No—precisely.”

“Surely I must have——”

“I suppose you have been otherwise engaged, father.”

Kate’s voice was still a little dry.

“Was Terence aware of this?”

“I don’t know, and don’t care. I won’t hear a word in the man’s favour. I hate him—I <i>hate</i> him, I tell you!”

“He has done us a serious injury, and I find it hard enough to find any excuse for him, Kate. But, as I said, I blame myself also. There has been selfish blindness on my part. And justice is justice. Terence has certainly not spared himself.
