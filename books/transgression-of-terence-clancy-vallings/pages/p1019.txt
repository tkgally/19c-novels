The volume on the sleeper’s knee was “In Memoriam,” the only modern poem that had much appeal for Sir Hamo. He had been steeping himself in the divine despair of the verse, and now perchance was dreaming of the wife with whom had departed his desire of life.

Simon’s yearning for reconciliation grew stronger; and here surely were conditions ready fitted to that end, for his own heart was humbled, and the tears of the poet must have been falling like dew upon his father’s. He stole softly into the house, making straight for the library door.
