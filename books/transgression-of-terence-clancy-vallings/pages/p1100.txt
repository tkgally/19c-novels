Nor had Terence a single free hour wherein to sit down and fairly confront the tangled situation. Patients were pouring in upon the new doctor until he hardly knew which way to turn. He was overworked, distracted, harassed almost beyond human endurance. The strain was telling heavily upon his health already. He wondered how much longer he would be able to stand up under the pressure of accumulating trouble.
