Terence had made many little preparations for her comfort, bustling about upon one small errand after another with the ardour of a schoolboy and the thoughtfulness of a woman; but, being called away into the town just as she was about to descend, he missed the pleasure of welcoming her back to the routine of happy everyday life.

About midday, however, he cantered up the hill again, and rushed in exultingly to greet his young wife.

But the brightness fell from him before he had taken two steps into the room. He found Nell lying back in an armchair, looking tearful and woebegone.
