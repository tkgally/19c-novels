“You insult me—you insult me!” cried Nell, with quivering lips.

It was the first time Nell had ever “flown out” at him, as the homely phrase has it; and the man’s natural kindliness was at once awakened.

“There, there, little girl. I know my tone was abominable, and my words nearly as bad. Forgive me, Nellie!”
