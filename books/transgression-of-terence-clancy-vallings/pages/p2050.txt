“But—but—my <i>dear</i> boy——”

“I shall call for you again precisely at four o’clock, when we shall proceed to the assault of Moor Gates.”

Julius drew his trembling father into the hall, helped him into a light top-coat, placed a stick in his hand, led him out on to the gravel sweep, and fairly started him for the west lodge gate, proposing to pick him up in a few minutes’ time.

Immediately afterwards Mr. Children received an order which turned his arrogant heart to water.

“Send my cart round at once; I’m going to drive your master into the town. On my return in thirty minutes you will await me in the billiard-room, with all the other servants, gardeners, grooms, helpers—do you hear?”
