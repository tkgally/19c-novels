The subaltern was commenting scorchingly upon the play, the people, the rustic toilettes, the vicar’s old coat and baggy trousers, and such things. Rush hardly looked at him at first, but when he had quite done, said in a calm reflective way, as though remarking upon the points of a horse, “What a d—d little snob you are, Pardington!”

Mrs. French-Chichester had to go further behind the bushes to enjoy it. Soon afterwards Pardington joined the ladies, blushing beautifully.

“Well, have you had your chat with the distinguished captain?” asked the malicious widow. “Did he put you up to any ‘tips,’ as you call them?”

He only muttered something inaudible.
