Mr. Syme, being little in the way of society, had never yet actually spoken to Mrs. Clancy. Terence noted with pride how almost awestruck with admiration the man was when first presented to his bride. Her sweet and winning manner, however, seemed to act upon Jack like a charm, and the more so since he had looked for the cold superiority with which the town and neighbourhood were wont to treat him. He sat down—almost at ease, comparatively speaking—took his cup of tea from Nell’s small hand, and did his honest best to make himself agreeable.
