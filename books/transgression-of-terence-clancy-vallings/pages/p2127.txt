# Chapter V.

WHEN the summer and early autumn had passed; when cricket was over and the crops had been gathered in; when the weary British subaltern, exhausted by cricket, tennis, C. O.’s parades, whist, billiards, and other such labours incident to the military career, was beginning to think of rest and peace in the bosom of his family; in a word, towards the end of October, the return of Captain Julius Rush, of the 20th Dragoon Guards, was being eagerly looked for at Bickington Park.
