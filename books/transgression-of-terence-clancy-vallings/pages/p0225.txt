Nell was soon in high spirits again, running over with talk and laughter. Rush, the sardonic, found himself obliged to laugh with the others, and the sliding Culmer joined its music to the merriment of the party. Before they broke up their camp the broad sun had gone behind the western moors, and the pool at their feet was giving back the delicate rose and opal of a placid evening sky.
