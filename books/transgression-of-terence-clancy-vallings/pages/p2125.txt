Save for the fear of displeasing her, it is probable that his present good resolution would have gone the way of most of his others, passing gently through procrastination to oblivion. Not that Nell urged him by a single word to go straight to Jack Syme, but he knew she expected this, and he left her the moment they entered the house.

Finding his assistant in the surgery, he opened the question at once, explained that he had lately seen a good deal of Ezekiel, and from one thing and another had been led to suspect the existence of an aortic aneurism.

Jack listened, shrugging his shoulders. He totally disbelieved in any ailment of Ezekiel’s other than hysteria.
