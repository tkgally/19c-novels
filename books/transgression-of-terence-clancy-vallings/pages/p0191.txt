He rightly conceived that she would not be taking all this pains for the mere gratification of an old grudge against Simon, but what further motive could she have? This problem was beyond him at present, but he meant to work out a solution ere long.

Meanwhile she poured forth such a stream of anecdote and banter that her auditors only recovered from one fit of laughter in time to fall headlong into another. Most of her tales were about Simon and his fads; for in the art of administering a full dose of disparagement, disguised in a froth of chaff and raillery, she had few equals, and this opportunity was not to be thrown away.
