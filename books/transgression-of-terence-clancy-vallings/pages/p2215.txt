Afterwards he had pushed on to Moor Gates, and rapidly driven Kate back to Monks Damerel, though, as Nell declined to see even her sister, this well-considered step had been of no avail.

But Nell and Terence gave no thought to what might be going on in the outer world; their own despair was like a dark eddy, round which they were being whirled with deadened hearts, unable to move hand or foot—face to face, yet with a gulf between that neither could cross.

When Terence at length broke the silence his strange bitterness seemed to be pricking him like a goad.

“How long is it since Syme left me? How long have I been asleep or unconscious?”

“Many hours! It is now midnight, or close upon it.”
