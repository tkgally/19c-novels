[[cont]]Tredethlyn had concluded his not uncongenial task by stating flatly that he had no intention of returning home until Kate should be ready to receive him in a fitting spirit, and refrain from harping upon topics calculated to produce discord in a comfortable home.

It happened that this letter of parental admonition was now to fulfil a purpose quite outside its writer’s intentions.

For Kate was in the peculiar wrought-up condition in which a person’s real emotional self is apt to burst forth in unexpected fashion. Her wrath against her father had been boiling over for these four days, but anger was now streaked with the self-reproach of a woman generous at the core, and so moved to over-estimate the enormity of her own pettiness. She had been taking herself severely to task for past misconduct to the tailor, and now here was the tailor in person, shaming her with fresh kindness;
