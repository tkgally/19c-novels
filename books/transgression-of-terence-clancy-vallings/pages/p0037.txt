Then Kate gave both ponies a vicious lash, thus ensuring some hard work for her hands and wrists, set her teeth, and prepared for a plunge.

“Nell, I’ve got something on my mind.”

“What is it?”

“A sister—a most tiresome, Quixotic, infatuated, extravagantly conscientious little creature called Nell.”

“What is the matter with Nell?”

Both girls were somewhat flushed; both spoke and looked straight to the front, their glances declining to meet.

“I’ll tell you what is the matter with you, Nell—a swelled conscience. You have a tendency that way. Your conscience takes up too much room in your general constitution, and has a bad habit of getting further swollen through inflammation.”

“I don’t understand you in the least.”
