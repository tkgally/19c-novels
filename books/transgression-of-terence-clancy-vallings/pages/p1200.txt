Nor would a temporary edifice of falsehood suffice now; the building must needs be such as would last his lifetime. By saying little, by merely allowing her to drift with public opinion, he had hoped to leave himself a loophole for final confession; but once he had deliberately, of his own free will, laid his guilt upon Simon’s shoulders, he felt that no possible combination of circumstances could ever give him strength to own to such baseness; the terror of Nell’s scorn would keep him lying on so long as the power of speech remained to him.
