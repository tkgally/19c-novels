“Then Simon has been an exile a little over four years.”

“He’ll leave his bones in a foreign land, be very sure of that,” the squire continued, slyly bestowing a lump of sugar upon Young Hopeful.

“Give me the letter, please.”

“Give me your promise first, my dear Kate?”

“You shall have the boy from two till half-past.”

“From two till three, or you never see your letter!”

The squire held up the missive enticingly, address downwards, and Master Julius promptly snatched it out of his hand. Whereupon Kate wheedled the letter from her son, and cried—

“Julius, look—look! Simon’s handwriting, and the London postmark?”

“Posted by his solicitors, no doubt,” Julius answered calmly.
