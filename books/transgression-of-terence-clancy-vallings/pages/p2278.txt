“The fact is”—thus Kate summed up the situation one morning after discussing it at some length with her husband and the squire—“the fact is, Nell and Simon have now reached the highest pitch of intimacy that they will permit themselves; and I’ve quite made up my mind that nothing will ever come of it.”

“Have you?” asked Julius ironically. “Permit me to doubt that; for a woman’s romantic hope has ninety times nine lives. In fact, nothing will ever kill it.
