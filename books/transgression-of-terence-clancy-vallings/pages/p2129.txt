“Dear lad!” he exclaimed, “I’m no better than an old woman about you. Do you know—— But I really can’t confess to such folly.”

But, encouraged by the captain’s smile, he did confess.

“Do you know that I’ve been so fidgety about the rumoured rottenness of that bridge, that I had serious thoughts of offering to repair it at my own expense?”

Julius laughed so that the mare shied and swerved.

“It’s a fact, dear lad; I did actually contemplate such a thing!”

The squire laughed as heartily as his son, and they went on chattering nonsense for some time like any brace of schoolboys.

“Ah!” said the old man, as they presently drove through the lodge gates, “if you would but take a wife and retire, and come to live with me at Bickington, I should have nothing left to wish for.”
