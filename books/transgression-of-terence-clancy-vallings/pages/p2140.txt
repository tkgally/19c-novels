For the affairs of the cricket season were to be wound up at a general meeting of the club at three o’clock, when its new captain-president, Squire Clancy, as the principal speaker and great star of the occasion, could be studied in all his new glory.

Satisfied with this compromise, Mr. Rush was soon inditing of a more interesting matter—that is, his improved relations with Miss Tredethlyn. The friendly feeling between her and himself had maintained itself during the summer; had even increased a little, though of his ancient fear there still lingered enough to make the road to Moor Gates a somewhat steep one.
