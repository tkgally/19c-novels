Under the arches of the hall and about the gravel space which lay between the ancient building and the road a large crowd was gathered; and when a smart tandem-cart came flashing over the town bridge, there arose a murmur that presently swelled into a downright cheer. The young prince received an ovation and evidently revelled in it.
