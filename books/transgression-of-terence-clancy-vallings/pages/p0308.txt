Every morning, Simon would drive her over to see his invalid father; every afternoon be with her for some hours at Moor Gates, only leaving for his observatory when darkness set in. And then? Then Nell and her father would saunter in the cool air with their guest—or Mr. Tredethlyn would despatch the young people out-of-doors while he dozed over a book in his study. On these occasions, Terence would speak mournfully of the struggle he had gone through, of the lonely life to which he was looking forward; of how, but for the tie of gratitude to Simon, he would throw up his practice and go to seek his fortune in distant lands, where hardship and danger might help a man to forget his misery—and Nell’s melting eyes would rest
