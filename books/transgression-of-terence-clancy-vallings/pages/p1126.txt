The moment Jane disappeared, Terence entered by the French window, and sat down in the breakfast-room to recover himself. But his sickness of heart resisted all argument. He was in deadly fear. He could not persuade himself but that Doidge had come to denounce him—or worse.

Finding that delay only helped to unman him, he went palpitating down the stairs, and hovered palpitating outside the consulting-room door.

“He may have chanced upon some clue,” his white lips muttered. “He may be violent—he may kill me before I can summon help. I will never face him unarmed again, if—if I but come safely off this time.”
