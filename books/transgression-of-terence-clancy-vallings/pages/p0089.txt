But at present there was no critic at hand either to praise or blame; for by capturing all hearts Terence had secured a chorus of eulogy from all around him; while as for his conscience, that was not always proof against his own eloquence, indeed often needed backing up by outside opinion. An unbiased person, however, with a perfect knowledge of the workings of Terence’s mind at this juncture, might have offered some useful platitudes by way of corrective to the arguments furnished by himself in his own favour.
